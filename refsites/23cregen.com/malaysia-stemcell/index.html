Why cGMP Matters
While conventional GMP applies to general pharmaceuticals, handling living materials like cells requires cGMP (current GMP).
cGMP is a stringent quality management standard that reflects the latest regulations and technologies, and it is essential for ensuring cell safety and efficacy.
Facilities offering stem cell therapy should comply with cGMP.

What Are Stem Cells?

Stem cell therapy is an innovative treatment that repairs damaged tissues and harnesses the body’s regenerative potential.
Stem cells are the “source” of diverse cell types—muscle, nerve, skin, and more—and can self-renew while generating new cells.
Note that the regulatory and clinical environment for stem cell therapy varies by country.

Please Be Cautious About Animal-Derived Stem Cells

Some facilities offer treatments using animal-derived stem cells. However, this approach has insufficient scientific backing in places, and its safety and efficacy require careful consideration.

First, there is an infection risk: animals may harbor pathogens unfamiliar to humans, which could be transmitted via the cells. There is also concern that animal cells may trigger immune rejection, potentially harming health.

Furthermore, robust clinical evidence supporting efficacy is limited. There is no guarantee that animal-derived cells will integrate and function appropriately in human tissues, and long-term effects remain unclear, prompting caution among many experts.

To receive safe and effective treatment, choose clinics that use human-derived stem cells. Trustworthy institutions disclose cell provenance and manage cells in culture facilities that comply with international standards. When considering therapy, be sure to confirm cell origin, culture methods, and authorization status.
