6 Reasons to Choose 23C

Why

  • Reason 01Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells

    We use umbilical cord-derived stem cells with high regenerative potential, cultured using advanced technology. They have broad therapeutic applicability and are expected to be both safe and effective.

  • Reason 02Certified In-House Lab

    We have established one of Southeast Asia's largest cell cultivation labs, approved by Malaysia's NPRA. The facility meets international standards such as cGMP and cGTPs, producing stem cells with cutting-edge technology and stringent quality control.

  • Reason 03Over 20,000 Clinical Cases

    Stem cell treatments have been implemented in medical institutions in over 70 countries, with more than 20,000 clinical applications. The evidence from these cases supports the reliability of our treatments.

  • Reason 04Global-Standard Quality

    From cultivation to quality control and clinical application, everything is managed under international standards. NPRA-certified and third-party tested, we provide safe cells in a cleanroom environment.

  • Reason 05Strict Safety Management

    A 24-hour traceability system is implemented for all cells, ensuring thorough safety management and accurate tracking of which cells were used for which patients.

  • Reason 06Dedicated Staff Support

    From the first consultation to post-treatment follow-up, our experienced dedicated staff provide full support, addressing patients' concerns and ensuring a safe and comfortable environment.
