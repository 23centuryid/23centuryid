Reasons

  • Reason 01Using Wharton's Jelly MSC

    We use stem cells from Wharton's Jelly that have high regenerative capacity and are cultured with cutting-edge techniques. This therapy has wide application coverage with a focus on safety and efficacy.

  • Reason 02Internationally Certified Cell Culture Facility

    We have a large-scale cell culture laboratory approved by Malaysia's NPRA. Cell production follows international standards such as cGMP and cGTP, with quality control and cutting-edge technology.

  • Reason 03Over 20,000 Clinical Cases

    Our therapy has been applied in over 70 countries, with over 20,000 clinical applications. This evidence-based clinical experience supports confidence in the care we provide.

  • Reason 04International Standard Quality Assurance

    From cell culture to quality assurance and clinical application, the process is controlled according to international standards. We implement third-party testing and provide cells produced in a clean room environment.

  • Reason 05Strict Risk Management and Safety

    Each sample is equipped with a 24-hour tracking system to ensure safety. We are able to accurately trace the cells used for each patient, maintaining transparency and accountability.
