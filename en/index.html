6 Reasons to Choose 23Century.id

Reason

  • Reason 1Using Wharton's Jelly MSC

    We use stem cells from Wharton's Jelly, which have high regenerative capabilities and are cultured using cutting-edge techniques. This therapy has a wide range of applications, with a focus on safety and effectiveness.

  • Reason 2International Standard-Certified Cell Culture Facility

    We have a large-scale cell culture laboratory approved by NPRA Malaysia. Cell production follows international standards such as cGMP and cGTP, with quality control and the latest technology.

  • Reason 03More than 20,000 Clinical Cases

    Our therapy has been applied in more than 70 countries, with over 20,000 clinical applications. This evidence, based on clinical experience, supports the level of confidence in the treatment we provide.

  • Reason 04International Standard Quality Assurance

    From cell culture to quality control and clinical applications, processes are controlled according to international standards. We utilize third-party testing and provide cells produced in a cleanroom environment.

  • Reason 05Strict Risk Management and Security

    Each sample is equipped with a 24-hour tracking system to ensure security. We are able to accurately track the cells used for each patient, maintaining transparency and accountability.

  • Reason 06Staff ready to support

    From initial consultation to post-therapy follow-up, our experienced staff will provide full support to reduce patient concerns and ensure comfort.
