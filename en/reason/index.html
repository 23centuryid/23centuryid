Menggunakan Wharton's Jelly MSC

Di23Centurykami use Wharton's Jelly MSC (WJ-MSC) which have ability regenerative tinggi.

Wharton's Jelly MSC berasal from tali pusar (umbilikus) bayi baru lahir and merupakan mesenchymal stem cell which have ability proliferasi tinggi as well as can berdiferensiasi menjadi berbagai jenis cell.
Oleh because that, cell this effective for perbaikan jaringan which rusak and modulasi imun, so that many dimanfaatkan in bidang treatment regenerative.

Selain that, cell this also mensekresikan faktor pertumbuhan and zat anti-inflammation in jumlah large, membantu reduce peradangan and merangsang regeneration jaringan.
Hal this making it relevan for application pada gangguan saraf, disease autoimmune, disease sendi, and lain-lain.

Fasilitas Kultur Mandiri Bersertifikat Internasional

23 Century International Life Science Centre (23C) is lembaga research stem cell international which berbasis di Kuala Lumpur, equipped facility culture and manufacturing cell terbesar di Asia Southeast.

Recognized oleh National Pharmaceutical Regulatory Agency (NPRA) Malaysia and memenuhi requirement cGTPs as well as cGMP sesuai standard PIC/S, we menyediakan produk cell quality tinggi.

Lebih dari 20.000 Rekam Klinis

For memastikan efektivitas and keselamatan Stem cell therapy diperlukan akumulasi research and data clinical selama bertahun-tahun.

23C has perform more from 20.000 studi clinical Stem cell therapy, berkontribusi pada perkembangan technique treatment bersama lembaga medical di seluruh world.

We also menyediakan technology ke more from 70 country, berupaya expand cakupan application and standardisasi Stem cell therapy.

Sistem Jaminan Kualitas Berstandar Internasional

Quality stem cell sangat menentukan keberhasilan and keamanan therapy, so that diperlukan kontrol quality which ketat.

23C apply process production which sesuai with cGMP and cGTPs, equipped cleanroom Grade A & B for mengelola culture and production cell quality tinggi secara ketat.

We has lulus audit NPRA Malaysia and memenuhi standard PIC/S, mewujudkan jaminan quality standard international.

Manajemen Risiko dan Keamanan yang Ketat

For guarantee keamanan Stem cell therapy, we memastikan traceability for setiap cell and apply system pemantauan 24 jam.

For menjaga quality, produk akhir hanya provided after lolos uji quality which ketat seperti pengujian kontaminan, skrining virus, and pemeriksaan viabilitas.

Selain that, we perform evaluasi risiko berdasarkan standard PIC/S for menyediakan Stem cell therapy which safe.
