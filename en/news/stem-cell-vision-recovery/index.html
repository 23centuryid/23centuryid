A Man Regains Sight After 30 Years, A Miracle of Stem Cell Therapy

22 Agustus 2025
  • News

In Malaysia, a man who had been blind for over 30 years successfully regained his vision through innovative and advanced stem cell therapy. Tei Shion Tien had spent decades living in darkness; however, following this groundbreaking treatment, he is now able to perceive light once again.

This success demonstrates the significant potential within the medical field. Through the application of stem cell therapy, vision that had long been lost can finally be restored, offering hope to many.
