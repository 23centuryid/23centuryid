5-year-old Charlie received NK therapy at 23Century and demonstrated improvement.

2 Oktober 2025
  • News

On September 29, 2025, the Cambodian news site "Find Your News" reported on the journey of a 5-year-old boy named Ponleu Vireakbot (nicknamed Charlie) in his fight against stage 4 medulloblastoma (a malignant brain tumor).

Read the full report on Find Your News.

Charlie was diagnosed with a brain tumor on February 10, 2024, and subsequently underwent surgery in Thailand to remove the tumor. Following the surgery, he completed 30 sessions of radiotherapy and 10 courses of chemotherapy. Unfortunately, the tumor recurred, and doctors have recommended palliative care.

His family persevered and continued to explore alternative treatment methods, ultimately opting for 23Century, a Malaysia-based facility. At this facility, Charlie underwent immunotherapy utilizing NK (Natural Killer) cells. The treatment results were highly satisfactory, with tumor markers decreasing from 3.6 to 2.1. According to his family, following the treatment, Charlie's condition significantly improved, pain was reduced, and his quality of life enhanced.

Learn more about NK Cell Therapy

Following over a year of battling his illness, treatment at 23Century provided renewed hope for Charlie and his family. This story of perseverance and recovery underscores the immense potential of NK cell immunotherapy in confronting challenging cancers.
