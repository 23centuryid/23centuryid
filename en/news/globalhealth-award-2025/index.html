23C Secured an International Award for Vision Restoration Through Stem Cell Therapy

25 Juni 2025
  • News

We are proud to announce that 23 Century International Life Science Centre has received the prestigious award "2025 Stem Cell Therapy Center for Vision Restoration of the Year in Malaysia" at the GLOBALHEALTH Asia-Pacific Summit, Conference and Awards 2025, held in June 2025.

This international award is presented to the leading healthcare institution in the Asia Pacific region that has demonstrated exceptional dedication to the development of innovative therapies. This achievement reflects our commitment to excellence in cell regeneration research and the clinical application of stem cell therapy for the restoration of visual function.

We are deeply grateful for this recognition from the international medical community. This achievement is the result of the hard work of our entire team, ongoing research, and, most importantly, the trust of our patients who have entrusted us with their health.

This award motivates us to continuously innovate and elevate the standards of care in stem cell therapy. We are committed to delivering exceptional services using cutting-edge technology and medical approaches underpinned by robust scientific evidence.

We extend our sincere gratitude to all medical staff, researchers, and members of the 23Century team for their contributions to this success. We also thank all our patients for their trust.

We are dedicated to continuously delivering further benefits to enhance the quality of life for our patients through our innovative and effective stem cell therapy.
