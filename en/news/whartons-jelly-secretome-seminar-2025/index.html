Wharton's Jelly Stem Cell and Secretome Seminar 2025

Tanggal: 23 Maret 2025

Dengan hormat kami mengumumkan penyelenggaraan "Seminar Wharton's Jelly Stem Cell & Secretome 2025" sesuai dengan jadwal yang tertera di bawah.

Seminar Introduction

This exclusive seminar will feature an expert speaker from Malaysia who will share cutting-edge insights into Wharton's Jelly mesenchymal stem cells (WJ-MSC) and secretome therapy. The speaker will provide a detailed and accessible explanation of:

  • Recent advancements in Wharton's Jelly stem cell research
  • Mechanism of action and clinical benefits of secretome
  • Practical applications in regenerative medicine
  • International standards and safety of therapy
  • Successful case studies and clinical outcomes

This seminar is specifically designed for medical professionals with a profound interest in the advancements of stem cell therapy and regenerative medicine. Participants will have a unique opportunity to learn about the latest innovations directly from experienced experts.

Seminar Content

This seminar will feature in-depth presentations on:

  • Dasar-dasar Wharton's Jelly MSC: Sumber sel, karakteristik unik, dan keunggulan dibandingkan sel punca lainnya
  • Secretome dan Mekanisme Kerjanya: Komposisi, fungsi biologis, dan potensi terapi
  • Aplikasi Klinis: Indikasi medis, protokol terapi, dan hasil penelitian terkini
  • Standar Keamanan: Regulasi internasional (CGMP, PIC/S), quality control, dan good manufacturing practices
  • Sesi Tanya Jawab: Kesempatan untuk bertanya langsung kepada para ahli
