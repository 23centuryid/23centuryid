Seminar Wharton's Jelly Stem Cell & Secretome 2025

Tanggal: 23 Maret 2025

We are honored to announce the holding of "Seminar Wharton's Jelly Stem Cell & Secretome 2025According to the schedule listed below.

Seminar Introduction

This exclusive seminar will feature an expert speaker from Malaysia who will provide up-to-date insights into Wharton's Jelly mesenchymal stem cells (WJ-MSC) and secretome therapy. The speaker will provide a detailed and easily comprehensible explanation of:

  • Latest developments in Wharton's Jelly stem cell research
  • Mechanism of action and clinical benefits of secretome
  • Practical applications in regenerative medicine
  • International standards and therapy safety
  • Successful case studies and clinical outcomes

This seminar is specifically tailored for medical professionals with a profound interest in the advancements of stem cell therapy and regenerative medicine. Attendees will gain an unparalleled opportunity to explore cutting-edge innovations directly from experienced experts.

Seminar Content

This seminar features in-depth presentations on:

  • Dasar-dasar Wharton's Jelly MSC:Cell source, unique characteristics, and advantages compared to other stem cells
  • Secretome dan Mekanisme Kerjanya:Composition, biological function, and therapeutic potential
  • Aplikasi Klinis:Medical indications, therapy protocols, and latest research findings
  • Standar Keamanan: Regulasi internasional (CGMP, PIC/S), quality control, dan good manufacturing practices
  • Sesi Tanya Jawab:The opportunity to ask the experts directly
