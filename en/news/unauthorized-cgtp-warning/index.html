Warning Fasilitas Stem cell therapy Without Izin

22 April 2025
  • News

Malaysia Memiliki Aturan Ketat untuk Terapi Cell and Gen

For perform therapy regenerative seperti "stem cell therapy" or "therapy gen" in Malaysia, diperlukan kepatuhan terhadap aturan pemerintah which ketat.

Produk which used in therapy this dikelola with ketat oleh Badan Pengawas Drug National (NPRA) in bawah Kementerian Health Malaysia (MOH). Selain that, hukum Malaysia (Peraturan Pengendalian Drug and Kosmetik Years 1984) secara jelas menyatakan bahwa memproduksi, menjual, or provide produk which not terdaftar in pemerintah that sendiri is ilegal.

With kata lain, menyediakan cell therapy without izin can mengakibatkan pelanggaran hukum. For chose care which safe, important for memverifikasi is facility and produk has mendapatkan izin which tepat.

Panduan Resmi NPRA

Daftar Fasilitas Stem cell Bersertifikat NPRA

NPRA menerbitkan daftar facility manufacturing produk cell therapy and gen which have kepatuhan GMP for show with jelas facility which in in negeri which memenuhi standard in atas. According daftar latest per 29 Juli 2024, hanya there is 8 facility manufacturing produk cell therapy and gen which certified NPRA in Malaysia.

Facility Manufacturing Produk Cell therapy and Gen Certified NPRA

Facility which tercantum in daftar this has diverifikasi through inspeksi in location oleh NPRA for kepatuhan standard GMP, which berarti lingkungan and procedure manufacturing produk cell therapy dikelola sesuai standard international.

With kata lain, if facility which not tercantum in daftar resmi this memproduksi or provide stem cell secara clinical, possibility large facility tersebut belum mendapatkan certification NPRA.

Patient and professional health disarankan for memverifikasi is facility which menyediakan produk cell therapy and gen tercakup in daftar certification this sebagai wrong satu langkah important in memastikan keamanan.

Risk Menggunakan Fasilitas Without Izin

Produk cell therapy and gen which diproduksi in facility without izin have risk serius berikut this.

Risk Safety:

Produk cell therapy and gen which diproduksi and given in lingkungan without manajemen GMP/GTP which tepat possibility large not have kontrol sterilitas and pemeriksaan quality which memadai, so that enhance risk infeksi bakteri and virus as well as reaksi imun which not terduga.

Actually, in luar negeri has dilaporkan kasus-kasus in which patient which receive stem cell therapy without persetujuan experience infeksi serius, pembentukan tumor, and bahkan kematian, show bahwa kurangnya kontrol quality can present bahaya which mengancam nyawa.

Ketidakpastian Effectiveness and Kualitas:

In therapy which belum melewati verifikasi scientific, effectiveness and keamanan not guaranteed. Although mengeluarkan cost high, efek which diharapkan mungkin not tercapai, and in beberapa kasus can happen perburukan condition disease.

However, has known bahwa beberapa clinic swasta offer therapy without persetujuan with slogan for disease sendi, diabetes, anti-penuaan, and lain-lain. Patient harus berhati-liver so that not tertipu oleh promosi which berlebihan seperti that.

Problem Hukum and Etika:

Seperti disebutkan before, facility without izin possibility large merupakan pelanggaran undang-undang, and if happen problem or kerusakan health in facility tersebut, sulit for mendapatkan bantuan public.

Therapy which given without izin from otoritas pengawas mungkin not have system informed consent which memadai or protokol respons for efek samping, which can menyebabkan ketidakjelasan about tanggung jawab if patient menderita disadvantage.

Selain that, spend time and cost in facility tersebut also berarti loss opportunity for receive care reguler which didukung bukti, so that diperlukan penilaian which cermat in pemilihan care health.
