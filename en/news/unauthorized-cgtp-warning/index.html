Warning: Unlicensed Stem Cell Therapy Facility

22 April 2025
  • News

Malaysia has strict regulations for Cell and Gene Therapy.

The conduct of regenerative therapies, such as stem cell therapy or gene therapy, in Malaysia necessitates strict compliance with stringent government regulations.

The products utilized in this therapy are strictly regulated by the National Pharmaceutical Regulatory Agency (NPRA) under the Ministry of Health Malaysia (MOH). Furthermore, Malaysian law (Control of Drugs and Cosmetics Regulations 1984) expressly stipulates that producing, selling, or supplying unregistered products is illegal.

In other words, providing unauthorized cell therapy can lead to legal violations. To ensure safe treatment, it is crucial to verify that both the facility and the products have obtained the necessary approvals.

NPRA Official Guide

Directory of NPRA-Certified Stem Cell Facilities

The NPRA publishes a list of cell and gene therapy product manufacturing facilities that demonstrate Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) compliance, clearly indicating which domestic facilities meet the aforementioned standards. According to the latest list as of July 29, 2024, there are only 8 NPRA-certified cell and gene therapy product manufacturing facilities in Malaysia.

NPRA-Certified Cell and Gene Therapy Product Manufacturing Facility

The facilities listed herein have been verified through on-site inspection by NPRA for compliance with GMP standards, signifying that the manufacturing environment and procedures for cell therapy products are managed in accordance with international standards.

In other words, if a facility not listed on this official register produces or clinically provides stem cells, it is highly probable that it has not obtained NPRA certification.

Patients and healthcare professionals are advised to verify whether facilities providing cell and gene therapy products are covered in this certification list as an essential step in ensuring safety.

The Risk of Unauthorized Facility Use

Cell and gene therapy products manufactured in unlicensed facilities pose the following serious risks.

Safety Risk

Cell and gene therapy products manufactured and administered in environments lacking proper GMP/GTP management are highly likely to lack adequate sterility and quality controls, thereby increasing the risk of bacterial and viral infections and unexpected immune reactions.

Internationally, cases have been documented where patients undergoing unapproved stem cell therapies developed severe infections, tumor formation, and even fatalities. This underscores that inadequate quality control can present life-threatening hazards.

Uncertainty of Effectiveness and Quality

For therapies lacking scientific validation, effectiveness and safety cannot be guaranteed. Despite significant expense, anticipated benefits may not be achieved, and in some cases, the patient's condition may deteriorate.

However, it has come to light that certain private clinics offer therapies without regulatory approval, promoted with claims for joint diseases, diabetes, anti-aging, and similar conditions. Patients are advised to exercise vigilance to avoid being deceived by such exaggerated promotions.

Legal and Ethical Issues

As previously mentioned, unlicensed facilities likely constitute a violation of the law, and should health issues or harm arise at such facilities, obtaining public assistance would be challenging.

Therapy administered without the authorization of a regulatory authority may lack adequate informed consent systems or protocols for responding to adverse effects, which can lead to ambiguity regarding liability if a patient suffers harm.

Furthermore, the expenditure of time and resources at such facilities also means foregoing the opportunity to receive regular, evidence-based care, thus necessitating meticulous evaluation in healthcare selection.
