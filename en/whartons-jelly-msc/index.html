Immunomodulatory Effect

Typically, cells from another individual are recognized as foreign by the immune system. However, Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells possess characteristics that make them less susceptible to immune attack.

It also releases compounds that suppress excessive immune responses, and possesses anti-inflammatory properties that help restore physiological balance.

By operating in this manner, they can be utilized for treatment even if HLA (immune compatibility) types do not perfectly match, which provides the advantage of broader patient applicability.

Furthermore, effects on autoimmune diseases attributable to a favorable immune status are also anticipated, and research is currently underway.

Paracrine Effect

"Paracrine effect" refers to the phenomenon in which cells secrete bioactive substances, such as cytokines and growth factors, into the extracellular space, where these substances then act on adjacent cells to elicit a variety of effects.

Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells secrete a variety of beneficial substances into their microenvironment, stimulating neighboring cells to promote tissue repair and reduce inflammation.

While stem cells themselves may not directly differentiate into damaged tissue, they can activate the function of surrounding cells via secreted factors, and ultimately aid in the healing of the damaged tissue.

For example, it has been reported that secretory factors contained within Wharton's Jelly Stem Cell conditioned medium (a medium derived from cultured cells) promote the proliferation of dermal fibroblasts (cells responsible for wound healing) and enhance the expression of genes involved in wound healing.

In this manner, through paracrine effects, Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells indirectly promote tissue repair via adjacent cells and exert therapeutic effects.
