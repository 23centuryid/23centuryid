The Best Stem Cells Unlocking the Future of Regenerative Therapy

Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells (WJ-MSC) are stem cells with very high regenerative capabilities among various types of stem cells.
We use Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells in our treatment.

Wharton's Jelly is a gel-like tissue found within the fetal umbilical cord (umbilical cord), and is a mesenchymal tissue that forms during fetal development.
This tissue contains stem cells that can differentiate into various types of cells, and the number of stem cells that can be extracted from a single umbilical cord is very large.

Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells are umbilical cord-derived stem cells extracted from the umbilical cord's gelatinous tissue. They are referred to as "0-year-old stem cells" because they originate from newborns, making them exceptionally young and full of vitality.

Due to their inherent immaturity, Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells possess superior self-renewal and differentiation capabilities, and have been scientifically proven to excel in tissue regeneration and immunomodulation.

Given the direct impact of cell quality on therapeutic efficacy in stem cell therapy, we utilize Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells with the highest potency to deliver superior therapeutic efficacy.

Effectiveness of Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells

Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells are mesenchymal stem cells extracted from the umbilical cord, and have garnered significant attention in the field of regenerative therapy due to their remarkable capabilities.
Specifically, the following characteristics lead them to be called "miracle stem cells," from which high therapeutic effectiveness can be expected.

High Proliferative Capacity

Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells have a very high proliferation rate.
This means the ability to significantly increase the number of cells in a short time.

Therefore, the required cells can be cultivated and prepared quickly, and can be used immediately in the field of regenerative therapy.
Reports have shown that the proliferation rate is faster compared to stem cells derived from adult tissues, such as bone marrow-derived stem cells.

Differentiation Capability

Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells possess superior differentiation capabilities, allowing them to differentiate into various cell types including bone, cartilage, adipose tissue, muscle, and neural cells.

With this flexible differentiation capability, it is possible to repair multiple tissues from a single type of stem cell, and it is expected to meet various medical needs, such as regenerative therapy for damaged organs and tissues.

Anti-inflammatory Effect

Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells have prominent anti-inflammatory effects (inflammation-suppressing action).
This modulates the overactive response caused by the immune system, suppresses the production of inflammatory cytokines (inflammatory substances released by immune cells), and reduces swelling and tissue damage.

This enables the alleviation of chronic inflammation in disease and the improvement of an environment conducive to tissue repair.

Immunomodulatory Effect

Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells have the power to precisely control immune function.
Therefore, the transplanted cells are not easily attacked by the body's immune system, and the risk of rejection can be suppressed.

Typically, cells from another individual are recognized by the immune system as foreign. However, Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells possess characteristics that make them less susceptible to immune attack.

It also secretes substances that suppress excessive immune responses, and possesses actions to mitigate bodily inflammation and promote homeostasis.

This method enables their use in treatment even with imperfect HLA (immune compatibility) matching, providing the benefit of applicability to a broader patient population.

Furthermore, effects on autoimmune diseases resulting from stochastic immune events are also anticipated, and research is currently underway.

Paracrine Effect

The paracrine effect refers to the phenomenon in which cells secrete bioactive substances, such as cytokines and growth factors, into their local environment, subsequently acting on adjacent cells to elicit a variety of effects.

Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells secrete a diverse array of beneficial factors into their surrounding microenvironment, which stimulate adjacent cells to promote tissue repair and mitigate inflammation.

Although stem cells themselves do not directly differentiate into damaged tissue, they are capable of stimulating the functions of adjacent cells via secreted factors, thereby ultimately facilitating the repair of the damaged tissue.

For example, it has been reported that secretory factors contained within Wharton's Jelly Stem Cell conditioned medium (the fluid obtained from cultured cells) promote the proliferation of dermal fibroblasts (cells responsible for wound healing) and enhance the expression of genes involved in wound healing.

In this manner, Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells, via paracrine effects, indirectly promote tissue repair by influencing surrounding cells and exert therapeutic effects.

Rigorous and Unique Cell Selection Standards 23C

23C not only uses Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells, but also selects cells with higher activity.
To maximize cell purity and differentiation ability, only cells up to the 4th generation (P4) were used, and senescent and low-functioning cells were removed.

What is the Cell up to 4th Generation (P4)?
This refers to the use of only cells up to the 4th passage (P4) when culturing stem cells.
When stem cells are cultured repeatedly, they gradually age, and their ability to divide and differentiate declines.
Therefore, by only using the youngest cells up to P4 and maintaining high activity, therapeutic effectiveness can be maximized and safety is guaranteed.
