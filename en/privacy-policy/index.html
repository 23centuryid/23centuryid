23Century.id (URL: https://23century.id) sets forth the following Privacy Policy (hereinafter, "this Policy") to ensure the proper protection of user personal information and guarantee privacy. We comply with applicable regulations.

Definition of Personal Information

"Personal Information" refers to information concerning a living individual that enables the identification of a specific individual based on details such as name, date of birth, address, phone number, or email address. Information that cannot directly identify an individual but can be readily combined with other information to identify that person also falls under the category of personal information. This includes gender, date of birth, IP address, or Cookie-generated identifiers.

Methods of Collection and Types of Personal Information Collected

We collect personal information from users through our "Contact Us" form or email, to the extent necessary for service provision. This includes name, contact information (phone number or email address), the content of the inquiry, and relevant health information.

When users utilize our services, we may receive information regarding health status, medical history, diagnoses from healthcare institutions, and details concerning treatment plans. This health information is highly sensitive, and we collect it only with the user's explicit consent.

We also automatically collect usage information, including browser type, IP address, Cookie data, referrer URL, time of access, type of access, and device operating system, when users visit our Site.

Purpose of Personal Information Use

The personal information we collect is primarily used for:

  • Providing our services (including service descriptions, medical consultations, and medical travel support)
  • Responding to user inquiries and requests (including identity verification).
  • Delivering essential user services, including medical travel coordination and cultivating relationships with local partners.
  • Managing communication with service providers and collaborative partners (e.g., sharing information with medical institutions in Malaysia)
  • Providing updates on the latest medical services and knowledge, subject to user consent.
  • Conducting surveys to enhance services and develop new features
  • Comply with laws and regulations and implement internal legal processes.
  • Another objective related to the above

Restriction on Use for Purposes Other Than Intended

We do not use personal data beyond the scope of the stated purposes. When modifying the purposes for which personal data is used, we will only do so if the new purpose is substantially related to the original purpose, or if we obtain the user's prior consent.

Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

We do not disclose personal information to third parties without prior user consent, except in the following cases:

  • When required by law
  • When the protection of life, health, or property is required, but user consent is difficult to obtain.
  • When particularly necessary for public interest or the healthy development of children, yet user consent is difficult to obtain.
  • When cooperation with national or local governments is required for the enforcement of law.
  • With user consent (e.g., sharing information with service providers or travel agents)
  • In the event of a corporate merger or business separation, personal data may be inherited.

When entrusting personal data processing to third parties, we oversee to ensure its proper handling.

The Use of Cookies and IP Addresses

Our website uses Cookie technology to enhance user experience and improve our services. Cookies are small data files stored on the user's device via the web browser. They enable us to store language settings and previous form information to customize the appearance of web pages.

Our website utilizes Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, to collect traffic data such as user flow and pages visited. Google Analytics employs cookies. The data collected is managed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. This data does not contain personally identifiable information and is used by us as a reference for service and content improvement.

Users may disable cookies via their browser settings; however, certain functionalities may not operate as intended.

Protection and Management of Personal Information

We implement regulatory-compliant security measures to protect collected personal information from leakage, loss, and damage. Information acquired via communication channels, such as contact forms, is encrypted using SSL connections and firewalls to prevent unauthorized access and alteration. We also provide data protection training to employees and partners to ensure secure handling.

Disclosure, Correction, Erasure, and Restriction of Personal Data Use

Upon a user's request for the disclosure of their personal information, we will verify their identity and endeavor to respond promptly, except where legally impermissible or where such disclosure would prejudice the legitimate rights of third parties.

Should a user find their personal information to be inaccurate, they may request its rectification or update. We will honor such requests upon successful identity verification, unless accurate information is unavailable or the retention of the information is legally mandated.

Should users wish to restrict or delete their personal information we hold, please contact us via our contact form. We will verify your identity before taking action; however, we may be unable to fulfill the request for deletion if we are legally required to retain the information.

Personal Information about Minors

The provision of personal information by individuals under the age of 18 requires the consent of their parents or guardians.

Legal Compliance and Continuous Improvement

We comply with applicable laws and regulations concerning the processing of personal data and are committed to its appropriate management through continuous improvement and periodic review.
