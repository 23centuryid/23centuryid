Keamanan and Regulasi Stem cell therapy in Luar Negeri - Country Which that Harus Dihindari?

14 Maret 2025
  • Stem cell & Regenerative Therapy

There is peningkatan minat terhadap pilihan stem cell therapy in beberapa years terakhir. Especially, although therapy which use stem cell autologous is arus main in Jepang, jumlah orang which ingin receive therapy with stem cell which more young continuously increased.

However, for receive therapy use stem cell young which not autologous, perlu chose facility medical in luar negeri. In hal this, many orang merasa sulit for make keputusan which tepat because information which can trusted terbatas mengenai country which which dipilih.

Oleh because that, artikel this explain about country-country in Asia Southeast which menyediakan stem cell therapy, especially country-country which memerlukan perhatian special terhadap keamanan.

Peningkatan Clinic Ilegal: Thailand

When You looking for "Thailand stem cell", artikel news with judul seperti "Semakin many orang asing to country which hanya in world in which stem cell therapy memungkinkan" sering appear in peringkat atas.

However, many from artikel this didasarkan on information from 2007 and mungkin different from situasi time this. When looking for information about stem cell therapy in luar negeri in bahasa Jepang, sering kali information not diperbarui with information latest, so that verifikasi liver-liver diperlukan.

In past, kecelakaan serius related with stem cell therapy happen in Thailand, and pemerintah then melarang stem cell therapy for disease selain disease tertentu (leukemia, multiple myeloma, imunodefisiensi bawaan, cancer blood lainnya).

Akibatnya, not there is standard keamanan integrated related budidaya stem cell, and there is report kasus in which stem cell which dibawa from luar negeri used.

Selain that, mengenai pengambilan stem cell, kelayakan donor not jelas, and in beberapa place there is kekhawatiran about possibility keterlibatan in penjualan organ ilegal.

Selanjutnya, iklan berlebihan related clinic stem cell in Thailand became problem, and according report date 31 Oktober 2024, more from 400 gugatan dilaporkan diajukan setiap years (Sumber: Nation Thailand).

In Thailand, penyediaan stem cell therapy without izin became problem, and on years 2024, pemerintah sedang mengungkap clinic stem cell which not tersertifikasi and mengeluarkan perintah penutupan for facility which run practice medical ilegal. According Kementerian Health Thailand (MOPH), stem cell therapy terbatas on "leukemia, multiple myeloma, imunodefisiensi bawaan, and cancer blood lainnya", and treatment for disease lain mungkin dianggap ilegal.

Sumber: Kementerian Health Thailand (MOPH)

Country Lain where Stem cell therapy Swasta Dianggap Ilegal

Bahkan in country-country in which regulation hukum dulu samar, now there is kasus in which penyediaan stem cell therapy secara jelas dianggap ilegal. In country-country this, facility medical and perusahaan which not tersertifikasi mungkin mencoba menyediakan stem cell therapy, and important for memastikan regulation hukum which sesuai.

Selain that, there is report kasus in which beberapa bisnis and clinic in Jepang mengiklankan stem cell therapy in luar negeri without menyadari bahwa hal that ilegal. For receive care which sesuai, important for secara comprehensive menyelidiki regulation hukum latest from country penyedia.

Korea

In Korea, budidaya stem cell in place selain perusahaan farmasi which tersertifikasi not diperbolehkan.

Oleh because that, there is kekhawatiran bahwa stem cell which ditanam secara ilegal mungkin beredar in pasar international, and diperlukan perhatian. There is also report about care which performed without izin for orang asing.

In Korea, budidaya and production stem cell diatur oleh Badan Keamanan Makanan and Drug-obatan (MFDS), and hanya perusahaan farmasi berlisensi and facility medical which ditunjuk which can melaksanakannya. Budidaya stem cell without izin or penjualan dianggap sebagai pelanggaran Undang-Undang Farmasi, and pelanggar mungkin dikenai sanksi.

Sumber: Badan Keamanan Makanan and Drug-obatan Korea (MFDS)

Taiwan

In Taiwan, undang-undang care regenerative and peraturan produk care regenerative diterima on years 2024, and undang-undang which jelas has ditetapkan.

  • Implementasi in fasilitas medical sangat important:
    Stem cell therapy hanya diperbolehkan disediakan in fasilitas medical that memiliki sertifikasi that sesuai.
  • Persetujuan pemerintah and kewajiban pendaftaran:
    Persetujuan from Departemen Kesejahteraan Health diperlukan, and pendaftaran more lanjut with otoritas yurisdiksi local diperlukan.
  • Standar kualitas produk stem cell therapy:
    Produk treatment regenerative diklasifikasikan sebagai medications, and manufaktur in fasilitas berstandar GMP diwajibkan. When diproduksi or diimpor oleh pembuat obat, aplikasi pemeriksaan and pendaftaran to Departemen Kesejahteraan Health diperlukan.

In Taiwan, "Undang-Undang Care Regenerative" and "Peraturan Produk Care Regenerative" disahkan on Juni 2024. With undang-undang this, penyediaan stem cell therapy terbatas on facility medical which approved pemerintah, and penyediaan in facility non-medical dianggap ilegal. For melaksanakan stem cell therapy, persetujuan from Department Kesejahteraan Health and pendaftaran more lanjut with otoritas yurisdiksi local wajib performed.

Produk care regenerative diklasifikasikan sebagai drug-obatan, and manufacturing in facility standard GMP diwajibkan. Time diproduksi or diimpor, application pemeriksaan and pendaftaran to Department Kesejahteraan Health very important, and pelanggaran mungkin dikenai sanksi.

Sumber: Department Kesejahteraan Health Taiwan (MOHW)

Indonesia

Pemerintah Indonesia sedang mengerjakan penyusunan standard for memastikan keamanan stem cell therapy.

  • Fasilitas penyedia stem cell therapy:
    Pada now, hanya hospital pendidikan country that can melakukan stem cell therapy, and stem cell therapy swasta dianggap ilegal.
  • Tindakan special:
    Sebuah zona ekonomi medical has didirikan in distrik Sanur in Bali, and ada kemungkinan bahwa technology stem cell from luar negeri will diperkenalkan in bawah kondisi tertentu.

In Indonesia, penyediaan stem cell therapy dibatasi oleh hukum and peraturan hanya for rumah pain pendidikan country. According Peraturan Kementerian Health No. 32 which ditetapkan on years 2014, facility medical which can menyediakan stem cell therapy terbatas on rumah pain which ditunjuk country, and penyediaan in clinic swasta umum dianggap ilegal.

Selain that, zona ekonomi medical has didirikan in distrik Sanur in Bali, and there is possibility bahwa technology stem cell from luar negeri will diperkenalkan in bawah condition tertentu.

Sumber: Kementerian Health Indonesia (Kemenkes)

Filipina

Pemerintah Filipina has memperkenalkan regulation ketat for memastikan keamanan stem cell therapy.

  • Hanya clinic stem cell that tersusun deep hospital big that diizinkan:
    Fasilitas that melakukan stem cell therapy terbatas pada fasilitas medical that has lulus pemeriksaan ketat.
  • Kerusakan health from treatment ilegal:
    Ada laporan kematian from treatment in clinic swasta ilegal, and pemerintah mendesak public untuk berhati-liver.

Pemerintah Filipina mengeluarkan Perintah Administratif AO 2013-0012 on years 2013, membatasi penyediaan stem cell therapy hanya on facility which recognized pemerintah. For melaksanakan stem cell therapy, facility tersebut harus became facility medical which recognized oleh Kementerian Health Filipina (DOH), and penyediaan in clinic lain dianggap ilegal.

There is report kerusakan health from stem cell therapy ilegal, and especially on years 2019, kematian patient which receive care without certification dikonfirmasi. Pemerintah Filipina run penegakan hukum which ketat terhadap care without izin, and pelanggar mungkin dikenai sanksi.

Sumber: Kementerian Health Filipina (DOH)

Vietnam

Although kerangka hukum which jelas related stem cell therapy in Vietnam belum sepenuhnya disiapkan, pernyataan has dirilis which mengatakan bahwa hal that ilegal kecuali for beberapa uji coba clinical and research.

  • Deep kasus uji coba klinis/research:
    Diperlukan untuk lulus tinjauan, evaluasi, and persetujuan from Komite Etika Riset Biomedis National.
  • Treatment stem cell without izin dilarang:
    Penyediaan stem cell therapy komersial tidak diizinkan now.

Although kerangka hukum which jelas related stem cell therapy in Vietnam belum sepenuhnya disiapkan, penyediaan stem cell therapy komersial without persetujuan Komite Etika Riset Biomedis National dilarang.

Time this, stem cell therapy recognized "hanya sebagai research clinical", and penyediaan without izin mungkin merupakan pelanggaran Undang-Undang Medical. On years 2023, clinic which run stem cell therapy without izin ditangkap, and pemerintah sedang enhance peraturan.

Sumber: Kementerian Health Vietnam (MOH)

With cara this, many country has menetapkan regulation hukum ketat and system related keamanan stem cell therapy for melindungi health masyarakat.

In sisi lain, there is pengusaha in Jepang which menyediakan therapy use stem cell which not approved oleh country manapun with membawanya from luar negeri. However, in many country, tindakan semacam that dianggap ilegal, and pertimbangan which cermat diperlukan.

Untuk Accept Stem cell therapy with Aman in Luar Negeri - Malaysia

Time this, in Asia, hanya Jepang and Malaysia which memungkinkan stem cell therapy which safe diterima.

Although therapy use stem cell autologous is arus main in Jepang, if You ingin receive therapy use stem cell Wharton's Jelly which young and healthy, Malaysia became pilihan which tepat.

Although not there is undang-undang special related regulation care stem cell therapy, there is regulation related keamanan and pedoman in care. In bawah pengawasan pemerintah, dimungkinkan for receive care in lingkungan in which keamanan and quality guaranteed.

Panduan Research and Treatment Stem cell and Based Cell (Edisi to-3)

Selain that, penyediaan stem cell therapy oleh facility medical without izin mungkin violate Undang-Undang Facility and Service Medical Swasta (PHFS Act 1998), and became subjek regulation oleh otoritas pengawas, so that dimungkinkan for receive care in lingkungan in which keamanan and quality guaranteed in bawah pengawasan pemerintah.

In Malaysia, regulation stem cell therapy dikelola berdasarkan "Pedoman Research and Treatment Stem cell and Based Cell" which ditetapkan on years 2009, and stem cell therapy hanya can dilaksanakan in facility medical berlisensi. Penggunaan stem cell embrionik human dilarang, and treatment with stem cell mesenchymal (MSC) dikelola with liver-liver sebagai research clinical.

For melaksanakan stem cell therapy, perlu mendapatkan persetujuan from Komite Research Etika Stem cell National (NSCERT) in bawah pengawasan Kementerian Health Malaysia (MOH). Selain that, manajemen quality produk stem cell therapy dikelola oleh Badan Regulation Farmasi National (NPRA), and kepatuhan terhadap standard GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) wajib performed.

Sumber: Kementerian Health Malaysia (MOH)

In Malaysia, penggunaan stem cell hematopoietik and stem cell which berasal from tali pusar (including stem cell Wharton's Jelly) recognized, and facility which menyediakan stem cell therapy harus mendapatkan persetujuan from MOH and Subkomite Etika Research Stem cell National (NSCERT). Penyediaan stem cell therapy oleh facility medical without izin mungkin violate Undang-Undang Facility and Service Medical Swasta (PHFS Act 1998) and became subjek regulation oleh otoritas pengawas.

Sumber: Badan Regulation Farmasi National (NPRA)
