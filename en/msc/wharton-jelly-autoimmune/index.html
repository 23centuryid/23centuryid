What Itu Disease Autoimun?

Jumlah patient disease autoimmune in Jepang diperkirakan achieve sekitar 8,5 juta when menggabungkan disease autoimmune sistemik umum and disease autoimmune spesifik organ seperti disease Crohn.

In antara disease autoimmune, many which developed without penyebab which jelas. Although beberapa disappear with sendirinya, some large is disease kronis, and penderita memerlukan pemantauan with drug sepanjang hidup.

Who that Menderita Disease Autoimun?

Selama bertahun-years, penyebab disease autoimmune dianggap not jelas, however research latest has mengungkapkan bahwa disease tersebut caused oleh infeksi with "Virus Epstein-Barr (EBV, virus EB)".

Virus EBV this menginfeksi sekitar 90-100% orang Jepang on usia dewasa. And tetap terinfeksi seumur hidup without dihilangkan.

Although there is perbedaan in usia onset and perbedaan gender tergantung penyakitnya, perlu diingat bahwa all orang have risk develop disease.

Therapy Disease Autoimun

In beberapa years terakhir, treatment disease autoimmune has increased secara significant. Selain drug steroid, drug imunosupresif which menekan reaksi kekebalan tubuh which berlebihan and reaksi inflammation in in tubuh has developed.

However, drug-obatan this not hanya menekan respons autoimmune tetapi also menekan respons kekebalan which diperlukan, make infeksi more mudah and enhance risk cancer. There is efek samping which serius seperti kerusakan kidney, tekanan blood high, gagal heart, disease nerve, radang sendi lungs, kerusakan liver, and diabetes, which merupakan challenge in mencegah efek samping.

Selain that, tujuan treatment is menekan symptom disease autoimmune, tetapi because some large disease became kronis after developed, beban fisik and psikologis on patient very large.

Especially, artritis reumatoid have many patient, and berbagai drug tersedia. Symptom artritis reumatoid developed most fast in 2 years pertama after onset. Oleh because that, important for mengunjungi rumah pain more awal and mulai mengobati for menghentikan kerusakan sendi and mempertahankan fungsi sendi.

Bisakah Disease Autoimun Diobati Without Obat?

On zaman now, semakin many orang which berusaha looking for recovery through treatment alternative seperti drug-obatan oriental without use drug-obatan.

However, bagi patient with disease autoimmune, very important for mempertimbangkan bahwa penundaan diagnosis and treatment can memperpendek usia.

Although 20 years lalu, disease kolagen, lupus eritematosus sistemik, which sering happen on wanita young, have tingkat survival 5 years hanya 50%, pengembangan drug steroid and imunosupresif has enhance tingkat survival 5 years became more from 90%.
