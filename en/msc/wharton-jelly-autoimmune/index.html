What Is Autoimmune Disease?

In Japan, the total number of patients with autoimmune diseases is estimated to be approximately 8.5 million, encompassing both common systemic autoimmune diseases and organ-specific autoimmune diseases such as Crohn's disease.

Among autoimmune diseases, many develop without a clear etiology. While some resolve spontaneously, the majority are chronic conditions, requiring lifelong medication and monitoring for affected individuals.

Who is affected by autoimmune diseases?

For many years, the cause of autoimmune diseases was considered unclear; however, recent research has revealed that these conditions are caused by infection with the Epstein-Barr Virus (EBV).

The EBV virus infects approximately 90-100% of Japanese adults, and individuals remain infected for life without viral clearance.

Despite disease-specific variations in age of onset and gender, it is crucial to remember that all individuals are at risk of developing a disease.

Treatment of Autoimmune Diseases

In recent years, the treatment of autoimmune diseases has improved significantly. In addition to steroid medications, immunosuppressive drugs have been developed to suppress the body's excessive immune and inflammatory responses.

However, these medications not only suppress autoimmune responses but also essential immune responses, increasing susceptibility to infections and elevating the risk of cancer. Serious adverse effects include renal damage, hypertension, heart failure, neurological disorders, pulmonary and articular inflammation, hepatic damage, and diabetes, which collectively present a significant challenge in their prevention.

Moreover, the therapeutic objective is to suppress the symptoms of autoimmune diseases. However, given that most of these conditions become chronic once established, the physical and psychological burden on patients is substantial.

Notably, rheumatoid arthritis affects a large patient population, with various therapeutic options available. The progression of rheumatoid arthritis symptoms is most rapid within the first two years following onset. Therefore, early medical consultation and initiation of treatment are crucial to halt joint damage and preserve joint function.

Can Autoimmune Diseases Be Treated Without Medication?

Currently, an increasing number of individuals are seeking healing through alternative therapies, such as traditional Eastern medicine, without the use of conventional pharmaceuticals.

However, for patients with autoimmune diseases, it is crucial to consider that delayed diagnosis and treatment can shorten life expectancy.

Although two decades ago, systemic lupus erythematosus, a collagen disease frequently affecting young women, had a 5-year survival rate of merely 50%, the development of steroid and immunosuppressive drugs has since increased this rate to over 90%.

It is ill-advised to rely solely on alternative medicine without consulting a physician.

Therapeutic Potential of Wharton's Jelly Cell Therapy for Autoimmune Diseases

Globally, numerous clinical trials of stem cell therapy are underway for various autoimmune diseases, including rheumatoid arthritis. Among these, stem cells derived from Wharton's Jelly (WJ-MSCs) stand out due to their potent immunomodulatory capabilities and strong anti-inflammatory effects.

The Therapeutic Potential of Wharton's Jelly Cell Therapy in Rheumatoid Arthritis

Wharton's Jelly stem cells are a potential therapeutic candidate for rheumatoid arthritis (RA) and other inflammatory joint diseases.

  • Efek anti-inflamasi: Telah ditunjukkan bahwa Wharton's Jelly stem cell memiliki efek anti-inflamasi dan dapat meringankan peradangan pada pasien artritis reumatoid.
  • Modulasi imun: Diharapkan bahwa Wharton's Jelly stem cell dapat mengembalikan toleransi imun dan menekan kerusakan jaringan dari penyakit autoimun. Ini dapat menghasilkan keadaan di mana obat imunosupresif dapat dihentikan di masa depan (penyembuhan atau remisi jangka panjang).
  • Regenerasi tulang rawan: Wharton's Jelly stem cell memiliki kemampuan untuk berdiferensiasi menjadi sel tulang rawan dan dapat memperbaiki dan meregenerasi tulang rawan yang rusak.
  • Prospek masa depan: Saat ini sedang diupayakan untuk menjadi obat yang disetujui secara medis, tetapi pada tahap saat ini, transplantasi Wharton's Jelly stem cell dan terapi Secretome tersedia sebagai perawatan medis pribadi di Malaysia.

Global research into Wharton's Jelly cells indicates that WJ-MSCs possess high plasticity and stem cell content, exhibiting potent anti-inflammatory properties while enhancing host immune tolerance. This positions them as a highly promising therapeutic candidate for autoimmune diseases such as rheumatoid arthritis.

The immunomodulatory mechanisms of WJ-MSCs involve not only direct cell-to-cell contact but also secreted cytokines and exosomes (Secretome). Studies in murine arthritis models have demonstrated that following the administration of WJ-MSCs pooled from multiple sources, there was a significant increase in immunosuppressive effects and a reduction in the severity of rheumatoid arthritis.

In WJ-MSC research, immunomodulatory effects were observed, characterized by increased expression of FOXP3, IL-10, and TGF-β1 in both naive and activated T cells, leading to the induction of regulatory T cells (Tregs). Additionally, key genes involved in immune tolerance mechanisms (e.g., IDO1) were identified.

MSCs can differentiate into various mesodermal cell types, including bone, cartilage, cardiac muscle, and adipose tissue, and also secrete various trophic factors that promote tissue repair and regeneration. For these reasons, MSCs are promising therapeutic candidates for bone and joint diseases such as RA.

Summary

Globally, clinical trials of stem cell therapy for various autoimmune diseases such as rheumatoid arthritis are extensively underway. Among these, Wharton's Jelly-derived mesenchymal stem cells (WJ-MSCs) stand out due to their exceptionally potent immunomodulatory and anti-inflammatory properties.

Pending future medical approval, Wharton's Jelly cell therapy and Secretome therapy are currently available as private medical treatments in Malaysia.
