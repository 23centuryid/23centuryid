Types of Stem Cells Used in Regenerative Therapy

20 Maret 2020
  • Stem Cell Therapy & Regenerative Care

Regenerative medicine is a cutting-edge medical field focused on restoring the function of tissues and organs damaged due to disease or injury.

In contrast to traditional therapeutic approaches, this facilitates the regeneration of previously intractable tissues, and by leveraging autologous cells, our objective is to achieve therapy with minimal adverse effects.

Among these cells, stem cells are specialized cells that possess the ability to differentiate into various body cell types (multipotency) and the capacity for self-renewal through self-replication (self-renewal potential).

Leveraging these characteristics, stem cell therapy is broadly applied in tissue regeneration and disease treatment. Research and practice continue to advance across numerous fields, including neurological diseases, cardiovascular diseases, joint diseases, and even treatments related to the immune system.

Types of Stem Cells

Types of Somatic Stem Cells

Stem cells present in various body tissues encompass numerous types, including hematopoietic stem cells, neural stem cells, skin stem cells, intestinal stem cells, mammary stem cells, testicular stem cells, and mesenchymal stem cells.

Stem cells possess "self-renewal potential," allowing them to proliferate and replenish themselves, and "differentiation potential," enabling them to divide and differentiate into various cell types.

Among somatic stem cells, mesenchymal stem cells, which differentiate into the widest range of cell types, are key players in current stem cell therapy. Their differentiation potential is extensive, including hematopoietic system cells, nervous system cells, epidermal cells, cartilage cells, muscle, liver, and heart.

Three Main Types of Stem Cells in Regenerative Therapy

There are three main types of stem cells used in regenerative therapy:

  • Sel ES (sel punca embrionik): Dibuat dari embrio blastokist dan dapat berdiferensiasi menjadi semua sel. Namun, penggunaan dibatasi karena masalah etika.
  • Sel iPS (sel punca pluripoten terinduksi): Dibuat dengan reprogramming sel somatik. Kemampuan diferensiasi tinggi, tetapi ada risiko pembentukan tumor.
  • Sel punca somatik (sel punca dewasa): Sudah ada di tubuh manusia, tidak ada masalah etika, dan keamanannya tinggi, sehingga paling praktis saat ini.

In the realm of stem cell therapy, autologous somatic stem cells are currently the safest and most practical option, posing no ethical concerns.

Types of Mesenchymal Stem Cells

Mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) are multipotent stem cells found in bone marrow, adipose tissue, and umbilical cord, with the capacity to differentiate into bone cells, cartilage, adipose cells, nerve cells, muscle cells, and various other cell types.

Furthermore, MSCs possess tissue repair and immunomodulatory functions and are utilized in the treatment of various diseases. Their research and clinical applications are continuously evolving, particularly in the fields of joint diseases, neurological diseases, cardiovascular diseases, and autoimmune diseases.

Mesenchymal stem cells utilized in regenerative therapy can be classified into three primary types based on their source of isolation.

Mesenchymal Stem Cells from Bone Marrow

  • Formerly the standard in regenerative therapy, it is currently transitioning to other MSCs.
  • Exhibiting a good balance between self-renewal capacity and differentiation potential.
  • It has a relatively high immune regulatory capacity and is applied in the treatment of autoimmune diseases.

Adipose-derived Mesenchymal Stem Cells

  • The burden on the body is minimal, and a substantial number of cells can be harvested. (Obtained via liposuction under local anesthesia)
  • Culture can readily and rapidly proliferate within a relatively short period.
  • It possesses anti-inflammatory effects and is utilized in the treatment of inflammatory diseases.

Mesenchymal Stem Cells from Umbilical Cord

  • Possessing the highest proliferative capacity and maintaining cells in a youthful state.
  • Low immunogenicity and suitable for allogeneic transplantation, with a low risk of rejection.
  • The collection is straightforward, with minor ethical considerations. (It is harvested from the umbilical cord of a newborn, imposing no burden on the donor.)
  • It secretes numerous growth factors and anti-inflammatory cytokines, and therapeutic effects are anticipated.

Autologous Stem Cells versus Allogeneic Stem Cells

  • Sel punca autolog: Metode pengobatan di mana sel punca yang diambil dari tubuh pasien sendiri dikultur, diperbanyak, dan dimasukkan kembali ke dalam tubuh.
  • Sel punca allogeneic: Metode pengobatan yang menggunakan sel punca yang diambil dari donor yang sehat.

Three Effects of Mesenchymal Stem Cells

Mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) possess the ability to repair and immunoregulate various tissues in the body. Their mechanism of action is achieved through three primary mechanisms:

Pasotrophic Effect

When inflammation or tissue damage occurs within the body, MSCs possess the ability to detect chemokines (inflammatory signals) emitted from the affected area and migrate to the site of damage via the bloodstream. This characteristic allows MSCs to accumulate near the damaged tissue and initiate repair. Specifically, they interact with fibroblasts, vascular endothelial cells, and immune cells to support tissue repair.

POIN: Efek pasotrofik berfungsi sebagai "langkah pertama" di mana MSC bergerak ke tempat yang tepat.

Homing Effect

Upon MSC migration to sites of inflammation or damage, they home there and contribute to the repair of damaged tissue. During this process, MSCs not only differentiate into locally required cells but also secrete cytokines and growth factors that facilitate repair, thereby supporting the restoration of function in the affected tissue.

At the site of injury, inflammatory cytokines and growth factors are locally elevated, attracting MSCs. MSCs receive these signals and interact with vascular endothelial cells and fibroblasts to effect tissue repair.

POIN: Melalui efek homing, MSC mencapai area peradangan atau kerusakan dengan akurat dan menangani "tahap kedua" dari perbaikan.

Paracrine Effect

MSCs secrete bioactive molecules such as cytokines, growth factors, and exosomes, thereby promoting the repair of damaged tissue. Through this mechanism, MSCs can support the restoration of function in surrounding cells even without direct differentiation. Notably, they regulate immune cell activity and suppress inflammatory responses.

This effect is highly significant in the treatment of autoimmune and chronic inflammatory diseases, as it simultaneously modulates immune cell activity and suppresses excessive inflammatory responses.

POIN: Perbaikan jaringan dipromosikan oleh faktor sekresi bahkan tanpa MSC berdiferensiasi secara langsung, dan dianggap bahwa sebagian besar efek terapi MSC dalam terapi regeneratif berasal dari efek parakrin ini.

Benefits of Mesenchymal Stem Cells from Umbilical Cord (Wharton's Jelly)

Mesenchymal stem cells derived from the umbilical cord (Wharton's Jelly) are expected to possess significant therapeutic potential due to their inherent characteristics.

If one only considers the risk of immune rejection, autologous stem cells might appear safest; however, their proliferative and differentiation capabilities decline with age.

Additionally, pre-existing disease information is retained in cultured cells, potentially increasing disease risk.

Autologous stem cells are the individual's own cells, which experience a decline in function with advancing age.

Umbilical cord-derived mesenchymal stem cells (Wharton's Jelly MSCs) are isolated from the Wharton's Jelly of newborn umbilical cords. A key benefit of this source is the absence of age-related cellular degeneration.

Furthermore, umbilical cord tissue contains numerous mesenchymal stem cells, and compared to cells derived from bone marrow or adipose tissue, their proliferative capacity is higher, and their differentiation potential can be maintained for an extended period.
