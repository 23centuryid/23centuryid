Jenis-Jenis Stem cell that Digunakan deep Terapi Regenerative

20 Maret 2020
  • Stem cell & Regenerative Therapy

Therapy regenerative is bidang medical most cutting-edge which bertujuan for memulihkan fungsi tissue and organ which rusak akibat disease or cedera.

Dibandingkan with method treatment traditional, hal this memungkinkan regeneration tissue which sulit diperbaiki before, and with leverage cell patient sendiri, we bertujuan for achieve treatment with efek samping minimal.

In antara cell-cell this, stem cell is cell special which have ability for berdiferensiasi became berbagai cell tubuh (potential multidisiplin) and ability for mempertahankan diri with mereplikasi diri sendiri (potential replikasi diri).

With leverage karakteristik this, stem cell therapy applied secara spacious in regeneration tissue and treatment disease, and research and practice continuously developed in many bidang including disease nerve, disease heart, disease sendi, and bahkan treatment which berhubungan with system kekebalan.

Jenis-Jenis Stem cell

Jenis-Jenis Stem cell Somatik

Stem cell which there is in berbagai tissue tubuh include many jenis including stem cell pembentuk blood, stem cell nerve, stem cell skin, stem cell usus, stem cell kelenjar susu, stem cell testis, and stem cell mesenchymal.

Stem cell have "potential replikasi diri" which add diri they sendiri and "potential diferensiasi" which membelah and berubah became berbagai cell.

In antara stem cell somatik, "stem cell mesenchymal" which berdiferensiasi became jenis cell terbanyak is pemain main in stem cell therapy time this, and potential diferensiasinya very spacious including cell system hematopoietik, cell system nerve, cell epidermis, cell bone rawan, muscle, liver, and heart.

Tiga Jenis Stem cell Main in Therapy Regenerative

There is tiga jenis large stem cell which used in therapy regenerative:

  • Cell ES (stem cell embrionik): Dibuat from embrio blastokist and can berdiferensiasi become all cell. Namun, penggunaan dibatasi because problem etika.
  • Cell iPS (stem cell pluripoten terinduksi): Dibuat with reprogramming cell somatik. Kemampuan diferensiasi high, tetapi ada risk pembentukan tumor.
  • Stem cell somatik (stem cell dewasa): Have ada in tubuh manusia, tidak ada problem etika, and keamanannya high, sehingga most praktis now.

In hal stem cell therapy, stem cell somatik which have there is in in tubuh human is which most safe and most praktis time this, without problem etika apapun.

Jenis-Jenis Stem cell Mesenchymal

Stem cell mesenchymal (MSC) is sejenis stem cell which there is in sumsum bone, lemak, tali center, and have ability for berdiferensiasi became cell bone, bone rawan, lemak, nerve, muscle, and berbagai cell lainnya.

Selain that, MSC have ability perbaikan tissue and fungsi regulation kekebalan, and applied in treatment berbagai disease, and research and application clinical continuously developed especially in bidang disease sendi, disease nerve, disease kardiovaskular, and disease autoimmune.

Stem cell mesenchymal which used in therapy regenerative can diklasifikasikan became tiga tipe main berdasarkan sumber pengambilan.

Stem cell Mesenchymal from Sumsum Bone

  • Dulu merupakan standard in therapy regenerative, tetapi time this sedang bertransisi to MSC lain.
  • Have keseimbangan which good antara ability replikasi diri and potential diferensiasi.
  • Have ability regulation kekebalan which relatif high and applied in treatment disease autoimmune.

Stem cell Mesenchymal from Lemak

  • Beban on tubuh minimal and can mengambil sejumlah large cell. (Diambil through liposuction in bawah anestesi local)
  • Budaya mudah and can developed biak in time which relatif short.
  • Have efek anti-inflammation and applied in treatment disease inflammation.

Stem cell Mesenchymal from Tali Center

  • Have ability proliferasi tertinggi and mempertahankan keuda cell which young.
  • Imunogenisitas low and cocok for transplantasi allogeneic. (Penolakan not mudah happen)
  • Pengambilan mudah and there is few problem etika. (Diambil from tali center bayi new lahir, without beban on donor)
  • Mensekresi many faktor growth and sitokin anti-inflammation, and efek therapy diharapkan.

Autologous Stem Cells vs Allogeneic Stem Cells

  • Stem cell autolog: Metode therapy where stem cell that diambil from tubuh pasien sendiri dikultur, diperbanyak, and dimasukkan back to deep tubuh.
  • Stem cell allogeneic: Metode therapy that use stem cell that diambil from donor that healthy.

Tiga Efek Stem cell Mesenchymal

Stem cell mesenchymal (MSC) have ability for improve and meregulasi kekebalan berbagai tissue in tubuh. Cara kerjanya diwujudkan through tiga mekanisme main:

Efek Pasotrofik

When peradangan or kerusakan tissue happen in in tubuh, MSC have ability for mendeteksi kemokin (sinyal inflammation) which dipancarkan from area tersebut and move to area kerusakan through aliran blood. Through karakteristik this, MSC berkumpul in dekat tissue which rusak and mulai perbaikan. Especially, they berinteraksi with fibroblas, cell endotel pembuluh blood, and cell kekebalan for support perbaikan tissue.

POIN: Efek pasotrofik function sebagai "langkah pertama" in which MSC move to place which tepat.

Efek Homing

After MSC move to area peradangan or kerusakan, they menetap (homing) in sana and berkontribusi on perbaikan tissue which rusak. In process this, not hanya MSC berdiferensiasi became cell-cell which dibutuhkan secara local, tetapi also mensekresi sitokin and faktor growth which assist perbaikan, support recovery fungsi tissue which rusak.

In area kerusakan, sitokin inflammation and faktor growth increased secara local, interesting MSC. MSC receive sinyal-sinyal this and berinteraksi with cell endotel pembuluh blood and fibroblas for perform perbaikan tissue.

POIN: Through efek homing, MSC achieve area peradangan or kerusakan with akurat and menangani "tahap kedua" from perbaikan.

Efek Parakrin

MSC mensekresi zat aktif secara biologis seperti sitokin, faktor growth, and exosome, and mempromosikan perbaikan tissue which rusak. With cara this, MSC can support recovery fungsi cell sekitarnya bahkan without berdiferensiasi secara langsung. Especially, they have efek organize aktivitas cell kekebalan and menekan respons inflammation.

Efek this very important in treatment disease autoimmune and disease inflammation kronis, and sekaligus organize aktivitas cell kekebalan while menekan reaksi inflammation which berlebihan.

POIN: Perbaikan tissue dipromosikan oleh faktor sekresi bahkan without MSC berdiferensiasi secara langsung, and dianggap bahwa some large efek therapy MSC in therapy regenerative berasal from efek parakrin this.
