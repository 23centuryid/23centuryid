Addressing Erectile Dysfunction (ED) Comprehensively with Regenerative Therapy

19 Januari 2025
  • Stem Cell Therapy & Regenerative Care

Approximately one in three men experience ED, and when combined with mild symptoms, an estimated 18 million men experience these symptoms.

The cause can be psychological factors; however, in men over the age of 40, the predominant factors are organic. These include vascular aging and damage to blood vessels resulting from conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, and other related issues.

Erectile Dysfunction Medication

Medications such as Cialis, Levitra, and Viagra are available nationwide, and obtaining a prescription through online diagnosis has become streamlined. However, many individuals are hesitant to continue their use.

Concerns about Side Effects

These medications function by acutely increasing blood flow, resulting in a variety of side effects dependent on usage and frequency. Common side effects include flushing and headaches; however, rare occurrences may involve irregular heartbeat, dizziness, hypertension, and difficulty breathing. In extreme cases, this could result in myocardial infarction.

If the cause of ED is hypertension, medication use can paradoxically elevate blood pressure, leading many patients to hesitate in using pharmacological treatments despite their concerns.

Matters relating to male dignity and life

Irrespective of age, ED is a significant concern impacting male dignity. However, more profoundly, it often masks serious underlying life issues.

The most common cause of organic erectile dysfunction is vascular aging. Erections occur when blood flows into the corpora cavernosa. Consequently, when blood vessels are damaged or narrowed by atherosclerosis, blood flow to the penis is compromised, making erections difficult to achieve or insufficiently rigid.

Blood vessels are distributed throughout the entire body, and penile blood vessels do not spontaneously sustain damage. All blood vessels throughout the body are subject to the aging process.

To illustrate: penile blood vessels are affected in erectile dysfunction (ED), cardiac coronary arteries during angina, and cerebral blood vessels during a stroke. The diameters of these vessels differ significantly. The penile artery typically measures 1-2 mm, the main coronary artery 3-4 mm, and the carotid artery 5-6 mm. As atherosclerosis often progresses in narrower vessels, it is highly probable that coronary arteries will become occluded after the penile arteries.

Age should not be a deterrent. It is essential to detect early signs of serious illness through ED, conduct a comprehensive lifestyle review, and proactively manage the aging process.

From the age of 40, temporary relief is insufficient; prioritize root cause treatment with regenerative therapy.

Domestically Acceptable Regenerative Therapy

In recent years, regenerative therapy utilizing stem cell conditioned medium or exosomes has emerged as a topic within the fields of aesthetics and rejuvenation. However, why is there limited discussion regarding its efficacy?

The limited adoption of this treatment method stems from the fact that its anticipated effects are not universally observed, leading to skepticism regarding its actual efficacy for individuals. Furthermore, a plausible explanation for the restricted public awareness of this therapeutic approach is that many individuals struggle to conceptualize regenerative therapy and possess insufficient knowledge regarding the preparation and mechanisms of action of the youthful biological components utilized.

Stem Cell Culture Supernatant and Exosomes

It is often not fully appreciated how many bioactive components are present within stem cell culture fluid or exosomes.

Stem cell conditioned medium

Kekurangan dan Risiko: Tidak semua yang disekresikan saat budidaya sel punca adalah yang baik. Ada kasus di mana cairan yang dipanen dari sel yang sudah dikultur untuk pengobatan orang lain digunakan langsung. Pertama-tama, cairan budidaya sel punca adalah cairan yang mengandung komponen untuk pertumbuhan sel punca, sehingga komponen yang efektif tersebar. Selain itu, standar keamanan untuk budidaya sel punca jelas, tetapi keamanan untuk pembuatan serum dipertanyakan.

Stem cell culture media are formulated with numerous components to support stem cell proliferation. In the event of bacterial or viral infection, stem cells may exhibit abnormal or accelerated proliferation. Cultures lacking established safety standards carry a high risk of infection. Additionally, while some culture media incorporate anti-infective agents to prevent contamination, they often contain numerous unnecessary additives that may outnumber components with actual therapeutic effects.

Exosome

While regenerative therapy holds promise for root regeneration, both patients and treating clinicians must avoid high-risk interventions and possess the knowledge to discern highly efficacious therapeutic options.
