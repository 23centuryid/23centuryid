Memahami Hubungan antara Sel Punca Mesenchymal dan Elastin melalui Peyer's Patches

1 November 2020
  • Stem Cell & Regenerative Therapy

Until saat this, we has perform many research and belajar about kecantikan and health, however seiring with kemajuan research, we menyadari bahwa semua this terkumpul in cerita about stem cell.

We has membagikan information about "elastin" sebagai pelopor, tetapi not there is pengecualian, and tanpa stem cell, we not can provide penjelasan which can dipahami about keindahan and efek health lainnya.

Kali this, I menemukan diagram which mudah dipahami about diferensiasi cell saat mencari di internet for penjelasan about diferensiasi cell. We menemukan diagram about Peyer's patches. Through research more lanjut, we menyadari bahwa hubungan antara stem cell mesenchymal and elastin, which we perjelas, and Peyer's patches is fondasi from system imun which mengatur health.

Sel Punca Mesenchymal dan Peyer's Patches

Peyer's patches dikenal sebagai center kendali fungsi kekebalan, however through research saat this, I has obtain many knowledge about stem cell therapy, and I mampu memahami spesifiknya with more konkret, memahami system kendali imun kekebalan and T cell. Sekarang I can menjelaskan kepada orang-orang with cara which jelas and mudah dipahami.

Peyer's patches terletak di bagian usus halus di mana vili belum berkembang. Di sana, antigen dikenali, and cell pembunuh seperti cell pembunuh dll muncul which sesuai dengannya.

In system kendali imun Peyer's patches, has menjadi jelas bahwa stem cell mesenchymal berfungsi secara langsung. Hubungan this dijelaskan oleh diagram which show diferensiasi cell.

Apakah Elastin Pendukung Sel Punca Mesenchymal?

Hingga saat this, research we about elastin show bahwa tulang rawan which dianggap not can diregenerasi and cell paru-paru which mati has diregenerasi, as well as peremajaan organ which mengandung many elastin, semua performed bersama with stem cell mesenchymal.

In stem cell therapy, elastin tampak few usang, however sebenarnya elastin is sesuatu which tak terpisahkan from stem cell therapy.
