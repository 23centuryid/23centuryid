Are Allogeneic Stem Cells Dangerous? Misconceptions and Actual Risks of Stem Cell Therapy

14 Maret 2025
  • Stem Cell Therapy & Regenerative Care

Terapi sel punca menarik perhatian sebagai garis depan perawatan regeneratif.
Namun, beberapa informasi yang salah dan rumor telah tersebar luas, menghalangi pengambilan keputusan yang tepat.

While the prospect is enticing, people remain hesitant given the ambiguity surrounding the associated risks.

In this article, we will thoroughly address these rumors, ensuring you can confidently undergo stem cell therapy in Malaysia.

Misunderstanding 1 Sel Punca Orang Lain Menyebabkan Penolakan dan Risiko Kematian

Typically, in organ or bone marrow transplantation for leukemia, cells from another individual necessitate donor matching. Should a mismatch occur, rejection reactions can ensue and may even prove fatal. I believe many are familiar with this concept.

From this perception, it is easy to imagine that other people's stem cells are dangerous.

Hingga saat ini, tidak pernah terjadi reaksi penolakan dengan terapi sel punca Wharton's Jelly dari orang lain.[1]
Alasannya adalah sel punca mesenchymal yang belum terdiferensiasi tidak memiliki "tipe" yang membedakan orang lain dari diri sendiri.[2]

This 'type' is Human Leukocyte Antigen (HLA), which is present on the cell membranes of nearly all cells. It distinguishes self-cells from foreign entities and possesses a mechanism to eliminate health-threatening foreign substances from within the body.

This is called a rejection reaction.

Should allogeneic cells be introduced into the body in significant numbers, immune dysregulation may ensue, culminating in a cytokine storm that can lead to respiratory failure, sudden hypotension, thrombosis, and ultimately, mortality.

Namun, sel punca tanpa antigen leukosit manusia tidak dikenali sebagai benda asing sama sekali.
Selain itu, sel punca Wharton's Jelly yang berasal dari tali pusar memiliki karakteristik imun khusus, mereka secara alami beradaptasi seperti sel sendiri sambil menyeimbangkan fungsi kekebalan, dan bertanggung jawab atas perbaikan jaringan.

The immunomodulatory properties of Wharton's Jelly stem cells are a globally recognized topic within the regenerative medicine industry. Consequently, if any physician or expert asserts that "allogeneic stem cells are inherently dangerous," it unfortunately reflects a significant lack of understanding of the current scientific consensus and established properties of these cells.

We recommend re-verifying the latest research and publications.

Kesimpulan
"Sel punca orang lain berbahaya" adalah klaim yang salah.
Khususnya, sel punca Wharton's Jelly yang berasal dari tali pusar diakui sebagai pengobatan yang aman dengan penolakan yang rendah.

Wharton's Jelly mesenchymal stem cells (WJ-MSCs) are a type of stem cell with high differentiation potential, possessing immunomodulatory properties, and are easily collected without raising legal or ethical concerns. WJ-MSCs exhibit several characteristics of embryonic stem cells in terms of phenotypic and genetic aspects.

WJ-MSCs possess immunoregulatory properties involved in innate and adaptive immunity. Various studies demonstrate that when WJ-MSCs are administered to critically ill patients unresponsive to standard treatment, beneficial effects are observed even when HLA (Human Leukocyte Antigen) is not perfectly matched.

Sumber: Int J Stem Cells. 2019; 12(2): 218–226. Published online 2019 Apr 30.
PMCID: PMC6657936

Misunderstanding 2 Terapi Sel Punca Memiliki Risiko Kanker

Belum pernah ada kasus sel punca yang ditransplantasikan mengalami kanker, dan tidak ada kemungkinan seperti itu.[3]

This rumor is believed to originate from confusion with induced pluripotent stem cells (iPS cells). iPS cells are created through genetic manipulation, which has led to suggestions of a cancer risk.

Untuk perbedaan antara sel iPS dan sel punca somatik yang digunakan dalam terapi sel punca, silakan lihat Types of stem cells used in regenerative medicine.

iPS cells (induced pluripotent stem cells), discovered by Professor Yamanaka of Kyoto University, won the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine in 2012 and garnered significant attention. However, their clinical realization presented substantial challenges, and subsequent research efforts encountered difficulties.

Itu adalah tantangan "sel iPS mengalami kanker".[4]

The cause is evident: during the generation of iPS cells, genes are recombined, and one of these recombined genes is an oncogene.

Setelah itu, dilaporkan bahwa mereka berhasil membuat sel iPS tanpa memasukkan gen kanker, tetapi realisasi masih menjadi jalan yang sulit.
Kami berharap penelitian akan berlanjut dan bermanfaat bagi banyak orang.

Kesimpulan
Klaim bahwa terapi sel punca memiliki risiko kanker adalah kesalahpahaman berdasarkan kebingungan dengan sel iPS.
Dalam pengobatan menggunakan sel punca mesenchymal, tidak ada risiko seperti itu yang dilaporkan.

Misunderstanding 3 Terapi Sel Punca Dihambat oleh Tekanan Kepentingan Farmasi

Ada kalanya terapi sel punca dianggap sebagai sesuatu yang bertentangan dengan perawatan medis tradisional dan obat-obatan.
Namun, kemajuan perawatan medis modern didukung oleh peningkatan sanitasi dan perkembangan metode perawatan medis.

Pharmaceuticals play a role in supplementing diminished cellular function and are crucial as part of appropriate treatment. Therefore, even if stem cell therapy becomes widespread, in some cases it is preferable to utilize stem cell therapy in conjunction with appropriate pharmacotherapy.

On the other hand, excessive drug administration can lead to adverse effects. In such cases, it may be possible to consider mitigating these effects or reducing the dosage through stem cell therapy.

Stem cell therapy does not supersede existing medical treatments, but rather aims to preserve patient health while establishing a complementary relationship with conventional care.

Kesimpulan
Penyebaran terapi sel punca tidak dihambat oleh industri farmasi.

In conclusion

Verification of rumors concerning the "risks associated with allogeneic stem cells" revealed that the majority of these claims lack scientific substantiation based on current medical research.

It is a fact that accurate information regarding stem cell therapy has not been fully disseminated, leading to widespread misconceptions about its safety. These misconceptions are not always attributable to malicious intent, but are rather considered a consequence of inadequate knowledge sharing.

At 23Century, we strive to provide information based on the latest scientific evidence regarding the safety and efficacy of stem cell therapy, and to impart accurate knowledge to our patients.
