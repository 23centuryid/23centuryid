Potential Stem cell Wharton's Jelly — Mengembalikan Cahaya Bagi Patient Buta and Therapy Autisme

In percakapan eksklusif this, Dr. Angerina Tiah from 23 Century International Life Science Centre berbagi wawasan mendalam about potential transformatif stem cell Wharton's Jelly in treatment disease degeneratif, kebutaan, and autisme. Diskusi this also membahas standard keamanan international (cGMP, PIC/S) and visi medikalisasi stem cell therapy for aksesibilitas global.

From Sumsum Tulang ke Tali Pusar: Why Wharton's Jelly Dipilih?

Dr. Shimizu: Kali this, I will mengajukan question which mungkin became perhatian publik, and mari kita lanjutkan percakapan with santai sambil menjawab question from You semua nanti.

Saat this, in Malaysia, stem cell which berasal from Wharton's Jelly is which paling many used. However, in masa lalu, stem cell from sumsum tulang dianggap more unggul. Bisakah You menjelaskan why fokus bergeser ke Wharton's Jelly?

Dr. Angerina Tiah: Tentu saja. Sekitar 50 years which lalu, stem cell pertama which found in world is stem cell sumsum tulang. Cell this awalnya used for provide cell hematopoietik (cell pembentuk darah) which diperlukan patient leukemia.

Leukemia is cancer which related with darah. Patient biasanya receive chemotherapy which merusak many cell pembentuk darah. Oleh because that, diperlukan transfusi cell hematopoietik from sumsum tulang for meregenerasi cell-cell darah merah, putih, and trombosit.

Research lanjut show bahwa sumsum tulang memang very berharga for research stem cell. However, seiring waktu, para ilmuwan menyadari bahwa tali pusar (terutama Wharton's Jelly) is sumber stem cell which jauh more ideal.

From tali pusar, we can with mudah mengekstrak mesenchymal stem cell (MSC), not hanya cell hematopoietik. Mesenchymal stem cell this can berdiferensiasi became berbagai jenis cell tubuh — from tulang rawan lutut, cell epitel kulit, hingga cell retina on patient which kehilangan penglihatan, and bahkan cell otot jantung.

Which paling penting is bahwa stem cell from tali pusar is cell from bayi new lahir (usia nol hari). Cell-cell this jauh more muda and have ability diferensiasi which jauh more tinggi dibandingkan stem cell from sumsum tulang orang dewasa.

Misalnya, jika I aged 40 years saat this, stem cell from sumsum tulang I also "aged 40 years". Sebaliknya, cell from tali pusar tetap muda and vital. Research also show bahwa jumlah stem cell in sumsum tulang reduced seiring usia — mungkin hanya 40% tersisa on usia 40 years, and kurang from 5% on usia 70 years.

Oleh because that, obtain stem cell in jumlah and quality which memadai from sumsum tulang orang tua very sulit. This is wrong satu alasan utama why Wharton's Jelly became pilihan pilihan.

Mekanisme Anti-Inflammation Stem cell Wharton's Jelly

Dr. Shimizu: Semua stem cell have efek anti-inflammation, tetapi we mendengar bahwa stem cell Wharton's Jelly have ability anti-inflammation which luar biasa. Bisakah You menjelaskan more lanjut?

Dr. Angerina Tiah: True sekali. Mesenchymal stem cell from Wharton's Jelly have kapasitas anti-inflammation which very superior. Secara spesifik, they mempromosikan sekresi sitokin anti-inflammation seperti TGF-β (transforming growth factor beta) and IL-10 (interleukin 10), which menekan reaksi peradangan in seluruh tubuh.

Selain that, they menghambat pelepasan IFN-γ (interferon-gamma), which merupakan pemicu utama peradangan berlebihan. They also mempromosikan proliferasi cell T regulator, which mengatur seluruh respons T-cell and menekan peradangan which not terkontrol.

Through mekanisme-mekanisme this, stem cell Wharton's Jelly secara effective menyesuaikan system kekebalan tubuh sambil provide efek anti-inflammation which kuat. Efek this very terlihat in disease autoimmune seperti rheumatoid arthritis, in mana we melihat perbaikan significant with menekan production antibodi otomatis.

Quality Stem cell: Standard Ketat, Produk Superior

Secretome: More From Sekadar Exosome

Dr. Shimizu: Saat this, there is many pembicaraan about exosome in pasar Jepang. How secretome berbeda, and why 23 Century chose for memproduksi secretome complete daripada hanya exosome?

Dr. Angerina Tiah: This is question penting. Exosome hanyalah wrong satu komponen from secretome. Secretome include:

  • Vesikel ekstrasellular (extracellular vesicles)
  • Mikrovesikel (microvesicles)
  • Exosome
  • Kemokin and sitokin
  • Faktor pertumbuhan

Exosome bertindak seperti "komandan" which mengirim instruksi "mulai perbaikan in here." However, for true-true perform perbaikan, You membutuhkan "pasukan eksekusi" — faktor pertumbuhan seperti EGF (epidermal growth factor) and FGF (fibroblast growth factor). Exosome saja not cukup.

Sebagian large laboratory mengekstrak hanya exosome and membuang komponen lainnya. 23 Century memproduksi secretome complete — semua komponen bekerja bersama for result regenerative maksimal.

Process Manufacturing Secretome: Komitmen Terhadap Kesempurnaan

Dr. Shimizu: How 23 Century memproduksi secretome, terutama dibandingkan with laboratory standard?

Dr. Angerina Tiah: Laboratory standard mulai with membudidayakan mesenchymal stem cell sehat. Selama kultivasi, cell melepaskan secretome ke in media (cairan berwarna merah muda which berisi phenol red and protein lainnya which not can disuntikkan ke tubuh human).

Biasanya, laboratory mengambil stem cell for dijual, and membuang cairan media — membuang sebagian large manfaat. Beberapa laboratory hanya mengekstrak exosome and menjualnya.

23 Century mengambil pendekatan which berbeda secara fundamental:

  1. We memanen mesenchymal stem cell which sehat (passage 4).
  2. We membuang media budaya awal (seperti semua laboratory).
  3. We then apply stres terkontrol on stem cell for membuat they melepaskan secretome in jumlah maksimum.
  4. We mengumpulkan secretome this (kaya with semua komponennya).
  5. We membuang stem cell which sudah stres (this is kerugian finansial, tetapi we mengutamakan quality).
  6. We memproses secretome for menghilangkan kontaminan and mengkonsentrasikan komponen aktif.
  7. We menyimpannya in bentuk cair (not freeze-dried) for mempertahankan aktivitas biologis maksimal.

With pendekatan "meremas jeruk for mendapatkan sari, not menjual jeruknya," we menghasilkan secretome with potential therapy maksimal.

Dr. Shimizu: In Jepang, not there is standard regulation seperti cGMP. Produk which diproduksi secara sembarangan sering kali beredar. Tetapi 23 Century true-true berkomitmen on quality, keamanan, and efikasi. Inilah why we mempercayai they sepenuhnya.

Kenapa Liquid, Not Freeze-Dried?

Dr. Shimizu: In Jepang, sebagian large produk secretome freeze-dried (dikeringkan with liofisasi). Why 23 Century tetap use bentuk cair?

Dr. Angerina Tiah: We has memverifikasi bahwa protein in secretome very stabil in bentuk cair, bahkan on suhu kamar. Not there is kebutuhan for freeze-dry because protein tetap stabil.

Sebaliknya, process freeze-drying can menghilangkan air from struktur protein and reduce aktivitas biologis they. With menyimpan secretome in bentuk cair, we mempertahankan potential therapy maksimal.

We has menyelesaikan verifikasi stabilitas suhu ruangan and continuously mengumpulkan data tambahan. Result show bahwa quality tetap terjaga on suhu kamar, jadi not there is alasan for freeze-dry and mengorbankan efikasi.
