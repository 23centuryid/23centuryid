The Potential of Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells — Restoring Vision for Blind Patients and Autism Therapy

In this exclusive discussion, Dr. Angerina Tiah from the 23 Century International Life Science Centre shares profound insights into the transformative potential of Wharton's Jelly stem cells in treating degenerative diseases, blindness, and autism. The discussion also addresses international safety standards (cGMP, PIC/S) and the vision for the medicalization of stem cell therapy to ensure global accessibility.

From Bone Marrow to the Umbilical Cord: Why is Wharton's Jelly Selected?

Dr. Shimizu: Kali ini, saya akan mengajukan pertanyaan yang mungkin menjadi perhatian publik, dan mari kita lanjutkan percakapan dengan santai sambil menjawab pertanyaan dari Anda semua nanti.

Currently, in Malaysia, Wharton's Jelly-derived stem cells are the most widely utilized. However, historically, bone marrow-derived stem cells were deemed superior. Could you please explain why the focus has shifted to Wharton's Jelly?

Dr. Angerina Tiah: Tentu saja. Sekitar 50 tahun yang lalu, sel punca pertama yang ditemukan di dunia adalah sel punca sumsum tulang. Sel ini awalnya digunakan untuk memberikan sel hematopoietik (sel pembentuk darah) yang diperlukan pasien leukemia.

Leukemia is a blood cancer. Patients typically undergo chemotherapy, which damages numerous hematopoietic cells. Consequently, a hematopoietic stem cell transplant from bone marrow is required to regenerate red blood cells, white blood cells, and platelets.

Further research demonstrated that bone marrow was indeed highly valuable for stem cell research. However, over time, scientists recognized that the umbilical cord (particularly Wharton's Jelly) represented a significantly more ideal source of stem cells.

The umbilical cord provides an accessible source for extracting mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs), not exclusively hematopoietic cells. These MSCs are capable of differentiating into a wide range of somatic cell types, including knee cartilage cells, skin epithelial cells, retinal cells for patients experiencing vision loss, and even cardiomyocytes.

Most importantly, umbilical cord stem cells are cells from a newborn infant (zero days old). These cells are significantly younger and possess a much higher differentiation potential compared to adult bone marrow stem cells.

For instance, if an individual is currently 40 years old, their bone marrow stem cells also reflect that biological age. In contrast, cells derived from the umbilical cord remain youthful and vital. Furthermore, research indicates that the quantity of stem cells in bone marrow declines with age — potentially only 40% remaining by age 40, and less than 5% by age 70.

Therefore, obtaining stem cells in sufficient quantity and quality from the bone marrow of elderly individuals is extremely difficult. This is one of the primary reasons why Wharton's Jelly has become the preferred option.

Restoring Light: An Inspiring Case of Blindness Treatment

Dr. Shimizu: Bisakah Anda berbagi kasus klinis yang mengesankan dari pekerjaan Anda dengan pasien kebutaan?

Dr. Angerina Tiah: Tentu. Kami telah meluncurkan aplikasi klinis baru untuk pasien kebutaan sekitar dua tahun lalu, dan telah melakukan sekitar 100 injeksi intraokular pada pasien yang telah kehilangan penglihatan selama lebih dari 30 tahun.

Most of our patients suffer from retinitis pigmentosa (RP), an inherited genetic disorder that causes progressive vision loss with age. One of the most remarkable cases involved a male patient who was diagnosed at age 4 and received our treatment at age 36 — meaning he had lived in complete blindness for 32 years.

A single injection of MSCs was administered into the subtenon space (behind the eye). Within 24 hours, the patient was able to perceive light for the first time in their life. The injection was performed at 3:00 PM, and upon waking the following morning at 7:00 AM, the patient reported perceiving light.

Retaining visual memories from the age of four, she correctly identified "Green!" when her mother asked, "What color is this?" This was truly inspiring. Following several subsequent treatments with Wharton's Jelly stem cells, her vision continued to improve significantly.

Dr. Shimizu: Saya kebetulan bertemu pasien ini berkali-kali selama perawatannya. Kami bahkan mengunjungi Raja Penang untuk memberikan laporan sebelum injeksi. Menyaksikan transformasi dari kegelapan ke cahaya benar-benar luar biasa dan menyentuh hati.

Novel Approach for Autism Therapy

Dr. Shimizu: Kami juga mendengar bahwa 23 Century telah mencapai hasil yang sangat mengesankan dalam pengobatan anak-anak dengan autisme. Bisakah Anda menjelaskan pendekatan tersebut?

Dr. Angerina Tiah: Ya, kami telah berhasil mengobati banyak anak dengan autisme, baik dari Malaysia maupun internasional. Kami menggunakan injeksi UC-MSC (sel punca mesenkimal dari tali pusar).

Upon entering the body, mesenchymal stem cells initially exert a potent anti-inflammatory effect. This is critical, as the brains of children with autism experience constant inflammation—akin to a perpetual "war" within their brains. This inflammation impedes their ability to concentrate and respond socially.

With the stem cell-mediated resolution of this inflammation, children exhibit improved concentration. Furthermore, stem cells are capable of differentiating into novel neurons, thereby enhancing neural plasticity (the brain's capacity to adapt and form new connections). This synergistic combination of anti-inflammatory and neuroplastic effects leads to significant improvements in concentration, cognitive behaviors, and responses among children with autism.

Anti-inflammatory Mechanisms of Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells

Dr. Shimizu: Semua sel punca memiliki efek anti-inflamasi, tetapi kami mendengar bahwa sel punca Wharton's Jelly memiliki kemampuan anti-inflamasi yang luar biasa. Bisakah Anda menjelaskan lebih lanjut?

Dr. Angerina Tiah: Benar sekali. Sel punca mesenkimal dari Wharton's Jelly memiliki kapasitas anti-inflamasi yang sangat superior. Secara spesifik, mereka mempromosikan sekresi sitokin anti-inflamasi seperti TGF-β (transforming growth factor beta) dan IL-10 (interleukin 10), yang menekan reaksi peradangan di seluruh tubuh.

Furthermore, they inhibit the release of IFN-γ (interferon-gamma), a primary driver of excessive inflammation. They also promote the proliferation of regulatory T cells, which regulate the overall T-cell response and suppress uncontrolled inflammation.

Through these mechanisms, Wharton's Jelly stem cells effectively modulate the immune system while exerting potent anti-inflammatory effects. These effects are particularly evident in autoimmune diseases such as rheumatoid arthritis, where we observe significant improvements by suppressing autoantibody production.

International Security Standards: A Foundation of Trust

Dr. Shimizu: Keamanan adalah perhatian utama bagi semua orang. Bagaimana 23 Century memastikan standar keamanan tertinggi dalam produksi sel punca?

Dr. Angerina Tiah: Di Malaysia, kami mengikuti standar PIC/S (Pharmaceutical Inspection Co-operation Scheme), yang mencakup 72 badan regulasi internasional termasuk FDA Amerika Serikat. Kami telah membangun laboratorium GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) yang mematuhi standar PIC/S ini sepenuhnya.

Our laboratory has obtained the highest level of licensing for human cell manufacturing, adhering to CGMP (Current Good Manufacturing Practice) standards. This license enables us to produce CGTP (Cell and Gene Therapy Products). The CGMP standard ensures that the entire manufacturing process complies with international standards and the most stringent cleanroom specifications.

As a result, our stem cell product has been recognized as Clinical Grade, meeting the highest quality and safety standards for human use.

Dr. Shimizu: Perbedaan filosofis penting di sini adalah bahwa 23 Century tidak hanya mematuhi standar keselamatan, tetapi juga membangun infrastruktur yang mengantisipasi status persetujuan obat-obatan di masa depan. Ini memastikan bahwa perawatan dapat diakses oleh lebih banyak orang melalui jaminan kesehatan.

Stem Cell Quality: Rigorous Standards, Superior Products

Dr. Shimizu: Bagaimana Anda mendefinisikan "kualitas" dalam sel punca?

Dr. Angerina Tiah: Standar kualitas kami sangat ketat. Dari awal, kami menyaring ibu hamil calon donor untuk memastikan tidak ada riwayat penyakit genetik. Kami melakukan tes darah lengkap, dan hanya tali pusar dari persalinan cukup bulan (C-section) yang diterima.

Infants are required to have an Apgar score of 8 or higher (a measure of newborn health). Samples will be rejected if the delivery is preterm or the infant has a low birth weight.

In our laboratory, we exclusively use GMP-grade or clinical-grade raw materials, not research-grade materials. We limit cell passages to a maximum of 4. Should cells fail to meet our quality standards, the batch is discarded, not sold.

Dr. Shimizu: Ini sangat penting — sebagian besar perusahaan lain akan mencoba menjual semua yang mereka produksi. Tetapi 23 Century benar-benar berkomitmen pada kualitas, bukan volume. Dari 15 cm tali pusar, Anda dapat memproduksi sekitar 30-50 paket dosis, tetapi hanya jika setiap batch memenuhi standar kualitas yang ketat. Ini benar-benar luar biasa.

Secretome: More Than Just Exosomes

Dr. Shimizu: Saat ini, ada banyak pembicaraan tentang exosome di pasar Jepang. Bagaimana secretome berbeda, dan mengapa 23 Century memilih untuk memproduksi secretome lengkap daripada hanya exosome?

Dr. Angerina Tiah: Ini adalah pertanyaan penting. Exosome hanyalah salah satu komponen dari secretome. Secretome mencakup:

  • Extracellular vesicles
  • Microvesicles
  • Exosome
  • Chemokines and Cytokines
  • Growth factor

Exosomes function as "commanders," dispatching instructions to initiate repair processes at specific locations. However, for these repairs to be effectively carried out, an "execution army" of growth factors, such as EGF (epidermal growth factor) and FGF (fibroblast growth factor), is indispensable. Exosomes alone are insufficient.

Most laboratories isolate only exosomes and discard other components. 23 Century produces a complete secretome, with all components working synergistically for optimal regenerative outcomes.

Secretome Manufacturing Process: Commitment to Excellence

Dr. Shimizu: Bagaimana 23 Century memproduksi secretome, terutama dibandingkan dengan laboratorium standar?

Dr. Angerina Tiah: Laboratorium standar mulai dengan membudidayakan sel punca mesenkimal sehat. Selama kultivasi, sel melepaskan secretome ke dalam media (cairan berwarna merah muda yang berisi phenol red dan protein lainnya yang tidak dapat disuntikkan ke tubuh manusia).

Conventionally, laboratories extract stem cells for commercial sale, discarding the culture media and thereby foregoing most of the benefits. Some laboratories solely extract exosomes for sale.

The 23rd Century adopted a fundamentally different approach.

  1. We harvest healthy mesenchymal stem cells (passage 4).
  2. We dispose of initial culture media (as is standard for all laboratories).
  3. Subsequently, we applied controlled stress to the stem cells to induce the maximal release of secretome.
  4. We isolated this secretome, which is rich in all its components.
  5. We discard stressed stem cells. Although this incurs a financial loss, we prioritize quality.
  6. We process the secretome to remove contaminants and concentrate active components.
  7. We store it in liquid form, rather than freeze-dried, to preserve maximal biological activity.

Through an approach focused on extracting the therapeutic essence rather than simply providing the raw material, we generate secretome with maximized therapeutic potential.

Dr. Shimizu: Di Jepang, tidak ada standar regulasi seperti cGMP. Produk yang diproduksi secara sembarangan sering kali beredar. Tetapi 23 Century benar-benar berkomitmen pada kualitas, keamanan, dan efikasi. Inilah mengapa kami mempercayai mereka sepenuhnya.

Why liquid instead of freeze-dried?

Dr. Shimizu: Di Jepang, sebagian besar produk secretome freeze-dried (dikeringkan dengan liofisasi). Mengapa 23 Century tetap menggunakan bentuk cair?

Dr. Angerina Tiah: Kami telah memverifikasi bahwa protein dalam secretome sangat stabil dalam bentuk cair, bahkan pada suhu kamar. Tidak ada kebutuhan untuk freeze-dry karena protein tetap stabil.

Conversely, freeze-drying can dehydrate protein structures and diminish their biological activity. Storing the secretome in liquid form allows us to preserve its maximal therapeutic potential.

Verification of room temperature stability has been completed, and additional data collection is ongoing. The results indicate that quality is maintained at ambient temperature; therefore, there is no justification for freeze-drying and potentially compromising efficacy.

Dr. Shimizu: Kami percaya bahwa dalam beberapa tahun ke depan, teknologi ini akan diakui secara luas. Terima kasih telah berbagi visi luar biasa ini dengan kami semua.
