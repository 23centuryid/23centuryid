Terapi Sel PuncaMesenchymal Stem Cell Therapy

Memperbaiki jaringan dengan kekuatan sel, dan
mengembalikan kesehatan

Restoring lost function through the regenerative capacity of stem cells.

Terapi Sel Punca melibatkan pemberian Mesenchymal Stem Cell (MSC) untuk merangsang perbaikan jaringan dan mengatur respons imun.
Sel punca memiliki kemampuan perbaikan diri yang membantu memulihkan fungsi sel yang menurun akibat penuaan atau penyakit.

di 23Century kami menggunakan Wharton's Jelly MSC (WJ-MSC) yang berasal dari tali pusat bayi baru lahir. Karena memiliki daya proliferasi dan kemampuan diferensiasi yang tinggi, WJ-MSC diharapkan dapat memberikan manfaat pada berbagai kondisi penyakit.
Selain itu, karena kami menggunakan sel allogenik yang telah dikultur, tidak diperlukan pengambilan sel dari pasien sendiri sehingga pengobatan dapat dimulai lebih cepat dibandingkan terapi autolog.

Key Benefits

Promotes tissue/organ repair and regeneration / Regulates immune function (suppressing inflammation, ameliorating autoimmunity)

Kemampuan regeneratif sel punca menunjukkan potensi untuk pengobatan gangguan sendi, penyakit neurologis, dan penyakit autoimun.
Khususnya, sifat anti-inflamasi dan fungsi pengaturan imun sel punca menjadikannya menjanjikan untuk perbaikan kondisi kronis dan penyakit sulit diobati.

Stem cell age affects therapeutic effectiveness.

Wharton's Jelly MSC
– sel punca yang menopang masa depan pengobatan regeneratif

Wharton's Jelly MSC diambil dari tali pusat bayi baru lahir sehingga tidak membebani donor dan memiliki kemampuan diferensiasi yang mendekati pluripotensi.
Karena kemampuan proliferasi dan efek anti-inflamasi yang kuat, WJ-MSC diharapkan dapat diaplikasikan pada berbagai penyakit neurodegeneratif dan penyakit autoimun yang sulit diobati.

In terms of safety, WJ-MSC exhibits low immunogenicity, consequently, the risk of immune rejection is relatively low. Adverse effects are generally mild, such as transient fever, and long-term complications are virtually unreported.

Terapi Sel NKNatural Killer Cell Therapy

Melawan kanker dan virus dengan kekuatan imun
yang diperkuat

A therapy that enhances NK cell activity and maximally activates innate immunity.

NK Cell Therapy involves the ex vivo proliferation and activation of Natural Killer (NK) cells, which are then reinfused into the patient to combat pathogens and abnormal cells.

This approach leverages the body's natural killer (NK) cell capabilities without the need for aggressive pharmaceuticals, making it a significant form of immunotherapy that is garnering attention in cancer treatment.

Enhanced NK cells will patrol the body, attacking and eliminating cancer cells or virus-infected cells upon detection. Side effects are generally mild, such as temporary fever or fatigue after infusion, and reports of serious adverse events are very rare.

Key Benefits

Elimination of abnormal cells to suppress cancer growth / Enhance resistance to viral infections

Extensive potential of NK Cell Therapy

Next-generation therapy for immune balance and health maintenance.

NK Cell Therapy enhances natural immune strength to support health. Beyond its role as an adjunctive therapy in cancer treatment—by helping to suppress tumor growth and prevent recurrence—it also holds potential to ameliorate immunosuppression and prevent infections.

The therapeutic regimen begins with lymphocyte collection via blood draw, followed by a culture period of approximately 1-2 weeks prior to intravenous administration, thereby enabling outpatient administration with minimal patient burden.

Terapi SecretomeSecretome Therapy

Mengeluarkan kekuatan perbaikan sel, dan
pengobatan regeneratif yang berkembang

A therapy utilizing stem cell-secreted regenerative factors to promote intrinsic cellular repair.

Secretome therapy utilizes bioactive substances secreted by stem cells (e.g., cytokines, growth factors, exosomes) to enhance cellular repair and regeneration capabilities—a novel approach in regenerative medicine.

Since this therapy utilizes bioactive components without transplanting whole cells, the risk of immune rejection and tumorigenesis is lower compared to introducing allogeneic cells into the body, thereby offering an attractive safety profile.

Moreover, by streamlining the whole cell culture and handling processes, this therapy can be rendered more cost-effective and alleviate patient burden, thus garnering significant interest in expanding clinical applications.

Harnessing the regenerative potential of cells through a safe and practical approach.

In Secretome Therapy, administered growth factors and cytokines help stimulate cell proliferation and tissue reconstruction in damaged areas, while simultaneously mitigating excessive inflammatory responses to reduce pain and swelling.

Research in this field, including applications in joint disorders, neurological diseases, and the aesthetic field, continues to increase. The combination of Stem Cell Therapy with Secretome is also being explored to produce synergistic effects that enhance the onset and effectiveness of therapy.
