In the 23rd Century, we utilize cutting-edge regenerative medical technology and provide three treatment options: Stem Cell Therapy, NK Cell Therapy, and Secretome Therapy.
Each therapy uses different approaches to promote cell regeneration, tissue repair, and enhanced immune function to support the patient's health recovery.

By combining multiple therapies, we can achieve a synergistic effect to enhance treatment outcomes.
We will recommend the most suitable treatment plan based on the condition and needs of each patient.

Terapi Sel PuncaMesenchymal Stem Cell Therapy

Repairing the network with cell strength, and
restore health

Restoration of lost function through the regenerative capabilities of stem cells.

Stem Cell Therapy involves the administration of Mesenchymal Stem Cells (MSCs) to stimulate tissue repair and regulate the immune response.
Stem cells have self-repair capabilities that help restore cell function diminished by aging or disease.

In the 23rd Century, we use Wharton's Jelly MSC (WJ-MSC) derived from newborn umbilical cords. Because they possess high proliferative capacity and differentiation ability, WJ-MSC are expected to provide benefits for various disease conditions.
In addition, because we use cultured allogeneic cells, there is no need to collect cells from the patient themselves, allowing treatment to begin more quickly than with autologous therapy.

Key Benefits

Promotes tissue/organ repair and regeneration / Regulates immune function (suppressing inflammation, ameliorating autoimmunity)

The regenerative ability of stem cells demonstrates potential for the treatment of joint disorders, neurological diseases, and autoimmune diseases.
Particularly, the anti-inflammatory properties and immune regulatory functions of stem cells make them promising for the improvement of chronic conditions and difficult-to-treat diseases.

Stem cell age influences therapy effectiveness.

Wharton's Jelly MSC
Stem cells that underpin the future of regenerative medicine

Wharton's Jelly MSCs are derived from newborn umbilical cords, thus not burdening the donor, and possess differentiation capabilities that approach pluripotency.
Due to their strong proliferative capacity and potent anti-inflammatory effects, WJ-MSCs are expected to be applicable in various difficult-to-treat neurodegenerative and autoimmune diseases.

In terms of safety, WJ-MSC demonstrates low immunogenicity, resulting in a relatively small risk of rejection reactions. Side effects are generally mild, such as transient fever, and long-term complications are rarely reported.

Terapi Sel NKNatural Killer Cell Therapy

Fighting cancer and viruses with immune power
that is reinforced

A therapy that enhances NK cell activity and optimally activates innate immunity.

NK Cell Therapy involves the ex vivo proliferation and activation of Natural Killer (NK) cells, which are then reinfused into the patient to combat pathogens and abnormal cells.

This approach harnesses the body's natural NK cell capabilities without the need for harsh drugs, positioning it as a form of immunotherapy gaining attention in cancer treatment.

Enhanced NK cells will patrol the body, attacking and eliminating cancer cells or virus-infected cells upon detection. Side effects are generally mild, such as temporary fever or fatigue after infusion, and reports of serious adverse events are very rare.

Key Benefits

Elimination of abnormal cells to suppress cancer growth / Enhance resistance to viral infections

Extensive potential of NK Cell Therapy

Next-generation therapy for immune balance and health maintenance.

NK Cell Therapy enhances natural immune strength to support health. In addition to its role as an adjunctive therapy in cancer treatment—by helping to suppress tumor growth and prevent recurrence—it also holds potential for ameliorating immunosuppression and preventing infections.

The therapeutic sequence commences with lymphocyte collection via phlebotomy, followed by a culture period of approximately 1-2 weeks prior to intravenous infusion. This approach facilitates outpatient administration, minimizing the burden on patients.

Terapi SecretomeSecretome Therapy

Unleashing the power of cellular repair, and
Emerging regenerative medicine

A therapy that harnesses stem cell-secreted therapeutic factors to stimulate cellular self-repair capabilities.

Secretome therapy utilizes bioactive substances secreted by stem cells (e.g., cytokines, growth factors, exosomes) to enhance cellular repair and regeneration—a novel approach in regenerative medicine.

As this therapy utilizes bioactive components without whole-cell transplantation, the risk of immune rejection and tumorigenesis is lower than with the introduction of allogeneic cells, thus offering an attractive safety profile.

Moreover, by simplifying the cell culture process and whole cell management, this therapy can be more cost-effective and reduce patient burden, thereby garnering significant interest in its expanding clinical applications.
