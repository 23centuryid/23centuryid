    Combating Cancer and Viruses through Immune Strength

    NK cell therapy is a treatment method that harnesses the power of natural killer (NK) cells, which are naturally present in the body.

    NK cell, an abbreviation for "Natural Killer Cell," is a type of white blood cell that constitutes a vital component of the innate immune system, which is present from birth. These cells constantly patrol the blood and organs, monitoring and surveilling abnormal cells that pose a threat to the body.

    Specifically, NK cells possess receptors on their cell surface to recognize abnormal cells. Upon encountering virus-infected or cancerous cells, they immediately initiate an attack.

    True to their name, Natural Killer (NK) cells possess a robust capacity to eliminate abnormal cells. In NK cell therapy, these cells are harvested from the body, expanded and activated ex vivo, and subsequently reinfused to enhance the body's disease-fighting capabilities.

    This treatment enhances the body's intrinsic immune strength, without relying on complex pharmaceuticals, and has garnered attention as a form of cancer immunotherapy.

    The Role of the Immune System and the Importance of NK Cells

    The human body possesses an immune system to protect itself from threats such as bacteria, viruses, and cancer cells. Immunity can be categorized into two types: innate immunity (natural immunity), which is present from birth, and adaptive immunity (acquired immunity), which is developed later.

    NK cells are key cells regulating innate immunity, and with their inherent capabilities, they can swiftly recognize and eliminate abnormalities. For instance, cells infected with influenza viruses or nascent cancerous cells that emerge daily are, in many cases, eliminated early by NK cells, thereby maintaining health.

    When NK cell function is compromised, individuals become susceptible to viral infections or allow cancer cells to proliferate, thus NK cells play a crucial role in maintaining health.

    Basic Mechanism of NK Cell Therapy

    Terapi NK cell adalah metode pengobatan yang secara aktif memanfaatkan kekuatan NK cell.
    Secara khusus, NK cell dari pasien itu sendiri atau dari donor diambil dari tubuh, kemudian di lingkungan khusus, cell ini diperbanyak dan diaktifkan (ditingkatkan vitalitasnya), lalu dikembalikan ke dalam tubuh pasien.

    Using a method known as cell culture, the number of NK cells is significantly expanded. Subsequently, by stimulating them with agents such as Interleukin 2 (IL-2) and Interferon Gamma (IFN-γ), NK cells are activated into a more potent state than their baseline activity.

    Kemudian dengan memberi NK cell kembali melalui transfusi, NK cell bekerja keras dalam tubuh pasien untuk melawan sel kanker dan sel yang terinfeksi virus.
    Dengan kata lain, terapi NK cell dapat dikatakan sebagai pengobatan yang meningkatkan pertahanan penyakit dengan meningkatkan jumlah dan kekuatan patroli kekebalan tubuh.

    NK Cell Mechanism

    How NK Cells Work in the Body

    NK cells continuously function as a surveillance mechanism within the body. To differentiate normal from abnormal cells, NK cells examine information present on the cell surface.

    Sel yang sehat mengirim sinyal "aku adalah sel normal", tetapi sel yang terinfeksi virus atau sel kanker menunjukkan sinyal yang terganggu atau hilang.
    NK cell mendeteksi gangguan ini melalui "antena" (reseptor) dan ketika menilai "sel ini mungkin berbahaya", segera mengambil tindakan.

    Kerja NK cell sangat cepat, dan karena telah diprogramkan sejak lahir, dapat segera menemukan dan mengatasi musuh tanpa perlu belajar terlebih dahulu.
    Benar-benar berfungsi sebagai pasukan respons cepat dalam tubuh, menangani infeksi pada tahap awal atau tunas kanker yang sangat kecil.

    Mechanism of Attack Against Cancer Cells and Abnormal Cells

    Upon detecting abnormal cells, NK cells directly attack them. This mechanism is broadly divided into two.

    Firstly, NK cells make contact with abnormal cells and release perforin. Perforin creates pores in the membrane of the target cell, a mechanism by which NK cells damage the abnormal cells.

    The second is, after creating a pore, to deliver enzymes called granzymes. Granzymes enter the enemy cell and "press the self-destruct button" to cause that cell to die.

    Through these mechanisms, NK cells eliminate virally infected or cancerous cells internally and safely process them. Furthermore, because NK cell-mediated responses are executed without awaiting specific commands, NK cells can reduce the pathogen load more rapidly than adaptive immune responses (T cells or B cells), which are activated subsequent to pathogen infection.

    Benefits of NK Cell Therapy (Natural Immune Enhancement, Cancer Suppression Effects, etc.)

    NK cell therapy offers several advantages. Firstly, it enhances natural immunity. By strengthening the patient's intrinsic immune capabilities, overall bodily defenses are improved, which can lead to the prevention of recurrence and other infections.

    Secondly, a cancer suppressive effect is anticipated. Activated NK cells not only directly attack and reduce cancer cells but also, by damaging and rendering them vulnerable, facilitate a synergistic effect when combined with other therapeutic modalities (e.g., chemotherapy or radiotherapy).

    Furthermore, NK cell therapy is considered relatively safe, with serious side effects rarely occurring (a point that will be elaborated upon later). In addition, because NK cells can target a wide range of cancers and viruses, there is the potential to eliminate a broad spectrum of abnormal cells, rather than merely specific targets.

    In this manner, NK cell therapy is characterized as a novel treatment option that bolsters the body's intrinsic ability to combat disease.

    NK Cell Therapy Application

    Anticipated Diseases and Symptoms Associated with NK Cell Therapy (e.g., Cancer, Immunosuppression, Viral Infections)

    NK cell therapy is expected to find widespread application across diverse fields.

    A particularly representative application is cancer treatment. Ongoing research is exploring the potential efficacy of NK cell therapy against both solid tumors (e.g., lung, liver, and breast cancers) and hematological malignancies (leukemia and lymphoma). In clinical trials involving NK cell therapy for cancer patients, reports are emerging of tumor regression or slowed progression.

    Furthermore, in situations where immunocompromise is observed, NK cell therapy is anticipated. For instance, in elderly individuals or patients with severe underlying conditions experiencing immune suppression, or patients with diminished immune cell counts following bone marrow transplantation or chemotherapy, supplementing NK cells offers the potential to assist in infection prevention and the restoration of vitality.

    Furthermore, its application in viral infections is also being considered. Given that NK cells can target virus-infected cells, research is currently underway for chronic viral infections (e.g., hepatitis B and C viruses) and as a strategy to prevent severe outcomes for newly emerging viruses.

    Finally, as a distinctive area, attention is also given to anti-aging and lifestyle disease prevention. It is understood that NK cell function declines with age, and with NK cell therapy, by maintaining active immune surveillance, research is exploring its potential to prevent the onset of age-related diseases or extend healthy lifespan.

    Thus, NK cell therapy has the potential to meet diverse needs, ranging from cancer treatment to immune support and preventive medicine.

    Expected Effects (Cancer Immunotherapy, Viral Infection Prevention, Anti-aging)

    The following is a summary of the specific effects that can be expected from NK cell therapy, grouped by area.

    • Efek sebagai Imunoterapi Kanker:
      NK cell yang diaktifkan berpatroli dalam tubuh dan secara langsung menyerang sel kanker, diharapkan efek mengecilkan tumor atau menekan pertumbuhan. Selain itu, dengan merusak sel kanker dan melemahkannya, ada kemungkinan meningkatkan efektivitas perawatan lain (kemoterapi atau radioterapi). Ini juga dapat menyebabkan pengurangan risiko kekambuhan setelah pengobatan.
    • Pencegahan Infeksi Virus:
      Karena NK cell dapat menghilangkan sel yang terinfeksi virus lebih awal, diharapkan efek mengurangi jumlah virus dalam infeksi virus kronis, misalnya, atau mencegah keparahan ketika terkena infeksi virus. Dengan menambah NK cell pada orang yang kekebalan tubuhnya melemah, ada kemungkinan bermanfaat untuk pencegahan infeksi sekunder seperti influenza atau pneumonia.
    • Anti-Aging dan Pemeliharaan Kesehatan:
      Ketika imun alami diaktifkan melalui terapi NK cell, daya yang menekan abnormalitas sel yang terjadi sehari-hari (tunas kankerisasi, akumulasi sel tua yang dihasilkan dari regenerasi) diharapkan meningkat. Hasilnya, ada kemungkinan untuk mencegah penyakit yang disertai penuaan atau meningkatkan dan mempertahankan status kesehatan. Misalnya, ada laporan kasus pasien yang merasa efek kesehatan sehari-hari seperti "menjadi lebih tahan flu" atau "tidak mudah lelah".

    Seperti di atas, terapi NK cell diharapkan memiliki berbagai efek tidak hanya dari efek pengobatan itu sendiri, tetapi juga dari perspektif pencegahan dan peningkatan kualitas hidup.
    Namun, penting untuk dicatat bahwa efek dapat bervariasi antar individu, dan tidak semua pasien akan menunjukkan efek serupa.

    With the accumulation of data from ongoing clinical studies, a more comprehensive understanding of efficacy and precise indications will be gained.

    NK Cell Therapy Methods

    Workflow from NK Cell Collection to Administration (Blood Draw → Culture → Transfusion)

    NK cell therapy broadly involves three steps: "NK cell collection" → "expansion and activation" → "reintroduction into the body". The general process is as follows:

    • Pengumpulan NK Cell (Tes Darah):
      Darah diambil dari lengan pasien. Volume yang diperlukan bervariasi tergantung pengobatan, tetapi biasanya hanya beberapa puluh mililiter darah yang cukup (sekitar 1-beberapa jarum suntik besar, jumlah yang lebih sedikit dari donor darah). NK cell yang terkandung dalam darah ini akan diambil kemudian. Dalam terapi autolog, darah dari pasien sendiri digunakan, sedangkan dalam terapi allogenik, NK cell dikumpulkan dari darah donor atau darah tali pusat.
    • Kultur dan Aktivasi NK Cell:
      Darah yang dikumpulkan dikirim ke fasilitas kultur sel khusus. Di sana, NK cell dipisahkan dari sel lain dan dikultivasi (proses memperbanyak sel). Selama periode 1-2 minggu, jumlah NK cell ditingkatkan hingga miliaran sel. Secara bersamaan, dengan menambahkan IL-2 (Interleukin 2) dan zat perangsang lainnya, NK cell diaktifkan untuk diubah menjadi kondisi yang lebih mudah menyerang sel kanker. Selama kultur, lingkungan steril ketat dipertahankan dan perhatian ekstrim diberikan untuk mencegah infeksi atau mutasi.
    • Pemberian NK Cell (Transfusi):
      NK cell yang telah mencapai jumlah cukup melalui kultur dan diaktifkan, dimasukkan ke dalam kantong transfusi dan dikembalikan ke pasien. Kemudian diberikan ke dalam tubuh melalui transfusi atau injeksi. Dalam kasus transfusi, sama seperti transfusi darah biasa atau transfusi obat, NK cell perlahan-lahan dimasukkan melalui vena di lengan. Pemberian itu sendiri selesai dalam puluhan menit hingga sekitar satu jam, tanpa rasa sakit yang signifikan. Dalam beberapa kasus, dapat juga dilakukan injeksi lokal ke bagian spesifik (misalnya lokasi tumor). Setelah pemberian, pasien istirahat sejenak dan kondisi dipantau untuk memastikan tidak ada masalah.

    The above outlines the standard procedure for a single session of NK cell therapy. As a culture period of approximately 1-2 weeks is required from blood collection to administration, preliminary scheduling is conducted.

    For patients, blood test and transfusion dates are scheduled. During the interim, patients can maintain their normal routines. Once the culture results are available, patients return to the medical facility for their transfusion. Inpatient admission is not required, and in most cases, both blood tests and transfusions can be performed on an outpatient basis.

    Administration Method Options (Transfusion, Local Injection, Single Administration vs. Continuous Treatment)

    The method of NK cell therapy administration and its frequency are tailored based on the patient's condition and therapeutic objectives. The primary options are as follows:

    Rute Pemberian: Umumnya, pemberian intravena umum melalui transfusi dipilih. Dengan transfusi, NK cell yang diaktifkan menyebar ke seluruh tubuh, yang memiliki keuntungan dapat bekerja pada sel kanker atau sel yang terinfeksi virus di mana pun letaknya dalam tubuh.

    On the other hand, when it is desired to target therapeutic effects to specific areas, direct local injection may be considered. For example, for hepatic cancer, it can be injected via a catheter into the hepatic vasculature, or for cutaneous tumors, it can be injected directly peritumorally.

    However, local injections are restricted to facilities capable of administering them, and their systemic effects are not entirely localized, potentially impacting other systemic conditions; consequently, decisions are made on a case-by-case basis.

    Frekuensi dan Jadwal Pengobatan: Ada pemberian tunggal (pengobatan sekali) dan pengobatan berkelanjutan (dilakukan beberapa kali). Dalam banyak kasus, terapi NK cell dilakukan dengan jadwal multi-sesi.

    This is because infused NK cells function in the body for a temporary period but their numbers diminish over time. Therefore, to maintain and enhance the effect, periodic administration of NK cells is recommended.

    For instance, transfusions are administered once every two weeks, with this regimen repeated over several months for a total of 5-6 sessions (representing one course of treatment). Depending on the patient's condition, the treatment period may be extended, or the intervals may be adjusted.

    Conversely, if the objective is cancer recurrence prevention or the maintenance of immune strength, it can also be performed at longer intervals, with additions made only as necessary, subject to ongoing situational assessment.

    The method of administration and frequency are determined by considering the balance between treatment efficacy and patient burden. The physician consults with the patient to establish an optimal plan, so if you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask. The number of administrations and the intervals between them vary among individuals but will be explained in detail during the treatment planning stage.

    Treatment Duration and Procedure Schedule (Patient-Specific Frequency and Number)

    The duration of NK cell therapy varies for each patient as described above; however, a common example is provided here.

    For example, in some patients, a regimen was established in which a total of 6 transfusions were administered over approximately 3 months as a single course, and patient progress was monitored.

    In this case, following an initial blood test, a culture period of approximately 2 weeks was completed, after which the first transfusion was administered. Subsequently, the cycle of blood test, culture, and transfusion was repeated bi-weekly for a total of six times (approximately 12 weeks). Upon completion of the sixth transfusion, an efficacy evaluation was conducted. Based on the results, a decision was made regarding whether to terminate treatment, establish a drug-free period, or proceed with a subsequent course.

    Conversely, some regimens are designed for short-term observation, while others involve long-term periodic maintenance. In cancer treatment, these are often administered intensively multiple times. However, for immune system maintenance or anti-aging purposes, case reports describe administration at a frequency of approximately once every few months to six months, on an ongoing basis.

    It is essential to flexibly adapt the treatment plan, considering the patient's physiological state and the manner in which effects manifest. Should it be determined during the course of treatment that the therapeutic effects are insufficient, a transition to alternative treatment modalities may be warranted. Conversely, if the patient's condition is stable, the interval can be extended prior to transitioning to ongoing surveillance.

    The treatment schedule should be thoroughly discussed in advance, considering its impact on lifestyle. Any concerns regarding visit frequency, duration, or the possibility of schedule modification should be addressed with your treating physician.

    NK cell therapy is a treatment with a relatively low impact on patients, but it still necessitates routine visits. Therefore, establishing a self-paced plan is crucial for successful outcomes.

    Comparison with Other Immunotherapies (CAR-T, PD-1 Inhibitors, etc.)

    Regarding the safety of NK cell therapy, it is often compared with other immunotherapies such as CAR-T cell therapy and immune checkpoint inhibitors (e.g., PD-1 inhibitors). All of these are treatment modalities that have recently garnered attention; however, there are differences in their side effect profiles.

    Perbandingan dengan Terapi CAR-T: Terapi CAR-T adalah metode pengobatan kanker yang sangat kuat menggunakan sel T yang telah dimodifikasi secara genetik. Namun karena kekuatannya, efek samping seperti cytokine release syndrome (sistem imun keluaran kontrol, demam tinggi, penurunan tekanan darah) atau neurotoksisitas (gejala saraf seperti gangguan kesadaran atau kejang) dapat terjadi. Ada laporan kasus yang memerlukan manajemen di unit perawatan intensif.

    On the other hand, in NK cell therapy, the incidence of such serious side effects is currently very rare. NK cells tend not to proliferate extensively within the body or persist for extended periods, and are therefore believed not to easily become dysregulated beyond what is required.

    Therefore, it is said to have a wide safety margin. However, unlike CAR-T therapy, which can achieve a profound one-time therapeutic effect, NK cell therapy often requires repeated treatments.

    Perbandingan dengan Inhibitor Checkpoint Imun: Inhibitor PD-1 (di Jepang, nivolumab dan pembrolizumab terkenal) adalah obat yang melepaskan rem pada sistem imun dalam tubuh, memungkinkan sel T untuk melawan kanker dengan lebih kuat.

    Despite its high efficacy, immune disinhibition can result in autoimmune-related adverse events. Specifically, these may involve the immune system attacking self-organs or tissues, causing pneumonitis (inflammation of the lungs), colitis, hepatitis, skin rash, endocrine abnormalities, and various other potential adverse events.

    Given its potential to be life-threatening in severe cases, close monitoring is essential during checkpoint inhibitor therapy, and should adverse events occur, management with immunosuppressants is required.

    Conversely, NK cell therapy involves the administration of exogenous cells into the body. However, as these cells are themselves innate immune cells, they rarely attack the body's own healthy tissues in an autoimmune-like fashion. This means that NK cells specifically target abnormal cells and are fundamentally non-damaging to normal cells, thereby resulting in a limited spectrum of side effects.

    However, when using allogeneic NK cells, caution regarding allergic reactions and other potential adverse events is required, as previously mentioned.

    In conclusion, NK cell therapy presents a lower risk of side effects compared to other advanced immunotherapies. While every treatment carries inherent benefits and drawbacks, and there are instances where alternative therapies may be more appropriate, from a safety perspective, NK cell therapy can be considered a relatively less burdensome option for patients.

    NK cell therapy is a cutting-edge treatment method that harnesses the full potential of the body's natural immune system. By activating and enhancing NK cells, which directly target cancer cells and virus-infected cells, we bolster the body's ability to combat disease.

    Specifically, a major advantage is the minimal side effects and low physiological burden. When integrated with immunotherapy or conventional cancer treatments, further synergistic effects can also be anticipated. Moreover, its role in maintaining immune strength and its application as an anti-aging treatment are garnering interest. Continuous administration is also feasible as part of a comprehensive approach to promoting overall health and well-being.
