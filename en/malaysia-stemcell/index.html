What Are Stem Cells?

Terapi sel punca adalah metode perawatan inovatif yang memperbaiki jaringan yang rusak dan membawa kekuatan regenerasi tubuh.
Mereka menjadi "sumber" dari semua sel seperti otot, saraf, dan kulit, dan memiliki kemampuan untuk mereplikasi diri sambil menghasilkan sel-sel baru.
Selain itu, lingkungan dan sistem terapi sel punca berbeda dari negara ke negara, dan ada perbedaan besar antara Indonesia dan Malaysia.

Allogeneic stem cells can utilize neonatal cells, thereby possessing higher regenerative and proliferative capacities and demonstrating superior immunomodulatory effects.

Furthermore, no collection is required, patient burden is zero, and treatment can be accessed immediately, which is a significant advantage.

Furthermore, the stability of therapeutic efficacy is enhanced by the prior utilization of quality-controlled cultured cells. Allogeneic stem cells are employed for intractable diseases, including neurological and immune disorders, with research and clinical applications expanding globally.

For those seeking to undergo the latest regenerative therapy safely and with minimal patient burden, allogeneic stem cell therapy presents an optimal option.

Exercise caution with animal-derived stem cells.

In the field of stem cell therapy, some facilities provide treatments using animal-derived stem cells. However, this method lacks adequate scientific support, and careful consideration is required regarding its safety and efficacy.

The primary risk associated with animal-derived stem cells is infection. Animals may harbor pathogens unknown to humans, which could potentially transmit infections via these stem cells. Additionally, the introduction of animal cells into the body triggers an immune rejection response. This not only compromises the intended therapeutic effect but also poses a significant health risk.

Furthermore, clinical data demonstrating the effectiveness of animal-derived stem cell treatments is very limited. Stem cell therapy is primarily intended to achieve cell engraftment and functional recovery; however, there is no guarantee that animal-derived stem cells will integrate successfully into human tissues and function effectively. Conversely, given the uncertain long-term effects, many medical professionals adopt a cautious stance.

To receive safe and effective treatment, it is crucial to choose a clinic that utilizes human-derived stem cells. Reputable institutions clearly identify the source of their stem cells and cultivate them in culture facilities that adhere to international medical standards. When considering stem cell therapy, be sure to verify the source of the cells used, the culture methods, and the presence or absence of regulatory approvals, and undergo treatment with demonstrated safety.

Imbalance May Be Attributable to Aging

Saat orang menua dan merasakan penurunan dari usia muda, mereka menyadari "penuaan".
Namun, saat ini, sangat sedikit orang yang berpikir mendalam tentang penuaan dan memahami mekanismenya.
Sebenarnya, apa itu "penuaan"?

Decreased cell number and impaired cell function

Tubuh kita memiliki sekitar 372 miliar sel, tetapi itu hanya sampai usia 20-25 tahun.
Mulai dari sekitar usia 26 tahun, sel mulai berkurang, dan dalam waktu sekitar 75 tahun hingga usia 90 tahun, mereka berkurang menjadi sekitar setengahnya.

As individuals age, the formation of wrinkles, skin laxity, and volume loss is not solely due to the degradation of muscle and collagenous tissue, but also to a reduction in cellularity itself.

Pada saat yang sama, metabolisme, yang menggantikan sel lama dengan sel baru, juga menurun.
Sedangkan pergantian kulit membutuhkan 28 hari pada usia 20-an, itu membutuhkan 100 hari pada usia 60-an.

The inability to cure diseases or injuries arises from the escalating annual challenge of replacing damaged or aged cells with new ones.

Previously, damaged or senescent cells were promptly degraded and eliminated from the body. However, when the rate of new cell replenishment declines, cells must be retained and utilized within the body, even if damaged or aged, to mitigate cellular depletion.

Cells continuously produce substances to sustain life, form tissues, and maintain organ function daily. However, as cells age, their function diminishes. The reduction in cell count and the aging process contribute to various ailments, collectively known as aging phenomena, manifesting in our bodies.
