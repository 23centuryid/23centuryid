AABB originated in the United States; however, its standards and certification system have been widely recognized and adopted internationally.

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) regulates blood establishments within its jurisdiction; however, AABB Standards and FDA regulations are complementary. AABB certification often assists facilities in preparing for FDA inspections and maintaining regulatory compliance.

Foundation for the Accreditation of Cellular Therapy (FACT)

FACT is an international organization that provides accreditation for cell therapy and hematopoietic stem cell transplantation facilities. FACT Standards largely align with or complement AABB Standards, and some facilities hold accreditation from both organizations.

JACIE (Joint Accreditation Committee ISCT & EBMT - Europe)

JACIE is a certification body in Europe that accredits transplant and cell therapy facilities. JACIE standards adopt principles similar to those of AABB and FACT, reflecting consistent international norms.
