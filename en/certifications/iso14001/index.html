Ikhtisar and Latar Belakang

ISO 14001 is standard international for System Manajemen Lingkungan (Environmental Management System - EMS). Standard this disusun oleh International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and provide kerangka kerja bagi organization for melindungi lingkungan and mengelola risk lingkungan which related with aktivitas bisnis they.

Since penerbitan pertama on 1996, ISO 14001 has direvisi berkali-kali (2004, 2015) for menyesuaikan with development isu lingkungan global and practice best international. Versi 2015 menekankan kesesuaian with standard ISO lainnya and integrasi pertimbangan lingkungan to in strategi bisnis secara keseluruhan.

Time this many organization in seluruh world membangun EMS sesuai ISO 14001 and obtain certification from party ketiga independen. Berdasarkan statistic resmi ISO, certification this tersebar in more from 170 country and became wrong satu standard system manajemen which most many used, sejajar with ISO 9001 (manajemen mutu).

Latar belakang munculnya standard this berkaitan with meningkatnya perhatian terhadap isu lingkungan global since 1990-an, including change iklim, pengurangan lapisan ozon, deforestasi, and pencemaran air. ISO develop standard this with merujuk practice-practice national (seperti BS 7750 in Inggris) for create norma international which konsisten and can recognized in berbagai country.

Requirement Main and Standard

ISO 14001 mensyaratkan organization for membangun and run System Manajemen Lingkungan (EMS) as well as continuously apply perbaikan berkelanjutan. Ketentuan rinci tercantum in standard, however poin-poin main meliputi:

Kebijakan and Komitmen Lingkungan

Organization harus menetapkan kebijakan lingkungan tertulis which mencerminkan komitmen pimpinan puncak terhadap perlindungan lingkungan. Kebijakan this harus dikomunikasikan kepada seluruh staff and party eksternal which relevan. Pimpinan puncak diharapkan not hanya menyetujui kebijakan, tetapi aktif terlibat so that budaya manajemen lingkungan became bagian from tata kelola organization.

Identifikasi Aspek and Impact Lingkungan

Organization harus mengidentifikasi all aktivitas, produk, and service which can impact on lingkungan — good impact langsung maupun not langsung. This include penggunaan energi, limbah, emisi, penggunaan air, and lainnya. Aspek which significant harus diprioritaskan for manajemen more lanjut.

Perencanaan and Penetapan Target Lingkungan

Berdasarkan identifikasi aspek, organization harus menetapkan target and program lingkungan which terukur and can achieved in jangka time tertentu. For example, reduce konsumsi energi sebesar 20% in dua years or reduce limbah B3 (Bahan Berbahaya and Beracun) through program daur ulang.

Implementasi and Operasional

Organization harus mengalokasikan sumber daya (orang, anggaran, peralatan) for melaksanakan program lingkungan. Procedure operasional harus developed and didokumentasikan for memastikan kegiatan performed sesuai plan. Staff harus dilatih about tanggung jawab they in manajemen lingkungan, and kesadaran lingkungan harus ditanamkan in seluruh organization.

Pemantauan, Pengukuran, and Evaluasi

Organization harus secara rutin memantau and measure kinerja lingkungan use indikator which has ditetapkan. Data harus dianalisis for mengevaluasi achievement target and mengidentifikasi area perbaikan. Audit internal and review manajemen berkala harus performed for memastikan effectiveness EMS.

Manajemen Risk and Opportunity

Organization harus mengidentifikasi risk lingkungan (for example, kecelakaan lingkungan, impact change iklim terhadap surgery) and opportunity (for example, efisiensi energi which can reduce cost). Kedua hal this harus dimasukkan to in EMS and ditangani through tindakan pencegahan and perbaikan.

Kepatuhan Hukum and Regulasi

Organization harus memastikan kepatuhan terhadap peraturan lingkungan national and international which berlaku. This merupakan requirement minimum; in atas that, organization can menetapkan target sukarela which more ketat for achieve komitmen lingkungan which more high.

Signifikansi and Benefit Kepatuhan

For Organization and Bisnis

Apply ISO 14001 help organization mengidentifikasi and mengelola risk lingkungan secara sistematis, which can reduce cost operasional (through efisiensi energi, pengurangan limbah) and enhance reputation perusahaan. Kepatuhan terhadap regulation lingkungan also became more mudah with adanya system manajemen which terstruktur.

Organization which apply ISO 14001 sering mendapatkan advantage kompetitif in pasar, because konsumen and partner bisnis semakin memperhatikan tanggung jawab lingkungan. Akreditasi ISO 14001 also memfasilitasi akses to pasar international and partner global which mempersyaratkan komitmen lingkungan.

For Masyarakat and Lingkungan

Many organization which menetapkan and melaksanakan target lingkungan sesuai ISO 14001 can berkontribusi on achievement tujuan lingkungan berskala large, seperti pengurangan emisi CO₂ and pelestarian ekosistem. In jangka long, masyarakat will mendapatkan benefit from quality udara and air which more good, as well as pengurangan kejadian cuaca ekstrem.

Selain that, if perusahaan or institution place orang work or learn apply ISO 14001, opportunity bagi individual for terlibat in aktivitas lingkungan will increased and kesadaran lingkungan will grow. Hal this can impact on change kebiasaan sehari-day menuju gaya hidup which more berkelanjutan.

Impact terhadap Produk, Service, and Konsumen

Produk and service from organization which apply ISO 14001 cenderung have jejak lingkungan which more small. For example, kemasan produk mungkin use bahan which can didaur ulang, or process manufacturing dioptimalkan for reduce emisi.

Secara keseluruhan, ISO 14001 is mekanisme which mengubah aktivitas bisnis in balik produk and service became more ramah lingkungan. Hasilnya, standard this support tercapainya "masyarakat which ramah lingkungan", so that konsumen get benefit berupa lingkungan which more bersih and future which berkelanjutan.

ISO 14001 memainkan peran important in create siklus in which perusahaan and konsumen bersama-same face isu lingkungan and berkontribusi on pembangunan berkelanjutan (sustainable development).

Organization International and Badan Regulation Related

ISO (International Organization for Standardization)

ISO is organization international nirlaba which beranggotakan badan standarisasi national from berbagai country (for example JISC in Jepang, BSI in Inggris, ANSI in Amerika Serikat). ISO 14001 disusun berdasarkan konsensus in komite teknis ISO/TC 207 (Environmental Management), with partisipasi specialist from country-country member.

Oleh because that, ISO 14001 that sendiri bukanlah undang-undang, however many country mengadopsinya sebagai standard national. In Jepang, standard this dikenal sebagai JIS Q 14001; in country-country lain also have versi national which selaras with ISO 14001. With demikian, ISO 14001 function sebagai standard global de facto for manajemen lingkungan.

Pemerintah and Badan Regulation National

Pemerintah in berbagai country sometimes support achievement certification ISO 14001 through kebijakan or program, for example dukungan administratif, subsidi for usaha small and menengah, or pengurangan pajak. In many country, kepatuhan terhadap peraturan lingkungan national can difasilitasi through penerapan ISO 14001.

Uni Eropa - EMAS (Eco-Management and Audit Scheme)

In Uni Eropa terdapat skema manajemen lingkungan named EMAS which can proceed paralel and saling melengkapi with ISO 14001. EMAS have requirement which more ketat in beberapa aspek, especially in hal pelaporan public and audit eksternal.

Institution Certification and Akreditasi

Certification ISO 14001 not dikeluarkan langsung oleh ISO; process audit and pemberian certificate performed oleh badan certification party ketiga which berwenang in each-each country. For example, institution certification seperti Japan Quality Assurance Organization (JQA) and British Standards Institution (BSI) perform audit; if dianggap memenuhi requirement, they menerbitkan certificate.

Keandalan institution certification this biasanya guaranteed oleh badan akreditasi national (in Jepang: JAB — Japan Accreditation Board). Secara international, IAF (International Accreditation Forum) mengkoordinasikan saling pengakuan antar badan akreditasi national so that certification from country different can dipandang setara. With mekanisme berlapis this, keandalan certification ISO 14001 terjamin in seluruh world.

WHO and Initiative Lingkungan Global Lainnya

ISO 14001 berperan sebagai equipment which mendorong upaya lingkungan sukarela perusahaan and melengkapi regulation lingkungan national. Belakangan, kerangka pelaporan and target related iklim seperti TCFD (Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures) and Science-Based Targets (SBT) get perhatian; ISO 14001 can became landasan manajemen lingkungan which melingkupi initiative-initiative tersebut.

ISO 14001 also berkontribusi on achievement Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) PBB, especially SDG 12 (konsumsi and production which bertanggung jawab) and SDG 13 (aksi iklim).

Singkatnya, ISO 14001 function sebagai center berbagai initiative lingkungan and menyediakan "bahasa" which same so that organization can menangani berbagai isu lingkungan secara sistematis and terukur, good for kepatuhan regulation maupun for tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan and keberlanjutan bisnis jangka long.
