    Ikhtisar and Latar Belakang

    cGTP (current Good Tissue Practice) is standard which manage pengelolaan produk based cell and tissue human for memastikan keamanan and quality mulai from pengambilan donor hingga penggunaan clinical.

  • cGTP merupakan singkatan from "current Good Tissue Practice" and mengacu on standard regulation which developed oleh FDA (Food and Drug Administration) in Amerika Serikat. Standard this special berlaku for penanganan cell and tissue human (HCT/P - Human Cells, Tissues, and cellular and tissue-based Products).

    Privacy Policy

    • Latar belakang pengembangan cGTP is meningkatnya request will procedure transplantasi and cell therapy, disertai with kekhawatiran about possibility penularan disease menular through produk biologis. For mencegah penyebaran infeksi seperti HIV, hepatitis, or agen infeksi lainnya from donor to penerima, FDA menetapkan requirement cGTP on years 2004 and mulai berlaku penuh on 2005.

    With adoption cGTP, institution which menangani tissue and cell human in seluruh world (especially which bermitra with institution in AS) also apply prinsip serupa. Standard this became dasar bagi practice which safe in penyimpanan stem cell, pemrosesan tissue transplantasi, and manajemen produk biotechnology based cell.

    Evaluasi and Pemeriksaan Kelayakan Donor

    Penyedia or donor from cell and tissue harus underwent comprehensive examination terhadap riwayat health and tes laboratory for mendeteksi infeksi berbahaya. Tes which diminta include pemeriksaan terhadap HIV, hepatitis B, hepatitis C, sifilis, and agen infeksi lainnya which can ditransmisikan. Donor which have risk infeksi harus dikeluarkan, and produk they not boleh used.

    Pengendalian Fasilitas and Lingkungan

    Facility which memproses tissue and cell harus designed for surgery aseptik (steril). This including zona pembersihan (clean rooms), lemari safety biologis, system filtrasi udara, and pemantauan lingkungan teratur through pengujian mikroorganisme. Peralatan harus divalidasi and dipelihara secara berkala for memastikan fungsi optimal.

    Manajemen and Kontrol Bahan Baku

    Ringkasan and Latar Belakang

    All bahan which bersentuhan with tissue and cell—including larutan culture, larutan pengawet, wadah, and label—harus dikendalikan kualitasnya and dilacak with cermat. Setiap batch or lot material harus dicatat, and if there is problem, can dilacak returned to sumbernya for tindakan perbaikan.

    cGTP (current Good Tissue Practice) is standard which manage pengelolaan produk based cell and tissue human for memastikan keamanan and quality mulai from pengambilan donor hingga penggunaan clinical.

    Sering dibandingkan with GMP for drug, cGTP berfokus on requirement special for penanganan tissue and produk which berasal from cell human (HCT/P).

    Proses Pengambilan, Pemrosesan, Penyimpanan, and Pengiriman

    Regulation this diberlakukan oleh badan pengawas seperti FDA in AS for mencegah penularan disease through produk biologis and memastikan practice pengambilan, pemrosesan, penyimpanan, and distribusi which safe.

    Method pengambilan tissue harus performed secara aseptik (steril). After pengambilan, tissue harus diproses with immediately in condition which terkontrol, for example dicuci, dikonsentrasi, or dibekukan with true. Selama penyimpanan, suhu and kelembaban harus dipantau and dipertahankan. Pengiriman harus performed with system kontrol suhu (cold chain) which terjaga with good.

    Requirement Main

    Kontrol Mutu and Pengujian Produk

    Inti from cGTP is pencegahan penularan disease menular. Requirement include evaluasi kelayakan donor, kontrol lingkungan facility, manajemen bahan baku and reagen, as well as dokumentasi and pelacakan sepanjang process.

    Setiap tahap production harus include pemeriksaan and tes mutu. For example, tes sterilitas for memastikan not there is bakteri or jamur, pengukuran jumlah and viabilitas cell, pemeriksaan endotoksin, and lainnya. Result tes harus didokumentasikan and dievaluasi before produk dinyatakan layak pakai or dijual.

    Evaluasi Kelayakan Donor

    Dokumentasi and Pelacakan

    Donor harus disaring through pemeriksaan riwayat health and tes laboratory for menyingkirkan risk infeksi seperti HIV or hepatitis.

    All process harus didokumentasikan with procedure surgery standard tertulis. Catatan for setiap batch or lot produk harus menyimpan information donor, date pengambilan, result tes, penyimpanan, and distribusi. Pelacakan complete this memungkinkan investigasi fast if there is problem and memfasilitasi penarikan returned produk if diperlukan (recall).

    Pengendalian Fasilitas and Lingkungan

    Training and Kompeten Personel

    Facility harus have area bersih and ability for surgery aseptik, including pemantauan lingkungan berkala and pemeliharaan peralatan.

    All staff which terlibat in penanganan tissue and cell harus terlatih memadai in procedure aseptik, safety biologis, and requirement cGTP spesifik. Training harus didokumentasikan, and staff new harus through program orientasi before perform pekerjaan secara mandiri.

    Manajemen Bahan and Proses

    All bahan which bersentuhan with tissue harus dikendalikan kualitasnya and dilacak per batch. Process pengambilan, pemrosesan, penyimpanan, and pengiriman harus mempertahankan condition which meminimalkan kontaminasi.

    Signifikansi and Benefit Kepatuhan

    Kontrol Mutu and Dokumentasi

    For Institution and Facility Medical

    Setiap langkah harus terdokumentasi with procedure surgery standard and catatan which memungkinkan pelacakan as well as tindakan korektif bila happen anomali.

    Kepatuhan terhadap cGTP membuktikan komitmen institution terhadap keamanan and quality. This enhance trust from partner clinical, regulator, and which most important, patient. Facility which mematuhi cGTP more mudah bermitra secara international, receive audit regulation with more lancar, and mendapatkan certification from badan international seperti AABB or FACT.

    Secara short, cGTP mencegah risk penularan through produk tissue and cell with apply kontrol which konsisten in seluruh rantai pasokan and process production.

    For Patient and Penerima

    Kepatuhan cGTP langsung melindungi patient with reduce risk penularan disease through transplantasi or infusi cell. Patient which receive tissue or cell from facility certified cGTP can yakin bahwa produk has disaring and diproses with standard safety international tertinggi.

    Benefit Certification cGTP

    Kepatuhan terhadap cGTP show komitmen terhadap keamanan and quality, which enhance trust partner clinical, regulator, and patient.

    Impact on Produk, Service, and Konsumen

    Produk tissue and cell which diproses sesuai cGTP show standard mutu which superior. Risk kontaminasi reduced drastis, viabilitas cell terjaga more good, and kinerja clinical increased. When transplantasi or cell therapy successful with komplikasi minimal, kepuasan patient increased and trust terhadap regeneration medical and cell therapy semakin strong in masyarakat.

    Selain that, facility which patuh will more mudah berpartisipasi in cooperation international and mendapatkan penilaian which more good in inspeksi regulatori.

    Bagi industri biotechnology and medical secara keseluruhan, cGTP create lingkungan which more safe bagi konsumen and mendorong innovation berkelanjutan in cell therapy and regeneration tissue.

    Organization and Regulation Related

    Even though cGTP is konsep which dipelopori oleh FDA AS, prinsip-prinsipnya dibagikan secara international and relevan with panduan from badan seperti WHO, as well as standard certification from organization seperti AABB and FACT.

    Organization International and Badan Regulation Related

    FDA (Food and Drug Administration) - Amerika Serikat

    FDA is badan which menetapkan and mengelola regulation cGTP in Amerika Serikat. Division CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research) bertanggung jawab atas pengawasan standard this and perform inspeksi facility for memastikan kepatuhan. Pelanggaran cGTP can menghasilkan sanksi hukum which serius.

    WHO (World Health Organization)

    WHO mengeluarkan panduan about quality and safety cell and tissue for country-country developed, with prinsip-prinsip which sejalan with cGTP.

    AABB (Association for the Advancement of Blood & Biotherapies)

    AABB menyediakan program certification international for facility which menangani blood, cell, and produk tissue. Standard AABB some large menggabungkan prinsip cGTP and sering diterima sebagai bukti kepatuhan cGTP.

    FACT (Foundation for Accreditation of Cellular Therapy)

    FACT menyediakan akreditasi for facility cell therapy and transplantasi stem cell hematopoietik, with requirement which sejalan with or melebihi cGTP.

    Badan Regulation National Lainnya

    In Jepang, PMDA (Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Agency) apply standard serupa. In Eropa, EMA (European Medicines Agency) also menetapkan standard quality which serupa. This show bahwa prinsip cGTP has became standard global in penanganan cell and tissue biologis.
