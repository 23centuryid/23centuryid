    Ikhtisar and Latar Belakang

    cGTP (current Good Tissue Practice) is standard which mengatur pengelolaan produk berbasis cell and jaringan human for memastikan keamanan and quality mulai from pengambilan donor hingga penggunaan clinical.

  • cGTP merupakan singkatan from "current Good Tissue Practice" and mengacu pada standard regulation which developed oleh FDA (Food and Drug Administration) di Amerika Serikat. Standard this special berlaku for penanganan cell and jaringan human (HCT/P - Human Cells, Tissues, and cellular and tissue-based Products).

    Privacy Policy

    • Latar belakang pengembangan cGTP is meningkatnya permintaan will procedure transplantasi and cell therapy, disertai with kekhawatiran about possibility penularan disease menular through produk biologis. For mencegah penyebaran infeksi seperti HIV, hepatitis, or agen infeksi lainnya from donor ke penerima, FDA menetapkan requirement cGTP pada tahun 2004 and mulai berlaku penuh pada 2005.

    With adoption cGTP, institusi which menangani jaringan and cell human di seluruh world (terutama which bermitra with lembaga di AS) also apply prinsip serupa. Standard this menjadi dasar bagi praktik which safe in penyimpanan stem cell, pemrosesan jaringan transplantasi, and manajemen produk biotechnology berbasis cell.

    Evaluasi dan Pemeriksaan Kelayakan Donor

    Penyedia or donor from cell and jaringan harus menjalani comprehensive examination terhadap riwayat health and tes laboratory for mendeteksi infeksi berbahaya. Tes which diminta include pemeriksaan terhadap HIV, hepatitis B, hepatitis C, sifilis, and agen infeksi lainnya which can ditransmisikan. Donor which have risiko infeksi harus dikeluarkan, and produk they not boleh used.

    Pengendalian Fasilitas dan Lingkungan

    Facility which memproses jaringan and cell harus designed for operasi aseptik (steril). This termasuk zona pembersihan (clean rooms), lemari keselamatan biologis, system filtrasi udara, and pemantauan lingkungan teratur through pengujian mikroorganisme. Peralatan harus divalidasi and dipelihara secara berkala for memastikan fungsi optimal.

    Manajemen dan Kontrol Bahan Baku

    Ringkasan and Latar Belakang

    Semua bahan which bersentuhan with jaringan and cell—termasuk larutan culture, larutan pengawet, wadah, and label—harus dikendalikan kualitasnya and dilacak with cermat. Setiap batch or lot material harus dicatat, and jika there is masalah, can dilacak kembali ke sumbernya for tindakan perbaikan.

    cGTP (current Good Tissue Practice) is standard which mengatur pengelolaan produk berbasis cell and jaringan human for memastikan keamanan and quality mulai from pengambilan donor hingga penggunaan clinical.

    Sering dibandingkan with GMP for drug, cGTP berfokus pada requirement special for penanganan jaringan and produk which berasal from cell human (HCT/P).

    Proses Pengambilan, Pemrosesan, Penyimpanan, dan Pengiriman

    Regulation this diberlakukan oleh badan pengawas seperti FDA di AS for mencegah penularan disease through produk biologis and memastikan praktik pengambilan, pemrosesan, penyimpanan, and distribusi which safe.

    Method pengambilan jaringan harus performed secara aseptik (steril). After pengambilan, jaringan harus diproses with segera in kondisi which terkontrol, misalnya dicuci, dikonsentrasi, or dibekukan with true. Selama penyimpanan, suhu and kelembaban harus dipantau and dipertahankan. Pengiriman harus performed with system kontrol suhu (cold chain) which terjaga with good.

    Requirement Utama

    Kontrol Mutu dan Pengujian Produk

    Inti from cGTP is pencegahan penularan disease menular. Requirement include evaluasi kelayakan donor, kontrol lingkungan facility, manajemen bahan baku and reagen, as well as dokumentasi and pelacakan sepanjang process.

    Setiap tahap production harus include pemeriksaan and tes mutu. Misalnya, tes sterilitas for memastikan not there is bakteri or jamur, pengukuran jumlah and viabilitas cell, pemeriksaan endotoksin, and lainnya. Result tes harus didokumentasikan and dievaluasi before produk dinyatakan layak pakai or dijual.

    Evaluasi Kelayakan Donor

    Dokumentasi dan Pelacakan

    Donor harus disaring through pemeriksaan riwayat health and tes laboratory for menyingkirkan risiko infeksi seperti HIV or hepatitis.

    Semua process harus didokumentasikan with procedure operasi standard tertulis. Catatan for setiap batch or lot produk harus menyimpan information donor, tanggal pengambilan, result tes, penyimpanan, and distribusi. Pelacakan complete this memungkinkan investigasi cepat jika there is masalah and memfasilitasi penarikan kembali produk jika diperlukan (recall).

    Pengendalian Fasilitas dan Lingkungan

    Pelatihan dan Kompeten Personel

    Facility harus have area bersih and ability for operasi aseptik, termasuk pemantauan lingkungan berkala and pemeliharaan peralatan.

    Semua staff which terlibat in penanganan jaringan and cell harus terlatih memadai in procedure aseptik, keselamatan biologis, and requirement cGTP spesifik. Pelatihan harus didokumentasikan, and staff baru harus through program orientasi before perform pekerjaan secara mandiri.

    Manajemen Bahan dan Proses

    Semua bahan which bersentuhan with jaringan harus dikendalikan kualitasnya and dilacak per batch. Process pengambilan, pemrosesan, penyimpanan, and pengiriman harus mempertahankan kondisi which meminimalkan kontaminasi.

    Signifikansi and Manfaat Kepatuhan

    Kontrol Mutu dan Dokumentasi

    For Institusi and Facility Medical

    Setiap langkah harus terdokumentasi with procedure operasi standard and catatan which memungkinkan pelacakan as well as tindakan korektif bila terjadi anomali.

    Kepatuhan terhadap cGTP membuktikan komitmen institusi terhadap keamanan and quality. This enhance kepercayaan from partner clinical, regulator, and which paling penting, patient. Facility which mematuhi cGTP more mudah bermitra secara international, receive audit regulation with more lancar, and mendapatkan certification from badan international seperti AABB or FACT.

    Secara singkat, cGTP mencegah risiko penularan through produk jaringan and cell with apply kontrol which konsisten di seluruh rantai pasokan and process production.

    For Patient and Penerima

    Kepatuhan cGTP langsung melindungi patient with reduce risiko penularan disease through transplantasi or infusi cell. Patient which receive jaringan or cell from facility certified cGTP can yakin bahwa produk has disaring and diproses with standard keselamatan international tertinggi.

    Manfaat Certification cGTP

    Kepatuhan terhadap cGTP show komitmen terhadap keamanan and quality, which enhance kepercayaan partner clinical, regulator, and patient.

    Dampak pada Produk, Service, and Konsumen

    Produk jaringan and cell which diproses sesuai cGTP show standard mutu which superior. Risiko kontaminasi berkurang drastis, viabilitas cell terjaga more good, and kinerja clinical meningkat. When transplantasi or cell therapy successful with komplikasi minimal, kepuasan patient meningkat and kepercayaan terhadap regeneration medical and cell therapy semakin kuat di masyarakat.

    Selain that, facility which patuh will more mudah berpartisipasi in kerja sama international and mendapatkan penilaian which more good in inspeksi regulatori.

    Bagi industri biotechnology and medical secara keseluruhan, cGTP menciptakan lingkungan which more safe bagi konsumen and mendorong inovasi berkelanjutan in cell therapy and regeneration jaringan.

    Organisasi and Regulation Related

    Walaupun cGTP is konsep which dipelopori oleh FDA AS, prinsip-prinsipnya dibagikan secara international and relevan with panduan from badan seperti WHO, as well as standard certification from organisasi seperti AABB and FACT.

    Organisasi International and Badan Regulation Related

    FDA (Food and Drug Administration) - Amerika Serikat

    FDA is badan which menetapkan and mengelola regulation cGTP di Amerika Serikat. Division CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research) bertanggung jawab atas pengawasan standard this and perform inspeksi facility for memastikan kepatuhan. Pelanggaran cGTP can menghasilkan sanksi hukum which serius.

    WHO (World Health Organization)

    WHO mengeluarkan panduan about quality and keselamatan cell and jaringan for country-country berkembang, with prinsip-prinsip which sejalan with cGTP.

    AABB (Association for the Advancement of Blood & Biotherapies)

    AABB menyediakan program certification international for facility which menangani darah, cell, and produk jaringan. Standard AABB sebagian large menggabungkan prinsip cGTP and sering diterima sebagai bukti kepatuhan cGTP.

    FACT (Foundation for Accreditation of Cellular Therapy)

    FACT menyediakan akreditasi for facility cell therapy and transplantasi stem cell hematopoietik, with requirement which sejalan with or melebihi cGTP.

    Badan Regulation Nasional Lainnya

    Di Jepang, PMDA (Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Agency) apply standard serupa. Di Eropa, EMA (European Medicines Agency) also menetapkan standard quality which serupa. This show bahwa prinsip cGTP has menjadi standard global in penanganan cell and jaringan biologis.
