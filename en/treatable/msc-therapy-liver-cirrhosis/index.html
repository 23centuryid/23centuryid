About Stem Cell Therapy for Liver Cirrhosis

  • Home

  • About Stem Cell Therapy for Liver Cirrhosis

"Is liver cirrhosis incurable?" – This question frequently evokes profound anxiety and despair in many patients and their families.

Abdominal distension due to fluid accumulation (ascites), yellowing of the skin and eyes (jaundice), and lightheadedness resulting from hepatic encephalopathy... These characteristic symptoms of liver cirrhosis significantly disrupt daily life and cause considerable suffering for patients. In modern medicine, it is challenging to fully cure advanced liver cirrhosis, and it has been stated that "liver transplantation is the only radical treatment." However, recently, a new approach in regenerative therapy, known as Stem Cell Therapy, has brought a beacon of hope for liver cirrhosis.

What is Liver Cirrhosis? What are the Current Limitations of Treatment?

Sirosis hati adalah penyakit di mana hati mengalami fibrosis dan mengeras, menyebabkan komplikasi serius, tetapi pengobatan saat ini membuat pemulihan mendasar sulit terjadi, dan harapan pada terapi regeneratif baru semakin meningkat.

Hepatic cirrhosis refers to a condition where normal liver tissue is replaced by hard, fibrous scar tissue due to chronic liver damage, leading to significant atrophy of the entire liver and a substantial loss of its function.

Imagine a fatty liver hardening like concrete after years of inflammation.

Causes of Liver Cirrhosis and its Widespread Impact

There are an estimated 400,000 patients with liver cirrhosis in Indonesia, and its primary causes are as follows.

  • Hepatitis Virus (B dan C): Hepatitis jangka panjang secara bertahap merusak hati.
  • Kerusakan Hati Alkoholik: Konsumsi alkohol bertahun-tahun merusak sel-sel hati.
  • Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD): Penyebab sirosis hati tipe penyakit gaya hidup yang meningkat pesat baru-baru ini.

Regardless of the cause, chronic inflammation continues to damage normal liver cells, fibrosis develops, and eventually liver cirrhosis occurs.

Should it develop into life-threatening complications

As liver cirrhosis progresses, it can lead to the following serious complications.

  • Asites: Air menumpuk di perut, menyebabkan kesulitan bernapas dan rasa tertekan.
  • Ikterus: Kulit dan mata menguning, menyebabkan kelelahan umum dan gatal-gatal.
  • Ensefalopati Hepatik: Gangguan kesadaran atau koma karena penurunan fungsi detoksifikasi.

These complications severely restrict patients' daily lives, leading to persistent anxiety regarding future hospitalizations and the potential for recurrent loss of consciousness.

The Current Reality of Medicine: A Focus on Symptomatic Therapy

Currently, there is no treatment that can reverse liver cirrhosis. Treatment is based on two approaches.

  • Pengobatan Penyebab: Penghilangan virus atau pantang alkohol.
  • Penanganan Komplikasi: Terapi obat dengan diuretik dan obat penghilang amonia.

However, a liver that has become fibrotic and hardened cannot regenerate. The only definitive treatment is liver transplantation, but

  • Chronic donor shortage
  • Major operative risks
  • Lifelong burden of immunosuppressive therapy

The reality is that the barriers are substantial.

The Required New Strategy for 'Liver Regeneration'

Thus, hepatic cirrhosis is a disease that, with modern medical treatments, can have its progression halted but cannot be reversed. It is for this reason that regenerative therapies, such as Stem Cell Therapy, are currently garnering significant interest.

Restoring pliability and function to a hardened liver — such a hope will become a reality.

Key Points

  • Liver cirrhosis is a condition characterized by the loss of liver elasticity and function due to fibrosis.
  • Causing serious complications such as ascites, jaundice, and hepatic encephalopathy.
  • Current treatment focuses on etiologic strategies and complication management; hepatic regeneration is challenging.
  • Given the absence of a definitive treatment other than liver transplantation, hope for regenerative therapy is increasing.

Is Regenerative Therapy Effective for Liver Cirrhosis? Why is Stem Cell Therapy Attracting Interest?

Sebagai pengobatan baru untuk sirosis hati, terapi regeneratif menggunakan sel punca mesenkimal (MSC) menarik perhatian secara global. Khususnya, MSC yang berasal dari tali pusat diharapkan sebagai "sel harapan" yang memiliki kelembutan dan kekuatan regenerasi yang kuat.

Apa yang menarik perhatian dalam beberapa tahun terakhir untuk "mengobati" sirosis hati adalah Terapi Sel Punca.

Stem cells are unique cells within our bodies that possess the remarkable ability to develop into various cell types and simultaneously repair damaged tissues. In essence, they are the body's master cells.

What is Stem Cell Therapy?

Stem cell therapy is a medical treatment method that involves extracting stem cells from either the patient (autologous) or a donor (allogeneic), reintroducing them into the body, and promoting the regeneration of damaged organs. In cases of liver cirrhosis, the objective is to deliver stem cells to the damaged and fibrotic liver tissue to restore liver function and repair the tissue.

Multiple approaches to regenerative therapy are available.

Several approaches for liver regeneration are being investigated.

  • Research on the generation and transplantation of miniature liver organoids derived from iPS cells.
  • Treatment utilizing autologous stem cells derived from bone marrow or peripheral blood.

Di antara ini, metode yang menarik perhatian sebagai pengobatan yang lebih praktis adalah penggunaan sel punca mesenkimal (MSC).

What are Mesenchymal Stem Cells (MSCs)?

Mesenchymal Stem Cells (MSCs) are stem cells primarily found in bone marrow, adipose tissue, and the umbilical cord at birth. They possess the following characteristics.

  • Kemampuan proliferasi dan diferensiasi tinggi: Dapat berubah menjadi berbagai jaringan sesuai kebutuhan.
  • Penolakan imun jarang terjadi: MSC orang lain dapat diberikan dengan aman dengan kemungkinan tinggi.
  • Tindakan anti-inflamasi dan pemulihan yang kuat: Mendukung jaringan yang rusak dan mempromosikan regenerasi.

Due to these properties, MSCs are considered an ideal cell source for liver repair.

Umbilical cord-derived MSCs are a bright star.

Di antara MSC, khususnya MSC yang berasal dari Wharton's Jelly yang diekstraksi dari tali pusat (WJ-MSC) adalah sel yang superior dalam hal kelembutan dan kekuatan.

  • Sel Punca Berusia 0 Tahun: Karena baru lahir, kemampuan self-replication dan diferensiasi sangat tinggi.
  • Risiko Tumorigenesis Rendah: Secara genetik dekat dengan keadaan awal, keselamatan tinggi.
  • Jarang Menyebabkan Penolakan Imun: Ekspresi HLA (antigen leukosit) rendah, mudah beradaptasi bahkan dengan transplantasi allografik.

Furthermore, the collection imposes no burden on the infant or mother and raises no significant ethical issues, which is a considerable advantage in medical applications.

Our clinic also utilizes umbilical cord-derived WJ-MSC, with the aim of achieving a definitive regenerative effect for liver cirrhosis through these young and healthy cells.

Key Points

  • Stem Cell Therapy is the most advanced regenerative therapy aimed at repairing damaged liver.
  • Mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) exhibit high immunocompatibility and superior safety.
  • WJ-MSCs derived from particularly young umbilical cords demonstrate superior regenerative capacity and an excellent safety profile.
  • Application for liver cirrhosis is anticipated worldwide.

How Does Stem Cell Therapy Work in Liver Cirrhosis?

Terapi Sel Punca menghadapi sirosis hati dari tiga arah: perbaikan sel hati yang rusak, penghambatan fibrosis, dan penipisan peradangan kronis, mempromosikan regenerasi hati.

Ketika sel punca disuntikkan ke dalam tubuh, bagaimana hati yang mengeras dapat diregenerasi? Kuncinya adalah bahwa sel punca "melengkapi sel yang hilang", "mengeluarkan faktor perbaikan", dan "menghambat pelarian kekebalan" dalam pendekatan terpadu tiga arah.

Regeneration of Damaged Liver Cells (Replacement Effect)

A subset of stem cells differentiate into hepatocytes and directly compensate for the lost liver function.

Ketika MSC mencapai hati, sebagian kecil dari mereka berdiferensiasi menjadi sel yang mirip sel hati, langsung menggantikan sebagian jaringan hati yang rusak.
Dengan ini, pemulihan fungsi hati seperti detoksifikasi dan sintesis protein dapat diharapkan.

Secreting Repair Factors for Hepatic Repair (Paracrine Effect)

Stem cells secrete a multitude of regenerative factors, thereby supporting comprehensive hepatic recovery.

MSC mengeluarkan sitokin dengan aksi anti-inflamasi dan faktor pertumbuhan yang mempromosikan perbaikan jaringan.
Dengan ini,

  • Resolution of chronic inflammation
  • Inhibition of fibrosis progression (scarring)
  • Pro-resolving macrophage activation promoting tissue regeneration

Such an effect is achieved, and the hepatic environment is rendered more amenable to regeneration.

Modulating Immunity to Reduce Inflammation (Immunomodulatory Effect)

Suppressing excessive immune responses, preventing new damage to the liver.

MSC suppresses the overactivity of T and B cells, thereby attenuating chronic inflammation. Consequently, the cycle of liver damage is interrupted, which also leads to the prevention of complication exacerbation.

Dengan cara seperti ini, Terapi Sel Punca menghadapi sirosis hati dengan pendekatan terpadu tiga arah: "melengkapi sel hati baru, memperbaiki luka hati, dan menenangkan peradangan". Kemampuan regenerasi yang tidak dapat diperoleh dengan pengobatan lain ini dapat dikatakan sebagai fitur terbesar Terapi Sel Punca.

Key Points

  • Some MSCs differentiate into liver-like cells, supplementing lost function.
  • Releasing repair factors to prepare the hepatic regeneration environment.
  • Inhibits the progression of fibrosis and maintains liver elasticity.
  • Mitigating chronic inflammation through immune modulation, preventing further damage.
  • A three-pronged integrated approach enables a fundamental approach to liver cirrhosis.

How Effective is Stem Cell Therapy? Clinical Evidence Demonstrates Its Effectiveness.

Penelitian klinis di seluruh dunia mulai menunjukkan bahwa Terapi Sel Punca untuk sirosis hati memberikan efek pada perbaikan fungsi hati, penghilangan gejala, pengurangan fibrosis, bahkan peningkatan tingkat kelangsungan hidup.

"Apakah Terapi Sel Punca benar-benar berhasil?" -- Ini adalah poin yang paling penting bagi pasien dan keluarga. Baru-baru ini, bukti ilmiah yang mendukung efektivitas Terapi Sel Punca untuk sirosis hati terus dipublikasikan dari berbagai belahan dunia.

Elevated Liver Function Test Results

In several meta-analyses and clinical trials, significant improvements have been reported in the blood test parameters (e.g., ALT, AST, bilirubin, albumin) of patients undergoing Stem Cell Therapy. Consequently, basic liver functions such as detoxification, metabolism, and nutrient storage were enhanced, and symptoms like jaundice and swelling were also alleviated.

Worsening of Disruptive Symptoms and QOL

From the patient's perspective, ongoing reports document a reduction in fatigue, restoration of appetite, and improvement in abdominal distension due to ascites, leading to an enhanced quality of life (QOL).

Beyond mere numerical improvements, tangible changes in daily life, such as enhanced mobility and the ability to enjoy meals, have been realized.

Fibrosis Repair and Liver Regeneration

Following Stem Cell Therapy, cases of increased liver volume on CT or MRI, and a reduction in liver fibrosis, have been reported. While complete normalization was not observed, it has been demonstrated that tissue regeneration may be commencing.

Improved Survival Rate

Long-term monitoring data revealed that patients with severe liver cirrhosis who underwent Stem Cell Therapy exhibited a significantly higher survival rate compared to the control group. The expectation is not merely an extension of life, but rather a 'healthy lifespan extension' accompanied by an improved quality of life.

Key Points

  • ALT, AST, albumin, and other liver function indicators are elevated.
  • Fatigue, ascites, and other symptoms are reduced, with an improvement in quality of life (QOL).
  • Reduced fibrosis and hepatic tissue regeneration have been reported.
  • Improved survival rates are also suggested in patients with severe liver cirrhosis.
  • Developing into a promising new treatment option for liver cirrhosis.

Is Stem Cell Therapy Safe? Concerned about Side Effects?

Dalam penelitian klinis sebelumnya, telah dikonfirmasi bahwa Terapi Sel Punca (terutama MSC yang berasal dari Wharton's Jelly) memiliki efek samping serius yang sedikit dan keselamatan tinggi.

When initiating new medical treatments, safety is the foremost concern. Although regenerative therapy is an emerging field, previous clinical studies on Stem Cell Therapy have clearly demonstrated a very low incidence of serious adverse effects.

Assessing Safety from Previous Clinical Trial Data

According to a recent meta-analysis, in the treatment of liver cirrhosis using bone marrow- or umbilical cord-derived MSCs, no difference was observed in the incidence of adverse events between the treatment and control groups, and no serious adverse effects were confirmed.

Khususnya, MSC yang berasal dari tali pusat memiliki imunogenisitas rendah, yang merupakan fitur bahwa reaksi penolakan imun hampir tidak terjadi bahkan dari penyediaan orang lain, dan unggul dalam hal keselamatan.

Reported Potential Side Effects

The majority of reported side effects have been extremely mild, temporary, and without serious risk.

  • Transient fever
  • Mild headache
  • Mild pain or discomfort at the injection site

It typically resolves spontaneously, and additional treatment is rarely required.

Cancer Risk

"Jika saya memasukkan sel punca, apakah saya akan terkena tumor?" Ada juga kekhawatiran seperti itu, tetapi penelitian sebelumnya tidak menunjukkan insidensi hepatoma hati baru akibat injeksi MSC. Sebaliknya, telah disarankan bahwa risiko kanker hati berkurang pada kelompok yang menerima Terapi Sel Punca.

Currently, stem cell therapy (particularly treatments utilizing Wharton's Jelly-derived MSCs) is considered a treatment option with a very high safety profile for patients with liver cirrhosis. While long-term follow-up is still necessary, it shows significant promise as a regenerative therapy with a reduced risk profile compared to traditional liver transplantation.

Key Points

  • Negligible serious side effects have been reported.
  • Mild and temporary side effects (e.g., fever, headache) are rare.
  • Immune rejection is exceedingly rare, particularly with umbilical cord-derived MSCs.
  • The risk of cancer is also refuted by current research.
  • Compared to liver transplantation, this is a treatment option with a reduced patient burden.

Hope for the Future: Transformation Through Regenerative Therapy

Stem cell therapy for hepatic cirrhosis represents a cutting-edge regenerative approach currently undergoing accelerated research worldwide. For hepatic cirrhosis, a condition previously deemed 'incurable,' efforts utilizing autologous or allogeneic stem cells to regenerate the liver have begun to demonstrate promising outcomes.

Notably, umbilical cord-derived mesenchymal stem cells (WJ-MSCs) are anticipated to be a potent therapeutic agent for the future treatment of liver cirrhosis, owing to their youthfulness, high regenerative capacity, and favorable safety profile. For patients and families affected by liver cirrhosis, Stem Cell Therapy is emerging as a new beacon of hope.

The courage to take a single step may well transform the future. The day when traditional concepts of liver cirrhosis are redefined through regenerative therapy is not far off.
