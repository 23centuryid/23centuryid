Tentang Stem cell therapy untuk Sirosis Liver

  • Tentang Stem cell therapy untuk Sirosis Liver

"Is sirosis liver have not can disembuhkan?" – Many patient and family which mendengarnya merasa cemas and putus asa.

Perut membuncit because air tertimbun (asites), skin and eye menguning (ikterus), kepala terasa berkunang-kunang akibat ensefalopati hepatik... Symptom-symptom khas sirosis liver this very disturb kehidupan sehari-day and menyebabkan penderitaan bagi patient. In kedokteran modern, sulit for heal sirosis liver which have lanjut sepenuhnya, and has dikatakan bahwa "transplantasi liver is satu-satunya treatment radikal". However new-new this, pendekatan new in therapy regenerative which disebut Stem cell therapy has bring light hope for sirosis liver.

What that Sirosis Liver? What Keterbatasan Treatment Time This?

Sirosis liver is disease where liver experience fibrosis and mengeras, menyebabkan komplikasi serius, tetapi therapy now make pemulihan mendasar sulit happen, and hope pada terapi regenerative new semakin meningkat.

Sirosis liver merujuk on condition in which tissue liver which soft digantikan with tissue parut which hard (tissue fibrosa) because kerusakan liver kronis, seluruh liver menyusut, and fungsinya disappear large-besaran.

Bayangkan liver which gemuk berubah became hard seperti beton after bertahun-years peradangan.

Penyebab Sirosis Liver and Impact that Meluas

Diperkirakan there is sekitar 400.000 patient sirosis liver in Indonesia, and penyebab utamanya is sebagai berikut.

  • Hepatitis Virus (B and C): Hepatitis jangka long secara bertahap break liver.
  • Kerusakan Liver Alkoholik: Konsumsi alkohol years break cell-cell liver.
  • Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD): Penyebab sirosis liver tipe disease gaya hidup that meningkat pesat new-new ini.

Terlepas from penyebabnya, peradangan kronis continuously menyebabkan kerusakan cell liver normal, fibrosis developed, and finally sirosis liver happen.

If Develop Become Komplikasi that Mengancam Jiwa

When sirosis liver developed, can menyebabkan komplikasi serius berikut.

  • Asites: Air menumpuk in perut, menyebabkan difficulty bernapas and rasa tertekan.
  • Ikterus: Skin and eye menguning, menyebabkan kelelahan umum and gatal-gatal.
  • Ensefalopati Hepatik: Gangguan kesadaran or koma because penurunan fungsi detoksifikasi.

Because komplikasi this, kehidupan sehari-day patient very terbatas, and they harus continuously face kecemasan "when will dirawat inap lagi" and "akankah I loss kesadaran lagi".

Realitas Therapy Now: Terfokus pada Terapi Simptomatik

Time this, not there is treatment which can mengembalikan sirosis liver to keadaan normal. Dasar care is dua pendekatan.

  • Therapy Penyebab: Penghilangan virus or pantang alkohol.
  • Penanganan Komplikasi: Terapi obat with diuretik and obat penghilang amonia.

However, liver which sekali experience fibrosis and mengeras not can diregenerasi. Satu-satunya treatment radikal is transplantasi liver, tetapi

  • Kekurangan donor kronis
  • Risk surgery bedah large
  • Beban therapy imunosupresi seumur hidup

kenyataannya obstacle very high.

Strategi New "Regenerasi Liver" that Diperlukan

With cara this, sirosis liver is disease which in treatment modern can "mencegah progress" tetapi not can "returned normal". Inilah why therapy regenerative which disebut Stem cell therapy interesting perhatian now.

"Mengembalikan kelembutan and fungsi to liver which mengeras" -- hope seperti that will became kenyataan.

POIN IMPORTANT

  • Sirosis liver is condition in which elastisitas and fungsi liver disappear because fibrosis
  • Menyebabkan komplikasi serius seperti asites, ikterus, and ensefalopati hepatik
  • Treatment time this berfokus on strategi penyebab and manajemen komplikasi, regeneration liver sulit
  • Because not there is treatment radikal selain transplantasi liver, hope on therapy regenerative increased

Is Therapy Regenerative Effective for Sirosis Liver? Why Stem cell therapy Interesting Perhatian

Sebagai therapy new untuk sirosis liver, terapi regenerative use stem cell mesenkimal (MSC) interesting perhatian secara global. Especially, MSC that berasal from tali pusat diharapkan sebagai "cell hope" that memiliki kelembutan and kekuatan regenerasi that strong.

What which interesting perhatian in beberapa years terakhir for "mengobati" sirosis liver is Stem cell Therapy.

Stem cell is cell special in tubuh we which have ability for grow became berbagai cell and sekaligus ability for improve tissue which rusak. Singkatnya, this is cell master tubuh.

What itu Stem cell therapy?

Stem cell therapy is method treatment which mengambil stem cell from patient sendiri or donor, mengembalikannya to in tubuh, and mempromosikan regeneration organ which rusak. In kasus sirosis liver, tujuannya is mengirim stem cell to tissue liver which rusak and experience fibrosis, for memulihkan fungsi liver and improve tissue.

Ada Beberapa Pilihan Pendekatan Terapi Regenerative

Beberapa pendekatan for regeneration liver sedang diteliti.

  • Research about pembuatan and transplantasi organoid liver mini from cell iPS
  • Treatment use stem cell which berasal from sumsum bone or blood tepi patient sendiri

In antara this, method which interesting perhatian sebagai treatment which more praktis is penggunaan mesenchymal stem cell (MSC).

What itu Stem cell Mesenkimal (MSC)?

MSC (Mesenchymal Stem cell) is stem cell which especially there is in sumsum bone, tissue lemak, and tali center (tali pusar) time lahir. Have karakteristik berikut.

  • Kemampuan proliferasi and diferensiasi high: Can berubah become berbagai tissue sesuai kebutuhan.
  • Penolakan imun jarang happen: MSC orang lain can diberikan with aman with kemungkinan high.
  • Tindakan anti-inflamasi and pemulihan that strong: Mendukung tissue that rusak and mempromosikan regenerasi.

Because sifat-sifat this, MSC dianggap sebagai sumber cell which ideal for perbaikan liver.

MSC that Berasal from Tali Pusat is "Bintang Bright"

In antara MSC, especially MSC which berasal from Wharton's Jelly which diekstraksi from tali center (WJ-MSC) is cell which superior in hal kelembutan and kekuatan.

  • Stem cell Berusia 0 Year: Because new lahir, kemampuan self-replication and diferensiasi sangat high.
  • Risk Tumorigenesis Low: Secara genetik dekat with keadaan awal, safety high.
  • Jarang Menyebabkan Penolakan Imun: Ekspresi HLA (antigen leukosit) low, mudah beradaptasi bahkan with transplantasi allografik.

Selain that, pengambilan not provide beban on bayi or ibu, and not have problem etika which berarti, which merupakan advantage large in application medical.

Clinic we also use WJ-MSC which berasal from tali center this, with tujuan efek regeneration which pasti from cell-cell young and healthy for sirosis liver.

POIN IMPORTANT

  • Stem cell therapy is therapy regenerative tercanggih which bertujuan improve liver which rusak
  • Mesenchymal stem cell (MSC) have kompatibilitas imun high and safety unggul
  • WJ-MSC which berasal from tali center especially young, ability regeneration and safety berkelas atas
  • Application for sirosis liver diharapkan in seluruh world

How Stem cell therapy Work on Sirosis Liver?

Stem cell therapy menghadapi sirosis liver from tiga arah: perbaikan cell liver that rusak, penghambatan fibrosis, and penipisan peradangan kronis, mempromosikan regenerasi liver.

When stem cell disuntikkan to in tubuh, how liver which mengeras can diregenerasi? Kuncinya is bahwa stem cell "melengkapi cell which disappear", "mengeluarkan faktor perbaikan", and "menghambat pelarian kekebalan" in pendekatan integrated tiga arah.

Cell Liver that Rusak Lahir Back (Efek Penggantian)

Beberapa stem cell berubah became cell liver and secara langsung melengkapi fungsi liver which disappear.

When MSC achieve liver, some small from they berdiferensiasi became cell which mirip cell liver, langsung menggantikan some tissue liver which rusak.
With this, recovery fungsi liver seperti detoksifikasi and sintesis protein can diharapkan.

Mengeluarkan Faktor Perbaikan untuk Heal Liver (Efek Paracrine)

Stem cell mengeluarkan faktor-faktor which mempromosikan regeneration in jumlah large, support recovery keseluruhan liver.

MSC mengeluarkan sitokin with aksi anti-inflammation and faktor growth which mempromosikan perbaikan tissue.
With this,

  • Penipisan peradangan kronis
  • Penghambatan progress fibrosis (parut)
  • Aktivasi makrofag tipe recovery which mempromosikan regeneration tissue

efek seperti this obtained, and lingkungan liver became more mudah for diregenerasi.

Manage Kekebalan untuk Calm Peradangan (Efek Modulasi Imun)

Menekan respons kekebalan which berlebihan, mencegah kerusakan new on liver.

MSC menekan kerja berlebihan cell-cell T and B, menekan peradangan kronis. With this, siklus kerusakan liver terputus, which also mengarah on pencegahan memburuknya komplikasi.

With cara seperti this, Stem cell therapy face sirosis liver with pendekatan integrated tiga arah: "melengkapi cell liver new, improve luka liver, and calm peradangan". Ability regeneration which not can obtained with treatment lain this can dikatakan sebagai fitur terbesar Stem cell therapy.

POIN IMPORTANT

  • Beberapa MSC berdiferensiasi became cell seperti liver, melengkapi fungsi which disappear
  • Mengeluarkan faktor perbaikan for mempersiapkan lingkungan regeneration liver
  • Menghambat progress fibrosis and maintain elastisitas liver
  • Calm peradangan kronis through modulasi kekebalan, mencegah kerusakan new
  • Pendekatan integrated tiga arah memungkinkan pendekatan mendasar for sirosis liver

Seberapa Effective Stem cell therapy? Bukti Clinical Show Efektivitasnya

Research klinis in seluruh world mulai show bahwa Stem cell therapy untuk sirosis liver give efek pada perbaikan fungsi liver, penghilangan symptom, pengurangan fibrosis, bahkan peningkatan tingkat kelangsungan hidup.

"Is Stem cell therapy true-true successful?" -- This is poin which most important bagi patient and family. New-new this, bukti scientific which support effectiveness Stem cell therapy for sirosis liver continuously dipublikasikan from berbagai belahan world.

Peningkatan Data Tes Fungsi Liver

In beberapa meta-analisis and uji clinical, nilai tes blood patient which underwent Stem cell therapy (ALT, AST, bilirubin, albumin, dll.) dilaporkan increased secara significant. With this, fungsi dasar liver seperti detoksifikasi, metabolisme, and penyimpanan nutrisi improved, and symptom seperti ikterus and pembengkakan also reduced.

Peningkatan Symptom Disturb and QOL

From perspektif patient sendiri, there is report berkelanjutan about pengurangan kelelahan, recovery nafsu eat, and perbaikan distensi abdomen because asites, with peningkatan quality of life (QOL).

Not hanya perbaikan angka, tetapi change kehidupan sehari-day seperti "can move" and "can menikmati eat" has terbentuk.

Perbaikan Fibrosis and Regenerasi Liver

After Stem cell therapy, has dilaporkan adanya kasus peningkatan volume liver on CT or MRI, and reduksi fibrosis (pengerasan) liver. Although not sepenuhnya returned normal, has shown possibility bahwa regeneration tissue mulai happen.

Peningkatan Tingkat Kelangsungan Hidup

In data pemantauan jangka long, terungkap bahwa patient sirosis liver berat which underwent Stem cell therapy have tingkat kelangsungan hidup which secara significant more high dibandingkan with group kontrol. Not hanya perpanjangan hidup, tetapi "perpanjangan usia healthy" with quality of life diharapkan.

Is Stem cell therapy Safe? Khawatir about Efek Samping?

Deep research klinis before, has dikonfirmasi bahwa Stem cell therapy (especially MSC that berasal from Wharton's Jelly) memiliki efek samping serius that few and safety high.

When run treatment new, hal which most dikhawatirkan is safety. Although therapy regenerative masih merupakan bidang which sedang developed, from research clinical before about Stem cell therapy, has became jelas bahwa insiden efek samping serius very few.

See Safety from Data Research Klinis Before

According meta-analisis latest, in treatment sirosis liver use MSC which berasal from sumsum bone or tali center, not there is perbedaan in tingkat kejadian peristiwa merugikan antara group treatment and group kontrol, and not there is efek samping serius which dikonfirmasi.

Especially, MSC which berasal from tali center have imunogenisitas low, which merupakan fitur bahwa reaksi penolakan imun hampir not happen bahkan from penyediaan orang lain, and unggul in hal safety.

Kemungkinan Efek Samping that Dilaporkan

Some large efek samping ringan which very ringan has dilaporkan, tetapi semuanya bersifat sementara and not bring risk serius.

  • Demam sementara
  • Pain kepala ringan
  • Sense pain or ketidaknyamanan ringan in location injeksi

This biasanya mereda secara natural, and hampir not there is kasus which memerlukan treatment tambahan.

Tentang Risk Cancer

"If I memasukkan stem cell, is I will terkena tumor?" There is also kekhawatiran seperti that, tetapi research before not show insidensi hepatoma liver new akibat injeksi MSC. Sebaliknya, has disarankan bahwa risk cancer liver reduced on group which receive Stem cell therapy.

Time this, Stem cell therapy (especially treatment use MSC which berasal from Wharton's Jelly) dianggap sebagai pilihan treatment with safety which very high bagi patient sirosis liver. Pengamatan jangka long masih diperlukan, tetapi dibandingkan with transplantasi liver traditional, this very menjanjikan sebagai therapy regenerative with risk which more few.

POIN IMPORTANT

  • Hampir not there is efek samping serius which dilaporkan
  • Hanya efek samping ringan and sementara (demam, pain kepala, dll.) which rarely happen
  • Reaksi penolakan imun very rarely happen (especially MSC which berasal from tali center)
  • Risk cancer also ditolak oleh research time this
  • Dibandingkan with transplantasi liver, this is pilihan care with beban patient which more small
