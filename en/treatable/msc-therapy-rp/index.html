About Stem cell therapy for Retinitis Pigmentosa

Awal Mula: Condition RP and Berkah Hope

Retinitis Pigmentosa (RP) is disease genetik langka in which cell-cell visual retina secara bertahap reduced because kelainan gen, menyebabkan lapang pandang menyempit and finally became gangguan vision berat which progresif and sulit disembuhkan.

Hingga time this, not there is method treatment which can heal RP secara radikal. Therapy hanya sebatas bersifat simptomatik, seperti suplemen vitamin A and surgery katarak, which hanya mampu few menunda development disease. Akibatnya, many patient and their family hidup with kecemasan bahwa "penurunan vision not can dihentikan."

However in beberapa years terakhir, report mulai bermunculan bahwa beberapa treatment true-true can enhance fungsi visual. Light hope mulai menerangi bidang medical. Sebagai contoh, in treatment gen therapy for jenis mutasi gen RP tertentu, kasus-kasus in which peningkatan lapang pandang and vision diamati mulai appear.

However, RP is disease which very beragam which melibatkan more from 260 jenis gen. Oleh because that, pendekatan make drug treatment for setiap gen have keterbatasan. With latar belakang this, hope terhadap stem cell therapy—which bertujuan for "melindungi retina that sendiri terlepas from jenis gen"—increased with pesat.

Improve and meregenerasi retina with kekuatan stem cell, menghentikan development disease. Possibility this have mulai shown sebagai "result" on beberapa patient RP. Era in which diperlukan for "give up on vision" berubah became era "mungkin can dijaga." Stem cell therapy sedang open pintu that now.

Possibility Peningkatan Vision from Stem cell therapy—Tiga Fungsi which Support "Kekuatan Melindungi Retina"

Mempromosikan "Regeneration" Retina which Terluka

Stem cell have ability for berubah became cell with sifat serupa cell visual or cell epitel pigmen retina (cell RPE) in condition tertentu. With use ability this, diharapkan can mengganti cell visual which reduced and mengembalikan ability merespons light.

Sebagai contoh, there is report bahwa when stem cell ditransplantasikan to patient RP with penurunan vision which significant, ketebalan retina and respons listrik pulih. With kata lain, bahkan if not can memulihkan vision sepenuhnya, there is hope bahwa "mungkin some fungsi visual can dikembalikan."

"Melindungi" Cell Visual which Masih Bertahan

Stem cell mengeluarkan faktor neurotrofik (zat which maintain cell tetap healthy) and faktor growth in retina. With demikian, support fungsi cell visual which tersisa, memudahkan pemeliharaan vision time this.

Efek this also disebut "efek parakrin = stem cell provide influence positif in sekitarnya," and bahkan if not berubah became cell new, that provide benefit large bagi retina.

Memang, there is report bahwa beberapa patient feel "lapang pandang melebar after treatment" or "became few more strong in darkness."

Menekan "Peradangan and Respons Imun" which Mempercepat Development

Has became jelas bahwa development RP melibatkan not hanya kerusakan genetik on cell visual, tetapi also reaksi peradangan kronis and kesalahan fungsi imun. Stem cell also have fungsi for menekan peradangan and kerusakan imun this.

Sitokin anti-peradangan which disekresikan oleh stem cell dianggap can calm lingkungan retina and reduce kerusakan on cell visual. This on dasarnya "mengerem imun which rusak, calm lingkungan retina so that cell visual can fokus on recovery."

Tiga Kekuatan This Became Fondasi for Face Development RP

Daya tarik stem cell therapy is bahwa ia not terbatas on satu fungsi saja, tetapi have kekuatan for support retina from berbagai arah.

  • For cell which reduced: Kekuatan for add cell new (regeneration)
  • For cell which lelah: Kekuatan for maintain and support (perlindungan)
  • For peradangan which berlebihan: Kekuatan for calm and menyeimbangkan (penyesuaian)

With work bersama in beberapa cara, seluruh lingkungan retina tertata with good, create fondasi for pemeliharaan vision.

POIN IMPORTANT:

  • Stem cell have ability for berubah became cell visual or cell RPE for help regeneration
  • Diharapkan can support pemeliharaan vision through sekresi faktor nutrisi = melindungi cell visual which masih there is
  • There is possibility for memperlambat kecepatan development with menekan peradangan and respons imun which abnormal
  • Nilai intrinsik stem cell therapy is dukungan from tiga arah: "regeneration, perlindungan, and penekan peradangan"

Uji Clinical Latest on Patient RP and Hasilnya

Peningkatan Vision and Lapang Pandang with Pemberian Tunggal MSC Berasal from Umbilikal

There is research in which stem cell berasal from umbilikal (WJ-MSC) ditransplantasikan sekali to bagian belakang eye (in bawah tenon) patient RP. In pemantauan 1 years pasca care:

  • Vision increased, and jumlah karakter which can dibaca increased more from 10 karakter (peningkatan sekitar 2 baris in pengujian vision)
  • Ketebalan retina pulih and bagian which feel light (lapisan cell visual) increased
  • In tes which memeriksa fungsi retina (elektrogram retina), respons also became more good

Peningkatan this diamati terlepas from jenis gen (dominan or resesif), jadi bagi they which khawatir "is efek will there is for gen I," result this provide hope.

Konfirmasi Peningkatan Vision and Keamanan with Stem cell Sumsum Bone Autolog

In Universitas California Davis, research performed in which stem cell which dikumpulkan from bone patient they sendiri disuntikkan to in eye (7 orang berpartisipasi). Hasilnya:

  • Peningkatan vision objektif dikonfirmasi on 4 from 7 orang
  • Lapang pandang also dipertahankan or some increased
  • Not there is efek samping, keamanan very good

Keselarasan antara experience patient which mengatakan "cara melihatnya became more good" and nilai tes have arti which large. This is time "change" terlihat on development which "not will pernah stop" in RP.

Penekanan Development Dikonfirmasi in Pemantauan 3 Years

In research lain, patient RP which receive pemberian berkala WJ-MSC dikombinasikan with rehabilitasi visual (training stimulasi magnetik) dipantau selama 3 years. Dibandingkan with group which not receive treatment:

  • Laju penyusutan lapang pandang jauh more slow
  • Tes which memeriksa fungsi retina (elektrogram retina) also show tren pemeliharaan
  • Vision rata-rata stabil, many kasus without memburuk

Hanya with "menghentikan" or "memperlambat" development, perasaan safe in kehidupan sehari-day patient RP berubah large. Percobaan this show result berharga bahwa "stem cell therapy can mengubah alur disease."

"Fakta bahwa There is Kasus Peningkatan" is Hope Terbesar

RP is disease in which peningkatan which terlihat jelas rarely happen, so that seringkali appear pemikiran "not peduli what which I lakukan, not berguna." However, fakta from research clinical this bahwa there is orang which experience peningkatan fungsi visual, bahkan if hanya beberapa contoh, became turning point trust bahwa "masih there is which can performed."

POIN IMPORTANT:

  • Pemberian tunggal WJ-MSC reported peningkatan vision, lapang pandang, and respons retina
  • With stem cell sumsum bone autolog, peningkatan vision objektif dikonfirmasi on 4 from 7 orang, with keamanan which good
  • In research pemantauan 3 years, penekanan development which jelas shown
  • "Fakta bahwa there is contoh in which fungsi visual increased" is hope terkuat bagi patient RP

Why Safe? Alasannya Terletak on "Sifat Cell" and "Method Pemberian"

Stem cell, especially WJ-MSC, secara inheren have sifat which not mudah merangsang imun.

Bahkan cell from orang lain, tubuh not mudah show reaksi penolakan, so that can dikatakan sebagai cell which safe with efek samping low. Selain that, WJ-MSC young and penuh vitalitas, and risk mutasi and malignasi also very low in many kasus.

  • Injeksi intravitreal (injeksi in deep eye): Teknik standar that juga digunakan pada disease makula yellow terkait usia
  • Pemberian in bawah tenon (pemberian in bawah membran sisi luar bola eye): Pendekatan that soft memungkinkan without menyentuh retina secara langsung

Keduanya is method which has terbukti and many used in oftalmologi, and hingga time this not there is report komplikasi serius.
