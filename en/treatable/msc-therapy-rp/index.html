About Stem cell therapy for Retinitis Pigmentosa

Awal Mula: Condition RP and Berkah Hope

Retinitis Pigmentosa (RP) is disease genetik langka in mana cell-cell visual retina secara bertahap reduced because kelainan gen, menyebabkan lapang pandang menyempit and finally became gangguan penglihatan berat which progresif and sulit disembuhkan.

Hingga saat this, not there is method treatment which can menyembuhkan RP secara radikal. Therapy hanya sebatas bersifat simptomatik, seperti suplemen vitamin A and surgery katarak, which hanya mampu few menunda perkembangan disease. Akibatnya, many patient and their family hidup with kecemasan bahwa "penurunan penglihatan not can dihentikan."

However in beberapa years terakhir, report mulai bermunculan bahwa beberapa treatment true-true can enhance fungsi visual. Cahaya hope mulai menerangi bidang medical. Sebagai contoh, in treatment gen therapy for jenis mutasi gen RP tertentu, kasus-kasus in mana peningkatan lapang pandang and penglihatan diamati mulai muncul.

However, RP is disease which very beragam which melibatkan more from 260 jenis gen. Oleh because that, pendekatan membuat drug treatment for setiap gen have keterbatasan. With latar belakang this, hope terhadap stem cell therapy—which bertujuan for "melindungi retina that sendiri terlepas from jenis gen"—increased with pesat.

Improve and meregenerasi retina with kekuatan stem cell, menghentikan perkembangan disease. Possibility this sudah mulai shown sebagai "result" on beberapa patient RP. Era in mana diperlukan for "give up on penglihatan" berubah became era "mungkin bisa dijaga." Stem cell therapy sedang membuka pintu that sekarang.

Possibility Peningkatan Penglihatan from Stem cell therapy—Tiga Fungsi which Support "Kekuatan Melindungi Retina"

Mempromosikan "Regeneration" Retina which Terluka

Stem cell have ability for berubah became cell with sifat serupa cell visual or cell epitel pigmen retina (cell RPE) in condition tertentu. With use ability this, diharapkan can mengganti cell visual which reduced and mengembalikan ability merespons cahaya.

Sebagai contoh, there is report bahwa when stem cell ditransplantasikan ke patient RP with penurunan penglihatan which significant, ketebalan retina and respons listrik pulih. With kata lain, bahkan jika not can memulihkan penglihatan sepenuhnya, there is hope bahwa "mungkin sebagian fungsi visual can dikembalikan."

"Melindungi" Cell Visual which Masih Bertahan

Stem cell mengeluarkan faktor neurotrofik (zat which menjaga cell tetap sehat) and faktor pertumbuhan in retina. With demikian, support fungsi cell visual which tersisa, memudahkan pemeliharaan penglihatan saat this.

Efek this also disebut "efek parakrin = stem cell provide pengaruh positif in sekitarnya," and bahkan jika not berubah became cell new, that provide manfaat large bagi retina.

Memang, there is report bahwa beberapa patient merasakan "lapang pandang melebar after treatment" or "became few more kuat in kegelapan."

Menekan "Peradangan and Respons Imun" which Mempercepat Perkembangan

Has became jelas bahwa perkembangan RP melibatkan not hanya kerusakan genetik on cell visual, tetapi also reaksi peradangan kronis and kesalahan fungsi imun. Stem cell also have fungsi for menekan peradangan and kerusakan imun this.

Sitokin anti-peradangan which disekresikan oleh stem cell dianggap can menenangkan lingkungan retina and reduce kerusakan on cell visual. This on dasarnya "mengerem imun which rusak, menenangkan lingkungan retina so that cell visual can fokus on recovery."

Tiga Kekuatan This Became Fondasi for Face Perkembangan RP

Daya tarik stem cell therapy is bahwa ia not terbatas on satu fungsi saja, tetapi have kekuatan for support retina from berbagai arah.

  • For cell which reduced: Kekuatan for menambah cell new (regeneration)
  • For cell which lelah: Kekuatan for menjaga and support (perlindungan)
  • For peradangan which berlebihan: Kekuatan for menenangkan and menyeimbangkan (penyesuaian)

With bekerja bersama in beberapa cara, seluruh lingkungan retina tertata with good, menciptakan fondasi for pemeliharaan penglihatan.

POIN PENTING:

  • Stem cell have ability for berubah became cell visual or cell RPE for membantu regeneration
  • Diharapkan can support pemeliharaan penglihatan through sekresi faktor nutrisi = melindungi cell visual which masih there is
  • There is possibility for memperlambat kecepatan perkembangan with menekan peradangan and respons imun which abnormal
  • Nilai intrinsik stem cell therapy is dukungan from tiga arah: "regeneration, perlindungan, and penekan peradangan"

Uji Clinical Latest on Patient RP and Hasilnya

Peningkatan Penglihatan and Lapang Pandang with Pemberian Tunggal MSC Berasal from Umbilikal

There is research in mana stem cell berasal from umbilikal (WJ-MSC) ditransplantasikan sekali ke bagian belakang mata (in bawah tenon) patient RP. In pemantauan 1 years pasca care:

  • Penglihatan increased, and jumlah karakter which can dibaca increased more from 10 karakter (peningkatan sekitar 2 baris in pengujian penglihatan)
  • Ketebalan retina pulih and bagian which merasakan cahaya (lapisan cell visual) increased
  • In tes which memeriksa fungsi retina (elektrogram retina), respons also became more good

Peningkatan this diamati terlepas from jenis gen (dominan or resesif), jadi bagi they which khawatir "apakah efek will there is for gen I," result this provide hope.

Konfirmasi Peningkatan Penglihatan and Keamanan with Stem cell Sumsum Tulang Autolog

In Universitas California Davis, research performed in mana stem cell which dikumpulkan from tulang patient they sendiri disuntikkan ke in mata (7 orang berpartisipasi). Hasilnya:

  • Peningkatan penglihatan objektif dikonfirmasi on 4 from 7 orang
  • Lapang pandang also dipertahankan or sebagian increased
  • Not there is efek samping, keamanan very good

Keselarasan antara experience patient which mengatakan "cara melihatnya became more good" and nilai tes have arti which large. This is saat "perubahan" terlihat on perkembangan which "not will pernah berhenti" in RP.

Penekanan Perkembangan Dikonfirmasi in Pemantauan 3 Years

In research lain, patient RP which receive pemberian berkala WJ-MSC dikombinasikan with rehabilitasi visual (pelatihan stimulasi magnetik) dipantau selama 3 years. Dibandingkan with kelompok which not receive treatment:

  • Laju penyusutan lapang pandang jauh more lambat
  • Tes which memeriksa fungsi retina (elektrogram retina) also show tren pemeliharaan
  • Penglihatan rata-rata stabil, many kasus tanpa memburuk

Hanya with "menghentikan" or "memperlambat" perkembangan, perasaan safe in kehidupan sehari-hari patient RP berubah large. Percobaan this show result berharga bahwa "stem cell therapy can mengubah alur disease."

"Fakta bahwa There is Kasus Peningkatan" is Hope Terbesar

RP is disease in mana peningkatan which terlihat jelas rarely terjadi, so that seringkali muncul pemikiran "not peduli what which I lakukan, not berguna." However, fakta from research clinical this bahwa there is orang which mengalami peningkatan fungsi visual, bahkan jika hanya beberapa contoh, became titik balik kepercayaan bahwa "masih there is which bisa performed."

POIN PENTING:

  • Pemberian tunggal WJ-MSC reported peningkatan penglihatan, lapang pandang, and respons retina
  • With stem cell sumsum tulang autolog, peningkatan penglihatan objektif dikonfirmasi on 4 from 7 orang, with keamanan which good
  • In research pemantauan 3 years, penekanan perkembangan which jelas shown
  • "Fakta bahwa there is contoh in mana fungsi visual increased" is hope terkuat bagi patient RP

Why WJ-MSC Berasal from Wharton's Jelly Optimal for Patient RP

Ekstraksi Bebas Sense Pain, Kesegaran Cell, and Vitalitas Berbeda

WJ-MSC is stem cell which diekstraksi from tali center bayi new lahir. This obtained from jaringan which secara alami dibuang after melahirkan, so that can obtained tanpa membebani tubuh patient that sendiri.

Selain that, karakteristik terbesar is bahwa stem cell this is cell which "muda" from kelahiran.

  • Kecepatan proliferasi cepat
  • Not mudah menua
  • Vitalitas tinggi, hope for kelanjutan efek therapy

Bahkan when dibandingkan with stem cell which berasal from sumsum tulang or lemak, research multiple show bahwa daya tahan hidup in culture and jumlah pembagian more unggul. Artinya, from bahan which more few, can with pasti mengamankan jumlah cell which diperlukan for care, in arti bahwa WJ-MSC achieve keseimbangan antara quality and kuantitas.

Reaksi Penolakan Rendah, Mudah and Safe for Used

Hal which became perhatian in treatment use stem cell is reaksi penolakan imun, tetapi WJ-MSC has terbukti have risiko this which very rendah.

  • Cell orang lain can with mudah diterima oleh tubuh
  • In research clinical aktual, there is report tanpa efek samping selama 1 years
  • Not mudah merangsang imun, and bahkan have tindakan for menekan peradangan

From karakteristik this, WJ-MSC dianggap have kepraktisan tinggi in treatment "pemberian allogeneik" (treatment use cell from donor). Artinya, not perlu waktu for ekstraksi and persiapan cell sendiri, not perlu surgery, tetapi when "ingin underwent care," cell which disiapkan can langsung used sebagai keuntungan large.

Have Komponen Kaya for Melindungi Cell Visual and Mempersiapkan Lingkungan Retina

WJ-MSC dihargai tinggi not hanya because ability regeneration, tetapi also for fungsi "perlindungan" and "penyesuaian."

  • Faktor neurotrofik which melindungi cell saraf (BDNF, NGF, dll.) disekresikan in jumlah large
  • Faktor anti-peradangan (IL-10, TGF-β, dll.) also melimpah
  • Faktor pertumbuhan which terlibat in aliran darah and metabolisme (VEGF, HGF, dll.) also disertakan

Zat-zat this dianggap can support cell visual which melemah in RP, menyiapkan lingkungan retina secara keseluruhan, and membantu memperlambat perkembangan or mempertahankan fungsi visual which tersisa.

Sudah Used in Treatment Aktual, Efek Terlihat

Seperti disebutkan sebelumnya, in research clinical use WJ-MSC, there is beberapa report peningkatan penglihatan and lapang pandang. Selain that, WJ-MSC which used is from donor (provided donor), and fakta bahwa tetap safe and effective is jaminan large bagi patient.

Also, kekuatan unik WJ-MSC is lebar application, seperti perform pemberian beberapa kali with use stem cell which sama.

POIN PENTING:

  • WJ-MSC is cell "muda and penuh vitalitas," unggul in pengaturan jumlah cell which diperlukan for care
  • Reaksi penolakan rendah, not perlu ekstraksi cell patient sendiri, beban reduced
  • Zat which melindungi cell visual and enhance lingkungan retina disekresikan secara melimpah
  • Sudah used in klinik aktual, report keamanan and keefektifan there is

Menuju Treatment which Can Dipilih with Percaya Diri—How Keamanan Stem cell therapy Dievaluasi

Catatan "Tanpa Efek Samping" which Dilaporkan in Research Clinical

  • In uji transplantasi WJ-MSC Turki (pemantauan 1 years), secara jelas disebutkan bahwa not there is efek samping which berbahaya which related with mata or seluruh tubuh.
  • Bahkan in uji stem cell CD34+ from Universitas California Davis in Amerika, not there is report infeksi or komplikasi.
  • On beberapa patient, kemerahan ringan on mata or ketidaknyamanan sementara diamati, tetapi semuanya pulih secara alami in 24 jam tanpa meninggalkan efek jangka panjang.

Percobaan-percobaan this semuanya is research clinical formal on patient RP aktual, and can dikatakan result which overcome kesan "stem cell therapy = berbahaya" is result praktis.

Why Safe? Alasannya Terletak on "Sifat Cell" and "Method Pemberian"

Because Cell which Ramah Tubuh

Stem cell, khususnya WJ-MSC, secara inheren have sifat which not mudah merangsang imun.

Bahkan cell from orang lain, tubuh not mudah show reaksi penolakan, so that can dikatakan sebagai cell which safe with efek samping rendah. Selain that, WJ-MSC muda and penuh vitalitas, and risiko mutasi and malignasi also very rendah in many kasus.

Because Method Pemberian Sudah Terbukti

Technique which used (method pemberian cell) also merupakan elemen penting which mempengaruhi keamanan. In treatment stem cell, method praktis berikut this used:

  • Injeksi intravitreal (injeksi di dalam mata): Teknik standar yang juga digunakan pada penyakit makula kuning terkait usia
  • Pemberian di bawah tenon (pemberian di bawah membran sisi luar bola mata): Pendekatan yang lembut memungkinkan tanpa menyentuh retina secara langsung

Keduanya is method which has terbukti and many used in oftalmologi, and hingga saat this not there is report komplikasi serius.

POIN PENTING

  • In research clinical on patient RP which receive stem cell therapy, not there is report efek samping serius
  • WJ-MSC not mudah merangsang imun, risiko penolakan and malignasi very rendah
  • Technique injeksi intravitreal or pemberian in bawah tenon, which sudah terbukti safe, used for administrasi

Kesimpulan: Melampaui Era in Mana Not There is Treatment—Bring Hope kepada Patient RP

Stem cell therapy bring hope pasti bagi RP, in mana diperlukan for give up on penglihatan.

RP lama has became disease which "not there is treatment." Sulit for mengembalikan penglihatan, and cara for menghentikan perkembangan terbatas. However, with datangnya stem cell therapy, asumsi dasar that berubah with significant.

In uji clinical aktual, peningkatan penglihatan and lapang pandang, as well as penekanan perkembangan dilaporkan, and sekarang era dimulai in mana can berpikir with positif "penglihatan mungkin can dijaga."

Can bekerja with beragam jenis RP, not hanya jenis gen tertentu, and not there is report efek samping serius. Peningkatan opsi treatment which pasti is hope terbesar bagi patient RP.

Jangan buru-buru, hargai care best which can performed sekarang, and percayakan on kemajuan treatment. Era in mana not perlu give up on penglihatan sudah tiba.
