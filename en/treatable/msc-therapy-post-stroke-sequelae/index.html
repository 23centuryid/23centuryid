Sekawal Pasca Stroke and Challenge Treatment Time This

When seseorang terkena stroke (infark serebral or pendarahan serebral), seringkali happen symptom lanjutan seperti kelumpuhan on satu sisi tubuh which make sulit move, or difficulty mengucapkan kata-kata with lancar.

In kedokteran modern, treatment akut immediately after stroke meliputi therapy fibrinolitik (drug for melarutkan gumpalan blood) and intervensi endovaskular with kateter, which has developed pesat and menyelamatkan many nyawa.

However, belum there is method treatment which terbukti secara radikal improve cell-cell nerve brain and tissue which rusak akibat stroke.

Akibatnya, symptom lanjutan seperti kelumpuhan sering take place long, and bahkan with rehabilitasi, recovery sering kali achieve titik stagnan seiring berjalannya time.

On kenyataannya, many patient tetap have kelumpuhan or gangguan bahasa selama periode long and memerlukan care jangka long, became beban large bagi patient and their family.

Ketidakmampuan for cukup enhance disabilitas pasca stroke this has became challenge large, and hope terhadap method treatment new semakin increased.

Stem cell therapy for Sekawal Pasca Stroke and Possibility New Drug Regenerative

Stem cell therapy, which merupakan wrong satu cabang drug regenerative, get perhatian in upaya overcome sekawal pasca stroke.

Drug regenerative, with kata lain sederhana, is jenis treatment which bertujuan for make ulang tissue and organ which rusak in tubuh.

Stem cell therapy is method which khas for hal this, in which cell from patient that sendiri (cell autologus) or cell which provided oleh donor lain (cell alogenik) dimasukkan to in tubuh for achieve recovery fungsi tissue which disappear.

Stem cell have ability for grow (berdiferensiasi) became berbagai tissue, and cell from berbagai sumber has diteliti, mulai from sumsum bone and tissue lemak hingga plasenta and tali center which obtained time kelahiran.

In antara this, mesenchymal stem cell (WJ-MSC) which diekstraksi from tissue seperti jeli which disebut "Wharton's Jelly" in tali center has receive perhatian special in beberapa years terakhir.

Stem cell which berasal from Wharton's Jelly is cell which berasal from tali center which obtained on time kelahiran and mudah dikumpulkan, and have karakteristik bahwa reaksi penolakan not mudah happen bahkan when ditransplantasikan to orang lain.

Stem cell this can dibayangkan sebagai cell which have "penghalang" for melindungi from system kekebalan ibu time bayi masih in in kandungan, and berkat sifat this, bahkan when ditransplantasikan to orang lain, cell-cell this not mudah diserang (ditolak) oleh system kekebalan tubuh.

With use WJ-MSC with sifat unggul seperti this, treatment can performed without difficulty or beban mengambil cell from patient that sendiri.

Research treatment sekawal pasca stroke use stem cell sedang performed in seluruh world, and kini there is possibility bahwa patient will mendapatkan recovery fungsi nerve which before not can didapatkan with treatment konvensional.

For example, stem cell disampaikan to in tubuh through suntikan intravena (IV) or with memasukkan jarum through bone pinggul to ruang cairan bone belakang (pemberian intrathecal) for improve brain which rusak.

If therapy rehabilitasi konvensional is pendekatan "memaksimalkan fungsi which tersisa", maka Stem cell therapy is pendekatan which bertujuan for "heal bagian which rusak that sendiri".

Therapy regenerative which breakthrough this has open possibility new bagi patient and family which menderita sekawal pasca stroke.

Mekanisme Kerja Stem cell therapy for Sekawal Pasca Stroke

Lalu, how secara spesifik stem cell which dimasukkan to in tubuh can improve kerusakan brain? Mekanisme kerja Stem cell therapy, singkatnya, is "membangkitkan kekuatan healing diri which tertidur in brain which rusak".

Stem cell which diinjeksikan sendiri can grow and menggantikan cell-cell which diperlukan (for example cell nerve), tetapi which more important is many "zat which mendorong recovery" which disekresikan oleh stem cell.

Bayangkan brain sebagai sebuah taman.

In taman which rusak oleh "badai" from stroke, rumput liar (peradangan berbahaya) grow subur, and tanaman important (cell nerve) not can grow.

Then pendatang - stem cell - datang sebagai petani, eliminate rumput liar (perbaikan lingkungan tanah), menyebarkan pupuk (faktor growth) and help daun-daun new (cell nerve new and pembuluh blood) grow.

Efek main which diharapkan from Stem cell therapy is sebagai berikut.

Menekan peradangan and menyiapkan lingkungan healing:

Stem cell berkumpul in site kerusakan and memproduksi zat which calm respons peradangan which berlebihan and menyesuaikan keseimbangan kekebalan. This function for memadamkan "api peradangan" in brain which rusak and create lingkungan which kondusif for healing (tanah).

This reduce kerusakan sekunder cell nerve and create fondasi for recovery.

Mendorong regenerasi pembuluh blood and cell nerve new:

Faktor growth and sitokin which disekresikan oleh stem cell strong mendorong angiogenesis (pembentukan kapiler new) and regeneration nerve.

For example, faktor neurotrofik which berasal from brain (BDNF) and faktor growth hepatosit (HGF) which dilepaskan from stem cell has terbukti melindungi cell nerve which rusak and menumbuhkan tunas nerve new.

This memompakan returned blood and nutrisi to brain which rusak, mendorong kelangsungan hidup and growth cell nerve that sendiri.

Memfasilitasi reorganisasi tissue nerve brain:

Diharapkan bahwa Stem cell therapy mendorong rekoneksi (remodeling) sirkuit nerve which terputus in brain.

In percobaan hewan, dilaporkan bahwa stem cell enhance connection new (sinapsis) antara cell nerve. This seperti merekonstruksi jalur transmisi sinyal bahkan with make rute alternative.

Hasilnya, became mungkin bagi bagian brain which tersisa for mengambil peran new and mengkompensasi fungsi, for example with menggerakkan member badan which lumpuh through jalur alternative.

Melengkapi cell that disappear:

Although in jumlah small, there is indikasi bahwa stem cell which diinjeksikan mungkin can berdiferensiasi became cell nerve that sendiri or cell pembuluh blood and menggantikan which rusak. With kata lain, function melengkapi cell bagian which rusak secara langsung.

However, sejauh which known time this, alih-alih stem cell that sendiri tetap bertahan long in brain and continuously work, diyakini bahwa efek from zat which dilepaskan stem cell (disebut secretome) is which main.

Faktanya, in percobaan hewan, bahkan after stem cell which ditransplantasikan menghilang from tubuh seiring time, has dilaporkan bahwa efek therapy which cukup masih happen berkat zat which disekresikan selama periode tersebut.

With cara this, stem cell became "factory drug" that sendiri and mengeluarkan zat which diperlukan, secara diam-diam support healing diri brain.

Bukti Effectiveness Stem cell therapy for Sekawal Pasca Stroke

In research this, when stem cell secara berkala disuntikkan to ruang cairan bone belakang (ruang in sekitar sumsum bone belakang from bone pinggul), perbaikan gangguan bahasa terlihat in beberapa kasus (pidato became more jelas), and kasus in which fungsi motorik member badan which lumpuh increased also dilaporkan.

Selain in atas, in berbagai research clinical large and small which performed in berbagai country in seluruh world, peningkatan fungsi motorik and symptom neurologis akibat Stem cell therapy has dilaporkan secara berturut-turut.
