Disease Arteri Perifer (PAD) is condition in which arteri which bring blood from heart to feet menyempit or tersumbat because aterosklerosis (penumpukan plak), menyebabkan aliran blood which terbatas.

Because pembuluh blood in feet not can mensuplai blood with cukup, patient experience symptom seperti nyeri betis, kesemutan time proceed (which mereda time istirahat), and on kasus which parah, nyeri feet which great bahkan time istirahat, luka which sulit sembuh, or ulkus in ujung feet.

PAD which developed disebut Iskemia Member Gerak Kritis (Critical Limb Ischemia, CLI), condition serius in which if dibiarkan can mengakibatkan gangren and on kasus terburuk amputasi feet.

When arteri menyempit because plak, aliran blood memburuk, and muscle and skin in feet not receive oksigen and nutrisi which cukup, which became penyebab symptom PAD.

For PAD, treatment pertama-tama bertujuan mencegah development and meringankan symptom through modifikasi gaya hidup and therapy farmakologis (drug for mengencerkan blood or melebarkan pembuluh blood, dll).

Selain that, when penyumbatan pembuluh blood parah, procedure angioplasti based kateter (for example angioplasti transluminal perkutan (PTA) or penempatan stent) or surgery rekonstruksi vaskular bedah seperti bypass performed.

Although many patient increased with treatment this, there is many kasus in which aterosklerosis meluas to seluruh feet make surgery sulit, or patient not can mentoleransi surgery because komplikasi parah.

Faktanya, sekitar 10-30% patient CLI parah dikatakan "not there is indikasi (no-option)" for rekonstruksi vaskular, and time this pilihan treatment which effective terbatas. In kasus-kasus sulit for diobati this, nyeri and ulkus not improved, and on finally some feet mungkin harus diamputasi.

In antara keterbatasan treatment time this, there is kebutuhan strong for pengembangan method treatment new bagi patient which berharap "entah how mempertahankan feet I sendiri".

Disease Arteri Perifer and Stem cell therapy: Possibility New Drug Regenerative

Oleh because that, which get perhatian new-new this is pendekatan new through drug regenerative use stem cell therapy. Stem cell therapy is method treatment which use "stem cell", cell which membentuk dasar tubuh kita, for mempromosikan regeneration tissue which rusak.

For feet with aliran blood which terbatas because penyumbatan arteri, tujuannya is for menyuntikkan stem cell and create pembuluh blood new from in tubuh for enhance aliran blood. Daripada "secara artifisial make jalur bypass" with surgery bypass, citra this is mempromosikan pembentukan bypass natural (sirkulasi kolateral) with kekuatan which dimiliki stem cell.

Faktanya, for patient CLI which not have pilihan treatment konvensional, pendekatan drug regenerative seperti this diharapkan sebagai strategi new.

There is berbagai jenis stem cell, tetapi which especially used in drug regenerative for disease arteri perifer is mesenchymal stem cell (Mesenchymal Stem Cells, MSC) or cell mononuklear which berasal from sumsum bone and blood perifer.

Secara special, MSC can diekstrak from berbagai bagian tubuh seperti sumsum bone, tissue lemak, and tali center (tali center), and with membudayakan and meningkatkannya, jumlah cell which diperlukan can diamankan, make MSC sebagai sumber cell which strong for drug regenerative.

MSC have ability which unggul in menghasilkan faktor growth which meregenerasi pembuluh blood and menekan peradangan, and has show bahwa that can berkontribusi on perbaikan disease arteri perifer. In antara which get perhatian new-new this is stem cell which berasal from tali center which diambil from "Wharton's Jelly", tissue seperti gelatin in tali center (Wharton's Jelly-derived MSC, WJ-MSC).

This is stem cell which obtained from tissue which obtained on time kelahiran bersama plasenta, with many advantage including ability proliferasi and diferensiasi which high, not perlu mengekstrak cell from tubuh patient sendiri reduce beban, and can provided for many patient with membudayakan and meningkatkannya.

Selain that, dilaporkan bahwa WJ-MSC have ability high in mensekresikan zat which terlibat in angiogenesis and anti-inflammation, and mengharapkan many hal sebagai sumber cell for drug regenerative for disease arteri perifer.

Mekanisme Kerja Stem cell therapy for Disease Arteri Perifer

Stem cell therapy dianggap effective for disease arteri perifer because stem cell is "terapis multifungsi" which perform many peran secara bersamaan. Mekanisme aksi berikut known for stem cell therapy secara spacious.

Menumbuhkan pembuluh blood new (promosi angiogenesis):

Stem cell mensekresikan sejumlah large zat which disebut faktor growth and sitokin which function sebagai sinyal "make pembuluh blood" in tissue which rusak. Berkat this, cell sekitar diaktifkan and kapiler new memanjang, membentuk bypass pembuluh blood natural on tingkat cell (disebut formasi sirkulasi kolateral).

For example, cell mononuklear which berasal from sumsum bone mengandung cell prekursor endotel (EPC), and with dimasukkan to in dinding pembuluh blood new sebagai bagian from pembuluh blood which new terbentuk, also dilaporkan for memperkuat tissue pembuluh blood.

Tindakan menekan peradangan (anti-inflamasi and modulasi imun):

In daerah with aliran blood bad, selain kerusakan tissue from penyumbatan pembuluh blood, there is reaksi inflammation which happen, and peradangan this more break pembuluh blood and tissue in siklus which merugikan.

Stem cell also melepaskan sitokin anti-inflammation which calm peradangan, apply gaya penyelamat for system kekebalan which overexcited. With this, kerusakan tissue from peradangan which not perlu and sense pain mereda, and lingkungan healing disusun.

Tindakan melindungi tissue and mempromosikan perbaikan (perlindungan cell and anti-fibrosis):

Zat which disekresikan oleh stem cell not hanya angiogenesis and anti-inflammation tetapi also tindakan melindungi cell-cell tissue and menekan fibrosis (parut).

With send faktor perlindungan cell (faktor sitoprotektif) which work "bertahananlah for hidup!" to cell muscle rangka which difficulty because kekurangan oksigen, help menghentikan kematian tissue. Selanjutnya, peradangan which parah selama periode kekurangan oksigen sering menyebabkan tissue which terluka became kaku and mengeras (fibrosis). Stem cell have fungsi mencegah hal this.

Stem cell therapy melibatkan suntikan cell seperti mesenchymal stem cell which berasal from sumsum bone (BM-MSC), cell mononuklear (BM-MNC), or cell prekursor from blood perifer (EPC) to area feet with aliran blood which bad. Cell-cell this, pertama-tama, mensekresikan zat bioaktif seperti faktor growth, sitokin, and enzim for mempromosikan regeneration pembuluh blood. With this, will more mudah for membentuk kapiler new in tissue sekitar, and aliran blood improved.

More lanjut, stem cell melepaskan zat which calm peradangan, menekan kerusakan which happen akibat reaksi kekebalan which berlebihan. Selain that, they also have fungsi mencegah "fibrosis (parut)" in which tissue which rusak became kaku.

With tindakan-tindakan this from stem cell, cell-cell seperti muscle rangka which experience kerusakan because kekurangan oksigen and nutrisi dilindungi, and struktur and fungsi muscle and skin more mudah dipertahankan.

With cara this, stem cell therapy not hanya "make pembuluh blood" tetapi also recovery aliran blood, pengendalian peradangan, and perlindungan and perbaikan tissue through many efek, berkontribusi on peringan symptom disease arteri perifer and promosi recovery.

Bukti Effectiveness Stem cell therapy for Disease Arteri Perifer

Stem cell therapy have hope teoritis, tetapi poin "is true-true effective?" is which important.

Faktanya, selama sekitar 20 years terakhir, many research clinical stem cell therapy on patient PAD and CLI has performed in seluruh world. Sebagai hasilnya, data positif which show effectiveness stem cell therapy secara bertahap terakumulasi.

For example, in Jepang, on years 2002, uji coba pemberian suntikan cell sumsum bone patient CLI they to feet performed, and peringan sense pain feet, percepatan healing ulkus, and bahkan penurunan tingkat amputasi dilaporkan. Research this (uji coba TACT oleh Tanabe and Yuasa) get perhatian international and became pendahulu cell therapy for PAD.

Akhir-akhir this, not hanya uji coba small tetapi also meta-analisis which menganalisis bersama result from many uji clinical has diterbitkan. Wrong satunya mengintegrasikan analisis 23 uji coba terkontrol acak which menargetkan patient CLI (total 962 orang), and effectiveness and keamanan stem cell therapy dievaluasi (PubMed ID: 29977308).

Sebagai result analisis, group which receive stem cell show peningkatan berikut dibandingkan with group which hanya receive treatment konvensional:

  • Luka ulkus feet which sulit disembuhkan became more mudah sembuh (tren perbaikan luka)
  • Indikator angiogenesis secara significant increased (pembentukan kapiler new dipromosikan)
  • Peningkatan aliran blood and indikator oksigen seperti rasio tekanan blood pergelangan feet-lengan (ABI) and tekanan oksigen transkutan (TcO₂)
  • Pengurangan nyeri feet time istirahat and perpanjangan jarak which can proceed without sense pain (peningkatan ability proceed)
  • Pengurangan risk amputasi feet (amputasi tungkai bawah) in periode tertentu

With cara this, patient which receive stem cell therapy show peningkatan in indikator objektif aliran blood, symptom and ability motorik increased, and more lanjut proporsi which until on amputasi large dikurangi has shown.

Sementara beberapa research show variasi in tingkat effectiveness, secara keseluruhan, possibility bahwa feet which not can diselamatkan with treatment konvensional can diselamatkan with stem cell therapy disarankan.

More lanjut, research clinical use stem cell sumber cell new, stem cell which berasal from tali center (WJ-MSC) sedang appear. For example, in uji clinical fase I which dilaporkan on years 2024, WJ-MSC which obtained from donor lain disuntikkan to in muscle feet on patient CLI berat which disertai with diabetes, and keamanan and efektivitasnya diamati.

Sebagai hasilnya, all patient which receive treatment menghindari amputasi feet (including amputasi jari feet) selama enam month, and not there is efek samping which serius dilaporkan. Selain that, 6 month after treatment, peringan which significant in sense pain feet (skor VAS) and perpanjangan time when can proceed without sense pain, as well as peningkatan in skor fungsi feet (FADI) diamati.

Although jumlah patient small, this is report which bermakna which show peningkatan large which obtained with therapy WJ-MSC latest which "sense pain feet reduced and can proceed" (PubMed ID: 38112806). Selain that, berbagai uji clinical therapy PAD use berbagai stem cell sedang take place in country-country in seluruh world, and bukti new continuously terakumulasi.

Akumulasi research this bring stem cell therapy more dekat to pilihan treatment which more mapan.
