Lupus Eritematosus Sistemik (SLE) is disease autoimmune in which system kekebalan tubuh secara keliru menyerang tissue tubuh sendiri, menyebabkan peradangan in berbagai organ and tissue.

Condition disease experience gelombang remisi (when disease mereda) and eksaserbasi (when disease memburuk). Treatment main time this is pemberian steroid and imunosupresif, however unfortunately, on more from half patient, eksaserbasi not can sepenuhnya dicegah.

Selanjutnya, when jumlah drug increased for menekan symptom disease, risk efek samping serius seperti infeksi berat also increased. Penggunaan steroid jangka long menyebabkan efek samping (for example osteoporosis, diabetes, infeksi berat) which influence quality of life patient secara significant.

Selain that, drug biologis new which new-new this appear for SLE hanya satu which approved oleh FDA AS in beberapa dekade terakhir (belimumab), and bahkan drug this not provide efek which cukup kepada all patient.

With demikian, treatment SLE time this have keterbatasan in hal effectiveness and efek samping, and patient mengharapkan method treatment new which more safe and mendasar.

Lupus Eritematosus Sistemik and Stem cell therapy: Possibility New Drug Regenerative

Sementara therapy drug konvensional berfokus on "menekan" symptom, stem cell therapy through drug regenerative is pendekatan new which bertujuan for "improve and menormalkan" fungsi tubuh that sendiri.

In disease autoimmune seperti SLE, system kekebalan which seharusnya membela tubuh experience disfungsi and menyerang diri sendiri. Stem cell therapy have potential for "mereset" reaksi imun abnormal this and membimbing tubuh returned to keadaan normal.

Especially, jenis stem cell which disebut mesenchymal stem cell (MSC) get perhatian special, and research and uji coba clinical sedang take place in seluruh world. MSC can diambil from sumsum bone, tissue lemak, tali center, and lainnya, with opsi use cell patient sendiri (autologus) or cell from donor (allogenik).

In antara they, "stem cell which berasal from Wharton's Jelly (WJ-MSC)" which terkandung in tali center dianggap very menjanjikan. Wharton's Jelly is tissue seperti gelatin in in tali center which obtained time kelahiran.

MSC which diambil from bagian this dilaporkan more rarely ditolak oleh system kekebalan dibandingkan with MSC which berasal from sumsum bone, and have kapasitas proliferasi which high. Oleh because that, diharapkan bahwa sejumlah large stem cell can provided with beban minimal, and diharapkan cocok for pemberian kepada patient.

Stem cell therapy is garis depan drug regenerative which bring new hope bahkan for SLE which sulit diobati (kasus which tahan terhadap treatment konvensional) which before not have pilihan treatment which effective.

Mekanisme Kerja Stem cell therapy for Lupus Eritematosus Sistemik

MSC dianggap effective for SLE because can work from many sudut for overcome patofisiologi disease. We will explain tiga mekanisme main how stem cell therapy work on SLE.

Tindakan penyesuaian imun (penyesuaian ulang):

MSC calm system kekebalan which terlalu aktif and memulihkan keseimbangan. Bayangkan seolah-olah MSC menemui "center komando" imun and mengajarkan returned bahwa "target serangan bukanlah diri sendiri".

Secara spesifik, MSC enhance cell T regulator which menekan reaksi diri which berlebihan, and menghambat kegilaan cell B which memproduksi autoantibodi. With cara this, MSC provide rem on kegilaan imun.

Tindakan anti-inflamasi and perbaikan tissue:

MSC melepaskan many substansi aktif and vesikel sekresi small which secara kolektif disebut "secretome". Substansi-substansi this work in seluruh tubuh for calm peradangan and mempromosikan perbaikan tissue which rusak.

For example, with reduce substansi which memicu peradangan and sebaliknya memasok faktor growth which help perbaikan and regeneration tissue, diharapkan can enhance kekuatan healing organ which rusak.

Perlindungan and fungsi organ:

SLE sering menyebabkan kerusakan on organ important seperti kidney and lungs. MSC meringankan serangan on organ-organ this and function for menghentikan kerusakan. Faktanya, in model hewan and research awal, pemberian MSC has improve indikator fungsi kidney and reduce kerusakan tissue organ secara histologis.

Stem cell therapy is pendekatan "healing" and "pengendalian" which komprehensif which influence patofisiologi SLE from many arah. Singkatnya, this is method treatment which provide rem on imun which kegilaan while help improve place which rusak. Research sedang berlanjut for mengungkap mekanisme which more terperinci, tetapi patient mengharapkan this sebagai "care which enhance konstitusi tubuh and mendekati akar disease".

Bukti Effectiveness Stem cell therapy for Lupus Eritematosus Sistemik

There is research in which WJ-MSC from donor lain (allogenik) disuntikkan through infus to 15 patient SLE with aktivitas high and respons treatment konvensional not memadai. Hasilnya, in time less more satu years after pemberian, aktivitas disease increased drastis on all patient, and indikator kerusakan organ seperti fungsi kidney also menurun.

Poin important is not there is report efek samping serius. Although beberapa patient show kambuhnya kerusakan kidney (proteinuria) satu years after dimulainya treatment, some large patient can mempertahankan keadaan remisi.

Temuan this, bahwa keamanan and effectiveness dipertahankan in jangka long, is bukti important which enhance trust on stem cell therapy.

In uji coba this, aktivitas disease menurun secara jelas on 5 from 6 patient SLE which sulit diobati after pemberian MSC, and efeknya bertahan selama satu years. Sebagai efek sekunder, pengurangan drug steroid also successful (5 from 6 patient successful reduce steroid became 10 mg/day or less).
