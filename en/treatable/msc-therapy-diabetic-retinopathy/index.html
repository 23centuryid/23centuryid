Retinopati Diabetes and Challenge Treatment Time This

Retinopati Diabetes (DR) is disease in which pembuluh blood smooth retina terluka oleh diabetes, menyebabkan penurunan vision. When kadar gula blood tetap high in jangka long, pembuluh blood retina tersumbat or bocor, menyebabkan retina kekurangan oksigen and not can mempertahankan fungsi visual normal.

Time disease developed, pembuluh blood new which abnormal (neovaskularisasi) grow in retina, which menyebabkan pendarahan and ablasi retina, and can mengakibatkan blindness. Time this, treatment for retinopati diabetes especially include therapy simptomatik berikut:

  • Kontrol gula blood and pemantauan: Hal pertama is mengelola kadar gula blood with good. Namun, sayangnya, bahkan with pengelolaan gula blood that tepat, ada kasus where retinopati terus develop.
  • Fotokoagulasi laser: Laser diterapkan pada retina untuk membakar area that membutuhkan oksigen high, menghambat development pembuluh blood new. Ini is therapy that effective untuk mempertahankan vision, tetapi tidak secara mendasar fix kerusakan retina.
  • Injeksi intravitreal obat anti-VEGF: Untuk menekan edema (pembengkakan) that happen in retina and pembuluh blood abnormal, obat that menghambat faktor growth endotel vaskular (VEGF) secara berkala disuntikkan to deep bola eye. Ini menekan development lesi pada many pasien, tetapi memerlukan injeksi sekitar sekali sebulan, that merupakan beban ekonomi and fisik...
  • Operasi vitreous: When happen pendarahan vitreous or ablasi retina that disertai with pembuluh blood that grow, operasi vitreous eliminate pendarahan deep eye and tissue parut. Ini is operasi that memerlukan peralatan medical tingkat lanjut and keterampilan, tetapi become upaya terakhir untuk menghentikan lesi that develop.

However, all method treatment time this have tujuan "memperlambat or menghentikan development" and not treatment radikal which can heal retinopati diabetes that sendiri. On kenyataannya, bahkan with continuously perform treatment, sulit for sepenuhnya menghindari kerusakan retina in future and penurunan vision.

Sebagai contoh, there is report which show bahwa bahkan after continue treatment anti-VEGF selama 2 years, hanya sekitar 30% patient experience peningkatan vision which significant, show keterbatasan efek treatment. Selain that, beban kunjungan rutin and injeksi make many kasus sulit for continue treatment.

With cara this, although treatment time this can menekan development retinopati, sulit for improve retina which rusak to keadaan semula, so that hope terhadap sarana treatment new increased.

Gula blood high jangka long break pembuluh blood in seluruh tubuh. This seperti jalan which retak because hujan and angin bertahun-years and sulit dilalui. Arteri healthy is seperti jalan width in which blood mengalir with lancar, tetapi when aterosklerosis happen, "plak (benjolan)" seperti kolesterol menumpuk in in pembuluh blood, jalan became sempit. Then aliran blood memburuk and oksigen as well as nutrisi which dibutuhkan not until.

In retinopati diabetes, fenomena this happen on pembuluh blood smooth in retina in belakang eye. When pembuluh blood tersumbat and aliran blood memburuk, cell-cell retina became kekurangan oksigen and rusak. Treatment time this especially berupa therapy simptomatik for menekan "proliferasi pembuluh blood abnormal new" or "pendarahan" which appear sebagai result kerusakan this. With kata lain, fokusnya is menekan problem which happen time that.

However, which true-true ingin achieved is "improve pembuluh blood which rusak and tissue retina that sendiri." Sebagai pendekatan mendasar seperti that, stem cell therapy has interesting perhatian large in beberapa years terakhir. Stem cell have ability for help improve tissue which rusak and mendorong regeneration pembuluh blood, and diharapkan can menata ulang lingkungan retina.

Possibility New Stem cell therapy and Kedokteran Regenerative for Retinopati Diabetes

With development kedokteran regenerative in beberapa years terakhir, method treatment new use stem cell sedang dijelajahi for retinopati diabetes. Stem cell is cell special which have ability for berdiferensiasi became berbagai cell and ability for improve tissue, and with kata lain they is "cadangan tubuh".

With provide stem cell to tubuh patient, dimungkinkan for meregenerasi and improve tissue retina which rusak, and can bertujuan on recovery vision and healing lesi which before sulit with treatment before.

Actually, research about stem cell therapy for retinopati diabetes sedang take place in seluruh world, and in eksperimen on hewan, has dikonfirmasi bahwa stem cell memperlambat development retinopati and meringankan symptom. Sebagai contoh, there is research in which stem cell ditransplantasikan to retina tikus which blind for merekonstruksi fungsi cell nerve visual, and research in which faktor which berasal from stem cell given kepada hewan in keadaan gula blood high for menekan peradangan retina.

With kesuksesan research pra-clinical this, stem cell therapy sedang receive hope large sebagai pilihan treatment new for retinopati diabetes.

There is beberapa jenis stem cell. Stem cell which berasal from sumsum bone patient and stem cell which berasal from tissue lemak perut, as well as stem cell pluripoten artificial (cell iPS) which dibuat secara artifisial sedang diteliti. In antaranya, which most diperhatikan is mesenchymal stem cell which berasal from umbilikal (Wharton's Jelly-derived MSC: WJ-MSC) which diekstraksi from bagian which disebut Wharton's jelly in umbilikal.

WJ-MSC which merupakan tissue which berasal from janin have karakteristik very young and have kapasitas proliferasi which high. Selain that, tali center which obtained on time kelahiran is tissue which biasanya dibuang, and can diambil with safe without membebani bayi or ibu hamil, and not there is problem etika. Selanjutnya, WJ-MSC known have reaksi penolakan imun which low (toleransi imun high), and cell which obtained from tali center orang lain can used... Oleh because that, WJ-MSC dianggap sebagai kandidat strong for stem cell therapy terhadap retinopati diabetes.

With leverage kekuatan stem cell and kedokteran regenerative, dimungkinkan for improve tissue retina which rusak that sendiri and secara fundamental mengobati penurunan vision which caused oleh retinopati diabetes. This is advantage unique from kedokteran regenerative which not there is in therapy simptomatik time this, and will became hope large bagi patient.

Mekanisme Kerja Stem cell therapy for Retinopati Diabetes

Menekan Peradangan and Proliferasi Pembuluh Blood Abnormal (Mengerem Imun)

In retinopati diabetes, reaksi peradangan kronis happen in retina because stres from gula blood high, and kerusakan meluas. Stem cell melepaskan berbagai zat which meredakan peradangan, with kata lain mengerem imun which rusak, calm lingkungan retina and menekan development change patologis.

Also, pembuluh blood abnormal (neovaskularisasi) which developed with peradangan also ditekan oleh faktor which disekresikan oleh stem cell. With cara this, stem cell calm lingkungan retina and menekan development change patologis.

Improve Pembuluh Blood and Mendorong Aliran Blood Normal

Stem cell have ability for improve tissue which rusak. In retinopati diabetes, pembuluh blood kapiler and tissue pembuluh blood which rusak became problem, tetapi stem cell can berdiferensiasi became cell which help improve pembuluh blood (seperti perisit which support in sekitar pembuluh blood) and dilaporkan can melakukannya.

Actually, in eksperimen hewan, when stem cell given to in eye, kepadatan kapiler retina increased and aliran blood increased has shown. With this, oksigen and nutrisi which cukup achieve retina, and kerusakan tissue and condition iskemia can diperbaiki...

Melindungi and Meregenerasi Cell Nerve Retina

Retina is bagian from tissue nerve, and there is cell-cell nerve important seperti cell visual and cell ganglion nerve. Stem cell mengeluarkan many faktor nutrisi nerve (seperti neurotrofin) which melindungi nerve, enhance kelangsungan hidup cell-cell nerve retina which terluka...

Sebagai contoh, in model in which nerve retina tikus rusak, when WJ-MSC human ditransplantasikan to vitreous, tindakan faktor anti-peradangan and faktor nutrisi nerve which disekresikan oleh stem cell menghasilkan penurunan significant kematian cell ganglion nerve retina...

With cara this, stem cell melindungi cell-cell nerve retina, and with mendorong diferensiasi to cell-cell new sesuai kebutuhan, dianggap mengarah on regeneration tissue retina. Especially, koleksi zat which disekresikan oleh stem cell also disebut "secretome," and include berbagai faktor which diwakili oleh exosom (gelembung small with diameter sekitar 50-150nm which dilepaskan cell).

Secretome inilah which became bintang efek therapy, and can dikatakan bahwa stem cell heal retina not hanya with "mengubah diri sendiri" tetapi also with "mengeluarkan perintah bermanfaat." Sebagai contoh, in research in which exosom which berasal from stem cell given to retina model diabetes, jalur sinyal tertentu (jalur Wnt/β-catenin) which sedang rusak in retina ditekan, and dengannya, stres oksidatif and peradangan, as well as development neovaskularisasi... Result this show possibility bahwa hanya zat which produced oleh stem cell can secara memadai mengerem retinopati.

Ringkasan Mekanisme Kerja

Mekanisme kerja stem cell therapy which influence retina not hanya satu, tetapi dianggap bahwa cooperation beberapa pekerjaan menghasilkan efek therapy. Tindakan main is sebagai berikut:

  • Menekan peradangan and proliferasi pembuluh blood abnormal (mengerem imun)
  • Improve pembuluh blood and mendorong aliran blood normal
  • Melindungi and meregenerasi cell nerve retina

Through beberapa tindakan seperti this, stem cell therapy have potential for menghentikan development retinopati diabetes and more jauh lagi memulihkan struktur and fungsi retina which rusak.

Bukti Effectiveness Stem cell therapy for Retinopati Diabetes

Temuan from Research Pra-Clinical (Eksperimen Hewan):

Berbagai research use model hewan retinopati diabetes has reported result which menjanjikan from stem cell therapy. Sebagai contoh, in eksperimen in which exosom which diekstraksi from mesenchymal stem cell which berasal from sumsum bone given kepada tikus diabetes, peradangan retina and proliferasi pembuluh blood abnormal ditekan, and symptom retinopati which caused oleh...

Hal this support "secretome" which disebutkan before, show bahwa keadaan retinopati improved not oleh cell that sendiri tetapi oleh faktor which dilepaskan oleh stem cell. Selain that, in research lain, stem cell CD34+ which berasal from sumsum bone (with ability for meregenerasi blood and pembuluh blood...) which applied on retina diabetes menghasilkan pemeliharaan tissue pembuluh blood retina. With kata lain, with use stem cell, tissue pembuluh blood retina dipertahankan, and kekurangan aliran blood because diabetes can diperbaiki.

Temuan from Research Clinical (Verifikasi on Human):

Efek stem cell therapy mulai dilaporkan in research clinical which menargetkan patient human. Sebagai contoh, in uji clinical which performed in Cina, infus intravena mesenchymal stem cell which berasal from tali center (WJ-MSC) performed on 61 patient with diabetes tipe 2, and pemantauan diperpanjang selama 3 years (PubMed ID: 27588104).

Hasilnya, group which receive injeksi stem cell show tingkat terjadinya komplikasi including retinopati diabetes more low dibandingkan with group kontrol which not receive injeksi. Actually, hingga 3 years then, patient which new develop retinopati is 16,1% on group stem cell therapy versus 26,7% on group kontrol, which secara significant more high, show possibility bahwa stem cell therapy berkontribusi on pencegahan and penekanan development retinopati.

More interesting lagi, in uji coba this, peningkatan on indikator kontrol gula blood (HbA1c and nilai peptida in blood) also diamati, show possibility bahwa stem cell therapy improve metabolisme gula seluruh tubuh and secara not langsung meringankan retinopati.

Bagaimanapun, this is result important which show bahwa stem cell therapy can have efek menguntungkan on journey retinopati diabetes on human, and hope for uji clinical which more large increased more jauh. Perlu dicatat bahwa in treatment this not there is efek samping which merugikan...

With demikian, day when stem cell therapy can used in practice clinical will immediately tiba.
