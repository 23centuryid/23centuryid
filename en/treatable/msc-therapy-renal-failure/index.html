Gagal Kidney and Pengobatannya - Why "Treatment Time This Saja Not Cukup"?

With terapi standar seperti dialisis and transplantasi kidney, tidak all kasus gagal kidney that progresif can ditangani, and many pasien feel keterbatasan deep kualitas hidup and pilihan therapy.

Disease kidney kronis (CKD) is disease which developed secara diam-diam which also dikenal sebagai "disease senyap", and diperkirakan influence more from 13 juta orang. When fungsi kidney menurun with significant hingga achieve gagal kidney tahap akhir (ESRD), diperlukan "therapy pengganti kidney" seperti dialisis hemodialisis, dialisis peritoneal, or transplantasi kidney.

However, in lapangan clinical nyata, jumlah patient which underwent dialisis continuously increased setiap years, and on years 2017, more from 330.000 orang underwent therapy dialisis kronis. In sisi lain, jumlah transplantasi kidney, which merupakan treatment kuratif, tetap in bawah 1.700 kasus per years, and many patient which want transplantasi tetapi not can mencapainya.

In balik situasi this there is kekurangan donor which serius. Selain that, although therapy dialisis is sarana important for support kehidupan, beban berikut on kehidupan sehari-day not terhindarkan:

  • Kunjungan rutin tiga kali per week with time care (4-5 jam per sessions)
  • Pembatasan makanan and cairan which berkelanjutan
  • Risk komplikasi seperti infeksi and disease kardiovaskular

Transplantasi kidney also face challenge including problem perolehan donor, penolakan, efek samping imunosupresan, and beban cost medical which high.

Care konservatif (therapy diet and farmakologi) can memperlambat development, tetapi not have kekuatan for memulihkan fungsi kidney that sendiri which has menurun. With kata lain, with system medical time this, "we can menghentikan pemburukan tetapi not can mengembalikan kidney to keadaan semula".

With latar belakang this, there is request which strong for strategi treatment new which can menggantikan or melengkapi dialisis and transplantasi.

POINT

  • CKD dianggap sebagai "disease national new" which influence sekitar 1 from 8 orang dewasa
  • Dialisis and transplantasi is treatment important tetapi not can memenuhi kebutuhan all patient
  • With care time this, very sulit for "mengembalikan" fungsi kidney that sendiri
  • Method treatment new diperlukan for expand pilihan quality of life and treatment

Opsi "Heal Kidney" for Gagal Kidney Mulai Terlihat

With progress kedokteran regenerative, Stem cell therapy that bertujuan untuk pemulihan fungsi kidney itu sendiri has become pilihan new that different from dialisis and transplantasi with kemungkinan praktis.

Treatment for gagal kidney selama this didominasi oleh "pengganti" seperti dialisis and transplantasi kidney. However, with development latest in kedokteran regenerative, ide "improve kidney that sendiri" has became kenyataan.

Perhatian special given on therapy use stem cell.

Stem cell is cell special which have "ability autoreplicating" for menduplikasi cell which identik with dirinya sendiri, and "kapasitas pluripotensial" for berubah became jenis cell lain sesuai kebutuhan. With mengmanfaatkan kapabilitas this, dasar pemikiran Stem cell therapy is meregenerasi organ and tissue which rusak.

In bidang kidney, stem cell mesenchymal (MSC) get perhatian special. MSC can obtained from sumsum bone, lemak, tali center (tali pusar), dll, and have berbagai tindakan including anti-inflammation, modulasi imun, and perbaikan tissue.

In research praklinik (hewan) and uji clinical awal, has shown bahwa MSC can menyeimbangkan immunity, reduce kerusakan kidney because peradangan, and berkontribusi on pemeliharaan and peningkatan fungsi kidney.

Karakteristik MSC that Berasal from Jelly Wharton (WJ-MSC)

In antara berbagai MSC, which dianggap most menjanjikan is stem cell which berasal from tali center (WJ-MSC).

WJ-MSC is cell which diekstraksi from tissue mirip jeli which disebut "Wharton Jelly" in in tali center which dibuang after kelahiran, and have karakteristik berikut:

  • Cell Young and Aktif

    Because berasal from bayi new lahir, cell-cell tersebut young, with kapasitas proliferasi that more high daripada that berasal from sumsum bone or lemak, and sekresi faktor growth that more big.

  • Few Ditolak

    Ekspresi antigen that terlibat deep imunitas low, sehingga when diberikan kepada orang lain, tidak mudah menimbulkan reaksi penolakan.

  • Few Problem Etika

    Tidak ada beban bagi donor pada time pengambilan, and because diperoleh from tali pusat that secara natural dibuang setelah lahir, ini is bahan that relatif mudah diterima secara etis.

  • Pasokan that Stabil Dimungkinkan

    Budaya and proliferasi in luar tubuh mudah dilakukan, and jumlah big can dijamin, that sesuai untuk aplikasi klinis.

WJ-MSC, sebagai "cell which mudah didapatkan, quality high, and can used from orang lain", became calon main for Stem cell therapy for gagal kidney.

In Jepang also, beberapa facility medical has mulai apply Stem cell therapy use WJ-MSC in bentuk care swasta, interesting perhatian sebagai pendekatan which different from transplantasi kidney traditional.

POINT

  • Stem cell therapy is wrong satu kedokteran regenerative which bertujuan for "heal kidney that sendiri"
  • MSC unggul in modulasi imun, anti-inflammation, and perbaikan tissue, with possibility enhance fungsi kidney
  • WJ-MSC young, aktif, few ditolak oleh system imun, and have pasokan which stabil
  • Sebagai strategi treatment new which not memerlukan organ donor, can became pilihan alternative from transplantasi kidney

How Stem cell Work on Kidney?

MSC (stem cell mesenchymal) memiliki berbagai tindakan deep kidney that calm lingkungan peradangan kidney, melindungi cell that rusak, and mempromosikan perbaikan and regenerasi.

In balik effectiveness Stem cell therapy in mengobati gagal kidney is berbagai aksi biologis which shown oleh MSC which disuntikkan in in kidney.

Especially, MSC which berasal from tali center (WJ-MSC) dikatakan have tindakan for "menekan peradangan, melindungi cell, and menyiapkan perbaikan" which mendekati akar problem kerusakan kidney.

In bawah this, tindakan utamanya dijelaskan from tiga perspektif.

Calm Peradangan: Modulasi Imun and Tindakan Anti-Inflamasi

Development disease kidney kronis and gagal kidney related erat with reaksi peradangan berlebihan and kelainan imun.
MSC bertindak sebagai rem for "immunity which lari liar" this and perform penyesuaian berikut:

  • Menekan aktivitas cell-cell imun which menyebabkan peradangan seperti cell T and makrofag
  • Reduce pelepasan sitokin inflammation (IL-6, TNF-α, dll)
  • Mencegah apoptosis (kematian diri cell) and menekan penghancuran tissue

With cara this, MSC memainkan peran in menghentikan kerusakan akibat peradangan kidney and create lingkungan which tenang in which perbaikan can dilanjutkan.

Melindungi Tissue Kidney: Dukungan Perbaikan and Anti-Fibrosis

When kidney rusak, area tersebut seiring time digantikan oleh tissue parut which hard (tissue fibrotik). This berarti penurunan fungsi kidney which not can diperbalikkan.
MSC show tindakan berikut terhadap development this:

  • Melepaskan faktor growth (VEGF, HGF, IGF-1, dll) and mempromosikan regeneration pembuluh blood (angiogenesis)
  • Support kelangsungan hidup and proliferasi cell which rusak
  • Mensekresikan sitokin which menekan fibrosis (menekan TGF-β, dll)

This memungkinkan pendekatan for "mencegah tissue parut and melindungi tissue kidney asli which function".

Perlindungan from Stres Oksidatif: Tindakan Antioksidan

In disease kidney kronis, stres oksidatif because spesies reaktif oksigen (ROS) also merupakan wrong satu penyebab kerusakan cell. MSC mendorong production bahan antioksidan and memperkuat pertahanan in tingkat cell.

Especially, eksosomnya (vesikel ekstrasulular) which dilepaskan oleh WJ-MSC which berasal from tali center mengandung many molekul which menekan production spesies reaktif oksigen, and has dikonfirmasi in eksperimen hewan bahwa stres oksidatif ditekan and cell-cell kidney dilindungi.

What itu Secretome? - Identitas Zat that "Work" Stem cell

Some large efek which given Stem cell therapy on kidney not because MSC that sendiri "berubah (diferensiasi)" became cell kidney. Actually, berbagai faktor which disekresikan oleh MSC work on cell kidney, and function through tindakan parakrin (sekresi sampingan) which mempromosikan perbaikan diri.

Koleksi faktor-faktor which disekresikan this disebut sebagai secretome.

Secretome include komponen-komponen berikut:

  • Sitokin and faktor growth (HGF, VEGF, IL-10, dll)
  • Molekul anti-inflammation and anti-fibrosis
  • Vesikel ekstrasulular small (seperti eksosomnya)

With kata lain, MSC is seperti "factory drug hidup" which send equipment which diperlukan for perbaikan to "lingkungan kidney which rusak".

POINT

  • MSC menekan lari liar imun and calm peradangan kidney
  • Support perbaikan cell which rusak and melindungi tissue with mencegah fibrosis
  • Against stres oksidatif and reduce kematian cell-cell kidney
  • Aksi main didasarkan on efek parakrin oleh secretome

Bisakah Gagal Kidney Dipulihkan? Possibility which Terlihat through Stem cell therapy

From eksperimen hewan and uji klinis, has dilaporkan data that show bahwa pemeliharaan and pemulihan fungsi kidney can diharapkan through Stem cell therapy.

Tindakan Pelindung Kidney that Dikonfirmasi deep Model Hewan

In research on hewan model in which nefritis or gagal kidney diinduksi, many contoh dilaporkan in which pemberian MSC menekan peradangan and fibrosis, and good struktur maupun fungsi kidney increased.

Tanda Perbaikan that Terlihat deep Uji Klinis

In uji coba terkontrol which dirandomisasi which menargetkan nefropati diabetik, MSC allogenik given satu kali secara intravena kepada patient CKD tingkat menengah hingga parah. Dibandingkan with group plasebo, tren berikut shown:

  • 12 week after pemberian, eGFR show tren perbaikan rata-rata +4 mL/min/1.73㎡
  • Group plasebo show penurunan eGFR
  • Possibility penghentian penurunan fungsi
  • Not there is efek samping serius which related with pemberian dilaporkan

Contoh Perbaikan pada Pasien Gagal Kidney Tahap Akhir

In kasus pria aged 70 years (CKD Tahap V) which receive infus intravena stem cell which berasal from Jelly Wharton (WJ-MSC), perbaikan berikut terlihat in 4 month since care:

  • eGFR: 12.5 → 16.4 mL/min (Tahap V → Tahap IV)
  • Kreatinin: 4.7 → 3.9 mg/dL
  • BUN: Tren perbaikan
  • Skor QOL (symptom which dirasakan oleh patient and quality of life): Peningkatan jelas

With cara this, Stem cell therapy not hanya "memperlambat development" tetapi also show possibility konkret for "memulihkan fungsi kidney".

POINT

  • MSC show penghambatan peradangan kidney, pengurangan fibrosis, and recovery fungsi in eksperimen hewan
  • Uji clinical awal also mengkonfirmasi tren perbaikan eGFR and keamanan
  • Kasus gagal kidney tahap akhir also show contoh perbaikan which cukup for menunda dialisis
  • Stem cell therapy receive perhatian sebagai possibility new which can memulihkan fungsi kidney

Is Stem cell therapy Safe? Verifikasi Efek Samping and Kemungkinannya

Stem cell therapy, especially terapi use stem cell mesenchymal (MSC), has dilaporkan deep research now show efek samping serius that few, sebagai metode therapy with keamanan high.

Because kebaruan therapy, many orang which mempertanyakan "Is this true-true safe?" However, in research and uji clinical which has performed, peristiwa merugikan which significant not dilaporkan on many patient.

Hampir Tidak Ada Efek Samping pada Time Pemberian

For example, in uji clinical which menargetkan nefropati diabetik, MSC which berasal from orang lain given satu kali secara intravena, and not there is efek samping serius which related with treatment which terlihat. Poin which perlu diperhatikan is bahwa antibodi anti-HLA (marker penolakan transplantasi) not terdeteksi in tubuh patient which receive treatment. This show possibility bahwa bahkan if cell from orang lain, not menimbulkan reaksi penolakan and work with safe in in tubuh.

Demikian pula, in kasus gagal kidney tahap akhir use WJ-MSC which berasal from Jelly Wharton, keamanannya very good. After infus intravena, not there is demam or reaksi alergi which terlihat, and treatment diselesaikan without problem.

Reaksi Samping Ringan Can Happen Sementara

Secara umum, efek samping which dilaporkan is which berikut ringan:

  • Demam ringan
  • Fluktuasi tekanan blood sementara
  • Ketidaknyamanan or sense pain in location suntikan

Semuanya is sifat sementara, and biasanya menghilang secara natural in beberapa day. Selain that, because MSC that sendiri have tindakan for calm system kekebalan tubuh, karakteristik anafilaksis seperti reaksi hipersensitivitas which serius rarely happen.

Area that Memerlukan Verifikasi Lanjutan

In sisi lain, because merupakan therapy with sejarah short, poin-poin berikut harus diperiksa with liver-liver in future:

  • Impact pemberian berkelanjutan which berulang kali
  • Keamanan when apply on lansia or patient beratnya berat sekalipun
  • Penangkapan risk efek samping through pengamatan jangka long

On time this, evaluasi main is "method care which safe and with efek samping serius which few", tetapi akumulasi data more lanjut diharapkan in future.

POINT

  • In care MSC, hampir not there is efek samping serius which dilaporkan after pemberian
  • Bahkan if from orang lain, reaksi penolakan not mudah happen, and keamanan imunologis high
  • If efek samping appear, this is which ringan seperti demam ringan and ketidaknyamanan location suntikan
  • In future, perlu for more memverifikasi keamanan pemberian berkelanjutan and on kasus berat

Kesimpulan: Future which Dibawa Stem cell

Possibility for menghindari dialisis - this have makna which most large bagi patient which face kenyataan gagal kidney.

Kedokteran regenerative use stem cell mesenchymal, especially WJ-MSC which berasal from Jelly Wharton, secara bertahap mengubah hope that became kenyataan.

Time this berada in tahap in which that provided oleh beberapa facility medical sebagai bagian from care swasta, tetapi in research in and luar negeri, result positif continuously dilaporkan including perbaikan fungsi kidney, kelegaan symptom, and keamanan high.

Before, when fungsi kidney has menurun secara significant, satu-satunya pilihan is dialisis or transplantasi. However, with munculnya Stem cell therapy, opsi for "heal kidney" finally became praktis.

With progress research stem cell, frase "Jangan give up on gagal kidney" not lagi teori kosong, tetapi became panduan praktis which nyata for hidup in future.
