Degenerasi Makula Terkait Usia (AMD) is disease eye that serius that can menyebabkan penurunan vision pusat and loss vision pada lansia.

Seiring bertambahnya usia, bagian tengah retina which disebut makula rusak, and ability for see secara bertahap disappear - this is Degenerasi Makula Related Usia (Age-related Macular Degeneration, AMD). What that Degenerasi Makula Related Usia (AMD)?

Ini is disease where makula retina rusak seiring bertambahnya usia, menyebabkan penurunan bidang pandang pusat or loss vision.

Peran makula and mekanisme pengembangan AMD

Makula is area in center retina which menangkap information visual most detail. When area this experience kerusakan seiring bertambahnya usia, vision center became buram or dark, provide impact serius on aktivitas sehari-day seperti read or mengemudi.

Dua Jenis AMD

There is dua jenis main degenerasi makula related usia berikut.

  • AMD Atrofik (Dry)
    Jenis where limbah (drusen) terakumulasi in bawah retina and cell-cell secara bertahap experience atrofi. Perkembangannya relatif slow, tetapi belum ada metode therapy fundamental that ditetapkan.
  • AMD Neovaskular (Wet)
    Jenis where pembuluh blood abnormal grow in bawah retina, menyebabkan pendarahan and kebocoran cairan, sehingga menurunkan vision with fast. Injeksi intravitreal with obat anti-VEGF is therapy standar, tetapi sulit untuk sepenuhnya menghentikan development, and ada risk kekambuhan.

Therapy Now and Keterbatasannya

With method treatment time this, penekanan development with drug anti-VEGF dimungkinkan for AMD tipe wet, tetapi not mungkin for improve cell retina which rusak and memulihkan vision. For AMD dry, praktis not there is agen farmakologis which effective.

Oleh because that, what which can performed is "not membiarkan semakin memburuk", tetapi "memulihkan vision which disappear" not can performed - this is batas from treatment AMD time this.
