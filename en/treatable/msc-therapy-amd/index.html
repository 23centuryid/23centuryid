On Stem Cell Therapy for Age-Related Macular Degeneration

  • On Stem Cell Therapy for Age-Related Macular Degeneration
Degenerasi Makula Terkait Usia (AMD) adalah penyakit mata yang serius yang dapat menyebabkan penurunan penglihatan pusat dan kehilangan penglihatan pada lansia.

With advancing age, the central part of the retina, known as the macula, deteriorates, leading to a gradual loss of vision. This condition is termed Age-related Macular Degeneration (AMD). In recent years, the global number of patients has steadily increased, making it an increasingly significant concern in aging societies.

In modern medicine, while treatments exist to delay progression, there are virtually no means of restoring lost vision. However, with advancements in regenerative medicine, novel approaches employing stem cells are attracting considerable interest.

What is Age-Related Macular Degeneration (AMD)?

Ini adalah penyakit di mana makula retina rusak seiring bertambahnya usia, menyebabkan penurunan bidang pandang pusat atau kehilangan penglihatan.

The Role of the Macula and the Mechanisms Underlying AMD Development

The macula is the central region of the retina responsible for sharp, detailed vision. As this area degenerates with age, central vision may become blurred or dark, significantly impairing daily activities such as reading or driving.

Two Types of AMD

There are two main types of age-related macular degeneration as follows.

  • AMD Atrofik (Kering)
    Jenis di mana limbah (drusen) terakumulasi di bawah retina dan sel-sel secara bertahap mengalami atrofi. Perkembangannya relatif lambat, tetapi belum ada metode pengobatan fundamental yang ditetapkan.
  • AMD Neovaskular (Basah)
    Jenis di mana pembuluh darah abnormal tumbuh di bawah retina, menyebabkan pendarahan dan kebocoran cairan, sehingga menurunkan penglihatan dengan cepat. Injeksi intravitreal dengan obat anti-VEGF adalah pengobatan standar, tetapi sulit untuk sepenuhnya menghentikan perkembangan, dan ada risiko kekambuhan.

Current Treatment and Its Limitations

Under current therapeutic approaches, the progression of wet AMD can be suppressed using anti-VEGF agents. However, it is not yet possible to repair damaged retinal cells and restore vision. For dry AMD, virtually no effective pharmacological agents are available.

Therefore, the current capability is to prevent further deterioration, but not to restore lost vision. This represents the current limitation of AMD treatment.

Item

  • Age-related Macular Degeneration (AMD) is a disease in which the retinal macula deteriorates with age.
  • Central vision decline results in significant impairment of daily living.
  • Anti-VEGF therapy is available for the wet form; however, a complete cure is challenging to achieve.
  • No effective treatment is currently available for the dry type.
  • The need for essential medical treatment is very high.

New Key to Vision Restoration: Stem Cells Derived from Wharton's Jelly

Dalam Terapi Sel Punca untuk Degenerasi Makula Terkait Usia (AMD), sel punca mesenchymal yang berasal dari Wharton's Jelly (WJ-MSC) menarik perhatian sebagai "harapan pemulihan penglihatan" yang menggabungkan kemampuan regeneratif tinggi dan keamanan.

Why is WJ-MSC Promising for AMD?

In age-related macular degeneration, the light-sensing cells in the center of the retina (photoreceptor cells) and their supporting retinal pigment epithelium (RPE) sustain damage, leading to the development of vision loss. In this context, mesenchymal stem cells derived from Wharton's Jelly (WJ-MSCs) are highly beneficial.

WJ-MSC possesses the following characteristics suitable for the treatment of AMD.

  • Secreting growth and neurotrophic factors that protect photoreceptor cells and RPE, thereby establishing a reparative retinal environment.
  • Suppressing chronic inflammation, reducing the causes of retinal tissue damage
  • Upon demand, it can differentiate into cells resembling retinal cells, potentially restoring lost cellular function.
  • Minimal effect on the immune system; can be safely administered even if derived from other sources (low immunogenicity).

A Multi-Faceted Approach to Vision Restoration

WJ-MSC tidak hanya "mengganti sel yang rusak", tetapi meningkatkan lingkungan retina itu sendiri, mendukung kelangsungan hidup sel fotoreseptor dan pemulihan fungsi.

For example, a "healing rain" on a retina damaged by inflammation would release growth factors and nutritional factors to prepare the environment. Furthermore, the "rewiring" of damaged nerve cells in the retina (promoting synapse formation) is also anticipated.

This not only inhibits disease progression but also presents the possibility of approaching the regeneration of visual function itself.

How Stem Cell Therapy Heals Eyes

WJ-MSC bekerja sebagai pendukung yang melindungi retina yang rusak dan mengaktifkan sel-sel sekitar, sehingga mengeluarkan kekuatan pemulihan yang dimiliki mata itu sendiri.

Mari kita lihat mekanisme bagaimana sel punca bekerja di dalam mata dan mengarah ke perbaikan penglihatan. Poin penting adalah bahwa WJ-MSC bekerja dalam dua aspek: "Peran melengkapi sel-sel baru itu sendiri" dan "Peran membantu sel-sel sekitar untuk mempromosikan perbaikan diri".

Analogously, it concurrently functioned as both a horticulturalist cultivating new growth for a dilapidated garden, and a nurse administering nutrients and hydration to revitalize weakened flora in its vicinity.

Capacity to Protect Surrounding Cells and Promote Regeneration (Paracrine Effect)

WJ-MSC's most significant advantage is its paracrine effect, defined as the ability to support surrounding cells. Even without engraftment in the surrounding tissue, its mere presence leads to the secretion of various beneficial substances (cytokines and growth factors).

These substances act to stimulate damaged cells and mitigate inflammation. For example, neurotrophic factors such as BDNF and CNTF protect retinal neurons (e.g., photoreceptor cells) and promote cell survival and functional maintenance.

Thus, the prevention of ongoing retinal cell death and the revitalization of compromised cellular function are anticipated.

Furthermore, WJ-MSCs exhibit anti-inflammatory and immunomodulatory properties. In Age-related Macular Degeneration (AMD), chronic inflammation and immune dysregulation are key contributors to retinal damage. However, the anti-inflammatory factors secreted by these stem cells interrupt this vicious cycle and protect the retina.

Furthermore, stem cells are also reported to support energy-deficient retinal cells by transferring their own mitochondria (the cell's powerhouses) to the damaged cells. This mechanism is analogous to supplementing a depleted battery with a new one, thereby supporting the restoration of cellular function.

Through such multi-faceted supportive effects, WJ-MSC prepares the retinal environment and enhances its self-repair capabilities. Unlike transient symptomatic therapies, harnessing the retina's intrinsic regenerative capacity is a key feature of Stem Cell Therapy.

Restoring Lost Photoreceptor Cells (Differentiation/Replacement Capacity)

Another critical task is the actual provision of new ocular cells.

Stem cells possess the ability to differentiate into specific cell types, and a subset of WJ-MSCs has been reported to acquire characteristics similar to photoreceptor cells or retinal pigment epithelial (RPE) cells in vivo. Indeed, in animal experiments, transplanted WJ-MSCs were integrated into the retina and functioned as light-responsive cells.

Specifically, given that AMD primarily damages retinal pigment epithelial (RPE) cells in the macular region of the retina, replenishing these is crucial. Therefore, the strategy of transplanting RPE cells derived from stem cells into the retina, thereby replacing the lost tissue, is attracting significant attention.

Future therapies directly replacing lost retinal tissue with WJ-MSC-derived cells are also anticipated.

Most importantly, the primary approach in current Stem Cell Therapy is via paracrine effects that assist neighboring cells and promote self-repair.

While novel cell replacement will continue to be investigated in the future, even now, the revitalization of compromised cells through stem cell potency, and the resulting improvement in visual function, have been reported.

Key Points

  • WJ-MSC secretes cytokines and growth factors into the local environment, protecting damaged retinal cells and promoting their repair.
  • It also functions to suppress inflammation and provide mitochondria to support cellular function.
  • Certain WJ-MSCs may possess characteristics akin to photoreceptor or RPE cells, potentially complementing retinal function as novel cells.
  • Currently, the approach centers on supporting surrounding cells, but the prospect of visual function improvement is highly anticipated.

The efficacy of Stem Cell Therapy in genuinely improving vision is a paramount concern for patients. In previous clinical trials and case reports, promising results have been steadily accumulating.

Promising Results in Retinal Degenerative Diseases – Growing Optimism for AMD

Retinitis pigmentosa (RP), a rare disease with pathophysiological similarities to age-related macular degeneration (AMD), has been treated with periocular transplantation of Wharton's Jelly-derived mesenchymal stem cells (WJ-MSC).

In a one-year follow-up study, it was reported that the thickness of the atrophied and thinned retina had been restored, and both the visual field range and light response had improved.

Specifically, a significant improvement was observed, with an average increase of more than 10 readable characters on the visual acuity chart. This is equivalent to a two-line improvement on the visual acuity chart and is reported to have brought about a substantial, perceptible change in patients' daily lives.

Furthermore, functional improvement has also been confirmed in ERG (electroretinogram) tests, an objective index of retinal function.

Both Retinitis Pigmentosa (RP) and Age-related Macular Degeneration (AMD) are diseases characterized by the impairment of photoreceptor cells and retinal pigment epithelial (RPE) cells. Therefore, the regenerative and protective effects observed in RP are considered to have high potential for application in AMD therapy.

In fact, in the treatment of WJ-MSC for RP,

  • Retinal tissue protection
  • Improvement of vision and visual field
  • Emphasis on development

Such effects have been demonstrated, indicating significant potential for the treatment of AMD. Furthermore, for AMD, therapies that "not only delay progression, but also protect and restore visual function" are becoming a more tangible reality.

Furthermore, the results indicate suppression of retinal disease progression.

In RP Stem Cell Therapy,

  • Within one year post-treatment, no progression of visual field constriction was observed.
  • Vision and visual field have remained stable for 3 years.

In progressive retinal diseases, vision and visual field are typically lost gradually. However, the anticipated ability of Stem Cell Therapy to decelerate the progression of these conditions offers substantial hope to patients.

Even in Age-related Macular Degeneration (AMD), from the perspective of preserving residual visual function and maintaining the status quo to the greatest extent possible, it holds significant importance.

Safety and Prospects of Healthcare in Indonesia and Abroad

Terapi Sel Punca menggunakan WJ-MSC dianggap memiliki keamanan tinggi, dan pilihan perawatan tersebar di luar negeri. Di Indonesia, masih dalam tahap penelitian, tetapi harapan pasti semakin dekat.

When undertaking new therapeutic challenges, concerns regarding safety are natural. However, in previous clinical studies, no serious adverse events were reported in Stem Cell Therapy utilizing WJ-MSC.

Even in studies targeting retinal diseases, no ocular deterioration or observable systemic adverse effects were noted; rather, instances where retinal inflammation subsided and improvements in visual function were observed to accrue.

The favorable immunological compatibility of WJ-MSC is considered to contribute to its high safety.

Healthcare Status in Indonesia

As of 2025, in Indonesia, treatment using Wharton's Jelly-derived stem cells (WJ-MSC) for age-related macular degeneration is generally not performed.

At university hospitals and research institutions, research on retinal cell regeneration using iPS cells and similar technologies is ongoing. However, treatment utilizing WJ-MSCs is not approved and has not yet reached a stage where it is made available to general patients.

Domestically, particularly within the scope of insurance coverage, anti-VEGF and similar therapies are the primary focus. Furthermore, even if Stem Cell Therapy is desired, the infrastructure for accessing treatment using WJ-MSC is not yet established.

Prospective Treatments Developing Abroad

On the other hand, internationally, the situation is different. In countries advanced in regenerative medicine, such as Malaysia, visual regenerative therapy utilizing WJ-MSC has progressed to clinical application.

At medical institutions that have met stringent local standards, patients from Indonesia are also accepted, and a system is in place to ensure a tranquil treatment experience.

Despite financial and logistical barriers, individuals who have undergone treatment have provided positive testimonials, such as "vision has become clearer" and "daily confidence has increased."

Given that Stem Cell Therapy necessitates highly advanced medical techniques, it is imperative that it be performed in a reputable facility, strictly adhering to established protocols. Globally, facilities exist that offer treatments without meeting appropriate standards, and historically, complications arising from improper procedures have been reported.
