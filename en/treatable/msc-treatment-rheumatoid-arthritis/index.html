Artritis Reumatoid: What That? Method Treatment Time This and Challenge which Tersisa

Artritis reumatoid is disease that menyebabkan kerusakan sendi and gangguan fungsi if develop, and healing sulit with treatment that ada, sehingga metode therapy new diperlukan.

Artritis reumatoid (RA) is disease in which peradangan kronis happen on sendi because kelainan imun. In Jepang, diperkirakan sekitar 1% from populasi (1 from 100 orang) menderita disease this, and especially sering happen on wanita.

Symptom main is nyeri sendi, pembengkakan, and kaku, with kaku pagi khas happen when wake in pagi day. If peradangan berlanjut, bone rawan and bone will hancur, menyebabkan deformitas sendi and pembatasan rentang movement.

If developed, jari-jari can tetap curved and not can diluruskan, or sendi can berubah bentuk so that disturb kehidupan sehari-day.

Gangguan fungsi akibat kerusakan sendi this very reduce quality of life (QOL) patient, and in kasus which parah, pekerjaan became sulit or care became diperlukan.

Terapi Farmakologis Main Now and Hasil Klinis

Sebagai method treatment for artritis reumatoid, until time this drug anti inflammation (pereda nyeri), metotrexat (MTX) and drug-obatan anti-reumatik traditional, as well as agen biologis (antibodi anti-TNFα, penghambat reseptor IL-6, dll) has used.

Since 1990an, with munculnya MTX and agen biologis, result treatment has increased secara dramatis, and artritis reumatoid has berubah from "disease which not can disembuhkan" became "disease which can dituju remisi with treatment".

Sebagai contoh, in uji clinical which provide agen biologis infliksimab selama 1 years, sekitar 38% patient achieve remisi or aktivitas disease low, and 58% patient can menghentikan development kerusakan sendi.

With provide treatment farmakologis which tepat since awal, many patient now can achieve perbaikan symptom which significant.

Challenge that Masih Belum Teratasi

However in sisi lain, drug-obatan treatment which tersedia time this belum achieve healing total. Although drug-obatan can menekan peradangan, sulit for secara mendasar eliminate penyebab autoimmune that sendiri, and not there is method treatment for improve sendi which have rusak became seperti semula.

Although development kerusakan sendi that sendiri can dihentikan, bone rawan and bone which has rusak not will grow returned secara natural, so that on patient which has menderita disease this for time which long and has mengakumulasi kerusakan serius on sendi they, bahkan with therapy farmakologis latest, gangguan fungsi masih can happen.

Selanjutnya, because therapy farmakologis main time this menekan fungsi imun, there is risk efek samping seperti infeksi, which became beban in continue treatment. However demikian, dilaporkan bahwa 20-40% patient (50% according beberapa report) not mendapatkan efek which cukup from treatment which there is.

With cara this, challenge treatment artritis reumatoid yaitu "menghentikan sepenuhnya success kekebalan" and "improve sendi which rusak" masih tetap there is, and pendekatan new for complete this has diminta.

POINT

  • Peradangan kronis happen on sendi because kelainan imun
  • When kerusakan bone rawan and bone developed, fungsi sendi menurun and quality of life (QOL) terganggu
  • Therapy farmakologis traditional can ditujukan for remisi symptom tetapi healing sulit
  • Risk efek samping from penekanan imun and kasus in which efek which cukup not obtained also there is, so that method treatment new diperlukan

Possibility New Therapy Regenerative: Why Stem cell therapy Get Perhatian

Stem cell therapy untuk artritis reumatoid is terapi regenerative generasi berikutnya that menormalkan kelainan imun while menargetkan perbaikan tissue sendi.

In beberapa years terakhir, "Stem cell therapy" has interesting perhatian sebagai method treatment revolusioner for artritis reumatoid.

Stem cell therapy is method treatment which mengambil stem cell from patient or penyumbang that sendiri and memasukkannya to in tubuh, use kapasitas regeneration and kekuatan penyesuaian imunnya for enhance condition patient. With menormalkan kelainan imun with soft, mendorong perbaikan and regeneration tissue which rusak, as well as show efek treatment through berbagai mekanisme seperti penekanan peradangan and efek parakrin (transduksi sinyal antar cell), this interesting perhatian sebagai therapy regenerative multi-fungsi.

There is berbagai jenis stem cell, tetapi mesenchymal stem cell (MSC) very interesting perhatian for artritis reumatoid. MSC there is in sumsum bone, tissue lemak, tali pusar (tali pusar), dll, and not hanya can berdiferensiasi became bone and bone rawan tetapi also equipped with fungsi for menormalkan fungsi imun which abnormal.

For reason this, daripada hanya menekan imun, with control secara soft reaksi imun which berlebihan, perbaikan mendasar from artritis reumatoid diharapkan. Selain that, because MSC also mendorong perbaikan and regeneration bone rawan and bone which rusak, this have keunggulan large because can work on penyebab artritis reumatoid (kelainan imun) and hasilnya (kerusakan sendi).

From fitur-fitur this, MSC disebut sebagai strategi treatment generasi berikutnya which menangani good "serangan (regeneration)" maupun "pertahanan (penyesuaian)", and research and application sedang take place in seluruh world.

Keunggulan MSC Berasal from Wharton's Jelly What?

MSC especially diekstraksi from tissue lemak, sumsum bone, tali pusar (Wharton's jelly), dll. In antara this, MSC which berasal from sumsum bone (BM-MSC), which dulunya became arus main, have beban large on tubuh which related with ekstraksi, and there is batasan on jumlah cell which can diekstraksi, so that dikatakan have many challenge.

Sebaliknya, MSC which berasal from tali pusar (WJ-MSC) which obtained secara natural time melahirkan very dihargai because can obtained with method non-invasif which not bring sense pain or risk, and also mudah diterima from perspektif etika sebagai sumber cell.

Selanjutnya, has terbukti bahwa MSC which berasal from tali pusar have kekuatan penyesuaian imun and kapasitas diferensiasi which setara with which berasal from sumsum bone, and because cell that sendiri young and have kekuatan pembelahan which unggul, there is advantage bahwa cell quality high mudah provided with stabil.

From karakteristik seperti this, MSC which berasal from tali pusar interesting perhatian sebagai pilihan which memungkinkan in application for disease imun and peradangan including artritis reumatoid.

POINT

  • Stem cell therapy is method treatment multi-fungsi which use kapasitas regeneration and efek penyesuaian imun
  • Mesenchymal stem cell (MSC) very menjanjikan especially for artritis reumatoid, disease autoimmune
  • MSC menormalkan kelainan imun with soft while mempromosikan regeneration bone rawan and bone
  • MSC which berasal from tali pusar can diambil with cara which not invasif, cell quality high and stabil provided

How Stem cell therapy Work on Artritis Reumatoid? ~ 3 Mekanisme Aksi ~

Stem cell (MSC) work secara multi-faset pada artritis reumatoid through tiga tindakan: penyesuaian imun, penekanan peradangan, and perbaikan sendi.

Trusted bahwa MSC (mesenchymal stem cell) show efek on artritis reumatoid through tiga lapis kerja: menormalkan autoimmune which meliar, meredam peradangan sendi with aksi anti-inflammation which strong, and mempromosikan perbaikan bone rawan and bone which rusak.

Different with agen farmasi traditional which fokus on "sementara menekan" peradangan, MSC menyesuaikan imun that sendiri, calm peradangan from akar, and more jauh lagi help regeneration tissue, which merupakan breakthrough in hal this.

Normalisasi Keseimbangan Imun
─ Rem pada Reaksi Imun that Meliar

In artritis reumatoid, imun which seharusnya melindungi tubuh experience kesalahan fungsi and menyerang sendi patient sendiri. MSC have aksi for calm "success imun" this with soft and menormalkan keseimbangan.

Secara special, known bahwa hal this menekan aktivitas berlebihan cell-T which menyebabkan peradangan (cell Th17 and cell Th1, dll) and enhance fungsi cell-T pengatur (cell Treg) which menekan imun.

Selanjutnya, hal this also work on cell-B which memproduksi antibodi and dilaporkan have efek for reduce production "autoantibodi" which became penyebab artritis reumatoid.

With cara this, MSC function sebagai "penyesuai" which calm system imun in keadaan success and mengembalikannya to condition normal. Terhadap mobil which berpacu with rem which rusak, MSC mengerem and mengembalikan kecepatannya──itulah imajnya.

Aksi Anti-Inflamasi that Strong
─ Penenangan Peradangan oleh Sitokin

MSC also have aksi for calm peradangan with mengeluarkan sitokin and faktor growth zat-zat effective. Sekresi this secara kolektif disebut "secretome" and langsung work on reaksi imun berlebihan in sendi.

Sebagai contoh, interleukin 10 (IL-10), prostaglandin E2 (PGE2), TGF-β and lainnya which disekresi oleh MSC menekan production sitokin which mempromosikan peradangan and memutus rantai peradangan which berkelanjutan in sendi.

Kerja seperti this disebut "efek parakrin (sekresi paracrine)" and merupakan wrong satu mekanisme important from therapy MSC.

Sederhananya, MSC memainkan peran "agen pemadam kebakaran" calm api peradangan, and through this, envisaged is pengurangan nyeri and pembengkakan sendi.

Promosi Perbaikan Tissue
─ Help Regenerasi Bone Rawan and Bone that Rusak

MSC have ability for mempromosikan perbaikan and regeneration tissue sendi which rusak. Sesuai kebutuhan, MSC that sendiri can berdiferensiasi became cell bone rawan or osteoblas, or mengeluarkan faktor growth which help regeneration cell sekitarnya, support recovery struktural sendi.

In artritis reumatoid, bone rawan and bone dihancurkan oleh peradangan, tetapi MSC have potential for help regeneration they.

Actually, in research in which MSC which berasal from tali pusar dikultur in condition which sesuai for pembentukan bone rawan, has dikonfirmasi bahwa ia memproduksi kolagen and proteoglikan in jumlah large, komponen main bone rawan sendi, and mampu berdiferensiasi became cell bone rawan matang.

More lanjut, because also mengeluarkan faktor which mempromosikan pembentukan bone, diharapkan berguna in perbaikan cacat bone. With kata lain, MSC also can memainkan peran help perbaikan tissue sendi which rusak.

Seperti which dijelaskan in atas, MSC membenarkan kelainan imun (pendekatan for penyebab), menekan peradangan, and meregenerasi tissue (pendekatan for result) through kerja multi-faset this, work secara komprehensif on patologi artritis reumatoid that sendiri.

Because karakteristik special this, diharapkan sebagai open jalan menuju "treatment akar from artritis reumatoid" which not can direalisasikan oleh care traditional.

POINT

  • MSC menyesuaikan reaksi imun which berlebihan and menekan kerusakan sendi oleh autoimmune
  • Mengeluarkan zat anti-inflammation and meredam peradangan in sendi from akar
  • Mengeluarkan faktor which help regeneration bone rawan and bone, berkontribusi on perbaikan tissue
  • Karakteristiknya is work on penyebab (kelainan imun) and result (kerusakan tissue)

Keamanan Stem cell therapy: Risk Efek Samping which Worry?

Stem cell therapy has dievaluasi sebagai memiliki safety high deep research sampai now, and hampir tidak ada laporan tentang efek samping that serius.

For Stem cell therapy (therapy MSC) terhadap artritis reumatik, in berbagai uji clinical which performed in in and luar negeri, there is hampir not there is report about efek samping serius, and dianggap sebagai method treatment with safety high.

Bahkan in meta-analisis which mengintegrasikan and menganalisis research berganda in seluruh world, not there is perbedaan significant in tingkat insiden acara berbahaya antara group perlakuan MSC and group kontrol, and disimpulkan bahwa risk not increased secara special dibandingkan with treatment traditional.

More lanjut, bahkan in uji clinical in which 800 juta MSC, jauh more many from biasanya, disuntikkan sekaligus, not there is efek samping which serius, show possibility penggunaan safe in rentang dosis which very spacious.

Reason MSC Dianggap Aman

Keamanan high MSC berasal from karakteristik biologisnya. MSC have ekspresi antigen HLA (antigen leukosit human) low on permukaan cell, jadi bahkan if cell which berasal from orang lain, in in tubuh, mudah dikenali oleh system imun and reaksi penolakan small kemungkinannya happen.

Through karakteristik this, cell MSC which provided oleh donor healthy, not hanya cell patient that sendiri, also can applied with safe for treatment.

Also, not seperti stem cell embrionik (cell ES) which berproliferasi secara abnormal and membentuk tumor (teratoma), not there is risk pembentukan tumor, which also merupakan advantage large MSC. Bahkan in eksperimen hewan in past, not there is pembentukan tumor or toksisitas serius which dikonfirmasi bahkan when many MSC ditransplantasikan in jangka long.

Of course, Stem cell therapy memerlukan langkah-langkah safety which ketat seperti kontrol quality and pengujian infeksi sebagai prasyarat, tetapi setidaknya seperti sifat cell that sendiri, safety high equipped, which merupakan kekuatan large for therapy regenerative.
