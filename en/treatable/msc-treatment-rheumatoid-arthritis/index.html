Rheumatoid arthritis is an autoimmune disease in which the body's immune system attacks the joints.

With advancements in pharmacological therapies in recent years, many patients can achieve symptom control or remission; however, it remains challenging to cure the disease itself, and it is impossible to restore damaged joints to their original state.

Furthermore, the current challenge arises from the fact that immunosuppressive treatments carry the risk of side effects, and some patients do not achieve sufficient efficacy from existing therapies. Amidst this situation, Stem Cell Therapy is attracting attention as a novel regenerative therapy.

Stem cell therapy is an unprecedented approach that not only normalizes an out-of-control immune balance but also promotes the regeneration of damaged tissue, bringing new hope for addressing rheumatoid arthritis.

Rheumatoid Arthritis: What Is It? Current Treatment Methods and Remaining Challenges

Artritis reumatoid adalah penyakit yang menyebabkan kerusakan sendi dan gangguan fungsi jika berkembang, dan penyembuhan sulit dengan perawatan yang ada, sehingga metode pengobatan baru diperlukan.

Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) is an autoimmune disease characterized by chronic inflammation of the joints. In Japan, approximately 1% of the population (1 in 100 individuals) is estimated to be affected by this condition, with a notably higher prevalence among women.

The primary symptoms are joint pain, swelling, and stiffness, with characteristic morning stiffness typically occurring upon waking. Should inflammation persist, it will lead to the degradation of cartilage and bone, resulting in joint deformity and restricted range of motion.

Should it progress, the digits may remain bent and unable to be straightened, or the joints may deform, thereby interfering with daily activities.

This functional impairment resulting from joint damage significantly diminishes patients' quality of life (QOL), and in severe cases, can render employment difficult or necessitate care.

Current Principal Pharmacological Therapy and Clinical Outcomes

To date, for the treatment of rheumatoid arthritis, anti-inflammatory drugs (pain relievers), methotrexate (MTX), conventional anti-rheumatic drugs, and biological agents (e.g., anti-TNFα antibodies, IL-6 receptor inhibitors, and others) have been employed.

Since the 1990s, with the advent of MTX and biological agents, treatment outcomes have dramatically improved, and rheumatoid arthritis has transformed from an "incurable disease" into a "treatable condition aiming for remission."

For example, in a 1-year clinical trial administering the biological agent infliximab, approximately 38% of patients achieved remission or low disease activity, and 58% of patients were able to halt the progression of joint damage.

The timely administration of appropriate pharmacological treatment now enables many patients to achieve significant symptomatic improvement.

Unresolved Challenges

Conversely, currently available therapeutic agents have not achieved a complete cure. While these medications can suppress inflammation, it is challenging to fundamentally eradicate the underlying autoimmune cause itself, and no therapeutic methods exist to fully restore already damaged joints to their original state.

Although the progression of joint damage itself can be halted, damaged cartilage and bone will not naturally regenerate. Consequently, in patients who have suffered from this condition for an extended period and have accumulated severe joint damage, functional impairment may still occur, even with the latest pharmacological therapies.

Furthermore, given that current primary pharmacological therapies suppress immune function, there is a risk of side effects such as infection, which complicates the continuation of treatment. Nevertheless, it is reported that 20-40% of patients (with some reports indicating up to 50%) do not achieve sufficient efficacy from existing treatments.

Dengan cara ini, tantangan pengobatan artritis reumatoid yaitu "menghentikan sepenuhnya keberhasilan kekebalan" dan "memperbaiki sendi yang rusak" masih tetap ada, dan pendekatan baru untuk menyelesaikan ini telah diminta.

Item

  • Chronic inflammation occurs in the joints due to an immune disorder.
  • As damage to cartilage and bone progresses, joint function declines and quality of life (QOL) is impaired.
  • Traditional pharmacological therapy may be directed towards symptom remission; however, achieving a cure is challenging.
  • The potential for adverse effects from immunosuppression and the failure to achieve adequate therapeutic outcomes also exist, necessitating new treatment methods.

New Frontiers in Regenerative Therapy: Why Stem Cell Therapy is Gaining Attention

Terapi Sel Punca untuk artritis reumatoid adalah terapi regeneratif generasi berikutnya yang menormalkan kelainan imun sambil menargetkan perbaikan jaringan sendi.

In recent years, Stem Cell Therapy has garnered attention as a revolutionary treatment method for rheumatoid arthritis.

Stem cell therapy is a treatment method that involves taking stem cells from the patient or a donor and administering them into the body, utilizing their regenerative capacity and immunomodulatory power to improve the patient's condition. By gently normalizing immune abnormalities, promoting the repair and regeneration of damaged tissues, and demonstrating therapeutic effects through various mechanisms such as inflammation suppression and paracrine effects (intercellular signal transduction), it is gaining attention as a multi-functional regenerative therapy.

While various types of stem cells exist, mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) have attracted considerable interest for rheumatoid arthritis. MSCs, found in bone marrow, adipose tissue, umbilical cord, and other tissues, can not only differentiate into bone and cartilage but are also endowed with the ability to normalize abnormal immune function.

For this reason, rather than merely suppressing the immune system, gently modulating excessive immune responses is expected to lead to a fundamental improvement in rheumatoid arthritis. Furthermore, as MSCs also promote the repair and regeneration of damaged cartilage and bone, this offers a significant advantage by addressing both the underlying cause of rheumatoid arthritis (immune dysfunction) and its resulting consequences (joint damage).

In light of these features, MSC is recognized as a next-generation therapeutic strategy addressing both 'attack' (regeneration) and 'defense' (adaptation), with global research and application efforts currently underway.

What are the advantages of MSCs derived from Wharton's Jelly?

Mesenchymal Stem Cells (MSCs) are primarily isolated from sources such as adipose tissue, bone marrow, and umbilical cord (Wharton's jelly). Of these sources, bone marrow-derived MSCs (BM-MSCs), which were formerly the primary source, are associated with a substantial patient burden during extraction and yield a limited number of cells. Consequently, they are considered to present numerous challenges.

In contrast, Wharton's Jelly-derived mesenchymal stem cells (WJ-MSCs), naturally acquired at birth, are highly valued due to their non-invasive acquisition method, which carries no pain or risk, and their ready ethical acceptance as a cell source.

Furthermore, it has been demonstrated that umbilical cord-derived MSCs possess immunomodulatory capabilities and differentiation capacity comparable to those derived from bone marrow. This, coupled with the fact that the cells themselves are inherently young and exhibit superior proliferative capacity, offers the advantage of readily and stably supplying high-quality cells.

Given these characteristics, umbilical cord-derived MSCs have garnered attention as a promising option for applications in immune and inflammatory diseases, including rheumatoid arthritis.

How Does Stem Cell Therapy Work in Rheumatoid Arthritis? ~ 3 Mechanisms of Action ~

Sel punca (MSC) bekerja secara multi-faset pada artritis reumatoid melalui tiga tindakan: penyesuaian imun, penekanan peradangan, dan perbaikan sendi.

It is believed that Mesenchymal Stem Cells (MSCs) exert effects on rheumatoid arthritis through three mechanisms of action: normalizing dysregulated autoimmune responses, attenuating joint inflammation through potent anti-inflammatory action, and promoting the repair of damaged cartilage and bone.

Unlike traditional pharmaceutical agents that focus on the temporary suppression of inflammation, MSC modulates the immune system itself, calming inflammation at its source and furthermore promoting tissue regeneration. This represents a significant breakthrough in this field.

Normalisasi Keseimbangan Imun
─ Rem pada Reaksi Imun yang Meliar

In rheumatoid arthritis, the body's normally protective immune system malfunctions and attacks the patient's own joints. MSCs act to modulate this pathological immune response and restore immune homeostasis.

Specifically, it is known that this suppresses the overactivity of pro-inflammatory T-cells (e.g., Th17 and Th1 cells) and enhances the function of immunosuppressive regulatory T-cells (Treg cells).

Furthermore, it also acts on antibody-producing B cells and is reported to reduce the production of "autoantibodies" implicated in rheumatoid arthritis.

Dengan cara ini, MSC berfungsi sebagai "penyesuai" yang menenangkan sistem imun dalam keadaan keberhasilan dan mengembalikannya ke kondisi normal. Terhadap mobil yang berpacu dengan rem yang rusak, MSC mengerem dan mengembalikan kecepatannya──itulah imajnya.

Aksi Anti-Inflamasi yang Kuat
─ Penenangan Peradangan oleh Sitokin

MSCs also possess anti-inflammatory actions, achieved through the secretion of effective substances such as cytokines and growth factors. These secretions are collectively termed the "secretome" and directly modulate hyperactive immune responses within the joints.

For example, interleukin 10 (IL-10), prostaglandin E2 (PGE2), TGF-β, and other factors secreted by MSCs suppress the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines and disrupt the self-perpetuating inflammatory cascade within the joint.

This type of work is called "paracrine effect (paracrine secretion)" and is one of the important mechanisms of MSC therapy.

Sederhananya, MSC memainkan peran "agen pemadam kebakaran" menenangkan api peradangan, dan melalui ini, envisaged adalah pengurangan nyeri dan pembengkakan sendi.

Promosi Perbaikan Jaringan
─ Membantu Regenerasi Tulang Rawan dan Tulang yang Rusak

MSCs possess the ability to promote the repair and regeneration of damaged joint tissue. As needed, MSCs themselves can differentiate into chondrocytes or osteoblasts, or secrete growth factors that aid in the regeneration of surrounding cells, thereby supporting the structural recovery of the joint.

In rheumatoid arthritis, cartilage and bone are destroyed by inflammation, but MSCs have the potential to aid in their regeneration.

In research, umbilical cord-derived MSCs cultured under chondrogenic conditions have been confirmed to produce significant amounts of collagen and proteoglycans, key components of articular cartilage, and to differentiate into mature chondrocytes.

Lebih lanjut, karena juga mengeluarkan faktor yang mempromosikan pembentukan tulang, diharapkan berguna dalam perbaikan cacat tulang. Dengan kata lain, MSC juga dapat memainkan peran membantu perbaikan jaringan sendi yang rusak.

As described above, MSCs modulate immune dysregulation (addressing the underlying cause), suppress inflammation, and regenerate tissue (addressing the outcome) through these multi-faceted actions, thereby comprehensively addressing the pathology of rheumatoid arthritis itself.

Due to these specific characteristics, it is expected to pave the way for a root cause treatment for rheumatoid arthritis, which traditional therapies have been unable to achieve.

Item

  • MSC modulates overactive immune responses and suppresses autoimmune-mediated joint damage.
  • Releasing anti-inflammatory compounds and suppressing joint inflammation at its source.
  • Releasing factors that aid cartilage and bone regeneration, contributing to tissue repair.
  • It is characterized by addressing both the cause (immune disorders) and the outcome (tissue damage).

Clinical Trials in Refractory RA Demonstrate Expected Treatment Effects of MSC Therapy

Bahkan pada artritis reumatoid yang sulit diobati dengan obat tradisional, Terapi Sel Punca (MSC) aman dan peningkatan gejala dan penekanan peradangan diharapkan.

Even in patients with rheumatoid arthritis (RA) refractory to previous treatments, Mesenchymal Stem Cell (MSC) Therapy has been reported to demonstrate a high safety profile and promising effects in improving symptoms and suppressing inflammation, in both domestic and international clinical trials.

Even in cases where conventional treatment proved insufficient, joint swelling and pain have been reduced through MSC treatment, and disease progression has been suppressed, with confirmed instances.

Placebo-Controlled MSC Trial

A confirmatory trial was conducted to verify the therapeutic effects of MSCs in 53 patients with rheumatoid arthritis refractory to conventional drug therapy. In the group that received MSCs, inflammation and joint pain were reduced, and no specific adverse events were reported.

At 3 months post-treatment, the proportion of individuals with significantly reduced disease activity was also high. This was highly regarded as an early-stage verification clinical trial to confirm the presence or absence of an effect.

Large-Scale MSC Clinical Trials

In a large-scale clinical trial involving 172 participants, joint condition and inflammatory markers (CRP, erythrocyte sedimentation rate, etc.) improved, and no safety concerns were identified.

Notably, instances have been reported where favorable conditions were sustained for 8 months to a maximum of 3 years with a single injection, and sustained effects are anticipated.

Early-Phase Clinical Trial of Mesenchymal Stem Cell Treatment in Rheumatic Patients

In a clinical trial administering MSC treatment to patients with early-stage rheumatoid arthritis, safety was confirmed, and a trend towards suppressing disease progression was also observed.

By introducing treatment at a stage before joint destruction, its preventive effects are also gaining attention.

This clinical trial demonstrated that MSC-based Stem Cell Therapy offers a promising new avenue for rheumatoid arthritis refractory to conventional treatments.

With reduced joint inflammation, alleviated pain, or halted disease progression, it is reported that patients can lead a more comfortable daily life. The fact that virtually no side effects are observed also provides significant reassurance for many patients.

Future research will investigate the optimal recipients and timing for injections, with the aim of developing a more accessible, safe, and practical treatment method for a broader population.

Item

  • MSC treatment demonstrates specific efficacy in rheumatoid arthritis patients who are refractory to conventional drugs.
  • In placebo-controlled trials and large-scale studies, increases in joint inflammation, pain, and disease activity have been reported.
  • Early disease management also aims to suppress disease progression and prevent joint damage.

Stem Cell Therapy Safety: Concerning Risks of Adverse Events?

Terapi Sel Punca telah dievaluasi sebagai memiliki keselamatan tinggi dalam penelitian sampai saat ini, dan hampir tidak ada laporan tentang efek samping yang serius.

Regarding Stem Cell Therapy (MSC therapy) for rheumatoid arthritis, various clinical trials conducted both domestically and internationally have reported virtually no serious adverse events. Thus, it is considered a therapeutic approach with a high safety profile.

Even in meta-analyses integrating and analyzing numerous global studies, there was no significant difference in the incidence rate of adverse events between the MSC treatment group and the control group, and it was concluded that the risk was not specifically elevated compared to traditional treatments.

Furthermore, even in clinical trials where 800 million MSCs, significantly exceeding typical doses, were administered in a single injection, no serious adverse events were observed, indicating the potential for safe use across an exceptionally broad dosage range.

Reasons MSC is Considered Safe

Keamanan tinggi MSC berasal dari karakteristik biologisnya. MSC memiliki ekspresi antigen HLA (antigen leukosit manusia) rendah pada permukaan sel, jadi bahkan jika sel yang berasal dari orang lain, di dalam tubuh, mudah dikenali oleh sistem imun dan reaksi penolakan kecil kemungkinannya terjadi.

These characteristics enable the safe therapeutic application of MSCs derived from healthy donors, extending beyond the patient's own autologous cells.

Additionally, unlike embryonic stem cells (ES cells) that exhibit abnormal proliferation and teratoma formation, MSCs pose no risk of tumor development, which is a major advantage. Moreover, previous animal experiments have confirmed the absence of tumor formation or serious toxicity, even when a large quantity of MSCs were transplanted long-term.

Indeed, stem cell therapy necessitates stringent safety measures, including quality control and infection testing, as prerequisites. However, the inherent nature of the cells themselves confers a high level of safety, which constitutes a major advantage for regenerative therapy.

"Ini adalah pengobatan baru jadi saya khawatir" juga ada suara seperti itu, tetapi dari pengetahuan sejauh ini, terapi MSC terhadap artritis reumatik juga merupakan pilihan pengobatan yang dapat dipercaya dengan cukup dalam hal keselamatan dapat dikatakan.

Item

  • There have been almost no reports of serious adverse events associated with MSC treatment for rheumatoid arthritis in clinical trials.
  • In placebo-controlled comparative trials, there were also no safety differences, and adverse reactions were limited to minor events.
  • In a meta-analysis, compared to conventional treatment, specific risks were not identified.
  • MSC have low HLA antigen expression, and rejection reactions are less likely, so they can be injected without immunosuppressive agents.
  • There is no risk of tumor formation, a key characteristic of which is the high intrinsic safety of the cells.

Moving forward, it is anticipated that Stem Cell Therapy will become a more accessible and practical treatment, significantly enhancing the quality of daily life for most patients afflicted with rheumatoid arthritis.
