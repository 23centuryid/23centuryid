Stem cell therapy untuk Diabetes

  • HOME

  • Stem cell therapy untuk Diabetes

Diabetes is disease which characterized with peningkatan kadar gula blood secara kronis, dibagi became tipe 1 and tipe 2.

On tipe 1, gangguan autoimmune menghancurkan cell-cell which memproduksi insulin, so that memerlukan suntikan insulin seumur hidup. On tipe 2, resistansi insulin increased and production menurun, which can memerlukan therapy insulin when disease developed.

In kedua kasus, treatment berfokus especially on kontrol gula blood, and sulit for sepenuhnya mencegah komplikasi jangka long. Face challenge this, in beberapa years terakhir, therapy regenerative which bertujuan for "perbaikan mendasar" has get perhatian.

Terlebih lagi, stem cell therapy (therapy MSC) which bertujuan for recovery sekresi insulin and penyesuaian lingkungan imun secara bersamaan diharapkan sebagai pilihan treatment new which menjanjikan.

Stem cell therapy sebagai Therapy Regenerative

Stem cell therapy is metode therapy new that bertujuan untuk perbaikan mendasar diabetes with memulihkan fungsi cell that memproduksi insulin and mengeluarkan kekuatan healing tubuh.

Treatment diabetes konvensional selama this berfokus on pendekatan "manajemen" for control kadar gula blood. However, seiring meningkatnya kebutuhan will "therapy fundamental" which mengembalikan fungsi pankreas that sendiri, therapy regenerative semakin get perhatian.

Therapy regenerative is bidang medical which improve and meregenerasi fungsi organ or tissue which rusak use kekuatan tubuh sendiri. In diabetes, berbagai technique regenerative sedang diteliti with tujuan for mengembalikan fungsi cell-β pankreas which menghasilkan insulin.

In antara all pilihan, therapy use stem cell show result which most menjanjikan. Stem cell have ability for berubah became cell lain (diferensiasi) or melepaskan zat-zat which diperlukan, and can dianggap sebagai dasar cell for "memelihara ulang" tissue.

Which became fokus special is cell which disebut "Mesenchymal stem cell (MSC)". MSC can diekstraksi from tissue seperti sumsum bone, lemak, and tali pusar, and have karakteristik have daya proliferasi which high as well as ability berubah became berbagai jenis cell (pluripotensial).

In bidang diabetes, MSC diharapkan have dua karakteristik berikut:

  • Kemungkinan untuk berdiferensiasi become cell-β and mengisi kekurangan sekresi insulin
  • Mengeluarkan sitokin and faktor growth untuk mempersiapkan lingkungan tissue sekitarnya and membangkitkan kekuatan pemulihan diri

Especially, MSC Wharton's jelly which berasal from tali pusar have keunggulan large in hal ability proliferasi which unggul and penolakan imun which rarely happen. Because diekstraksi from tali pusar bayi new lahir, cell tersebut masih young and dilaporkan rarely memicu reaksi penolakan genetik.

Selain that, therapy use cell patient sendiri mungkin memerlukan surgery ekstraksi, however MSC Wharton's jelly which berasal from pendonor can used with safe, so that can secara significant reduce beban fisik on tubuh.

With cara this, MSC not hanya diharapkan sebagai therapy "pengganti", tetapi sebagai therapy which "membangunkan kekuatan regenerative" tubuh, and semakin disorot sebagai pilihan new for diabetes.

POINT

  • In therapy regenerative for diabetes, recovery fungsi cell-β pulau pankreas which menghasilkan insulin is tujuannya
  • Mesenchymal stem cell (MSC) diharapkan have efek regenerative from kedua aspek pluripotensialitas and ability sekresi
  • MSC have dua fungsi yaitu melengkapi cell-β and menyesuaikan lingkungan tissue
  • MSC Wharton's jelly which berasal from tali pusar young, rarely experience penolakan imun, and safe used bahkan if berasal from pendonor
  • Fakta bahwa can given without surgery and with cara which less invasif also merupakan method therapy with beban minimal bagi patient

Mekanisme Aksi Multifaset which Dibawa oleh MSC

MSC is pendekatan terapi that can bertindak secara bersamaan from berbagai sudut pandang terhadap berbagai challenge deep diabetes including kelainan imun, kerusakan fungsi pankreas, and peradangan kronis.

Effectiveness therapy MSC not hanya terbatas on penggantian cell semata. Start from tingkat local pankreas hingga lingkungan metabolik seluruh tubuh, "penyesuaian, recovery, and aktivasi" can performed on tingkat which spacious, and true-true memainkan peran seperti "multi-function switch" in treatment diabetes.

In here, we will explain tiga tindakan representatif which dibawa oleh MSC.

Regulasi Imun (Penekan Autoimun)

In diabetes tipe 1, cell-β pankreas dihancurkan oleh autoimmune. MSC mengeluarkan sitokin anti-inflammation seperti IL-10 and TGF-β, which have efek for menekan aktivasi imun which berlebihan with soft.

Especially, has dikonfirmasi bahwa hal this enhance cell-T regulatori (Treg) and menekan production cell-T inflammation (system Th1/Th17) and IFN-γ, and diharapkan can calm lingkungan imun which very agresif in sekitar pulau pankreas.

Fungsi this seperti "system kontrol which mengerem machine kekebalan which hampir ketinggalan".

Promosi Perbaikan and Regenerasi Tissue

MSC mengeluarkan faktor growth seperti VEGF and HGF, which enhance perbaikan tissue which rusak and angiogenesis. In pankreas, selain help kelangsungan hidup and pemeliharaan fungsi cell-β which tersisa, also there is report which show possibility mempromosikan regeneration cell-β new which mensekresi insulin.

In eksperimen, also dilaporkan contoh MSC berdiferensiasi became cell-seperti β which have ability sekresi insulin. With kata lain, can dikatakan bahwa seperti mengirim team perbaikan to factory cell which rusak and support surgery ulang.

Penekan Peradangan and Perbaikan Lingkungan Metabolik

In diabetes tipe 2, peradangan kronis mendasari resistensi insulin. Sitokin anti-inflammation which disekresi MSC (seperti IL-10) menekan production sitokin inflammation which berlebihan seperti IL-6 and IL-1β.

Selain that, with mengubah komposisi cell imun from tipe which menginduksi inflammation became tipe penghambat, lingkungan metabolik seluruh tubuh berubah became "keadaan in which insulin work with good". Mekanisme this seperti "pekerjaan perbaikan jalur komunikasi" terhadap keadaan in which sinyal metabolik seluruh tubuh not tersampaikan with good.

Sebagaimana dijelaskan in atas, therapy MSC not hanya work on "location" which disebut pankreas, tetapi also work on seluruh system tubuh including imun, peradangan, and metabolisme. Ability for menyesuaikan beberapa sirkuit secara bersamaan with cara this merupakan keunggulan large stem cell therapy which not dimiliki oleh therapy lain.

POINT

  • MSC have aktivitas for menekan reaksi autoimmune which became problem in diabetes tipe 1
  • Mendorong perlindungan and regeneration cell-β pankreas which rusak, berkontribusi on recovery fungsi sekresi insulin
  • With menekan peradangan kronis and enhance resistensi insulin, also work on lingkungan metabolik diabetes tipe 2
  • Titik strong MSC is can perform pendekatan simultan terhadap imun, pankreas, and metabolisme on tingkat which different

Efek Stem cell therapy According Research Clinical

Efek pada Diabetes Tipe 1

MSC can menyebabkan peningkatan fungsi sekresi insulin and pengurangan suntikan insulin, with hasil that positif especially dilaporkan pada pasien deep fase awal disease.

In uji clinical therapy MSC for diabetes tipe 1, berbagai research has mengkonfirmasi peningkatan kontrol gula blood and penurunan penggunaan insulin. Especially, there is many contoh in which peningkatan Cpeptida (marker which show is insulin diproduksi in tubuh) and peningkatan HbA1c diamati, and also has show possibility bahwa suntikan insulin became not perlu.

Actually, in satu research, dilaporkan bahwa sekitar 20% from patient which receive transplantasi MSC can stop menyuntik insulin, and many patient lain also experience pengurangan jumlah which dibutuhkan secara significant.

There is also report bahwa effectiveness different tergantung on jenis stem cell. Dilaporkan bahwa MSC which berasal from tali pusar (Wharton's jelly MSC) mungkin have peningkatan HbA1c which more large and peningkatan Cpeptida dibandingkan with which berasal from sumsum bone, and more unggul in mempertahankan fungsi cell-β pankreas.

There is also report about effectiveness jangka long, and dilaporkan bahwa sekitar 80% from patient which underwent therapy MSC mempertahankan HbA1c secara stabil selama more from 3 years. Biasanya, kontrol gula blood cenderung memburuk seiring time, jadi this is titik perhatian which large. Penurunan Ceptida also more slow, and diharapkan efek mempertahankan fungsi pankreas.

Selain that, analisis which mengevaluasi berbagai uji clinical also menyimpulkan bahwa MSC possibility bermanfaat in reduce penggunaan insulin and menstabilkan kontrol gula blood.

POINT

  • Injeksi MSC has dilaporkan enhance Cpeptida and enhance HbA1c
  • Keefektifan disarankan with sekitar 20% patient which not lagi membutuhkan suntikan insulin
  • MSC which berasal from tali pusar mungkin have effectiveness which more high daripada which berasal from sumsum bone
  • There is also contoh mempertahankan HbA1c secara stabil selama more from 3 years, and efek jangka long get many perhatian
  • Berbagai research clinical which dikombinasikan support pengurangan kebutuhan insulin and peningkatan kontrol gula blood

Efek pada Diabetes Tipe 2

Pada diabetes tipe 2 juga, terapi MSC ditunjukkan untuk memiliki potential deep help stabilisasi gula blood, reduce resistensi insulin and peradangan, serta menekan risk komplikasi.

In research which menargetkan diabetes tipe 2, report about peningkatan manajemen gula blood through injeksi MSC semakin increase. Especially, on patient with disease berat which memerlukan insulin, recovery sekresi insulin and stabilisasi gula blood has dikonfirmasi.

In satu uji clinical, after injeksi MSC which berasal from plasenta, jumlah insulin which dibutuhkan reduced setengahnya, and peningkatan nilai Cpeptida also dikonfirmasi. In uji coba terpisah, use MSC which berasal from tali pusar, 41% from patient can stop menyuntik insulin, and dilaporkan bahwa 94% experience pengurangan penggunaan insulin.

Selain that, peningkatan also terlihat on indikator which berkaitan with imun and peradangan. After injeksi MSC, aktivitas cell-T inflammation ditekan and nilai sitokin inflammation seperti IL-6 and IL-1β also menurun. Change this diyakini berkontribusi on peningkatan resistensi insulin.

Selanjutnya, also dilaporkan adanya efek penekanan risk development komplikasi. In research MSC which berasal from tali pusar, 30% from patient not lagi memerlukan treatment insulin selama more from 1 years after therapy, and dilaporkan bahwa komplikasi seperti nefropati and neuropati also more few.

Because impact positif not hanya terhadap gula blood tetapi also terhadap keseluruhan keadaan metabolik tubuh and quality of life, therapy MSC interesting perhatian sebagai pilihan therapy new for diabetes.

POINT

  • In diabetes tipe 2 which parah, there is contoh pengurangan penggunaan insulin which significant
  • Peningkatan Cpeptida and HbA1c dilaporkan and efek positif on manajemen gula blood shown
  • Penekanan peradangan and peningkatan resistensi insulin has dikonfirmasi
  • Mitigasi risk komplikasi and stabilisasi gula blood jangka long also diharapkan
  • With work in tiga arah pankreas, imun, and metabolisme, this interesting perhatian sebagai therapy multifungsi

Keamanan and Risk Efek Samping Stem cell therapy

Terapi MSC is metode therapy with keamanan high that has dikonfirmasi untuk diabetes. Laporan tentang efek samping that parah sangat jarang, and because beban pada tubuh ringan, semakin get perhatian.

When memperkenalkan method therapy new, keamanannya is item pertimbangan which most important. In many uji clinical which has performed, in care use mesenchymal stem cell (MSC), hampir not there is report about efek samping which parah or komplikasi. Research domestic and international has mengklarifikasi bahwa injeksi MSC on patient diabetes show keamanan which high.

In antara which most important, MSC which berasal from tali pusar (Wharton's jelly MSC) which used in facility we also belum mengkonfirmasi peristiwa which merugikan in contoh penggunaan clinical aktual, which memvalidasi keamanannya which high. Selain that, meta-analisis new-new this also mengevaluasi therapy MSC for diabetes tipe 1 and tipe 2 sebagai method treatment which achieve keefektifan and keamanan.

In belakang keamanan this is rendahnya immunogenisitas MSC. Biasanya, when cell which berasal from orang lain dimasukkan to in tubuh, reaksi penolakan imun happen, tetapi MSC have sifat bahwa ekspresi HLA (antigen leukosit human) on permukaan cell low and sulit dikenali oleh system imun. Because reason this, bahkan with cell which berasal from pendonor, injeksi can performed with safe without use agen imunosupresi special.

Selanjutnya, therapy MSC also diapresiasi because beban on tubuh low. Treatment this performed especially through infus, and also can performed injeksi local, without disertai invasi seperti surgery umum. In many kasus, procedure can diselesaikan in clinic rawat jalan, reduce beban on patient sebagai care which not memerlukan rawat inap.

From sudut pandang this, stem cell therapy is therapy advanced for diabetes while mempertahankan karakteristik efek samping which rarely and sifat invasi low, layak for dipertimbangkan with cukup.

POINT

  • In therapy MSC, report about efek samping serius or peristiwa merugikan very rarely
  • MSC which berasal from tali pusar have catatan especially unggul in hal keamanan and stabilitas
  • MSC have immunogenisitas low and sulit experience penolakan, so that can disuntikkan without agen imunosupresi
  • Method pemberian through infus is care invasif low, interesting perhatian sebagai therapy which not memerlukan rawat inap
  • Diharapkan ekspansi pengenalan in future sebagai pilihan treatment which menggabungkan keamanan and kepraktisan

Future Treatment Diabetes

Stem cell therapy is therapy regenerative which bertujuan for perbaikan fundamental diabetes with secara bersamaan mempromosikan regeneration cell pulau pankreas and normalisasi imun.

Research domestic and international has reported result which positif in kedua aspek keamanan and keefektifan, and movement menuju implementasi praktis in future also developed secara stabil. Treatment which menstabilkan gula blood without bergantung on insulin, and care medical which mencegah development komplikasi secara progresif, became kenyataan through stem cell semakin dekat.

Because that is disease jangka long, pilihan new which bertujuan for perbaikan fundamental will bring hope.
