Disease Parkinson - Condition Time This and Challenge

Disease Parkinson is disease neurodegeneratif progresif in which neuron penghasil dopamin in brain secara bertahap disappear. Dilaporkan bahwa sekitar 1% from lansia aged 65 years to atas mengidap disease this.

Symptom utamanya meliputi gangguan movement seperti tremor (getaran), kekakuan muscle (rigiditas), and lambatnya movement (bradikinesia). Seiring perkembangannya, symptom non-motorik also appear seperti depresi, kecemasan, gangguan kognitif, and disfungsi otonom, which impact large on quality of life.

Treatment time this berfokus on therapy farmakologis which mengganti dopamin and therapy stimulasi brain in (DBS). Although this provide efek peredaman symptom sementara, effectiveness drug menurun seiring time, and efek samping as well as challenge new appear.

Selain that, not there is treatment which can menghentikan degenerasi neuro that sendiri, and symptom non-motorik also not get benefit which cukup. There is kebutuhan mendesak for pendekatan treatment new which not hanya menekan symptom tetapi also overcome development disease that sendiri.

Pendekatan New in Treatment Disease Parkinson through Therapy Regenerative

Melengkapi and fix cell nerve that disappear deep disease Parkinson with kekuatan stem cell—"therapy that before mustahil untuk meregenerasi nerve itu sendiri" has mengambil langkah big menuju kenyataan through terapi regenerative.

Pendekatan Intervensi in Tingkat Cell

On disease Parkinson, neuron penghasil dopamin in area substansia nigra in brain experience degenerasi and disappear. Hilangnya cell this is penyebab mendasar which memicu gangguan movement and berbagai symptom non-motorik.

Treatment traditional hanya meredakan symptom sementara through drug dopamin (seperti levodopa) and not can mengembalikan cell nerve which disappear.

Sebagai respons terhadap challenge this, "therapy regenerative" use stem cell which has get perhatian large in beberapa years terakhir.

Progress Stem cell therapy pada Disease Parkinson

On years 1980-an, percobaan performed for mentransplantasikan cell nerve penghasil dopamin which berasal from janin brain to patient. Although beberapa efek dilaporkan,

  • Problem etika (kekhawatiran sosial about penggunaan tissue janin)
  • Tingkat kelangsungan hidup cell transplantasi which low
  • Penolakan imun

There is problem serius seperti this, and not pernah diimplementasikan secara spacious.

Time this, research developed to arah use sumber cell which more safe and stabil, and mesenchymal stem cell (MSC), stem cell induksi pluripoten (iPSC), and stem cell nerve became perhatian.

Hope pada Stem cell Mesenkimal (MSC)

MSC is stem cell which can diekstraksi with relatif mudah from sumsum bone, tissue lemak, and tali center, and

  • Have kapasitas proliferasi which high
  • Can diinduksi for berdiferensiasi became cell nerve
  • Mengeluarkan sitokin which melindungi and improve tissue which rusak

have karakteristik seperti this.

Especially in disease Parkinson, possibility melengkapi neuron dopamin which disappear and enhance lingkungan brain that sendiri has shown, and in beberapa years terakhir has became wrong satu pendekatan which most interesting perhatian in bidang therapy regenerative.

POIN IMPORTANT

  • Hilangnya neuron dopamin is penyebab mendasar disease Parkinson
  • Treatment traditional not can meregenerasi nerve that sendiri
  • Stem cell therapy memungkinkan pendekatan fundamental "penggantian cell nerve which disappear"
  • Mesenchymal stem cell (MSC) is kandidat therapy which strong which menggabungkan ability diferensiasi and efek perlindungan

How Stem cell therapy Work on Disease Parkinson

Stem cell therapy (especially stem cell mesenkimal = MSC) mendekati secara multi-fasia hilangnya cell nerve that terlibat deep development disease Parkinson and deteriorasi lingkungan brain, with tujuan perbaikan fungsi mendasar.

Melengkapi Neuron Dopamin that Disappear

Has shown bahwa MSC have ability for berdiferensiasi became neuron penghasil dopamin (cell nerve dopamin) in condition which sesuai.

In eksperimen, when cell-cell which berasal from MSC ditransplantasikan to brain, they memperpanjang akson and membentuk sinapsis, and trusted berkontribusi on rekonstruksi sirkuit nerve which terputus with mengeluarkan dopamin.

With leverage MSC, not hanya perlindungan nerve, tetapi also possibility mengganti cell nerve which disappear has became jelas.

Melindungi Nerve that Rusak (Efek Neuroproteksi)

MSC, also disebut "factory cell hidup", mengeluarkan many sitokin and faktor growth which bermanfaat to sekitarnya (for example: BDNF, NGF, IGF-1, dll). With cara this,

  • Support kelangsungan hidup cell nerve which rusak
  • Mempromosikan perbaikan and regeneration nerve
  • Calm peradangan nerve

Diharapkan show efek neuroproteksi seperti this and menghentikan development disease Parkinson. In eksperimen hewan with use MSC which berasal from tali center, peningkatan IGF-1 intra-serebral also diikuti oleh peningkatan fungsi motorik and gangguan memori.

Menyesuaikan Lingkungan Brain Itu Sendiri

Eksosom which dikeluarkan oleh MSC mengandung zat which have promosi angiogenesis, aksi antioksidan, and aksi penghambatan kematian cell. Zat-zat this melintasi sawar blood-brain for achieve brain,

  • Pembentukan kapiler new
  • Regeneration lingkungan mikro (niche) in sekitar cell nerve
  • Penghambatan kematian cell neuron dopamin and recovery konsentrasi dopamin

Shown in eksperimen hewan. Stem cell therapy not hanya "penggantian cell", tetapi also pendekatan for "mengolah returned" brain sebagai ladang.

POIN IMPORTANT

  • MSC berdiferensiasi became neuron penghasil dopamin and merekonstruksi tissue nerve which disappear
  • Mengeluarkan faktor growth berlimpah and mempromosikan perlindungan and perbaikan cell nerve
  • Calm peradangan kronis and enhance seluruh lingkungan brain (niche) to keadaan which cocok for regeneration nerve
  • Pendekatan multi-fasia for perbaikan fungsi mendasar disease Parkinson

Perspektif Stem cell therapy in Enhance Symptom Disease Parkinson

Stem cell therapy untuk disease Parkinson has melaporkan many hasil that menjanjikan from eksperimen hewan hingga uji klinis, show perbaikan symptom.

Efek that Ditunjukkan deep Research Praklinik

In tahap research dasar (research praklinik), transplantasi MSC on model hewan disease Parkinson (tikus and mencit) has show many perbaikan in fungsi motorik and temuan patologi.

  • On model which diberi MSC which berasal from tali center, dilaporkan pengurangan gangguan motorik, penekanan peradangan nerve, and peningkatan tingkat kelangsungan hidup neuron dopamin.
  • Transplantasi MSC which berasal from sumsum bone also reduce kematian neuron dopamin in substantia nigra and efek perlindungan terhadap kerusakan cell nerve dikonfirmasi.
  • MSC which berasal from Wharton's Jelly show not hanya recovery symptom motorik tetapi also perbaikan symptom non-motorik seperti gangguan kognitif and hipersensitivitas sense pain dilaporkan.

More lanjut, research reported bahwa transplantasi MSC tali center memulihkan BDNF (Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor) and NGF (Nerve Growth Factor) in brain, and perbaikan in plastisitas sinap hipokampus (fungsi learning and memori) dilaporkan.

Efek that Diharapkan deep Research Klinis

In uji clinical on human also there is result positif although skala penelitiannya more small.

  • In tinjauan sistematis latest, with menganalisis data from 9 uji coba which include 129 patient disease Parkinson, group transplantasi stem cell show perbaikan symptom which significant dibandingkan with group treatment konvensional saja.
  • Skor UPDRS (Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale) down secara significant on group transplantasi, and efeknya dilaporkan bertahan setidaknya selama 1 years.
  • Secara special, MSC tali center diharapkan can provide efek superior because keadaan cell which young and kapasitas diferensiasi which high.

Also, in satu uji coba with transplantasi MSC which berasal from sumsum bone in in brain, with pengamatan hingga 3 years, skor UPDRS increased sekitar 23% on keadaan off-medication and sekitar 38% on keadaan on-medication, and there is report success pengurangan dosis drug oral.

Hope and Challenge in Future

Of course, data clinical time this masih terbatas and jumlah kasus not many. However demikian, have exceed tahap "effectiveness masih not jelas", and diharapkan bukti which more strong will terkumpul from uji clinical which sedang take place in seluruh world.

On time this also, jelas bahwa Stem cell therapy for disease Parkinson has became pilihan treatment new with possibility perbaikan symptom which jelas.

POIN IMPORTANT

  • Research praklinik has mengkonfirmasi efek perlindungan fungsi motorik and nerve.
  • In uji clinical, perbaikan skor UPDRS and tren mitigasi symptom terlihat.
  • MSC tali center especially have many hope because keadaannya which young and kapasitas diferensiasi which high.
  • Diharapkan effectiveness will more mapan through uji coba skala large in future.

Keamanan and Penilaian Risk Stem cell therapy for Disease Parkinson

Stem cell therapy (terapi MSC) has show keamanan high deep research before, and secara bertahap become opsi therapy that can dipilih with percaya diri oleh pasien Parkinson.

Laporan Research tentang Keamanan Terapi MSC

Especially treatment use mesenchymal stem cell (MSC) from donor lain menekan reaksi penolakan imun hingga minimum, and efek samping serius hampir not pernah dilaporkan.

  • In uji clinical which performed on patient Disease Parkinson, after injeksi intravena MSC from donor which different, not there is respons imun spesifik donor (reaksi penolakan) which terdeteksi, and keamanan as well as toleransi high shown.
  • In research pilot with transplantasi MSC autolog to in brain, with pengamatan hingga 36 month most long, not there is pembentukan tumor or efek samping serius which diamati.

In uji coba this, justru change biologis positif diperhatikan after pemberian cell seperti penurunan sitokin inflammation and peningkatan faktor nutrisi nerve (BDNF, IGF-1 dll), as well as peningkatan aliran blood brain.

Efek Samping Umum and Manajemen Risk

Efek samping which dilaporkan in therapy MSC terbatas on sense pain sementara in location infus, demam, and pain kepala ringan. Dibandingkan with treatment standard, treatment this dievaluasi sebagai treatment which very safe.

Especially, MSC which berasal from Wharton's Jelly is from bayi new lahir, with tingkat inisialisasi cell which high, and dianggap have risk kerusakan genetik and penuaan which low.

Diperlukan manajemen quality which komprehensif and technique professional seperti this. Time this, performed with safe in facility which memenuhi requirement this, and diharapkan optimasi more lanjut will performed in future.
