Stem Cell Therapy for Sensorineural Hearing Loss

  • Home

  • Stem Cell Therapy for Sensorineural Hearing Loss

For patients and families experiencing hearing limitations and concerned about daily conversations and environmental sounds, sensorineural hearing loss presents a significant barrier. This condition occurs when the hair cells in the inner ear, responsible for converting sound vibrations into electrical signals or auditory nerve impulses that transmit sound information to the brain, are damaged.

Once hair cells and auditory nerves are damaged, modern medicine is currently unable to naturally regenerate them. Consequently, achieving full hearing restoration through conventional treatment methods is exceedingly difficult, and palliative therapy, relying on hearing aids or cochlear implants, becomes the primary focus of treatment.

However, hearing aids are merely devices that amplify sound; they do not restore the function of damaged cells themselves. Cochlear implants are also devices that convert sound into electrical signals and transmit them, but they have limitations in the fidelity of the original sound and auditory discrimination capabilities.

With advancements in regenerative medicine, new approaches to repairing damaged inner ear cells are gaining attention. Specifically, stem cell-based treatments aim to regenerate inner ear hair cells and auditory nerves, with research ongoing worldwide.

Why Is Sensorineural Hearing Loss Considered "Incurable"?

Gangguan pendengaran sensorineural terjadi ketika sel telinga dalam yang merasakan suara atau saraf rusak. Sel-sel ini tidak meregenerasi secara alami, dan bahkan alat bantu dengar atau implan koklea tidak dapat sepenuhnya mengkompensasi fungsi yang hilang.

Auditory cells, once damaged, do not regenerate.

Sound reaches the ear as air vibrations and is transmitted from the eardrum to the inner ear (otocochlea) further inward. There, a crucial role is played by specialized sensory cells called "hair cells."

These hair cells are responsible for converting sound vibrations into electrical signals and transmitting them to the brain via the auditory nerve. In other words, they function as a transducer, transforming auditory input into signals that the brain can interpret.

To illustrate this function by analogy, hair cells are a "precision microphone within the ear."

However, human hair cells possess the characteristic that once damaged, they do not naturally regenerate. When injured due to advancing age, loud noise exposure, or ototoxic medications such as anticancer agents, the damage is irreversible, leading to gradual hearing loss.

Hearing aids and cochlear implants cannot "restore original sound."

In current medical practice, sensorineural hearing loss is primarily managed with devices such as hearing aids and cochlear implants.

Hearing aids are devices that amplify ambient sounds and transmit them to the ear. However, even if sound is amplified, if the underlying hair cells are damaged, sound cannot be accurately converted. This is analogous to placing a speaker in front of a broken microphone – the recording quality will not improve.

A cochlear implant is a medical device that attaches electrodes to the inner ear and transmits sound signals directly to the auditory nerve as electrical stimulation. While effective for severe hearing loss, there are limitations in distinguishing subtle differences in sound and understanding speech in noisy environments.

Furthermore, surgical intervention is necessary, which entails considerations of cost and physical burden. Given these circumstances, traditional medicine is considered unlikely to "restore lost hearing."

The concept of repairing the cells themselves

Amidst this situation, attention is directed towards efforts to restore the inner ear cells themselves through regenerative medicine.

Research is particularly advancing in a technique known as stem cell therapy. Stem cells are cells with the capacity to differentiate into various cell types within the body, serving as the foundational elements for regeneration that repair damaged tissues.

For sensorineural hearing loss, the objective is to deliver stem cells to the inner ear to stimulate the regeneration of damaged hair cells and auditory nerves.

The very idea of regenerative medicine was once considered a distant dream, but recent research is bringing these possibilities closer to reality. For those who feel limited by traditional medical treatments, Stem Cell Therapy offers a promising alternative, opening up new avenues of possibility.

Item

  • Hair cells, which convert sound vibrations into signals, are key to sensorineural hearing loss.
  • These cells do not naturally regenerate when damaged, nor can hearing aids or cochlear implants fully restore function.
  • Stem Cell Therapy is an innovative approach that seeks to regenerate lost cells.

Stem Cells for Sensorineural Hearing Loss and Therapeutic Options

Dalam kedokteran regeneratif untuk gangguan pendengaran sensorineural, sel punca mesenchymal (MSC) dianggap sebagai pilihan yang menjanjikan, dan ada dua pendekatan untuk pengobatan: transplantasi sel punca dan terapi sekretosom.

The type of stem cell used

Mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) are central to regenerative medicine for sensorineural hearing loss. MSCs are derived from bone marrow, adipose tissue, and particularly from the umbilical cord.

Umbilical cord-derived stem cells (WJ-MSC) possess the following characteristics:

  • Younger cells with high proliferative and regenerative capacity
  • Minimal burden during extraction and minimal ethical issues.
  • Upon allogeneic transplantation, immune rejection is less likely to occur.

Therefore, WJ-MSCs are applicable to patients unable to provide autologous cells, demonstrating considerable potential for future clinical applications.

Treatment approach

Two methods of Stem Cell Therapy are currently under consideration.

Method of Stem Cell Transplantation into the Body

  • Injeksi intravena:
    Metode pemberian sel punca melalui pembuluh darah dan mencapai telinga dalam melalui sirkulasi tubuh. Beban pada tubuh relatif kecil, dan penelitian klinis sudah sedang berlangsung.
  • Injeksi lokal (injeksi tympanic, dll):
    Metode mengirimkan sel punca ke tempat lebih dekat ke telinga dalam melalui gendang telinga dan telinga tengah. Ini adalah pendekatan yang bertujuan untuk tindakan langsung, tetapi menyeimbangkan invasivitas adalah masalah.
  • Pengenalan bedah:
    Teknik bedah untuk mengirimkan sel punca langsung ke telinga dalam. Masih dalam tahap eksperimental, tetapi dapat diterapkan pada kasus berat.

Secretosome Therapy

It is not the stem cells themselves, but rather only the beneficial substances (such as growth factors, cytokines, etc.) secreted by the stem cells during cultivation that are extracted and administered.

  • Due to the non-transplantation of cells, the safety is high.
  • Given the provision of only components, management is simplified, and the potential for adverse effects is minimized.

Animal study results have reported that secretosome application enhances protective and reparative effects on inner ear tissue, with future clinical application anticipated.

Item

  • Mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) are primarily used for sensorineural hearing loss.
  • Umbilical cord-derived WJ-MSC are highly promising due to their youthful state and immunocompatibility.
  • Treatment methods include stem cell transplantation and the use of secretosomes (secretory components).

How Does Stem Cell Therapy Work for Hearing?

Terapi Sel Punca bekerja pada gangguan pendengaran sensorineural melalui tiga mekanisme: "melengkapi sel", "melindungi sel", dan "mengurangi peradangan" untuk mendukung pemulihan telinga.

Restoring lost hair cells and auditory nerves.

Stem cells are characterized by their differentiation capability, allowing them to transform into various cell types. For sensorineural hearing loss, it is anticipated that this differentiation can restore damaged hair cells and auditory nerves in the inner ear.

For example, in an experiment where mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) from the umbilical cord were treated with specific growth factors (BDNF and NT-3), it was confirmed that they differentiated into cells resembling auditory nerve cells.

Furthermore, when stem cells were transplanted into model mice for age-related hearing loss, hair cell regeneration and hearing improvement have been reported.

The secretosome protects remaining cells and promotes repair.

Stem cells not only generate new cells but also release beneficial components (secretosomes) into the extracellular environment, which additionally serve to protect existing cells. Secretosomes contain,

  • BDNF (Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor)
  • NT-3 (Nerve Growth Factor)
  • IGF-1 (Insulin-like Growth Factor-1)

and other components that facilitate cell survival and repair, particularly supporting damaged hair cells and auditory nerve cells.

In animal studies, when secretosomes were administered to a model of inner ear damage induced by the anticancer drug cisplatin, it has been reported that hearing loss was attenuated and hair cell loss was reduced.

Alleviating inner ear inflammation and creating an environment for recovery.

In some cases of sensorineural hearing loss, inner ear inflammation or autoimmune reactions are implicated. Excessive inflammation can accelerate tissue damage and impede hearing recovery. Stem cells demonstrate immunomodulatory effects on such inflammation.

Specifically,

  • In autoimmune hearing loss models, stem cell administration reduces aggressive immune cells (autoreactive T cells).
  • Conversely, inflammation-suppressing regulatory T cells and anti-inflammatory cytokines (e.g., IL-10) are elevated.

The results from animal experiments demonstrated the attenuation of inner ear tissue damage and the promotion of hearing maintenance and recovery.

Item

  • Stem cells directly replace lost cells in the inner ear.
  • Secretosomes support the survival and repair of remaining cells.
  • By modulating the immune system and mitigating inflammation, an inner ear environment conducive to recovery is created.

Research-backed evidence: To what extent can hearing recover?

Efek peningkatan gangguan pendengaran sensorineural oleh Terapi Sel Punca sedang dikonfirmasi dalam kedua percobaan pada hewan dan uji klinis awal, dan hasil yang penuh harapan terus dilaporkan.

Potential demonstrated by Stem Cell Therapy in animal studies

In animal experiments utilizing mice and rats, Stem Cell Therapy has been repeatedly confirmed to have a positive effect on hearing.

  • Model of noise-induced hearing loss

    Ketika MSC yang berasal dari tali pusat manusia ditransplantasikan ke tikus yang telinga dalamnya rusak oleh suara keras, penurunan pendengaran secara signifikan tertahan. Setelah transplantasi, aktivasi gen yang terkait dengan imunomodulasi dan antioksidan terlihat, dan efek penekanan kematian sel juga dikonfirmasi.

  • Model of age-related hearing loss

    Ketika MSC ditransplantasikan ke tikus berusia lanjut, sel rambut diregenerasi dan pendengaran pulih ke tingkat yang dekat dengan ketika mereka muda. Dianggap bahwa zat anti-inflamasi yang disekresikan oleh sel punca (apelin) juga berkontribusi pada penekanan peradangan telinga dalam.

  • Ototoxicity-induced hearing loss model

    Dalam model gangguan pendengaran yang diinduksi oleh obat antikanker cisplatin, dengan memberikan sekretosom yang berasal dari MSC, keburukan pendengaran dicegah, dan kehilangan sel rambut juga berkurang secara signifikan.

  • Autoimmune hearing loss model

    Ketika MSC diberikan kepada tikus dengan kerusakan telinga dalam karena reaksi autoimun, efek imunomodulasi menekan kerusakan jaringan telinga dalam, dan pendengaran meningkat secara signifikan.

    • These findings strongly suggest the possibility that Stem Cell Therapy makes "ear regeneration" a reality.

    Emerging insights from early clinical trials

    Stem cell therapy in humans has also reported results, although still in its early stages.

    • Treatment of adult hearing disorders with autologous bone marrow MSCs (Korea)

      Dalam uji klinis di mana MSC yang berasal dari sumsum tulang pasien mereka sendiri diberikan melalui IV kepada pasien dengan gangguan pendengaran sensorineural berat, tidak ada efek samping serius yang terdeteksi, dan keamanan dikonfirmasi. Selanjutnya, pada beberapa pasien, ada tren peningkatan dalam nilai tes pendengaran yang kecil.

    • Treatment of hearing loss in children with umbilical cord blood MSCs (US)

      Dalam uji coba memberikan sel punca yang berasal dari darah tali pusat kepada 11 pasien anak dengan gangguan pendengaran berusia 6 bulan hingga 6 tahun, tidak ada kejadian merugikan serius pada semua kasus, dan perawatan dapat dilakukan dengan aman. Setelah pengobatan, perbaikan dalam respons auditori brainstem (ABR) terlihat pada beberapa anak, dan efek positif pada perkembangan bahasa diharapkan.

    Item

    • The restoration of hearing through Stem Cell Therapy has been repeatedly confirmed in animal experiments.
    • In early human clinical trials, high safety was demonstrated, and indications of improvement were reported.
    • Stem Cell Therapy for sensorineural hearing loss is becoming more of a reality, rather than just a pipe dream.

    Stem Cell Therapy Safety: A Proper Understanding of Concerning Adverse Effects

    Terapi Sel Punca, terutama terapi MSC yang berasal dari tali pusat, menunjukkan keamanan tinggi dari penelitian hingga saat ini, dan tidak ada efek samping serius yang dilaporkan.

    Key Safety Findings in Stem Cell Therapy

    • Immune rejection is rare.

      MSC memiliki fungsi untuk memodulasi imun, dan diketahui bahwa bahkan ketika dimasukkan ke dalam tubuh, reaksi penolakan yang kuat tidak mudah terjadi. Khususnya, WJ-MSC memiliki karakteristik bahwa tipe HLA (human leukocyte antigen) belum matang, sehingga lebih mudah diterima bahkan dari sumber lain. Tidak ada reaksi penolakan imun serius yang dilaporkan dalam percobaan hewan atau uji klinis sebelumnya.

    • Risk of tumorigenicity is very low.

      Berbeda dengan sel punca embrionik atau sel iPS, MSC diketahui memiliki risiko sangat rendah dalam membentuk tumor atau teratoma. Faktanya, dalam terapi MSC yang dilakukan di seluruh dunia, hampir tidak ada laporan kanker yang terjadi.

    • Long-term security is also being confirmed.

      Dalam penelitian follow-up selama bertahun-tahun pada pasien gangguan pendengaran yang telah menjalani Terapi Sel Punca, tidak ada masalah besar yang terdeteksi bahkan 3 tahun setelah pengobatan. Hal ini menunjukkan bahwa MSC bertahan stabil dalam tubuh atau hilang secara alami setelah menyelesaikan perannya.

    • Minimal short-term side effects.

      Ketika MSC diberikan melalui infus IV, demam ringan atau nyeri di tempat injeksi mungkin terlihat untuk waktu yang sangat singkat. Juga, dalam pemberian lokal di sekitar telinga dalam, pusing sesaat atau tinnitus telah dilaporkan dalam beberapa kasus, tetapi semuanya pulih secara alami dalam waktu singkat, dan tidak ada komplikasi serius yang dikonfirmasi.

    Comprehensive Security Evaluation

    Stem Cell Therapy, particularly treatments utilizing umbilical cord-derived MSCs, can be considered a highly safe regenerative medicine approach with a low risk of immune rejection and tumorigenicity.

    Admittedly, regenerative medicine remains a relatively nascent field, necessitating close observation moving forward. Nevertheless, the currently available data suggest a more favorable safety profile compared to traditional surgical interventions or pharmacological treatments.

    Item

    • MSCs exhibit low immunogenicity, with umbilical cord-derived MSCs demonstrating even greater tolerability.
    • 腫瘍化リスクは極めて低く、長期的な安全性も確認されつつある
    • Mild side effects have been observed, but no serious complications have been reported to date.
    • At this point, it can be considered a highly safe regenerative medicine option.

    Pioneering the Future of Hearing Restoration: The Promise of Stem Cell Therapy

    Sensorineural hearing loss has long been considered an "incurable hearing disorder."

    Once the hair cells and auditory nerves of the inner ear are damaged, they do not regenerate. Therefore, only the remaining function can be compensated for with hearing aids or cochlear implants, a reality many patients are compelled to accept.

    However, with the advancements in regenerative medicine, specifically Stem Cell Therapy, the conventional understanding of hearing loss treatment is undergoing a dramatic transformation. Based on findings from animal studies and preliminary clinical research, the prospect of regenerating damaged hair cells and auditory nerves, thereby restoring hearing, is becoming a tangible reality.

    Notably, umbilical cord-derived mesenchymal stem cells (UC-MSCs) demonstrate superior regenerative capabilities and safety. Global expectations are mounting for their potential as a breakthrough solution for sensorineural hearing loss.

    For individuals suffering from sensorineural hearing loss, Stem Cell Therapy offers great promise for the future. We earnestly hope that the day will soon arrive when this promise restores the invaluable gift of sound to countless individuals.
