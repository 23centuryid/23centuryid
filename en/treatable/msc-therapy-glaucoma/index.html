  • Tentang Stem cell therapy untuk Glaukoma

Glaukoma is disease progresif in which nerve optik in bagian belakang eye secara bertahap rusak and bidang visual became semakin sempit. Disease this sering developed without symptom which jelas and patient not menyadari until some from bidang visual disappear, and on kasus which parah can menyebabkan blindness.

Wrong satu faktor which berkontribusi is peningkatan tekanan in in eye (tekanan intraokular), tetapi there is also tipe glaukoma which happen on tekanan eye normal (normal-tension glaucoma). Cell which membentuk nerve optik (cell ganglion retina) is bagian from system nerve center, and sekali rusak and mati, they not can meregenerasi secara natural. For reason this, on titik time this, dianggap not mungkin for mengembalikan bidang visual which disappear.

Faktanya, glaukoma is wrong satu penyebab main blindness in world, with perkiraan 76 juta orang which terkena on years 2020, and diperkirakan more from 111 juta orang on years 2040.

Status Care Glaukoma and Dinding which Harus Diatasi

Manajemen tekanan intraokular is therapy main now:

Glaukoma is disease in which tekanan in in eye increased and menyebabkan kerusakan on nerve optik secara bertahap. Oleh because that, treatment standard time this berfokus on menurunkan tekanan eye for reduce beban on nerve optik. Care main meliputi:

  • Tetes eye: Farmakologi for menurunkan tekanan intraokular (agen prostaglandin, beta blocker, dll)
  • Treatment laser: Laser trabeculoplasty for enhance aliran humor akueus (cairan in in eye)
  • Surgery: Trabekulotomi and penyisipan tabung shunt for menurunkan tekanan eye

Care this can "menunda" development kerusakan visual, tetapi time this not there is cara for improve nerve optik which rusak.

Keterbatasan deep penekanan development saja:

Actually, many kasus in which bidang visual continuously memburuk secara bertahap bahkan with treatment berkelanjutan. Bagi patient, there is frustrasi large which tersisa: "semuanya which performed hanyalah mencegah development, and bidang visual which disappear not will returned".

In practice medical time this, not mungkin for true-true heal kerusakan nerve optik which caused oleh glaukoma, and treatment hanya tetap became therapy simtomatik which menunda development.

For reason this, there is kebutuhan mendesak for pendekatan treatment new which can meregenerasi and improve nerve optik that sendiri.

Menuju Regeneration Nerve Optik: Challenge Stem cell therapy in Glaukoma

Pendekatan regenerative that exceed batasan konvensional:

Treatment glaukoma konvensional before berfokus on menurunkan tekanan eye, and not there is cara for memulihkan nerve optik which rusak. For overcome challenge this, perhatian developed on stem cell therapy, which merupakan cabang from drug regenerative.

Stem cell therapy is method treatment in which cell-cell young and healthy from patient that sendiri or donor diperkenalkan to in tubuh for mempromosikan perbaikan tissue which rusak or recovery fungsi. In glaukoma also, there is hope bahwa nerve optik which disappear can dikompensasi and membimbing recovery visual.

MSC that berasal from tali pusat that interesting perhatian:

In antara stem cell, especially mesenchymal stem cell (MSC) diharapkan in drug regenerative in bidang oftalmologi. MSC there is in sumsum bone, tissue lemak, tali center, and lainnya, tetapi perhatian special given on MSC which berasal from tali center (WJ-MSC), many from which terkandung in Wharton's Jelly, tissue in tali center:

  • Keamanan pengambilan: Tali center secara natural obtained time melahirkan, so that not membebani donor (bayi new lahir or ibu)
  • Cell young and aktif: With kemerosotan related usia minimal, they mempertahankan kapasitas proliferasi and diferensiasi which high bahkan in budaya jangka long
  • Privelese imunologi: Transplantasi to orang lain also not mudah ditolak secara imun
  • Keamanan high: Risk tumorigenesis (change became cancer) very low

With karakteristik this, WJ-MSC is sumber cell ideal which cocok for drug regenerative and research clinical as well as penerapan clinical sedang take place in seluruh world. Faktanya, review research latest also reported bahwa treatment use MSC tali center show result which menjanjikan for kelainan nerve optik including glaukoma.

Mekanisme Kerja Stem cell on Nerve Optik Glaukoma

Melindungi cell nerve (efek neuroproteksi):

MSC secara berlimpah mensekresi protein pelindung nerve seperti faktor neurotrofik which berasal from brain (BDNF) and faktor growth nerve (NGF). Faktor-faktor this support cell ganglion retina which melemah with nutrisi and enhance tingkat kelangsungan hidup they. Bayangkan, seperti provide air and pupuk which melimpah kepada tanaman which mulai mengering for membuatnya grow fresh returned. "Koktail healing" which dilepaskan oleh MSC soft heal kerusakan cell nerve and mendukungnya with strong.

Calm peradangan (efek modulasi imun):

MSC calm reaksi inflammation which berlebihan and create lingkungan which tenang for nerve optik. Secara special, reduce production sitokin inflammation (TNF-α, IL-6, dll) and sebaliknya mempromosikan sekresi sitokin anti-inflammation (TGF-β, IL-10, dll). Seperti with fast memadamkan api which menyebar spacious, MSC menekan peradangan in eye and mempersiapkan tanah in which cell nerve can hidup with tenang.

Improve aliran blood and mengantarkan oksigen and nutrisi:

Faktor growth which disekresikan oleh MSC (VEGF, HGF, dll) mendorong neovaskularisasi in sekitar nerve optik and mengaktifkan aliran blood. With this, cell nerve receive cukup oksigen and nutrisi, and lingkungan which healthy dibangun for menahan kerusakan. Sebaliknya, this seperti bring saluran air new to bidang which menderita kekeringan, help tanaman grow with gemilang.

Kemungkinan perbaikan and regenerasi tissue:

MSC have ability for berdiferensiasi became cell tertentu sesuai kebutuhan, help perbaikan tissue which rusak. In glaukoma, diharapkan regeneration on dua target:

  • Perbaikan trabekula: If MSC berdiferensiasi became cell trabekuler which help pengeluaran humor akueus, there is possibility for secara fundamental enhance kontrol tekanan eye.
  • Regeneration cell ganglion retina: Open possibility MSC melengkapi cell nerve visual retina and membangun tissue nerve new. Eksperimen reported bahwa cell nerve which berasal from stem cell successful terjerat in retina and merespons stimulus light for send sinyal visual, and ekspektasi semakin increased in future.

Efek which Diharapkan from Stem cell therapy for Glaukoma

Hasil menjanjikan that ditunjukkan pada model hewan:

Research before has show bahwa stem cell therapy show efek menjanjikan for glaukoma on model hewan. When MSC which berasal from sumsum bone disuntikkan to in eye, tingkat kelangsungan hidup cell ganglion retina (RGC) increased and perlindungan serat nerve optik (akson) dikonfirmasi. In research terpisah, dilaporkan bahwa MSC menetap in dekat trabekula and improve aliran humor akueus with menurunkan tekanan eye.

Result this show potential MSC for melindungi nerve optik and control tekanan eye from kedua arah.

Tanda-tanda cerah deep uji coba klinis manusia:

Hope also increased for keamanan and effectiveness stem cell therapy in research clinical on human. In uji coba clinical awal, treatment transplantasi perangkat which berisi stem cell to in eye performed with safe and tren perbaikan ringan in tes bidang visual diamati. Selain that, pengurangan ketebalan lapisan serat nerve optik (RNFL) also ditekan, show possibility menghentikan development atrofi nerve optik.

More lanjut, in meta-analisis which diterbitkan on years 2024, treatment stem cell given kepada 66 eye patient with disease nerve optik (including glaukoma), and hasilnya show peningkatan vision rata-rata which significant secara statistic. Result this dianggap bring possibility new to konsep which before dianggap umum bahwa "bidang visual which disappear not can dipulihkan".

Keamanan which Diharapkan from Stem cell therapy for Glaukoma

Track record keamanan that has ditunjukkan:

Treatment MSC mesenchymal stem cell for glaukoma has show keamanan which good in research clinical time this. MSC which berasal from tali center have karakteristik which not mudah ditolak bahkan when disuntikkan to orang lain. Faktanya, in uji coba clinical which performed in bidang oftalmologi, not there is report efek samping serius (infeksi, tumorigenesis, dll). MSC from tali center maintain keremajaan cell, and risk abnormalitas genetik or keganasan in process budaya proliferasi has dikonfirmasi very low. With cara this, MSC dievaluasi sebagai cell with keamanan which very good because have kapasitas diferensiasi natural tetapi not have plastisitas tak terkontrol.

Catatan perhatian and manajemen keamanan operasi:

Stem cell therapy is tindakan medical, and important for melaksanakannya in bawah manajemen which tepat. On injeksi cell to in eye, manajemen aseptik diperlukan for mencegah infeksi and peradangan in beberapa kasus langka, tetapi this ditutup oleh tindakan which same with treatment which there is seperti injeksi anti-VEGF. After pemberian MSC, eye red sementara and kekaburan eye sometimes can dilihat, tetapi some large reaksi ringan and sementara and increased secara natural.

In report before, not there is kasus in which visi became jauh more bad because stem cell therapy, and sebaliknya, many patient merasa change positif after surgery. For receive care with safe, important bahwa care performed in bawah system manajemen professional in institution medical in which manajemen quality budidaya stem cell and technique aseptik improved.

Perspektif New which Dibawa Drug Regenerative

Stem cell therapy open possibility new in area regeneration nerve optik which before not terjangkau. Era in which drug regenerative bring new hope kepada fungsi visual which disappear because glaukoma sedang mendekat. Kekuatan and keamanan perbaikan which dimiliki stem cell expand pilihan treatment sekaligus bring prospek positif kepada many patient.

Time this, peneliti in seluruh world continuously work hard for penerapan clinical, and langkah-langkahnya advanced with pasti. Glaukoma has long dianggap disease which not can disembuhkan, tetapi with komitmen for research and development, bayangan hope mulai bersinar.

For patient and family with glaukoma which khawatir about blindness, harap ketahui bahwa progress in stem cell therapy continuously berlanjut and future therapy bring many hope.
