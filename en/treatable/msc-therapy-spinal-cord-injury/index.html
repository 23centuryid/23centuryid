Cedera bone belakang is condition serius in which kabel nerve bone belakang rusak akibat kecelakaan mendadak, menyebabkan kelumpuhan berat and gangguan sensasi.

In care medical time this, fokusnya is on fiksasi bone secara bedah, penekan peradangan with drug-obatan, and rehabilitasi for mencegah development more lanjut, tetapi method treatment which true-true "heal" fungsi nerve which disappear belum ditetapkan.

For reason this, patient cedera bone belakang harus face impact jangka long seperti kelumpuhan. However on years-years terakhir, Stem cell therapy sebagai "drug regenerative" has interesting perhatian, and possibility new has appear for mengejar perbaikan without give up on cedera bone belakang.

In bawah this, we explain challenge care time this, prospek new from Stem cell therapy, mekanisme aksi, bukti effectiveness, keamanan, and topik related with cara which mudah dipahami oleh masyarakat umum.

Cedera Bone Belakang and Challenge Care Time This

Bone belakang can digambarkan sebagai "jalan raya nerve main" which bring perintah from brain to tubuh.

When bone belakang this rusak, sinyal to sirkuit nerve in bawahnya terputus, and seperti jalan raya main which terputus oleh kecelakaan lalu lintas, "sinyal" from brain not lagi melewati.

Akibatnya, on area in bawah location kerusakan, tangan and feet not can move, and there is loss sensasi, mengakibatkan kelumpuhan. Selain that, fungsi system nerve otonom seperti buang air small, buang air large, and berkeringat also terganggu, impact large on quality of life.

In treatment standard time this, pertama-tama, on fase akut immediately after kecelakaan, surgery bedah performed for eliminate kompresi bone belakang from bone which patah and disk intervertebralis which menonjol, and bone belakang diperkuat. On time which same, perlakuan seperti pemberian steroid performed for menekan perluasan kerusakan sekunder (kerusakan nerve from peradangan and pembengkakan).

After that, rehabilitasi became fokus, melatih tubuh for move sebanyak mungkin with fungsi nerve which tersisa.

However, serat nerve bone belakang which sekali putus not mudah beregenerasi secara natural, so that with treatment time this saja sulit for sepenuhnya memulihkan fungsi which disappear.

Kurangnya care which effective for symptom cedera bone belakang is challenge large in Jepang, with more from 150.000 patient in in negeri and sekitar 5.000 new lagi setiap years menderita cedera.

Although demikian, pendekatan new which provide hope bagi patient and family they sedang appear.

That is Stem cell therapy through drug regenerative.

Before dikatakan bahwa "recovery not can diharapkan on fase kronis (keadaan after time berlalu since cedera)", tetapi penemuan research latest mulai membantah kebijaksanaan konvensional this. With kedatangan Stem cell therapy, possibility perbaikan fungsi has terlihat bahkan for they which time berlalu since cedera bone belakang.

Cedera Bone Belakang and Stem cell therapy: Possibility New Drug Regenerative

Stem cell therapy is method treatment which use "stem cell" which have ability for berdiferensiasi became berbagai cell in tubuh or help perbaikan tissue, for achieve regeneration tissue which rusak.

Sebagai drug regenerative for cedera bone belakang, treatment which mentransplantasikan stem cell to site kerusakan bone belakang or memasukkannya to in tubuh through infus sedang diteliti and diimplementasikan in seluruh world.

There is beberapa jenis stem cell. For example, stem cell which berasal from sumsum bone or lemak from patient that sendiri, or stem cell which berasal from tali center (umbilical cord) from donor lain.

In antara they, "stem cell which berasal from Wharton's Jelly (WJ-MSC)" which terdapat in tali center have daya proliferasi high and ability diferensiasi, as well as karakteristik which not mudah ditolak oleh system kekebalan tubuh, and dianggap menjanjikan in treatment cedera bone belakang (rincian karakteristik stem cell which berasal from Wharton's Jelly dihilangkan in artikel this, tetapi efektivitasnya has terindikasi).

Treatment use stem cell this have potential for improve and meregenerasi bone belakang which rusak that sendiri, and perbaikan and peningkatan fungsi kelumpuhan which not can obtained with treatment konvensional diharapkan.

Sebagai possibility new from drug regenerative, research clinical and beberapa care have performed in luar negeri. For example, in uji clinical which performed in Mayo Clinic in Amerika, treatment use stem cell which berasal from lemak patient that sendiri menghasilkan peningkatan tingkat keparahan kelumpuhan on 7 from 10 patient, and keamanan also dikonfirmasi.

In Jepang also, Universitas Keio perform upaya pertama world mentransplantasikan cell prekursor nerve which berasal from iPS, and dilaporkan bahwa peningkatan fungsi motorik terlihat on 2 from 4 patient cedera bone belakang.

With cara this, drug regenerative use stem cell diperhatikan in seluruh world sebagai new hope for cedera bone belakang.

Mekanisme Kerja Stem cell therapy for Cedera Bone Belakang

Why memasukkan stem cell can improve cedera bone belakang?
Large-besaran there is mekanisme aksi berikut which dipikirkan for Stem cell therapy.

Akumulasi to situs kerusakan:

Sekali memasuki tubuh, stem cell have sifat find and berkumpul in place which rusak oleh diri they sendiri. Seperti "team perbaikan" in tubuh, stem cell berkumpul to bone belakang which rusak and begin aktivitas healing.

Penekanan peradangan:

After cedera bone belakang, reaksi inflammation which berlebihan happen, and dibutuhkan "mengerem system kekebalan which liar" secara harfiah. Stem cell have ability manage system kekebalan, and calm peradangan which secara abnormal increased in place which rusak.

Secara konkret, they reduce kerusakan sekunder cell nerve with work langsung on cell kekebalan or mengeluarkan zat which menekan peradangan.

Sekresi faktor growth and zat serupa:

Stem cell mensekresikan sejumlah large zat bioaktif which disebut "sitokin" and "faktor growth". This also secara kolektif disebut secretome and merupakan kunci for help kelangsungan hidup and regeneration nerve.

For example, zat seperti BDNF (faktor neurotrofik which berasal from brain) and NGF (faktor growth nerve) dilepaskan, help cell nerve which rusak with nutrisi and perintah growth, mempromosikan regeneration akson (serat nerve).

Singkatnya, stem cell memainkan peran "memupuk taman which rusak with pupuk penuh nutrisi for menumbuhkan tunas new".

Perbaikan Tissue Parut and Pemulihan Aliran Blood:

When bone belakang rusak, tissue parut terbentuk in sekitarnya oleh cell-cell glial which function sebagai penghalang fisik and menghambat regeneration nerve.

Research has show bahwa stem cell have efek for some manage and melunakkan "dinding tissue parut" this.

Selain that, for memulihkan aliran blood seperti kapiler which reduced in location kerusakan, create pembuluh blood new also merupakan expertise stem cell. With cara this, lingkungan in site kerusakan dioptimalkan, create fondasi bagi nerve for grow and memanjang with bebas (mirip with gambar menyiapkan tanah kosong and menjembatani returned).

Actually, in eksperimen use stem cell and produk sekresinya (secretome), change struktur tissue parut akibat cedera, promosi angiogenesis, and promosi perpanjangan serat nerve (regeneration) has dikonfirmasi.

Penggantian Tissue and Cell:

There is also possibility bahwa stem cell that sendiri berdiferensiasi became cell which mendasari nerve or cell pendukung (seperti cell glial) and melengkapi cell which disappear.

However, in kasus bone belakang, proporsi in which stem cell langsung menggantikan neuron (cell nerve) that sendiri dikatakan not high, and sebaliknya, efek perbaikan not langsung through perbaikan lingkungan and tindakan perlindungan which disebutkan in atas dianggap important.

Bukti Effectiveness Stem cell therapy for Cedera Bone Belakang

Contoh in which fungsi cedera bone belakang true-true improved with Stem cell therapy mulai dilaporkan from seluruh world, although on tahap research. We will memperkenalkan beberapa bukti main (dasar scientific) tersebut.

Peningkatan fungsi sensori (uji klinis - fase kronis):

In uji clinical cedera bone belakang fase kronis which performed in Spanyol, care performed with menyuntikkan stem cell which berasal from tali center (WJ-MSC) sekali to area lumbar patient.

Sebagai hasilnya, has dikonfirmasi bahwa persepsi nyeri (sensasi jarum) secara significant improved in dermatom skin in bawah tingkat kerusakan. This is result which penuh hope bahwa bahkan on patient with cedera bone belakang which has berlalu selama bertahun-years, some saja sensasi returned.

Selain that, perbaikan system nerve otonom seperti fungsi kandung kemih (peningkatan volume penyimpanan and pengurangan hiperaktivitas) also terlihat on beberapa patient.

Peningkatan fungsi motorik (uji klinis - subacut hingga fase kronis):

In uji clinical mesenchymal stem cell which berasal from lemak (AD-MSC) which performed in Mayo Clinic in Amerika (CELLBERANDA trial), stem cell ditransplantasikan to 10 patient with cedera bone belakang traumatis, and kemajuannya diikuti selama 2 years.

Hasilnya, on 7 from 10 patient, grade fungsi nerve increased setidaknya satu tingkat (grade AIS※ naik), and especially 1 patient became "super-responder" with recovery fungsi member tubuh atas and bawah which terlihat after treatment.

Secara historis, known bahwa "kelumpuhan berat from cedera bone belakang not will improved", tetapi research this show bahwa Stem cell therapy can menghasilkan perbaikan kelumpuhan.

Peningkatan fungsi komprehensif (research klinis - Jepang):

In Jepang also, research clinical leading in drug regenerative sedang performed.

In research Universitas Keio, cell prekursor nerve which berasal from iPS ditransplantasikan to patient cedera bone belakang, and dilaporkan bahwa 2 from 4 kasus show peningkatan fungsi motorik seperti proceed and movement tangan.

This is upaya pertama world and diperhatikan sebagai bukti which berasal from Jepang.

Hasil menjanjikan deep percobaan hewan:

Not hanya kasus clinical on human, many research performed on model hewan.

When stem cell which berasal from sumsum bone or tali center diinformasikan to model cedera bone belakang on tikus or tikus, report has terakumulasi which show bahwa fungsi motorik member badan belakang improved, and regeneration akson and penurunan parut diamati in tissue bone belakang.

Berkat result research praklinik this, jalan has open for penerapan clinical on human.

※AIS Grade
Standar penilaian keparahan cedera bone belakang that digunakan secara international (American Spinal Injury Association Impairment Scale).
A is kelumpuhan complete, B-D is kelumpuhan tidak complete, E is normal.
From bukti-bukti in atas, hal itu disarankan bahwa Stem cell therapy mungkin memiliki efek tertentu deep improve kelumpuhan because cedera bone belakang.

Kasus in which sensasi returned, kasus in which movement increased, kasus in which fungsi system nerve otonom improved - sementara not achieve "healing" which dramatis, kasus in which peningkatan which significant which enhance quality of life patient has dilaporkan.
