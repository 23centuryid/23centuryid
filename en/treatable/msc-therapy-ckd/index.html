Disease Ginjal Kronis (CKD) is kondisi di mana fungsi ginjal menurun secara bertahap selama 3 bulan or more. When disease this berkembang, kondisi menjadi serius and can berkembang menjadi uremia and gagal ginjal terminal (End Stage Renal Disease, ESRD).

Disease this umum terjadi secara global and diprediksi will continuously meningkat di future. This is disease serius which memerlukan penanganan special.

Penyebab utama CKD termasuk diabetes, hipertensi, and glomerulonefritis kronis, which menyebabkan struktur and fungsi ginjal secara bertahap mengalami kerusakan. When disease berkembang, ginjal not can mengeluarkan limbah tubuh with cukup, mengakibatkan gejala seperti pembengkakan, kelelahan, kehilangan nafsu makan, and penurunan quality of life.

For kasus CKD with penurunan fungsi ginjal which signifikan, therapy pengganti ginjal seperti dialisis buatan or transplantasi ginjal menjadi penting for mempertahankan hidup.

Treatment CKD saat this terutama berfokus pada pengendalian disease penyebab and penundaan perkembangan, as well as therapy simptomatik for komplikasi.

Misalnya, pengelolaan tekanan darah and gula darah, therapy diet, and treatment (diuretik, inhibitor RAS, and baru-baru this inhibitor SGLT2) used for memperlambat perburukan fungsi ginjal. Baru-baru this, has shown bahwa inhibitor SGLT2 effective in menghambat perkembangan CKD, and drug this direkomendasikan in panduan treatment.

However, meskipun treatment berlanjut, tetap sulit for "sepenuhnya menghentikan penurunan fungsi ginjal", and many patient mengalami penurunan fungsi ginjal secara bertahap meskipun treatment dilanjutkan. Bahkan, pada patient CKD tahap 3 hingga 5 (sedang hingga terminal), kombinasi beberapa drug sering show efektivitas which not memadai or patient menderita efek samping.

Seiring with continuously-menerus hilangnya fungsi ginjal, akhirnya patient memerlukan dialisis beberapa kali seminggu or transplantasi ginjal. Dialisis memberi beban waktu and fisik which large pada patient, and transplantasi ginjal have risiko penolakan organ and kekurangan donor.

With cara this, treatment saat this have many keterbatasan, and there is kebutuhan mendesak for method treatment baru which can "improve ginjal that sendiri and memulihkan fungsinya".

Disease Ginjal Kronis and Stem cell therapy: Possibility Baru Drug Regenerative

Menghadapi tantangan this, pendekatan which mendapat perhatian baru-baru this is pendekatan drug regenerative use stem cell therapy. Stem cell therapy is method treatment which use "stem cell" (cell which membentuk dasar tubuh kita) for mempromosikan regeneration jaringan which rusak.

For ginjal with fungsi menurun akibat kerusakan, tujuannya is for menyuntikkan stem cell and mempromosikan regeneration struktural and fungsional from jaringan ginjal which rusak.

Faktanya, stem cell therapy for disease ginjal kronis sedang many diteliti, and research clinical and uji coba sedang berlangsung di seluruh world. Latar belakang research this is ability stem cell which baru-baru this terungkap for menghentikan penurunan fungsi ginjal and bahkan meningkatkannya.

Faktanya, many studi dasar and uji coba clinical has show bahwa treatment use stem cell (terutama mesenchymal stem cell) can enhance fungsi ginjal through modulasi imun and penekanan peradangan. With cara this, stem cell therapy is equipment treatment baru which memegang harapan large for CKD.

Khususnya, baru-baru this perhatian tinggi given pada stem cell which berasal from Wharton's Jelly in tali center (WJ-MSC) di antara mesenchymal stem cell (MSC).

Wharton's Jelly is jaringan seperti gelatin di in tali center, and stem cell which kaya from jaringan this have many keuntungan sebagai sumber cell for drug regenerative. Pertama, because diekstrak from tali center which obtained saat kelahiran, not there is beban pada donor and masalah etika minimal because jaringan this biasanya dibuang. Kedua, because berasal from cell neonatal, cell-cell that sendiri muda and have kapasitas proliferasi tinggi, memudahkan for mendapatkan sejumlah large stem cell through budaya.

More lanjut, dilaporkan bahwa MSC which berasal from janin have ability modulasi imun which tinggi, have risiko penolakan rendah, so that transplantasi allogenik (pemberian cell from donor kepada patient) also relatif mudah. Because donor can with mudah provided, WJ-MSC diharapkan sebagai stem cell which mudah provided for many patient. From karakteristik this, stem cell which berasal from Wharton's Jelly receive perhatian special in stem cell therapy regenerative for disease ginjal kronis, and penggunaan sedang maju di garis depan research.

Mekanisme Kerja Stem cell therapy for Disease Ginjal Kronis

Stem cell therapy dianggap effective for CKD because stem cell is "terapis multifungsi" which perform many peran sekaligus. Dua mekanisme utama berikut known for how stem cell therapy bekerja pada CKD.

Penyesuaian keseimbangan imun dan penekanan peradangan:

Pada CKD, peradangan kronis terjadi pada ginjal or jaringan ginjal rusak oleh reaksi autoimmune. Stem cell (terutama mesenchymal stem cell, MSC) bertindak sebagai "rem" terhadap reaksi imun which berlebihan seperti that.

Secara special, stem cell mensekresikan substansi which memodulasi imun in tubuh, reduce sekresi sitokin which merangsang peradangan and sebaliknya mengaktifkan fungsi cell T regulator which good for menenangkan peradangan.

Seperti "pemberi perintah for system kekebalan which sedang lepas kendali, berkata 'tenang'", stem cell menyeimbangkan imun. With tindakan this, siklus bad from serangan diri pada ginjal and peradangan terputus, and kerusakan jaringan ginjal more lanjut can dicegah.

Dukungan perbaikan dan regenerasi jaringan:

Stem cell not secara langsung berubah menjadi cell ginjal, tetapi through berbagai substansi aktif which they sekresikan (secara kolektif disebut secretome), they mempromosikan perbaikan jaringan ginjal di sekitarnya.

Secretome this mengandung faktor pertumbuhan and sitokin which membantu proliferasi and perbaikan cell, as well as faktor which membuat pembuluh darah baru, send sinyal "selamatkan diri" and "bekerja lagi" ke cell-cell ginjal.

Selain that, stem cell menekan fibrosis berlebihan di in ginjal (fenomena di mana jaringan which rusak menjadi kaku seperti bekas luka), mempromosikan pembentukan kapiler baru which membantu pasokan oksigen and nutrisi ke ginjal. With cara this, lingkungan internal ginjal diatur, and jaringan disusun ulang for memudahkan pemulihan.

Selanjutnya, substansi antioksidan which disekresikan oleh stem cell melindungi cell ginjal with menetralisir spesies oksigen reaktif (zat berbahaya which merusak cell) which produced di in ginjal, and dilaporkan also memainkan peran in mencegah kematian cell ginjal (apoptosis).

With cara this, stem cell therapy seperti "tukang perbaikan which bekerja di in ginjal". Provide rem pada system kekebalan which sedang lepas kendali, mengatakan "berjiwa" kepada cell-cell which terluka, mengatur aliran darah and lingkungan, and menciptakan keadaan which mudah for regeneration.

Hasilnya, not hanya menghentikan perkembangan tetapi also dimungkinkan for memaksimalkan fungsi ginjal which tersisa sambil mempromosikan pemulihan ginjal, which then menarik perhatian sebagai method treatment.

Bukti Efektivitas Stem cell therapy for Disease Ginjal Kronis

Stem cell therapy have harapan teoritis, tetapi poin penting is "apakah that true-true effective?"

Meskipun stem cell therapy masih merupakan bidang medical which baru, sejumlah large research and uji coba clinical has performed until saat this, and bukti which show efektivitasnya continuously terakumulasi.

Pertama, pada tahap research praklinis use model hewan, efek which jelas from stem cell therapy has berulang kali dikonfirmasi. Misalnya, when MSC given kepada model hewan gagal ginjal (misalnya nefropati diabetik or kerusakan ginjal hipertensi), fungsi ginjal meningkat and kerusakan jaringan ginjal diperbaiki dilaporkan.

Faktanya, research show bahwa WJ-MSC which berasal from Wharton's Jelly show ability which menonjol in memulihkan fungsi ginjal and improve jaringan ginjal which rusak pada model hewan with gagal ginjal kronis. With result this, konsep treatment ginjal through drug regenerative sedang ditetapkan from tahap eksperimen hewan.

More lanjut, uji coba clinical awal (fase I and II) menargetkan patient in jumlah small also sedang performed. Result secara keseluruhan good, and dilaporkan bahwa patient CKD which receive stem cell therapy show stabilisasi or peningkatan in indikator fungsi ginjal (GFR = glomerular filtration rate) and penurunan proteinuria (protein in urin).

With kata lain, indikator which seharusnya memburuk menuju gagal ginjal malah show perbaikan or pemeliharaan oleh stem cell therapy.

Misalnya, there is report di mana pada patient with nefropati diabetik berat (CKD tahap 5), WJ-MSC given through infus, and beberapa bulan after treatment, nilai tes fungsi ginjal sangat meningkat and quality of life patient also meningkat. In kasus this, nilai eGFR (perkiraan tingkat filtrasi glomerulus) meningkat, memungkinkan penundaan waktu dimulainya dialisis. Selain that, in uji coba clinical terpisah, when MSC given kepada patient with nefritis lupus, disease autoimmune which sulit diobati, proteinuria urin berkurang secara signifikan after treatment and pemeliharaan jangka panjang dilaporkan.

Meskipun this hanya contoh sebagian, secara keseluruhan result research which dilaporkan sejauh this sangat show potensi efektivitas stem cell therapy for CKD.

Poin penting more lanjut is bahwa efek terlihat in konteks keamanan which good. Seperti which will dijelaskan di bawah, treatment performed tanpa efek samping large, and bahwa disease ginjal stabil or membaik dilaporkan in beberapa research, that is materi which sangat cerah saat mempertimbangkan kelayakan method treatment this.

Tentu saja, sementara uji coba clinical which sedang berlangsung also include perlu for enhance jumlah kasus and continuously mengakumulasi bukti ilmiah, knowledge which terakumulasi secara bertahap di in and luar negeri show kemajuan which pasti. Stem cell therapy sebagai method treatment which can mengubah kebijaksanaan sebelumnya bahwa "disease ginjal not bisa disembuhkan" mulai show prospek which jelas.

Keamanan Stem cell therapy for Disease Ginjal Kronis and Efek Samping

When receive treatment baru, keamanan is wrong satu poin which paling dikhawatirkan patient. Mengenai stem cell therapy also, question alami muncul: "Apakah true-true safe?" and "Apakah there is efek samping?"

Mengenai poin this, data which obtained in research hingga saat this show result which sangat menenangkan. Pertama, peristiwa bad serius from pemberian MSC not dilaporkan in many uji coba clinical.

Patient CKD which menjalani stem cell therapy show perjalanan pasca-treatment tanpa efek samping which jelas and dilaporkan can performed with safe. Faktanya, pada uji coba clinical tertentu also disimpulkan bahwa "infus MSC can dilaksanakan with safe and not there is efek samping which diamati".

With cara this, pada titik this information which tersedia show bahwa stem cell therapy show profil keamanan which sangat good.

Why keamanan dianggap tinggi? Wrong satu alasannya is bahwa MSC that sendiri have karakteristik which not mudah ditolak secara imun.

MSC not cell which secara aktif merangsang imun, tetapi cell which menekan peradangan, so that bahkan when cell from donor lain given, reaksi penolakan which serius or komplikasi kekebalan not mungkin terjadi. With kata lain, they is "cell which mudah diterima".

Also, cell which used in treatment aktual dibesarkan di facility budidaya cell special di bawah pengendalian quality ketat, and after melewati tes keamanan (tes sterilitas, konfirmasi stabilitas genetik/kromosom, dll), barulah given kepada patient.

With system manajemen seperti that, risiko infeksi and proliferasi cell abnormal diminimalkan sebanyak mungkin. Selain that, method pemberian stem cell also use method which berat ringan pada tubuh seperti infus intravena, and not there is invasi seperti operasi bedah large.

From di atas, stem cell therapy can dianggap sebagai treatment which can dilaksanakan with safe menurut what which known saat this.

Adapun efek samping, meskipun there is possibility gejala ringan sementara seperti demam ringan or nyeri di tempat suntikan can terjadi, not there is efek samping which more serius from that which dilaporkan. Peneliti and doctor perform pengamatan which cermat, tetapi secara keseluruhan dianggap bahwa this is treatment with risiko minimal bagi patient.

Keamanan tinggi this also merupakan wrong satu alasan for optimisme about stem cell therapy.
