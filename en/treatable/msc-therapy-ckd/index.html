Disease Kidney Kronis (CKD) is condition in which fungsi kidney menurun secara bertahap selama 3 month or more. When disease this developed, condition became serius and can developed became uremia and gagal kidney terminal (End Stage Renal Disease, ESRD).

Disease this umum happen secara global and diprediksi will continuously increased in future. This is disease serius which memerlukan penanganan special.

Penyebab main CKD including diabetes, hipertensi, and glomerulonefritis kronis, which menyebabkan struktur and fungsi kidney secara bertahap experience kerusakan. When disease developed, kidney not can mengeluarkan limbah tubuh with cukup, mengakibatkan symptom seperti pembengkakan, kelelahan, loss nafsu eat, and penurunan quality of life.

For kasus CKD with penurunan fungsi kidney which significant, therapy pengganti kidney seperti dialisis artificial or transplantasi kidney became important for mempertahankan hidup.

Treatment CKD time this especially berfokus on pengendalian disease penyebab and penundaan development, as well as therapy simptomatik for komplikasi.

For example, pengelolaan tekanan blood and gula blood, therapy diet, and treatment (diuretik, inhibitor RAS, and new-new this inhibitor SGLT2) used for memperlambat perburukan fungsi kidney. New-new this, has shown bahwa inhibitor SGLT2 effective in menghambat development CKD, and drug this direkomendasikan in panduan treatment.

However, although treatment berlanjut, tetap sulit for "sepenuhnya menghentikan penurunan fungsi kidney", and many patient experience penurunan fungsi kidney secara bertahap although treatment dilanjutkan. Bahkan, on patient CKD tahap 3 hingga 5 (sedang hingga terminal), kombinasi beberapa drug sering show effectiveness which not memadai or patient menderita efek samping.

Seiring with continuously-menerus hilangnya fungsi kidney, finally patient memerlukan dialisis beberapa kali seminggu or transplantasi kidney. Dialisis memberi beban time and fisik which large on patient, and transplantasi kidney have risk penolakan organ and kekurangan donor.

With cara this, treatment time this have many keterbatasan, and there is kebutuhan mendesak for method treatment new which can "improve kidney that sendiri and memulihkan fungsinya".

Disease Kidney Kronis and Stem cell therapy: Possibility New Drug Regenerative

Face challenge this, pendekatan which get perhatian new-new this is pendekatan drug regenerative use stem cell therapy. Stem cell therapy is method treatment which use "stem cell" (cell which membentuk dasar tubuh kita) for mempromosikan regeneration tissue which rusak.

For kidney with fungsi menurun akibat kerusakan, tujuannya is for menyuntikkan stem cell and mempromosikan regeneration struktural and fungsional from tissue kidney which rusak.

Faktanya, stem cell therapy for disease kidney kronis sedang many diteliti, and research clinical and uji coba sedang take place in seluruh world. Latar belakang research this is ability stem cell which new-new this terungkap for menghentikan penurunan fungsi kidney and bahkan meningkatkannya.

Faktanya, many studi dasar and uji coba clinical has show bahwa treatment use stem cell (especially mesenchymal stem cell) can enhance fungsi kidney through modulasi imun and penekanan peradangan. With cara this, stem cell therapy is equipment treatment new which memegang hope large for CKD.

Especially, new-new this perhatian high given on stem cell which berasal from Wharton's Jelly in tali center (WJ-MSC) in antara mesenchymal stem cell (MSC).

Wharton's Jelly is tissue seperti gelatin in in tali center, and stem cell which rich from tissue this have many advantage sebagai sumber cell for drug regenerative. Pertama, because diekstrak from tali center which obtained time kelahiran, not there is beban on donor and problem etika minimal because tissue this biasanya dibuang. Kedua, because berasal from cell neonatal, cell-cell that sendiri young and have kapasitas proliferasi high, memudahkan for mendapatkan sejumlah large stem cell through budaya.

More lanjut, dilaporkan bahwa MSC which berasal from janin have ability modulasi imun which high, have risk penolakan low, so that transplantasi allogenik (pemberian cell from donor kepada patient) also relatif mudah. Because donor can with mudah provided, WJ-MSC diharapkan sebagai stem cell which mudah provided for many patient. From karakteristik this, stem cell which berasal from Wharton's Jelly receive perhatian special in stem cell therapy regenerative for disease kidney kronis, and penggunaan sedang advanced in garis depan research.

Mekanisme Kerja Stem cell therapy for Disease Kidney Kronis

Stem cell therapy dianggap effective for CKD because stem cell is "terapis multifungsi" which perform many peran sekaligus. Dua mekanisme main berikut known for how stem cell therapy work on CKD.

Penyesuaian keseimbangan imun and penekanan peradangan:

On CKD, peradangan kronis happen on kidney or tissue kidney rusak oleh reaksi autoimmune. Stem cell (especially mesenchymal stem cell, MSC) bertindak sebagai "rem" terhadap reaksi imun which berlebihan seperti that.

Secara special, stem cell mensekresikan substansi which memodulasi imun in tubuh, reduce sekresi sitokin which merangsang peradangan and sebaliknya mengaktifkan fungsi cell T regulator which good for calm peradangan.

Seperti "pemberi perintah for system kekebalan which sedang lepas kendali, berkata 'tenang'", stem cell menyeimbangkan imun. With tindakan this, siklus bad from serangan diri on kidney and peradangan terputus, and kerusakan tissue kidney more lanjut can dicegah.

Dukungan perbaikan and regenerasi tissue:

Stem cell not secara langsung berubah became cell kidney, tetapi through berbagai substansi aktif which they sekresikan (secara kolektif disebut secretome), they mempromosikan perbaikan tissue kidney in sekitarnya.

Secretome this mengandung faktor growth and sitokin which help proliferasi and perbaikan cell, as well as faktor which make pembuluh blood new, send sinyal "selamatkan diri" and "work lagi" to cell-cell kidney.

Selain that, stem cell menekan fibrosis berlebihan in in kidney (fenomena in which tissue which rusak became kaku seperti bekas luka), mempromosikan pembentukan kapiler new which help pasokan oksigen and nutrisi to kidney. With cara this, lingkungan internal kidney diatur, and tissue disusun ulang for memudahkan recovery.

Selanjutnya, substansi antioksidan which disekresikan oleh stem cell melindungi cell kidney with menetralisir spesies oksigen reaktif (zat berbahaya which break cell) which produced in in kidney, and dilaporkan also memainkan peran in mencegah kematian cell kidney (apoptosis).

With cara this, stem cell therapy seperti "tukang perbaikan which work in in kidney". Provide rem on system kekebalan which sedang lepas kendali, mengatakan "berjiwa" kepada cell-cell which terluka, manage aliran blood and lingkungan, and create keadaan which mudah for regeneration.

Hasilnya, not hanya menghentikan development tetapi also dimungkinkan for memaksimalkan fungsi kidney which tersisa while mempromosikan recovery kidney, which then interesting perhatian sebagai method treatment.

Bukti Effectiveness Stem cell therapy for Disease Kidney Kronis

Stem cell therapy have hope teoritis, tetapi poin important is "is that true-true effective?"

Although stem cell therapy masih merupakan bidang medical which new, sejumlah large research and uji coba clinical has performed until time this, and bukti which show efektivitasnya continuously terakumulasi.

Pertama, on tahap research praklinis use model hewan, efek which jelas from stem cell therapy has berulang kali dikonfirmasi. For example, when MSC given kepada model hewan gagal kidney (for example nefropati diabetik or kerusakan kidney hipertensi), fungsi kidney increased and kerusakan tissue kidney diperbaiki dilaporkan.

Faktanya, research show bahwa WJ-MSC which berasal from Wharton's Jelly show ability which menonjol in memulihkan fungsi kidney and improve tissue kidney which rusak on model hewan with gagal kidney kronis. With result this, konsep treatment kidney through drug regenerative sedang ditetapkan from tahap eksperimen hewan.

More lanjut, uji coba clinical awal (fase I and II) menargetkan patient in jumlah small also sedang performed. Result secara keseluruhan good, and dilaporkan bahwa patient CKD which receive stem cell therapy show stabilisasi or peningkatan in indikator fungsi kidney (GFR = glomerular filtration rate) and penurunan proteinuria (protein in urin).

With kata lain, indikator which seharusnya memburuk menuju gagal kidney malah show perbaikan or pemeliharaan oleh stem cell therapy.

For example, there is report in which on patient with nefropati diabetik berat (CKD tahap 5), WJ-MSC given through infus, and beberapa month after treatment, nilai tes fungsi kidney very increased and quality of life patient also increased. In kasus this, nilai eGFR (perkiraan tingkat filtrasi glomerulus) increased, memungkinkan penundaan time dimulainya dialisis. Selain that, in uji coba clinical terpisah, when MSC given kepada patient with nefritis lupus, disease autoimmune which sulit diobati, proteinuria urin reduced secara significant after treatment and pemeliharaan jangka long dilaporkan.

Although this hanya contoh some, secara keseluruhan result research which dilaporkan sejauh this very show potential effectiveness stem cell therapy for CKD.

Poin important more lanjut is bahwa efek terlihat in konteks keamanan which good. Seperti which will dijelaskan in bawah, treatment performed without efek samping large, and bahwa disease kidney stabil or improved dilaporkan in beberapa research, that is materi which very cerah time mempertimbangkan kelayakan method treatment this.

Of course, sementara uji coba clinical which sedang take place also include perlu for enhance jumlah kasus and continuously mengakumulasi bukti scientific, knowledge which terakumulasi secara bertahap in in and luar negeri show progress which pasti.
