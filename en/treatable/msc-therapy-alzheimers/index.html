Status and Challenge Treatment Disease Alzheimer

Disease Alzheimer masih belum memiliki therapy fundamental, and pendekatan new that can mengatasi berbagai aspek disease ini sangat dibutuhkan.

Therapy Now Fokus pada Penghambatan Development

Dementia tipe Alzheimer (disease Alzheimer) is disease brain progresif which menyebabkan gangguan memori and penurunan fungsi kognitif. This merupakan jenis dementia with jumlah penderita terbanyak, and pentingnya continuously increased seiring with penuaan populasi.

Treatment standard time this use inhibitor kolinesterase and antagonis reseptor NMDA for menyeimbangkan neurotransmitter and memperlambat development symptom.

However, drug-obatan this not can menghentikan development disease or meregenerasi cell-cell nerve which have disappear.

Obat Antibodi Terbaru Juga Appear, Namun Masih Ada Keterbatasan

Akhir-akhir this, antibodi terhadap protein amyloid-beta and protein tau—which dianggap sebagai penyebab disease Alzheimer—has developed, enhance hope. For example, in uji clinical antibodi anti-amyloid-beta "lecanemab", dilaporkan achieve effectiveness reduce penurunan fungsi kognitif sekitar 27% after 18 month.

However, treatment latest this hanya memperlambat development disease and belum achieve healing total. Selain that, there is risk efek samping seperti perdarahan brain and edema serebral (ARIA), which memerlukan penggunaan which liver-liver.

Patologi Kompleks Mempersulit Therapy

In in brain disease Alzheimer happen:

  • Penumpukan amyloid-beta
  • Akumulasi abnormal protein tau
  • Peradangan kronis in brain
  • Stres oksidatif
  • Hilangnya sinapsis (connection antar cell nerve)

Berbagai condition patologis this happen bersamaan secara simultan.

Because mekanisme disease which kompleks this, drug-obatan konvensional which work on target tunggal not provide effectiveness which cukup. Akibatnya, belum there is method which mapan for menghentikan penurunan fungsi kognitif. This became challenge large bagi patient and family they.

From latar belakang this, pendekatan treatment new which can work secara spacious terhadap berbagai aspek patologi very dibutuhkan.

POINT

  • Disease Alzheimer is disease gangguan kognitif progresif which representatif
  • Drug-obatan time this hanya menghambat development symptom, not treatment fundamental
  • Antibodi latest also provide effectiveness tertentu, tetapi not menyebabkan healing
  • Patologi bersifat multifaset, and treatment target tunggal have keterbatasan
  • Terdapat hope high on strategi treatment new which can overcome berbagai aspek patologi

Possibility New Treatment Alzheimer which Dibuka oleh Therapy Regenerative

Stem cell therapy interesting perhatian sebagai pendekatan that can membangkitkan back cell-cell nerve that disappear and membawa hope new deep therapy disease Alzheimer.

Stem cell therapy that Membawa "Regenerasi Nerve"

Disease Alzheimer developed through loss cell-cell nerve. Upaya improve "tissue brain which rusak this" became pilar main therapy regenerative, yaitu Stem cell therapy.

Stem cell is cell special which have ability for mereplikasi diri (self-replication capacity) and berubah became berbagai jenis cell (differentiation capacity). Stem cell which diekstraksi from patient or donor ditransplantasikan to in tubuh for improve tissue which terluka and memulihkan fungsi.

Keunggulan Stem cell therapy is mampu work secara multidimensional terhadap patologi kompleks which not can didekati oleh drug tunggal. Seperti "team perbaikan infrastruktur city which rusak", therapy this provide dukungan from berbagai sisi: calm peradangan nerve, melindungi cell, and meregenerasi tissue.

Tipe cell which get perhatian special is mesenchymal stem cell (MSC). Can diekstraksi from sumsum bone, lemak, and tali center bayi (umbilical cord), with keseimbangan good antara keamanan and effectiveness treatment, so that research continuously developed in seluruh world.

Why WJ-MSC Berasal from Tali Pusat Interesting Perhatian High?

Mesenchymal stem cell (WJ-MSC) which obtained from tissue seperti gel which terdapat in tengah tali center—Wharton's jelly—get perhatian high sebagai "sumber cell generasi berikutnya" in therapy regenerative.

Alasannya terbagi became 4 aspek main:

  • Cell young with vitalitas high, berasal from bayi new lahir so that experience kerusakan minimal from penuaan, and have kapasitas proliferasi which very high.
  • Sulit terdeteksi oleh system imun (immunogenisitas low), with ekspresi MHC kelas II which hampir not there is, so that reaksi penolakan pasca-transplantasi rarely happen.
  • Menggabungkan pluripotency and aktivitas regulation imun, mampu berdiferensiasi became berbagai jenis cell including bone, kartilago, lemak, and cell mirip nerve, sekaligus also have fungsi meredakan peradangan.
  • Not there is problem etika, because use tali center which obtained secara natural time persalinan, with kekhawatiran etika minimal.

Especially for disease seperti Alzheimer which break nerve center, pendekatan "cell young provide dukungan multidimensional" very important. In hal this, WJ-MSC can diibaratkan sebagai "pekerja young which kompeten and berdedikasi for membangun returned city brain which rusak".

With ciri-ciri this, WJ-MSC diharapkan became cell with potential innovative in treatment disease Alzheimer and disease neurodegeneratif lainnya.

POINT

  • Stem cell therapy is therapy medical leading which overcome loss cell-cell nerve and improve brain
  • Mesenchymal stem cell (MSC) have keseimbangan keamanan and effectiveness treatment which unggul
  • WJ-MSC berasal from tali center show keunggulan special in kemudaan cell, kapasitas proliferasi, and toleransi imun
  • Immunogenisitas low make reaksi penolakan pasca-transplantasi rarely happen, cocok for transplantasi

How Stem cell therapy Work on Disease Alzheimer?

Stem cell mesenkimal (MSC) work secara multidimensional terhadap brain disease Alzheimer, mendorong pemulihan fungsi memori and perbaikan kondisi patologi.

Stem cell therapy not hanya sekadar penggantian cell.

Mesenchymal stem cell (MSC) show keunggulan especially in work on patologi kompleks Alzheimer with peran ganda seperti "pemadam peradangan", "nutrisi nerve", "pembersih bahan berbahaya", and "tukang reparasi sirkuit"—all work secara simultan.

Mari kita jelaskan each-each mekanisme with cara which mudah dipahami.

Calm Peradangan in Brain

On disease Alzheimer, cell imun (mikroglia) meliar and menyebabkan peradangan kronis in brain. Sitokin inflammation (IL-1β, TNF-α, dll) dilepaskan secara berlebihan, break cell-cell nerve.

MSC memberi rem on "peradangan liar" this. For example, in percobaan hewan with pemberian MSC berasal from tali center secara intravena, sitokin anti-inflammation IL-10 increased, sedangkan sitokin inflammation menurun secara significant.

This help meringankan peradangan kronis in brain and create lingkungan in which cell-cell nerve terlindungi.

Mengirimkan Nutrisi to Cell-Cell Nerve

MSC also function sebagai "nutrisi nerve". In place transplantasi, MSC mensekresi berbagai faktor nutrisi including faktor neurotrofik which berasal from brain (BDNF), faktor growth nerve (NGF), faktor neurotrofik which berasal from glia (GDNF), and faktor growth seperti insulin (IGF-1).

Faktor-faktor this support cell-cell nerve which terluka and mendorong pembangunan sinapsis new (tissue nerve). Selain that, eksosoma (vesikel small) which dilepaskan oleh MSC also mengandung microRNA and protein with fungsi neuro-protektif.

Seperti hujan bergizi which down to tanah dry, cell-cell nerve in brain diberi kekuatan for bangkit returned.

Clean Limbah in Brain

Penumpukan plak amyloid-beta and akumulasi abnormal protein tau—karakteristik disease Alzheimer. Limbah berbahaya this menghalangi fungsi cell-cell nerve.

MSC not hanya menekan peradangan, tetapi also support pemecahan and pengeluaran amyloid-beta which terkumpul in brain. In percobaan, dilaporkan bahwa penumpukan amyloid-beta reduced secara significant after transplantasi MSC.

Seperti "team pembersihan for city brain which kotor", MSC throw protein abnormal and create lingkungan in which cell-cell nerve can mengambil alih kekuatan they.

Menyambungkan Back Sirkuit Memori that Rusak

Cell-cell nerve in brain terhubung through sinapsis for support memori and fungsi kognitif, tetapi on disease Alzheimer, sirkuit this rusak. MSC not hanya berubah langsung became cell nerve, tetapi also merangsang stem cell nerve in brain for mendorong pembentukan cell-cell nerve new and improve sirkuit which there is.

In percobaan hewan, peningkatan cell-cell nerve new in hippocampus (wilayah brain which manage memori) and peningkatan kepadatan sinapsis has dikonfirmasi. Selain that, MSC also berkontribusi on pengurangan stres oksidatif and perbaikan lingkungan brain secara keseluruhan.

With kata lain, MSC memainkan peran seperti "teknisi listrik which improve sirkuit memori which rusak".

POINT

  • MSC menekan peradangan kronis in brain and melindungi cell-cell nerve
  • Mensekresi faktor nutrisi for support kelangsungan hidup cell and membangun returned tissue nerve
  • Mempromosikan pembuangan amyloid-beta and protein tau which abnormal
  • Mendorong pembentukan cell-cell nerve new and rekonstruksi sinapsis
  • Can overcome secara spacious berbagai aspek patologi disease Alzheimer

Dukungan Bukti: Effectiveness which Terungkap Through Research

Effectiveness Stem cell therapy dilaporkan from hasil that penuh hope, good from percobaan hewan maupun uji klinis awal.

Mengenai possibility Stem cell therapy, uji coba hewan and research clinical on human continuously performed in seluruh world, and bukti-bukti which support efektivitasnya continuously terkumpul.
In here, we menyajikan result research perwakilan with cara which mudah dipahami.

Efek Perbaikan that Diakui deep Model Hewan

Research use model hewan disease Alzheimer (tikus and small which dimodifikasi secara genetik) show bahwa treatment with mesenchymal stem cell (MSC) berulang kali reported recovery fungsi kognitif and perbaikan patologi brain.

For example, on tikus which receive pemberian intravena MSC berasal from tali center human, fungsi learning and memori spasial increased secara significant 4 week after pemberian, and penumpukan amyloid-beta in brain also reduced. More lanjut, sitokin inflammation menurun and sitokin anti-inflammation (IL-10) increased, show bahwa peradangan kronis in brain teratasi.

Selain that, technique has developed for memandu MSC secara efisien to hippocampus (wilayah memori) tikus with menginkorpomasikan partikel nano magnetik to MSC and membimbing with medan magnet eksternal. On tikus this, kinerja tes memori and fungsi kognitif increased, and fungsi nerve important for learning also improved.

In percobaan hewan this:

  • Penurunan marker inflammation in brain
  • Pengurangan stres oksidatif
  • Peningkatan kepadatan sinapsis
  • Pengurangan amyloid-beta and protein tau

Perlindungan and perbaikan fungsi brain has recognized from berbagai sisi.
Seperti "team regeneration which improve sebuah city secara bersamaan", MSC merekonstruksi lingkungan brain from berbagai sudut pandang.

Hasil Awal Uji Klinis pada Pasien

Berdasarkan kesuksesan research dasar, uji clinical on patient disease Alzheimer also has start. Uji coba tahap awal especially bertujuan mengkonfirmasi keamanan, tetapi result which menjanjikan have mulai appear.

For example, in uji coba transplantasi fraksi cell stromal (SVF) which berasal from diri sendiri to in ventrikel brain, 8 from 10 patient show stabilisasi or peningkatan fungsi kognitif, and on 3 patient, phosphorylated tau and amyloid-beta in cairan cerebrospinal reduced.

Beberapa kasus also show recovery volume hippocampus which significant.

More lanjut, in uji coba pemberian intravena MSC berasal from tali center which performed in Korea, keamanan terjamin and kecenderungan pemeliharaan aktivitas kehidupan sehari-day (ADL) dikonfirmasi. (Uji coba this berlanjut to Phase IIa)

Which very interesting is uji coba acak double-blind terkontrol plasebo with MSC berasal from Wharton's jelly (nama produk: Lomecel-B™) which dilaksanakan in Amerika.

In uji coba this, group patient which receive MSC show:

  • Penekanan pengurangan volume brain keseluruhan sebesar ~48%
  • Penekanan pengurangan volume hippocampus sebesar ~62%
  • Konfirmasi pengurangan peradangan with MRI difusi-weighted
  • Tren peningkatan on skor fungsi kognitif and indikator quality of life

Result this membuktikan "possibility MSC menghentikan atrofi brain and memperlambat development symptom".

Seperti "pekerjaan menyangga returned jembatan which melemah and mencegah keruntuhan seluruh jembatan", stem cell memainkan peran provide rem on penurunan fungsi brain.

POINT

  • In percobaan hewan, recovery fungsi kognitif and perbaikan patologi brain has berulang kali dikonfirmasi
  • Penghambatan peradangan, pembuangan amyloid-beta, and rekonstruksi sinapsis is efek multidimensional which recognized
  • Uji clinical awal also reported efek stabilisasi fungsi kognitif and pemeliharaan volume brain
  • Uji coba Lomecel-B™ secara scientific membuktikan efek penghambatan atrofi brain
  • Stem cell therapy show possibility for menghentikan development disease Alzheimer

Is Keamanan Stem cell therapy Terjamin? Verifikasi Risk Efek Samping

Berdasarkan research hingga now, Stem cell therapy berasal from Wharton's jelly (WJ-MSC) has terbukti memiliki keamanan high, give jaminan big bagi pasien.

Mengenai Stem cell therapy, kekhawatiran pertama is "keamanan". Especially when use cell from orang lain (allogeneic MSC), kekhawatiran about reaksi penolakan and risk efek samping not can dihindari.

However, research ekstensif which has performed show bahwa treatment with MSC, including WJ-MSC, umumnya have profil keamanan which high.

Konfirmasi Keamanan deep Uji Klinis Awal

In uji coba pemberian intravena MSC berasal from tali center on patient disease Alzheimer which performed in Korea, symptom which appear pasca-pemberian is:

  • Demam sementara (9 from 9 kasus)
  • Pain kepala ringan (7 kasus)
  • Mual (5 kasus)

All symptom this disappear secara natural in time 36 jam. Not there is efek samping which meninggalkan konsekuensi jangka long.

On beberapa patient, demam or mual menyebabkan perpanjangan rawat inap satu day for precaution, tetapi semuanya improved with manajemen which tepat, and toksisitas serius (dose-limiting toxicity) not found.

More lanjut, pemantauan jangka long hingga 36 month not show efek samping serius which new appear. Kesimpulannya is pemberian intravena MSC berasal from tali center can dilaksanakan and safe.

Risk Efek Samping deep Perspektif Stem cell therapy Secara Keseluruhan

Mengenai Stem cell therapy secara umum, research meta-analisis (2022) which mengevaluasi keamanan and effectiveness in berbagai disease neurodegeneratif show:

  • Tingkat kejadian peristiwa bad which related with treatment is sekitar 3%

This very low.

Secara keseluruhan, "therapy based cell is equipment treatment which safe and can dilaksanakan in bidang disease nerve".

Kekuatan Keamanan Special WJ-MSC

Especially WJ-MSC have jaminan keamanan tambahan.

  • Have sifat "sulit terdeteksi" oleh system imun, so that can ditransplantasikan without imunosupresan
  • Reaksi penolakan very rarely happen
  • Risk transformation tumor very low

Stem cell which berasal from embrio (cell ES, cell iPS) berisiko membentuk tumor because belum terdifrensiasi sepenuhnya, tetapi MSC is "stem cell dewasa" which have achieve tingkat diferensiasi tertentu. In application clinical hingga time this, not there is report pembentukan tumor pasca-transplantasi MSC.

Justru sebaliknya, MSC have:

  • Aktivitas anti-cancer
  • Aktivitas penghambatan fibrosis (fenomena pengerasan tissue)

Dilaporkan, so that including cell treatment which more safe for used.

POINT

  • Keamanan Stem cell therapy WJ-MSC has terkonfirmasi in uji clinical hingga time this
  • Efek samping utamanya is demam sementara, pain kepala, and mual which disappear secara natural
  • Tingkat efek samping serius in therapy keseluruhan hanya sekitar 3%, very low
  • WJ-MSC have risk very low for penolakan imun and transformation tumor, provide ketenangan pikiran
