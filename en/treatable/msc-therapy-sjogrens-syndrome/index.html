Sindrom Sjögren is disease autoimmune in which system kekebalan tubuh secara keliru menyerang kelenjar air eye and kelenjar ludah, menyebabkan eye dry berat (dry eye) and mulut dry (dry mouth).

Because air eye and ludah sulit diproduksi, patient experience not hanya sense gatal and ketidaknyamanan in mulut, tetapi also penurunan vision and peningkatan risk gigi berlubang and infeksi. When disease developed, patient also experience symptom sistemik seperti nyeri sendi and kelelahan ekstrem, which secara significant menurunkan quality of life.

Time this, not there is treatment which secara fundamental heal kelainan imun, and treatment time this berfokus on therapy simtomatik. For example, patient Sjögren receive treatment berikut:

  • Strategi eye dry: Tetes air eye artificial and salep eye pelembab
  • Strategi mulut dry: Asupan air which sering, pijat kelenjar ludah, semprotan ludah artificial/gel, drug oral for mempromosikan sekresi ludah (agen pilokarpin, dll)
  • Therapy sistemik for symptom: Drug antiinflamasi seperti drug antirematik, steroid, imunosupresif, agen biologis (for example rituksimab) tergantung on symptom and tingkat keparahan

However, treatment-treatment this have beberapa keterbatasan.

Air eye and ludah artificial hanya provide kelembaban sementara and not effective terhadap kelainan imun which mendasar, so that perlu used berulang kali. Drug sekresi ludah also not sepenuhnya enhance symptom and not can menghentikan development disease that sendiri.

Imunosupresif and steroid can help meringankan peradangan sistemik, tetapi khawatir penggunaan jangka long enhance risk efek samping seperti infeksi, osteoporosis, and diabetes. More lanjut, drug-drug this sering not can sepenuhnya enhance symptom kekeringan that sendiri, and many patient merasa not puas with efeknya.

With demikian, masih there is kebutuhan medical which large which belum terpenuhi for treatment akar Sjögren's Syndrome.

Sindrom Sjögren and Stem cell therapy: Possibility New Drug Regenerative

Face situasi time this, pendekatan new from bidang drug regenerative get perhatian new-new this. Wrong satunya is "stem cell therapy".

Stem cell is cell special which have ability for membedakan became berbagai cell and ability for improve tissue which rusak. In antara they, mesenchymal stem cell (MSC) sedang diteliti sebagai cell which menjanjikan for treatment disease autoimmune.

MSC can diambil from berbagai place seperti sumsum bone, lemak, and tali center, and can dikultur and improved for used in treatment patient. Faktanya, research therapy use MSC for disease kronis including Sindrom Sjögren sedang performed in seluruh world, and "kekeringan can dikurangi".

In eksperimen and research clinical time this, dilaporkan bahwa stem cell therapy mengembalikan fungsi sekresi air eye and ludah on model hewan Sindrom Sjögren and patient, and infiltrasi cell inflammation in kelenjar ludah reduced.

This is benefit unique from drug regenerative which not dimiliki oleh therapy simtomatik konvensional.

Keunggulan stem cell that berasal from Wharton's Jelly (WJ-MSC):

There is beberapa jenis stem cell tergantung on sumber pengambilan, tetapi which get perhatian special new-new this is mesenchymal stem cell which berasal from "Wharton's Jelly" which terkandung in tali center.

Stem cell which berasal from Wharton's Jelly is cell young which can diambil without sense pain from tissue tali center which obtained time kelahiran, and dilaporkan have kapasitas proliferasi high and ability modulasi imun.

Because berasal from cell bayi which new lahir, this is "cell which young and healthy", and bahkan when cell donor ditransfer langsung kepada patient lain, reaksi penolakan rarely happen. Ability imunologis which special this dipercayakan for melindungi janin from kekebalan ibu, and cell which berasal from tali center and plasenta dianggap have many substansi in tubuh which menekan kekebalan.

Oleh because that, stem cell which berasal from Wharton's Jelly...

In research stem cell therapy, stem cell which berasal from sumsum bone and lemak also used, tetapi in hal ease pengambilan cell and beban minimal on donor, stem cell which berasal from tali center have advantage large.

With cara this, stem cell therapy diharapkan sebagai strategi treatment new which bring hope kepada patient Sindrom Sjögren. Time this masih in tahap research, tetapi efek and mekanisme which secara bertahap became jelas will dilihat more detail in bawah.

Mekanisme Kerja Stem cell therapy for Sindrom Sjögren

Why Sindrom Sjögren increased with stem cell?

Kuncinya terletak on ability modulasi imun and efek promosi regeneration tissue which dimiliki mesenchymal stem cell. In Sindrom Sjögren, seperti rem which rusak on mobil, system kekebalan became kegilaan and menyerang kelenjar air eye and ludah they sendiri.

Stem cell therapy work for provide rem on system kekebalan which kegilaan this. Secara spesifik, berbagai substansi which dilepaskan stem cell (sitokin and faktor growth, secara kolektif disebut "secretome") work on cell kekebalan, calm reaksi inflammation berlebihan.

Has dikonfirmasi bahwa sitokin anti-inflammation which dilepaskan oleh stem cell reduce production substansi inflammation jahat (seperti TNF-α and IL-17) and enhance substansi penindas peradangan (seperti IL-10).

With cara this, reaksi autoimmune which has break kelenjar air eye and ludah became santai.

Selain that, mesenchymal stem cell also have ability for help perbaikan tissue which rusak. Stem cell berkumpul in tissue kelenjar air eye and ludah which rusak, and seperti tukang perbaikan or tukang kebun, mempromosikan regeneration tissue.

Stem cell that sendiri can berdiferensiasi and add cell new, tetapi which more important is efek dukungan terhadap tissue sekitarnya through faktor growth and faktor angiogenesis which disekresikan. For example, seperti provide pupuk and air to ladang which kekeringan akibat kekeringan, stem cell provide nutrisi and sinyal which diperlukan to area which rusak for mendorong perbaikan diri cell sendiri.

With cara this, recovery fungsi tissue kelenjar which rusak (recovery ability make air eye and ludah) diharapkan. Selain that, MSC also have tindakan penekanan fibrosis tissue (pengerasan) and promosi pembentukan kapiler new, so that enhance lingkungan kelenjar tissue and make mudah for menghasilkan kelembaban.

More lanjut, mesenchymal stem cell also enhance "pembela kekebalan" which not can improved with treatment lain. For example, research reported bahwa pemberian MSC enhance cell T regulator penindas kekebalan (Treg) and reduce cell Th17 pembawa peradangan. With cara this, menggeser keseimbangan imun to arah normalisasi, efek putus rantai setan autoimmune can diharapkan.

Stem cell therapy not hanya ringan sementara for symptom tetapi work from dua sisi: "calm kegilaan kekebalan" and on time which same "mempromosikan regeneration tissue which rusak", so that dianggap sebagai perbaikan fundamental Sindrom Sjögren.

Bukti Effectiveness Stem cell therapy for Sindrom Sjögren

Stem cell therapy seperti what efektivitasnya? Research clinical small and report uji clinical has performed in berbagai belahan world, and some large reported result which menjanjikan.

Perbaikan fungsi sekresi:

Has dikonfirmasi bahwa stem cell therapy enhance volume air eye and ludah which disekresikan. Actually, in uji coba clinical Sindrom Sjögren which performed in Cina, on 3 month after treatment, fungsi sekresi kelenjar ludah and kelenjar air eye secara significant increased dibandingkan with group plasebo. In studi lain with pemberian MSC through infus intravena, many patient experience peningkatan jumlah ludah. Also about eye dry dilaporkan bahwa sekresi air eye cenderung increased, memungkinkan pengurangan symptom dry eye and dry mouth. Kedua-duanya.

Peringan symptom:

Patient which receive stem cell therapy reported perbaikan symptom subjektif including kelelahan sistemik and nyeri sendi in samping kekeringan.

Actually, skor evaluasi symptom patient (indeks which disebut ESSPRI) secara significant increased on group treatment dibandingkan with group plasebo, show perbaikan in quality of life including pengurangan sense pain and kelelahan.

Penurunan aktivitas disease:

Peningkatan in indikator which show kekuatan disease Sjögren (skor ESSDAI) also dilaporkan. For example, in satu research clinical, after treatment MSC, skor aktivitas disease down drastis, and 3 month after treatment, 46% patient show perbaikan 30% or more in disease, 6 month after that 71%, and 1 years after 83% from patient show peningkatan which significant 30% or more.

Tren peningkatan effectiveness from time to time also dikonfirmasi, and hope large ditempatkan on effectiveness berkelanjutan from stem cell therapy. Faktanya, in uji coba terpisah in Cina, skor ESSDAI down secara significant hingga 6 month after treatment. Selain that, indikator which show tingkat peradangan sistemik (ESR, sedimentasi eritrosit) and evaluasi fungsi patient (ESSPRI) also increased, membuktikan bahwa stem cell therapy menekan aktivitas disease that sendiri.

Koreksi kelainan imun:

Data also there is show bahwa stem cell therapy reduce autoantibodi and substansi inflammation which karakteristik on Sindrom Sjögren.

For example, dilaporkan bahwa konsentrasi blood anti-SSA/Ro and anti-SSB/La autoantibodi menurun on beberapa patient which underwent treatment, show perbaikan in indikator kelainan imun. Also in uji coba clinical before, on group stem cell therapy imunoglobulin berlebihan seperti IgG and IgM reduced. This berarti reaksi autoimmune berlebihan in tubuh has mereda, and this is bukti bahwa stem cell therapy enhance patofisiologi Sindrom Sjögren that sendiri. Selain that, in temuan biopsi tissue, after stem cell therapy, in kelenjar ludah infiltrasi limfosit (infiltrasi cell inflammation) reduced secara significant and reduced.

With cara this, dilihat from indikator multi-sudut, therapy stem cell MSC show efek which menjanjikan for Sindrom Sjögren. Although many from bukti this masih in tahap research, akumulasi continuously take place in seluruh world. Especially, bahwa symptom and data tes increased begitu many in care with keamanan high is kabar gembira large bagi patient.

Stem cell therapy mulai show possibility for provide efek fundamental which not can obtained with therapy simtomatik saja on symptom kekeringan which merupakan challenge long in Sindrom Sjögren.

Keamanan Stem cell therapy for Sindrom Sjögren and Efek Samping

Secara umum, in stem cell therapy efek samping ringan and bersifat sementara.

When infus intravena performed, rarely found demam sementara ringan after infus, pain kepala, or kelelahan, tetapi this improved with fast in time short and not meninggalkan efek jangka long.

There is also report from uji coba clinical in which from 64 patient which receive treatment, hanya 2 orang experience gatal skin sementara and not there is peristiwa bad lainnya which diamati. With cara this, stem cell therapy dianggap sebagai treatment with keamanan very high.

Selain that, stem cell hanya menyeimbangkan kekebalan, not seperti drug steroid which menurunkan kekebalan tubuh secara ekstrem. Oleh because that, not diharapkan peningkatan risk infeksi. Actually, not there is report infeksi berat which caused oleh experience stem cell therapy, and malah there is contoh MSC applied on kasus sulit for diobati infeksi (seperti pemberian MSC for GVHD berat which disertai with treatment lain).

Selain that, stem cell therapy not memerlukan chemotherapy dosis large or anestesi umum before seperti transplantasi sumsum bone, so that beban fisik on patient reduced. Oleh because that, sindrom Sjögren terhadap stem cell therapy can dikatakan sebagai opsi treatment with keamanan high and kekhawatiran efek samping which very few.

Kesimpulannya, keamanan research which good so that jauh is wrong satu reason for optimisme about stem cell therapy. From research time this, not there is efek samping serius which hampir dilaporkan, and bahkan patient with autoimmune disease 400 more which receive pemberian mesenchymal stem cell, tingkat peristiwa bad serius which caused treatment that sendiri very low and keamanan high shown.
