About Stem cell therapy for Cedera Kepala Traumatis

Pengenalan Awal

Cedera kepala traumatis (TBI) is gangguan which terjadi when jaringan brain rusak because dampak kuat on kepala from kecelakaan lalu lintas, jatuh, or hal serupa. In kasus which parah, can menyebabkan gejala serius seperti kehilangan kesadaran, kehilangan memori, kelumpuhan setengah badan, or gangguan berbicara, and sering terjadi bahwa cacat jangka panjang tersisa seumur hidup.

Saat this, patient TBI and their family bekerja on penyelamatan akut in fase akut and recovery fungsi through rehabilitasi, however dihadapkan on tantangan bahwa "cell-cell brain which rusak not will returned." Sebenarnya, not there is bukti ilmiah bahwa cell-cell brain which mati or rusak dilahirkan returned sebagai cell new, and sementara bagian brain which tersisa berusaha with good for menggantikan fungsi, masalah sering tersisa in ability motorik, pemikiran, and ability berbicara, or perubahan kepribadian and gejala depresi.

Cacat semacam this secara significant reduce quality of life (QOL) and became beban bagi patient and their family.

Cedera Kepala Traumatis (TBI) and Tantangan Treatment Saat This

Treatment standard saat this for TBI terutama fokus on pertolongan pertama on fase akut and rehabilitasi. Misalnya, immediately after cedera, performed penanganan bedah and care intensif seperti pengeluaran hematoma and kontrol tekanan intrakranial, and then performed therapy fisik and therapy okupasi for memaksimalkan and enhance fungsi which tersisa.

However, begitu jaringan brain mengalami kerusakan serius, kenyataannya is sulit for sepenuhnya menyembuhkannya returned normal hanya with drug-obatan or rehabilitasi. Brain is "organ which sulit disembuhkan," and hingga sekarang not pernah there is method treatment which meregenerasi cell-cell saraf that sendiri in area which rusak.

Because this, meskipun many patient mendapat beberapa perbaikan through rehabilitasi, they continuously hidup with beberapa jenis cacat jangka panjang seperti kelumpuhan or gangguan memori. Tantangan treatment saat this terletak on "how mengisi cell-cell brain which hilang" this. Patient and their family very menginginkan treatment new which secara fundamental improve brain.
