Stem Cell Therapy for Post-Myocardial Infarction Heart Failure

  • Home

  • Stem Cell Therapy for Post-Myocardial Infarction Heart Failure

Heart failure occurring after a myocardial infarction (heart attack) is a serious, life-threatening condition.

With current treatments, although symptoms can be alleviated, damaged cardiac muscle (myocardium) cannot be restored to its original state. Therefore, in recent years, regenerative therapy using stem cells has garnered attention as a novel approach.

This advanced treatment offers the potential to "repair damaged hearts from within."

Post-Myocardial Infarction Heart Failure: Realities and Limitations

Risiko berkembang menjadi gagal jantung setelah infark miokard tinggi, dan karena pengobatan konvensional tidak dapat memulihkan otot jantung yang rusak, harapan terhadap terapi regeneratif meningkat.

Why does heart failure occur after myocardial infarction?

Following a myocardial infarction, many patients face a persistent risk of chronic heart failure. Although advancements in medical technology have improved survival rates from myocardial infarction, the number of survivors who subsequently develop heart failure continues to rise.

Specifically, in patients with severe heart failure, the 5-year survival rate is reported to be approximately 50%, comparable to the survival rates for cancer—a disease with an extremely serious prognosis.

Limitations of Conventional Heart Failure Treatment

Current heart failure treatment focuses on symptom management as follows:

  • Terapi farmakologis: Penghambat ACE, beta-blocker dll untuk mengurangi beban jantung
  • Rekonstruksi aliran darah: Kateter atau operasi bypass untuk meningkatkan aliran darah
  • Terapi perangkat: Alat pacu jantung atau jantung buatan untuk mendukung fungsi pompa

However, this does not entail the regeneration of the necrotic myocardium itself. The dead tissue forms scar tissue, which remains unable to contribute to the heart's pumping function.

As a result, an excessive load is placed on the remaining healthy myocardium, which eventually fatigues, leading to the continued progression of heart failure.

Heart Transplantation: A Last Resort Treatment, Yet Impractical

Heart transplantation can be a definitive treatment, but there are practical obstacles:

  • Chronic donor shortage
  • Risk of major surgery
  • Burden of lifelong immunosuppressive therapy

Due to these practical constraints, only a small fraction of patients are able to undergo this surgery.

New Hope, Therapy for Cardiac Regeneration

Thus, the era of being resigned to a deteriorating heart has concluded, and expectations for regenerative therapy to repair damaged myocardium have surged.

Particularly, Stem Cell Therapy is gaining prominence as a next-generation therapeutic option.

Item

  • The incidence of heart failure following myocardial infarction is increasing, and the 5-year survival rate is approximately 50%.
  • Conventional treatment cannot regenerate damaged cardiac muscle and cannot fully halt the progression of heart failure.
  • Heart transplantation is theoretically a definitive treatment, but in practice, options are limited.
  • The demand for regenerative therapies aimed at repairing damaged hearts is rapidly increasing.

New Possibilities of Stem Cell Therapy for Addressing Post-Myocardial Infarction Heart Failure

Sel miokardium yang mati akibat infark miokard tidak dapat beregenerasi secara alami. Terapi Sel Punca adalah opsi baru yang bertujuan untuk "memulihkan fungsi jantung yang hilang".

After myocardial infarction, the necrotic cardiac tissue undergoes scarring and permanently loses its original contractile function. This leads to a reduction in cardiac pump function and, over time, progresses to chronic heart failure.

Regenerating Necrotic Myocardium

Conventional medicine cannot regenerate lost myocardial tissue and only manages to alleviate the heart's burden through medication or mechanical devices. In contrast, Stem Cell Therapy involves administered cells that operate in the following ways to directly facilitate the restoration of cardiac function:

  • Tetap berada di sekitar lokasi nekrosis dan mengirim sinyal promosi regenerasi miokardium
  • Mendorong angiogenesis (pembentukan kapiler baru), sehingga meningkatkan pasokan oksigen ke miokardium
  • Menenangkan peradangan untuk menghambat remodeling (penurunan) jaringan jantung

This is expected to not only maintain and enhance pump function, but also prevent the progression of heart failure.

Why are WJ-MSCs derived from Wharton's Jelly suitable for cardiac applications?

Among the various types of stem cells, umbilical cord-derived mesenchymal stem cells (Wharton's jelly MSCs) are considered suitable for cardiac treatment due to the following characteristics:

  • Kemampuan promosi angiogenesis yang tinggi: Membantu pasokan oksigen miokardium dan mendukung kelangsungan hidup sel miokardium
  • Aksi anti-inflamasi yang kuat: Menekan peradangan berlebihan yang terjadi setelah infark miokard dan mencegah penurunan kondisi
  • Immunogenisitas rendah: Mudah diberikan meski dari orang lain, tidak mudah terjadi penolakan

Furthermore, early-passage umbilical cord MSCs, compared to senescent cells, exhibit enhanced activity and superior regenerative capabilities. Consequently, they are considered to be more effective in myocardial tissue repair.

Item

  • Stem Cell Therapy is a novel approach aimed at regenerating necrotic myocardium.
  • Not only does it maintain and enhance cardiac pump function, but it also contributes to preventing the progression of heart failure.
  • WJ-MSCs derived from the umbilical cord possess potent pro-angiogenic capabilities and robust anti-inflammatory actions, making them suitable for cardiac regeneration.

Mechanism of Stem Cell Therapy Operating in the Post-Myocardial Infarction Heart

Terapi Sel Punca berfungsi sebagai "pabrik faktor perbaikan" di dalam jantung yang terluka, mendorong regenerasi miokardium melalui promosi angiogenesis dan penghambatan peradangan. Hanya sebagian kecil yang langsung menggantikan miokardium, tetapi efek parakrin (faktor sitokina dan pertumbuhan yang dilepaskan) memainkan peran utama.

Stem cells function as a "cardiac therapeutic dispensary."

Necrotic myocardial tissue resulting from myocardial infarction has very limited capacity for spontaneous regeneration. In Stem Cell Therapy, cells administered to the heart not only directly engraft into the damaged region but also release various beneficial substances into the surrounding microenvironment to promote repair.

The specific effects are as follows:

  • Promosi angiogenesis: Membentuk kapiler baru, meningkatkan pasokan oksigen dan nutrisi ke miokardium
  • Penenangan peradangan: Menekan respons peradangan berlebihan yang terjadi setelah infark miokard
  • Penghambatan kematian sel miokardium: Mencegah apoptosis (kematian terprogram) sel miokardium yang terluka
  • Penghambatan fibrosis (penyarutan): Menghambat pembentukan luka, mempertahankan jaringan otot yang fleksibel
  • Aktivasi sel prekursor miokardium intrinsik: Merangsang sel cadangan yang tersembunyi di jantung untuk mendorong regenerasi spontan

In this way, stem cells are not merely cell replacements, but rather orchestrate cardiac healing through diverse mechanisms.

Why does WJ-MSC from Wharton's Jelly hold promise?

Wharton's jelly-derived mesenchymal stem cells (WJ-MSCs) demonstrate significant superiority in cardiac treatment in the following respects:

  • Kapasitas sekresi faktor perbaikan yang tinggi
    Dibandingkan dengan MSC sumsum tulang, produksi "faktor pertumbuhan hepatosit (HGF)" yang mendorong regenerasi miokardium sekitar 55 kali lebih banyak. HGF adalah faktor yang penting untuk kelangsungan hidup sel miokardium dan angiogenesis.
  • Sekresi sitokina dan faktor pertumbuhan yang beragam
    Secara melimpah melepaskan VEGF (faktor pertumbuhan endotel pembuluh darah), IGF-1 (faktor pertumbuhan seperti insulin), bFGF (faktor pertumbuhan fibroblas dasar), dll, mendukung perbaikan aliran darah dan perlindungan miokardium.
  • Aksi regulasi imun yang kuat
    Menekan ledakan peradangan setelah infark miokard dan mencegah kerusakan jaringan. Telah dilaporkan bahwa WJ-MSC menekan proliferasi sel T yang teraktivasi lebih kuat daripada MSC sumsum tulang.

In other words, WJ-MSC can simultaneously achieve myocardial regeneration, improved blood flow, and inflammation control, and can therefore be referred to as the "ideal cell for heart failure."

Item

  • Stem cells establish a reparative factor production hub within the heart, facilitating multifaceted healing.
  • Numerous effects can be expected, including angiogenesis, inhibition of inflammation, and myocardial protection.
  • WJ-MSC possess high secretory capacity and immunomodulatory capabilities, and are considered an ideal cell type for cardioprotection post-myocardial infarction.

Is Stem Cell Therapy Truly Efficacious? Evidence of Cardiac Function Restoration

Untuk gagal jantung pasca-infark miokard, Terapi Sel Punca telah melaporkan efek yang jelas dalam penelitian klinis, termasuk peningkatan fungsi ventrikel kiri (LVEF), perbaikan struktur jantung, dan bahkan penurunan angka kematian.

Effect of Cardiac Function Restoration

In patients undergoing Stem Cell Therapy, a demonstrable increase in left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) has been consistently reported. Specifically, in clinical trials utilizing umbilical cord-derived WJ-MSC, LVEF at 1 year increased by an average of 7 percentage points, and the difference compared to the conventional treatment group was statistically significant.

This restores cardiac pump function, which also leads to the alleviation of heart failure symptoms such as shortness of breath and fatigue.

Safeguarding Cardiac Structure

In a clinical trial conducted shortly after acute myocardial infarction, the group receiving umbilical cord mesenchymal stem cells demonstrated enhanced blood flow at the infarct site and improved survival rates, as well as the inhibition of cardiac enlargement (remodeling).

This demonstrates the potential to prevent cardiac deformation and functional decline that commonly occur after myocardial infarction.

Enhanced Survival Rate and Reduced Readmission

In a large-scale meta-analysis (17 trials, 1,684 participants), patients receiving MSC treatment demonstrated:

  • Mortality risk decreases by approximately 22%.
  • Left ventricular function (LVEF) increased by an average of +3.4%.
  • The risk of rehospitalization due to heart failure also decreased.

Additionally, symptom indicators (NYHA classification) and exercise capacity (6-minute walk test) significantly improved.

Item

  • Through Stem Cell Therapy, the left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) significantly increased, and an improvement in cardiac function can be expected.
  • It is possible to suppress post-myocardial infarction cardiac remodeling and prevent long-term functional decline.
  • Decrease in mortality rate and increase in hospitalization rate reported in clinical studies.
  • WJ-MSC specifically possesses superior regenerative and protective effects, and more potent therapeutic effects are anticipated.

Safety and Current Understanding of Adverse Effects of Stem Cell Therapy

Meskipun Terapi Sel Punca adalah pengobatan inovatif, penelitian klinis sejauh ini hampir tidak melaporkan efek samping serius. Keamanannya telah dikonfirmasi dalam uji klinis, dan terapi regeneratif yang dapat diberikan dengan aman kepada pasien gagal jantung sedang berkembang.

Given that Stem Cell Therapy is the most advanced regenerative therapy, the rigorous verification of its safety remains an ongoing process.

To summarize, pre-clinical and clinical studies to date indicate that Stem Cell Therapy is feasible and safe for heart failure patients.

Acute Complications of Stem Cell Administration?

In clinical trials involving the intravenous infusion of umbilical cord-derived MSCs, no serious adverse events were associated with the administration. Allergic reactions or shock during infusion, as well as acute immune rejection reactions, were not observed. Furthermore, it was demonstrated that antibodies against donor cells were not generated following transplantation.

Therefore, unlike organ transplantation, there is no need to take immunosuppressive medication.

On Mid- to Long-Term Security

Even in long-term follow-up studies exceeding 18 months, no significant difference was observed between the Stem Cell Therapy group and the control group regarding the incidence of arrhythmias, abnormalities in tumor markers, and mortality from new malignancies.

In patients following myocardial infarction, MSC treatment did not increase the risk of fatal arrhythmias or worsening heart failure, and no major safety concerns were reported compared to standard treatment.

Stem Cell Therapy and Risk of Tumor Transformation

In novel therapeutic methods, the "risk of tumorigenesis" is often a concern, yet no tumor formation has been reported in treatments utilizing mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs).

Conversely, it has been demonstrated that MSCs possess anti-inflammatory and anti-tumor effects. No increase in cancer incidence was observed in the Stem Cell Therapy group; rather, a decrease was even suggested. This is attributed to the suppressive effect of MSCs on abnormal immune responses.

Thus, based on the clinical data accumulated to date, Stem Cell Therapy utilizing WJ-MSCs derived from Wharton's jelly is considered to have an excellent safety profile.

Certainly, regenerative therapy is a relatively nascent field, and further long-term data will be required. However, at present, it is considered a promising therapeutic approach that can be administered without substantial risk to patients.

Item

  • Clinical research to date has scarcely reported serious adverse events from Stem Cell Therapy.
  • Immune rejection is extremely rare, and immunosuppressants are not required.
  • There is no difference in arrhythmia rates, exacerbation of heart failure, and risk of new cancer.
  • There have been no reports of tumor formation from mesenchymal stem cell therapy to date.
  • Based on clinical research findings, Stem Cell Therapy is classified as a regenerative therapy with an excellent safety profile.

Transforming the Future of Regenerative Therapy - Addressing Post-Myocardial Infarction Heart Failure

Stem Cell Therapy for post-myocardial infarction heart failure is a novel approach that repairs the injured heart from within. By enabling myocardial regeneration, a process previously considered impossible with traditional treatments, this therapy holds the potential to fundamentally transform patient longevity and quality of life.

Specifically, Wharton's jelly-derived mesenchymal stem cells are attracting global attention as a promising therapeutic for next-generation heart failure treatment, owing to their high regenerative capacity and favorable safety profile.

Restoring robust cardiac function to hearts compromised by myocardial infarction. Stem Cell Therapy is transforming this once-aspirational vision into a tangible reality.

In ongoing clinical studies both domestically and internationally, favorable results continue to be reported, including improvements in left ventricular function, a reduction in the risk of rehospitalization, and improved survival rates.

Instead, an avenue for regenerating their own hearts began to open in earnest, replacing heart transplants or artificial hearts, which had previously been the sole hope.
