Why Gagal Heart Happen Setelah Infark Miokard?

After through infark miokard, many patient face risk gagal heart kronis (Heart Failure) which berkelanjutan. Although progress technology medical has enhance tingkat kelangsungan hidup from infark miokard, realitasnya is jumlah penyintas which menderita gagal heart continuously increased.

Especially on patient with gagal heart berat, angka kelangsungan hidup 5 years dilaporkan sekitar 50%, setara with tingkat kelangsungan hidup cancer—disease with prognosis which very serius.

Keterbatasan Therapy Gagal Heart Konvensional

Treatment gagal heart time this berfokus on healing symptom sebagai berikut:

  • Terapi farmakologis: Penghambat ACE, beta-blocker dll untuk reduce beban heart
  • Rekonstruksi aliran blood: Kateter or operasi bypass untuk improve aliran blood
  • Terapi perangkat: Alat pacu heart or heart artificial untuk mendukung fungsi pompa

However, all this not melibatkan recovery muscle heart which nekrotik that sendiri. Bagian which mati became tissue parut (luka), tetap not can berkontribusi on fungsi pompa heart.

Akibatnya, beban berlebihan jatuh on muscle heart normal which tersisa, muscle which healthy this pun finally became lelah, and gagal heart continuously developed.
