  • Stem cell therapy untuk Osteoarthritis

Osteoarthritis (OA) is disease sendi which very umum when bone rawan sebagai 'bantalan sendi' experience keausan, menyebabkan nyeri and difficulty in menggerakkan sendi.

Treatment which time this performed (therapy olahraga, drug penghilang sense pain, injeksi asam hialuronat, dll) especially fokus on 'therapy simptomatik' which hanya menekan sense pain, and not can mengembalikan bone rawan which rusak to condition semula.

Which now get perhatian is 'regeneration bone rawan' through 'stem cell therapy'.

Which get perhatian special is 'mesenchymal stem cell which berasal from Wharton's jelly (WJ-MSC)'. This is stem cell which diekstraksi from Wharton's jelly, tissue seperti gel in tali pusar bayi, and karakteristiknya is can obtain cell which young and healthy in jumlah large.

WJ-MSC this, bahkan if provided oleh orang lain, not mudah menimbulkan penolakan imun, and diharapkan sebagai 'bahan for therapy regenerative' with beban small on tubuh patient.

Efek Peringan Nyeri Sendi and Perbaikan Fungsi Sendi

Injeksi intra-artikuler stem cell Wharton's jelly memiliki effectiveness deep reduce nyeri and memulihkan fungsi sendi berdasarkan data klinis that ada.

With menyuntikkan stem cell which berasal from Wharton's jelly (WJ-MSC) to in sendi, nyeri lutut reduced and movement in kehidupan sehari-day became more mudah. Report about hal this semakin increase.

Sebagai contoh, in suatu research in which patient with osteoarthritis lutut disuntik with WJ-MSC, setahun then nyeri reduced significant, and skor which show ease pergerakan sendi (skor WOMAC and indeks Lequesne) also increased with jelas. Kepuasan patient which feel became "more good" also increased, and this konsisten with perbaikan on pemeriksaan MRI objektif and marker peradangan in cairan sinovial.

In uji clinical lainnya (Fase I), when WJ-MSC disuntikkan beberapa kali to patient with symptom which relatif ringan, hanya in 3 month skor nyeri (VAS) reduced from rata-rata 6,0 became 3,5. Evaluasi movement lutut also increased secara dramatis from 26,0 became 8,5.

Many patient merasa "more mudah naik down tangga" and "berbelanja not lagi became beban", and they reported bahwa ketidaknyamanan in kehidupan because nyeri sendi reduced.

Result this show bahwa therapy WJ-MSC not hanya reduce nyeri tetapi also have possibility for "improve condition sendi that sendiri". With kata lain, can dikatakan sebagai treatment which provide pemicu perbaikan on "bantalan sendi" which mulai rusak.

POIN MAIN

  • Suntikan WJ-MSC secara significant reduce nyeri (skor VAS) berdasarkan report research
  • Skor fungsi sendi (WOMAC and indeks Lequesne) also increased with jelas
  • MRI and marker biokimia also show change positif on condition sendi
  • Proceed and aktivitas sehari-day became more mudah, enhance quality of life
  • Not hanya meredakan symptom tetapi also show possibility for mengembalikan "tubuh which can move"

Bukti about Regeneration and Perbaikan Bone Rawan

WJ-MSC tidak hanya menekan nyeri tetapi juga show efek regenerasi that "mengembangkan back" bone rawan that have aus.

Daya tarik large from stem cell therapy is possibility for improve bone rawan which rusak in sendi that sendiri. In research latest use stem cell Wharton's jelly (WJ-MSC), "regeneration bone rawan" this true-true terlihat.

In suatu research, setahun after menyuntikkan WJ-MSC to in sendi lutut, jelas terlihat on MRI bahwa ketebalan bone rawan lutut increased. Especially on bagian persendian tibia and femur which mudah experience beban berat, terlihat regeneration which jelas.

More lanjut, when diperiksa use method "pemetaan T2" which can mengevaluasi quality bone rawan in MRI, terlihat bahwa bone rawan berubah from keadaan soft and aus became mendekati keadaan healthy and elastis.

Bahkan in pengamatan short 6-12 month, terlihat contoh "tissue seperti bone rawan new" terbentuk in bagian which dulunya aus, and keadaan bagian which mengeras in bawah bone rawan (bone subkondral) also increased.

This is change which very revolusioner, rarely terlihat in treatment before.

Selain that, bahkan in eksperimen hewan, treatment use WJ-MSC enhance regeneration bone rawan, and tissue which mirip with bone rawan healthy asli tercipta returned.

POIN MAIN

  • Dikonfirmasi on MRI bahwa ketebalan bone rawan increased
  • Pemetaan T2 also show "quality" bone rawan increased
  • There is contoh "tissue seperti bone rawan new" terbentuk in bagian which experience defisit
  • There is report bahwa keadaan bone in bawah bone rawan (bone subkondral) also increased
  • Bahkan in eksperimen hewan, regeneration bone rawan mendekati bone rawan asli which healthy has dikonfirmasi

Mekanisme Aksi Anti-inflammation and Modulasi Imun

WJ-MSC work sebagai "pengatur" that meredakan "lonjakan peradangan" in deep sendi and beralih to lingkungan that mengarah pada healing.

On osteoarthritis, peradangan happen on cairan sinovial and tissue in in sendi, which menyebabkan nyeri and kerusakan bone rawan. Stem cell Wharton's jelly (WJ-MSC) melepaskan many "zat anti-inflammation" for menekan peradangan this.

Zat which interesting perhatian special is IL-10 (Interleukin 10) and TGF-β (Transforming Growth Factor-β).

  • IL-10 menekan production berlebihan zat inflammation, menghentikan kerusakan tissue which became penyebab nyeri and pembengkakan.
  • TGF-β not hanya meringankan reaksi imun which berlebihan tetapi also help "factory perbaikan" bone rawan which rusak.

More lanjut, WJ-MSC mensekresikan VEGF (Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor) and HGF (Hepatocyte Growth Factor) for enhance aliran blood to daerah which rusak and support regeneration tissue.

From perspektif imun, WJ-MSC mengubah makrofag, "petugas keamanan" in sendi, from tipe penyerang (M1) became tipe perbaikan (M2), meredakan peradangan. Also, with memblokir keberuntungan cell T and cell B sebagai cell imun and enhance cell T regulation (Treg), menekan reaksi imun which berlebihan.

With cara this, WJ-MSC menyesuaikan tissue sitokin (pesan cell) in in sendi, work seperti saklar which mengubah from "kerusakan" became "perbaikan".

Actually, after penyuntikan WJ-MSC, zat which menyebabkan peradangan in cairan sinovial (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6) reduced, and keseimbangan adipokin (leptin, adiponektin), zat which berasal from lemak, also increased.

POIN MAIN

  • WJ-MSC mensekresikan zat anti-inflammation seperti IL-10 and TGF-β for menekan peradangan
  • VEGF and HGF support perbaikan aliran blood and regeneration tissue
  • Mengubah makrofag from tipe "penyerang" became tipe "perbaikan", menyesuaikan lingkungan sendi
  • Menekan keberuntungan cell T and cell B, menekan peradangan autoimmune
  • Akibatnya, memutus loop peradangan in sendi and create lingkungan for regeneration

Peningkatan Quality of life (QOL) Patient

Seiring with peringan nyeri from terapi WJ-MSC, laporan semakin increase bahwa kehidupan sehari-day and aktivitas hobi can dinikmati back.

Nyeri which caused oleh osteoarthritis membatasi all movement in kehidupan sehari-day, make orang menjauh from keluar rumah, hobi, and bahkan interaksi sosial. Stem cell therapy Wharton's jelly (WJ-MSC) get perhatian sebagai cara for overcome "obstacle hidup" this.

Sebagai contoh, seorang patient merasa nyeri reduced after treatment WJ-MSC, and feel change positif seperti "I can proceed-jalan lagi" and "I can naik tangga without takut".

In research clinical aktual, peningkatan nilai objektif berikut also dilaporkan:

With kata lain, this berarti not hanya "sense pain disappear" tetapi also "kehidupan which autentik returned". Also, when nyeri reduced, orang can sleep more nyenyak, and kecemasan and depresi mood also reduced in beberapa kasus.

"Perbaikan tubuh" also mengarah on "perbaikan liver" - therapy WJ-MSC can dikatakan sebagai treatment which support keduanya.

POIN MAIN

  • After therapy WJ-MSC, many patient experience peningkatan QOL (quality of life) which significant
  • Indikator seperti SF-36 and SF-12 show peningkatan health fisik and mental
  • Report impact positif on kehidupan seperti "I can proceed lagi" and "I can perform hobi"
  • Peringan nyeri also mengarah on peningkatan quality sleep and stabilitas mood
  • Therapy WJ-MSC not hanya treatment nyeri tetapi also terlibat in "rekonstruksi kehidupan"

About Keamanan and Efek Samping

Stem cell Wharton's jelly (WJ-MSC) dilaporkan memiliki keamanan high and efek samping that sangat minimal.

When hear "stem cell therapy", beberapa orang mungkin khawatir "is there is efek samping?" or "is tubuh not will refuse?". However, WJ-MSC has through many uji clinical in seluruh world, and mayoritas from they mengkonfirmasi "keamanan high".

Cell from orang lain jarang ditolak

WJ-MSC is stem cell which diekstraksi from tali pusar bayi. Biasanya, when cell orang lain dimasukkan to in tubuh, system imun can mulai menyerang sebagai "benda asing". Tetapi WJ-MSC have sifat which not mudah memicu reaksi imun this.

This because molekul which became "tanda imun" on permukaan cell (seperti MHC kelas II) very few. Actually, in uji clinical which has performed sejauh this, not there is report about problem serius akibat penolakan.

Is ada kekhawatiran tentang cancer?

Because stem cell have "kekuatan for developed biak" which strong, beberapa orang mungkin khawatir "is this will became cancer?". Tetapi WJ-MSC has dikonfirmasi in research sejauh this sebagai "non-tumorigenik", yaitu not have sifat membentuk tumor.

Not there is report about pembentukan benjolan after pemberian, and not there is perkalian abnormal which terdeteksi. Verifikasi sedang take place oleh beberapa institution research in in and luar negeri, and time this not there is risk serius which dikonfirmasi.

Efek samping ringan mungkin happen

After treatment, pembengkakan ringan in place suntikan, demam ringan, or kelelahan ringan can happen. However, kebanyakan from this pulih secara natural in beberapa day.

Actually, in satu uji clinical, efek samping sementara terlihat on 5 from 14 orang, tetapi not there is which memburuk and semuanya pulih secara natural.

With kata lain, although possibility efek samping not nol, some large is ringan and not disturb kehidupan sehari-day.

POIN MAIN

  • WJ-MSC rarely menimbulkan penolakan bahkan from cell orang lain
  • Efek samping berat seperti cancer (transformation tumor) not dilaporkan
  • There is beberapa reaksi ringan (pembengkakan, demam, kelelahan) tetapi pulih with fast
  • Because treatment is suntikan, beban on tubuh small
  • Research for penggunaan which more safe and more pasti diharapkan will continuously developed
