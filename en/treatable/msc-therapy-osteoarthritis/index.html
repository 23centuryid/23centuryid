Stem Cell Therapy for Osteoarthritis

  • Homepage

  • Stem Cell Therapy for Osteoarthritis

Osteoarthritis (OA) is a highly prevalent joint disease involving the degeneration of articular cartilage, which typically functions as a joint cushion, resulting in pain and restricted joint movement.

Current treatments (e.g., exercise therapy, analgesics, hyaluronic acid injections, etc.) primarily focus on 'symptomatic therapy,' which merely suppresses pain and cannot restore damaged cartilage to its original condition.

Currently, the focus is on cartilage regeneration through stem cell therapy.

Of particular interest are 'Wharton's jelly-derived mesenchymal stem cells (WJ-MSCs)'. These are stem cells extracted from Wharton's jelly, a gel-like tissue within the umbilical cord, and are characterized by their ability to yield a large quantity of young and healthy cells.

These WJ-MSCs, even when allogeneic, exhibit low immunogenicity and are expected to serve as 'regenerative therapeutic materials' with minimal burden on the patient's body.

Effect of Joint Pain Relief and Joint Function Improvement

Injeksi intra-artikuler sel punca Wharton's jelly memiliki efektivitas dalam mengurangi nyeri dan memulihkan fungsi sendi berdasarkan data klinis yang ada.

Intra-articular injection of Wharton's jelly-derived mesenchymal stem cells (WJ-MSCs) has been shown to alleviate knee pain and improve daily functional mobility. A growing body of literature supports these observations.

For instance, in a study where patients with knee osteoarthritis were injected with WJ-MSC, one year later, significant pain reduction was observed. Scores reflecting ease of joint movement (WOMAC scores and Lequesne index) also demonstrated clear improvement. Patient satisfaction, with individuals reporting feeling "better," also increased, and this was consistent with improvements observed in objective MRI examinations and inflammatory markers in the synovial fluid.

In a separate Phase I clinical trial, repeated administration of WJ-MSC to patients with relatively mild symptoms resulted in a reduction of the Visual Analog Scale (VAS) pain score from an average of 6.0 to 3.5 within 3 months. Furthermore, knee function assessment significantly improved from 26.0 to 8.5.

Many patients report experiencing greater ease with stair climbing and find shopping less burdensome. They also indicate a reduction in the discomfort in daily life caused by joint pain.

These results indicate that WJ-MSC therapy not only reduces pain but also has the potential to repair the joint's intrinsic condition. In other words, it can be described as a treatment that stimulates repair in degenerating articular cartilage.

Key point

  • WJ-MSC injection significantly reduced pain (VAS score), according to research reports.
  • Joint function scores (WOMAC and Lequesne index) also showed a clear increase.
  • MRI and biochemical markers also demonstrated improvements in joint condition.
  • The ease of walking and daily activities is enhanced, contributing to an improved quality of life.
  • It not only provides symptomatic relief but also demonstrates the potential to restore 'a movable body'.

Evidence of Cartilage Regeneration and Repair

WJ-MSC tidak hanya menekan nyeri tetapi juga menunjukkan efek regenerasi yang "mengembangkan kembali" tulang rawan yang sudah aus.

A major appeal of stem cell therapy is the potential to repair damaged cartilage within the joint itself. In recent research utilizing Wharton's jelly stem cells (WJ-MSC), this "cartilage regeneration" was indeed observed.

In a study, one year after the injection of WJ-MSC into the knee joint, MRI scans clearly demonstrated an increase in knee cartilage thickness. Notably, significant regeneration was observed specifically in the tibiofemoral joint regions susceptible to heavy loading.

Furthermore, upon examination using the T2 mapping method, which assesses cartilage quality via MRI, it was observed that the cartilage transitioned from a soft and degenerated state to one approaching a healthy and elastic condition.

Even within a brief 6-12 month observation period, instances of "new cartilage-like tissue" formation are observed in previously degenerated areas, and the condition of the subchondral bone (the hardened bone beneath the cartilage) also improved.

This represents a profoundly revolutionary change, rarely witnessed in previous medical practice.

Furthermore, even in animal models, WJ-MSC treatment promoted cartilage regeneration, leading to the regeneration of tissue resembling native healthy cartilage.

Key point

  • Increased cartilage thickness was confirmed on MRI.
  • T2 mapping also indicates improved cartilage quality.
  • Evidence of new cartilage-like tissue formed in the deficient area.
  • It has been reported that the condition of the bone underlying the cartilage (subchondral bone) also improved.
  • Even in animal experiments, cartilage regeneration closely resembling healthy native cartilage has been confirmed.

Mechanisms of Anti-inflammatory Action and Immune Modulation

WJ-MSC bekerja sebagai "pengatur" yang meredakan "lonjakan peradangan" di dalam sendi dan beralih ke lingkungan yang mengarah pada penyembuhan.

In osteoarthritis, inflammation occurs in the synovial fluid and intra-articular tissues, leading to pain and cartilage damage. Wharton's jelly mesenchymal stem cells (WJ-MSCs) release numerous anti-inflammatory factors to suppress this inflammation.

Of particular interest are IL-10 (Interleukin 10) and TGF-β (Transforming Growth Factor-β).

  • IL-10 suppresses the overproduction of inflammatory mediators, thereby halting tissue damage responsible for pain and swelling.
  • TGF-β not only mitigates overactive immune responses but also facilitates the regeneration of damaged cartilage.

Furthermore, WJ-MSCs secrete VEGF (Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor) and HGF (Hepatocyte Growth Factor) to enhance blood flow to the injured region and support tissue regeneration.

From an immunological perspective, WJ-MSCs modulate macrophages, shifting them from a pro-inflammatory (M1) to a reparative (M2) phenotype, thereby mitigating inflammation. Furthermore, by inhibiting the activity of T and B immune cells and enhancing regulatory T cell (Treg) function, they suppress excessive immune responses.

In this manner, WJ-MSCs modulate the cytokine network (cell signaling molecules) within the joint, functioning as a switch that transitions from "damage" to "repair."

Notably, following WJ-MSC injection, pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6) in the synovial fluid decreased, and the balance of lipid-derived adipokines (leptin, adiponectin) also improved.

Key point

  • WJ-MSC secrete anti-inflammatory factors such as IL-10 and TGF-β to suppress inflammation.
  • VEGF and HGF support improved blood flow and tissue regeneration.
  • Reprogramming macrophages from an inflammatory to a reparative phenotype, thereby modulating the joint microenvironment.
  • Suppressing T and B cell activity, suppressing autoimmune inflammation.
  • Ultimately, this disrupts the cycle of inflammation within the joint and fosters an environment conducive to regeneration.

Improving Patient Quality of Life (QOL)

Seiring dengan peringan nyeri dari terapi WJ-MSC, laporan semakin bertambah bahwa kehidupan sehari-hari dan aktivitas hobi dapat dinikmati kembali.

Pain caused by osteoarthritis severely restricts daily movements, leading individuals to withdraw from outdoor activities, hobbies, and even social interactions. Wharton's jelly stem cell therapy (WJ-MSC) is garnering attention as a potential method to address these profound limitations on daily living.

For instance, a patient reported a reduction in pain following WJ-MSC treatment, along with positive functional improvements such as restored ambulation and confidence in stair climbing.

In actual clinical studies, increases in the following objective measures were also reported:

  • The total score on the SF-36 Health Questionnaire increased by approximately 25% compared to pre-treatment.
  • On the SF-12 (a measure of physical and mental health-related Quality of Life), the score increased from 39.0 to 46.0.
  • Even on a Likert scale evaluating subjective symptom improvement, nearly all patients reported "improved."

In other words, this signifies not only the alleviation of pain but also the restoration of an authentic life. Furthermore, as pain subsides, individuals can achieve more restful sleep, and anxiety and depressive symptoms are also diminished in some cases.

Physical restoration also extends to inner well-being – WJ-MSC therapy can be described as a treatment that supports both.

Key point

  • Following WJ-MSC therapy, many patients experienced a significant improvement in QOL (quality of life).
  • Indicators such as SF-36 and SF-12 demonstrate improvements in physical and mental health.
  • Reports of positive life impacts, such as "I can walk again" and "I can pursue hobbies."
  • Pain relief also leads to improved sleep quality and mood stability.
  • WJ-MSC therapy is not merely pain treatment but also contributes to "life reconstruction."

On Safety and Side Effects

Sel punca Wharton's jelly (WJ-MSC) dilaporkan memiliki keamanan tinggi dan efek samping yang sangat minimal.

The mention of "stem cell therapy" often raises concerns for some regarding potential side effects or the risk of bodily rejection. However, WJ-MSC has undergone numerous clinical trials worldwide, and the vast majority of these have confirmed its high safety profile.

Allogeneic cells are rarely rejected.

WJ-MSCs are stem cells derived from neonatal umbilical cords. Typically, when allogeneic cells are introduced into the body, the immune system may initiate an attack, recognizing them as foreign. However, WJ-MSCs possess properties that do not readily trigger such an immune response.

This is attributed to the very low expression of immune-recognition molecules on the cell surface (such as MHC class II). Moreover, no serious complications due to rejection have been reported in clinical trials conducted thus far.

Are there any concerns about cancer?

Given the potent proliferative capacity of stem cells, concerns may arise regarding their potential for malignant transformation. However, WJ-MSCs have been consistently confirmed in research to date as non-tumorigenic, meaning they do not possess tumor-forming properties.

There have been no reports of lump formation following administration, and no abnormal proliferation has been detected. Verification is currently underway by several national and international research institutions, and at present, no serious risks have been confirmed.

Mild side effects may occur.

Post-treatment, mild swelling at the injection site, low-grade fever, or mild fatigue may occur. However, most of these reactions typically resolve spontaneously within a few days.

Notably, in a single clinical trial, transient adverse effects were observed in 5 of 14 participants; however, none deteriorated, and all experienced spontaneous recovery.

In other words, while a non-zero possibility of side effects exists, the majority are mild and do not disrupt daily life.

Key point

  • WJ-MSC rarely elicit rejection even from allogeneic cells.
  • Serious adverse effects such as cancer (tumor transformation) have not been reported.
  • Mild reactions (swelling, fever, fatigue) were observed, but recovery was rapid.
  • As the treatment is an injection, the physical burden is minimal.
  • Research for safer and more certain applications is expected to continue to advance.

Comparison with MSC from Other Sources (Differences from Adipose Tissue and Bone Marrow)

Sel punca Wharton's jelly (WJ-MSC) diperhatikan sebagai pengobatan yang meringankan nyeri sendi, memiliki kekuatan luar biasa untuk memperbaiki tulang rawan yang rusak, dan aman digunakan.

Gentle treatment with minimal physiological burden.

WJ-MSCs are stem cells that can be extracted from Wharton's jelly, a tissue located within the umbilical cord of a newborn. This tissue is typically discarded after birth, thereby posing no burden to either the donor or the recipient.

Methods for extracting stem cells from bone marrow or adipose tissue necessitate needle aspiration or surgical intervention. However, WJ-MSCs are not associated with such pain or risks.

The benefits can be confidently utilized, even when derived from allogeneic cells.

Typically, the introduction of allogeneic cells into the body carries a risk of rejection. However, WJ-MSCs exhibit low immunogenicity, meaning they are not readily identified as foreign. This characteristic allows for the confident use of cells provided by another individual.

This offers the advantage of earlier treatment initiation.

Cells with a special capacity to regenerate cartilage.

In osteoarthritis, pain arises from the degeneration of articular cartilage. WJ-MSC is reported to possess high potency for regenerating this degenerated cartilage.

Indeed, there is a growing number of reports, both domestically and internationally, indicating 'increased joint thickness' and 'smoother movement'.

Key point

  • Safely extractable cells at birth, without surgical intervention.
  • Infrequent rejection, even from allogeneic sources, enables rapid deployment.
  • Repairing damaged cartilage, working on the root cause of pain
  • Not only does it alleviate pain, but it can also be aimed at achieving a state of "joint mobility."

Conclusion

The use of Wharton's jelly-derived mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) in the treatment of osteoarthritis represents a novel regenerative therapeutic strategy, potentially offering a dual benefit of symptomatic relief and cartilage regeneration.

From current literature, WJ-MSC therapy has achieved promising results in alleviating joint pain, improving function, and cartilage repair. Its effects may also extend to disease-modifying effects through anti-inflammatory and immune modulation.

In terms of security, it is excellent overall, and has been confirmed to be feasible without serious adverse effects.

However, limitations exist regarding the sample size and uncertainty persists concerning long-term outcomes, and the accumulation of evidence through further large-scale randomized controlled trials and long-term follow-up studies is anticipated.

As a treatment aimed at improving patient quality of life (QOL) and preventing future artificial joint replacement, WJ-MSC therapy holds the potential to open new horizons in the management of osteoarthritis, and further research is highly anticipated.
