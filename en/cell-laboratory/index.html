Tentang PIC/S (Pharmaceutical Inspection Co-operation Scheme)

PIC/S is organization which merumuskan standard international for memastikan quality and keamanan produk drug. Time this more from 50 country and wilayah, including UE, AS, and Jepang, became anggotanya, mendorong standardisasi GMP secara international.

Tentang cGMP and cGTPs

cGMP (Current Good Manufacturing Practice) and cGTPs (Current Good Tissue Practices) is standard international for guarantee keamanan and mutu produk stem cell.

cGMP menekankan "pengendalian mutu on seluruh process production", sedangkan cGTPs bertujuan on "pengelolaan mutu and keamanan produk cell and tissue", which keduanya important for memastikan keandalan Stem cell therapy.

Why cGMP and cGTPs important?

  • Menjamin keamanan stem cell: Meminimalkan risk kontaminasi bakteri/virus and mempertahankan mutu that konsisten
  • Kesesuaian with standar international: Memenuhi persyaratan regulator seperti FDA (AS), EMA (UE), and NPRA (Malaysia)
  • Konsistensi mutu: Pemeriksaan and pengelolaan that ketat untuk memastikan setiap lot produksi give hasil that serupa
