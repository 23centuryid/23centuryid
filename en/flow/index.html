Our Registration Process

  • LANGKAH
    01

    Contact Us & Free Consultation

    Please contact us via our contact form. Our team of experts will listen to your needs and health condition, then explain the treatment options in detail. The consultation is completely free.
  • LANGKAH
    02

    Document delivery is required.

    To consider treatment, we ask you to submit medical documentation. This includes completing a medical form and recent test results or a diagnosis letter. These documents will help our doctor make an an informed decision.
  • LANGKAH
    03

    Expert's Care Plan

    Based on the information you provided, our medical regeneration specialist will plan the most suitable treatment for you. We will explain the stem cell injection method, the number of sessions, and the expected benefits.
  • LANGKAH
    04

    Estimated Cost

    We will provide a cost estimate based on the treatment plan. All costs (including treatment costs and travel support) are clearly explained. We will discuss with you until you are satisfied with the explanation. Payment in Rupiah is possible—no international transfer is needed.
  • LANGKAH
    05

    Schedule Adjustment & Early Reservation

    After you agree with the content and cost of the treatment, we will ask for your desired travel time and make an initial reservation for the treatment date.
  • LANGKAH
    06

    Payment of Maintenance Fees

    We require payment for the treatment fee two weeks prior to the treatment date. We accept bank transfers (to a domestic account in Indonesia).
  • LANGKAH
    07

    Trip to Malaysia

    Fly to Kuala Lumpur International Airport according to your booked schedule. Please arrive in Malaysia one day before your treatment—we are unable to perform treatment on the day of arrival.
  • LANGKAH
    08

    Local Clinic Care

    You will receive stem cell therapy at 23C. We will arrange transportation from your accommodation to the clinic. This treatment is administered non-invasively, such as an infusion, taking approximately 1-2 hours. We provide a dedicated lounge area with snacks and drinks for your comfort. Once the treatment is complete and you are feeling well, we will send you back to your hotel.

Schedule of Stay in Malaysia

  • HARI
    01

    Travel Day

    Fly from Indonesia to Malaysia (Kuala Lumpur). Upon arrival, get enough rest to recover from the journey and prepare for tomorrow's treatment. (No treatment on the day of arrival.)
  • HARI
    02

    Maintenance Day

    In the late morning, you will go to the clinic to receive stem cell therapy. We provide snacks and drinks in our dedicated lounge for your comfort and relaxation. After the treatment is complete and you are feeling well, we will transport you back to your hotel. In the evening, if you are feeling well, you can do some light sightseeing or other activities without any specific restrictions.
  • HARI
    03

    End of the day

    We will arrange transportation from your hotel to the airport. Flying from Kuala Lumpur back to Indonesia.

The above schedule is an illustrative example and can be customized based on your preferences and the type of treatment. For instance, if you wish to enjoy more sightseeing, we can extend your length of stay.

We believe that our relationship with patients extends beyond treatment, and we are committed to supporting your long-term health. Please do not hesitate to share even minor concerns.
